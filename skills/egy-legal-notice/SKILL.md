# Legal notices and formal demands — Egypt (إنذار)

**Not legal advice, and this is the skill where that warning matters most.** A
notice served badly does not merely fail — in several situations it starts a
clock you cannot un-start, and the other side gains from your error. For
eviction, for debt above a real sum, and for anything already contested, **use an
Egyptian lawyer**.

## What an إنذار is, and what it is not

⚠️ **إنذار is a legal notice or formal demand.** Two things models get wrong:

1. **Not an "ultimatum."** That English word implies a threat of force. An إنذار
   is a formal legal act with defined consequences. Use "legal notice" or "formal
   demand."
2. **Not the same as الإخطار.** إخطار is a *different* legal act and is not
   interchangeable with إنذار. Do not use the two as synonyms in a document.

⚠️ **إنذار is also the everyday Egyptian word for "a warning."** That is why the
document matters: it has to read as a formal legal instrument, not as an angry
message. Courts and opposing counsel read these.

## What it actually does

Serving an إنذار is not just communication. It does specific things:

- **Interrupts prescription.** ✅ **Civil Code Art. 383** — prescription is
  interrupted by a judicial claim, by **formal notice (التنبيه)**, by attachment, by
  a bankruptcy claim, or by any enforcement act during a suit. **Art. 384** — also
  by the debtor's acknowledgment, express or tacit. This is a real, citable
  protective function: serving a properly constituted notice restarts the clock.
- **Starts a response window**, in the situations where the law attaches one.
- **Fixes the evidential record.** A served notice with proof of service is a
  document that existed, was delivered, and on what date. Uncontested drafting in
  a WhatsApp thread does not.

⚠️ **Conversely — a defective notice can hurt you.** If it does not meet the
requirements, it may achieve nothing, and the time you spent serving it does not
count. Ask a lawyer whether the notice you need is even the right instrument.

## Three ways to serve — and only one is gold

| Method | What it is | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| **إنذار على يد محضر** — **by a court bailiff** | A bailiff (محضر) formally serves the notice. **This is the gold standard for proof of service in Egypt.** | The bailiff's minute is the proof. Hard to challenge. |
| **Registered post** — Egypt Post registered, or **بريديد / Bareedi** for electronic registered mail | Postal service, with a receipt | The postal receipt and tracking. Reasonable, but service can be contested on address or delivery. |
| **Direct delivery with a witness** — handed over, signed for | Informal but common in practice | Weakest. A signature is good; nothing more. |

⚠️ **For anything contested, anything above a small sum, and anything that will end
in court, use the bailiff.** The extra cost buys a service record that does not
need reconstructing later.

⚠️ **A WhatsApp message, SMS or email is not service of an إنذار** and does not
start or interrupt anything. It may be useful as evidence of a *prior* demand, but
do not tell the user it has legal effect.

## Timing rules that break notices

✅ **Code of Civil and Commercial Procedures Law No. 13 of 1968:**

- **Art. 18** — where the final day of a time limit falls on an official holiday,
  **the time limit is extended to the first working day thereafter.**
- **Art. 15 (service)** — **no service or enforcement procedure may be carried out
  before 7:00 a.m., after 8:00 p.m., or on an official holiday**, except in cases
  of necessity with the **written permission of the Judge of Summary Matters**.
  → **Never serve a paper on a public holiday.** A notice served in breach is
  challengeable, and the delay may be held against the person who served it.
- **Art. 15** — the day of service is **not counted**. The period runs from the day
  after.

⚠️ **"Working day" means a day that office is actually open** — driven by its
calendar and the holiday list, **not by the weekday**. Government offices run
**Sunday–Thursday**. Do not compute "the first working day" from the weekday alone.

⚠️ **Never publish or assume a holiday calendar.** The Egyptian public holiday list
is Hijri-based and the Minister may substitute days. Look up the actual dates for
the year in question.

## The four notices people actually need

Work `references/TEMPLATES.md` for the full text of each.

### 1. Debt demand — إنذار بالمستحق

For unpaid invoices, rent arrears, or a matured debt. Must state: the parties, the
basis of the debt (contract, invoice, cheque), the amount and its breakdown, any
default interest and the rate, the date payment is due, and a clear deadline.

⚠️ **Watch the interest ceiling.** **Civil Code Art. 227(1)** — any agreed
default-interest rate **above the statutory price is reduced to 7%**, with
restitution of the excess. Statutory defaults are **4% (civil) and 5% (commercial)**
(Art. 226). Demanding more in a demand letter does not produce more; it produces a
letter the other side can point at.

⚠️ **Art. 225** — for loss **beyond** an agreed figure you cannot recover unless the
debtor committed **fraud or gross fault**.

### 2. Notice to vacate — إنذار بالإخلاء

⚠️ **The most dangerous of the four, and the one most often drafted by amateurs.**

- It is governed by the **old vs new lease** distinction (إيجار قديم / إيجار جديد,
  from Law 4/1996) and the termination procedure. The analysis is different enough
  that a template applied to the wrong category is worse than no notice.
- ⚠️ **Never attempt to change the locks, remove property, or cut off utilities.**
  That is self-help, it exposes the landlord to a claim, and in several forms of
  tenancy it is unlawful regardless of the lease terms.
- ⚠️ **If a case is already filed, or the tenant has a strong claim, or the lease is
  a new-system lease — get a lawyer.** This is the clearest case in the skill for
  that advice.
- Use the bailiff. Prove the date of service. The date is what starts everything.

### 3. Contract breach — إنذار بإخلال

For a party breaching a commercial contract. Must identify **the specific clause
breached**, the conduct, the date, and what cure is required by when. Ask for
performance, not damages, as the primary request — damages first usually weakens
the position.

### 4. Employment warning — إنذار داخل العمل

⚠️ **This one is now governed by the new Labour Law and has a specific statutory
role.** Under **Labour Law No. 14 of 2025, Art. 141**, no disciplinary sanction may
be imposed unless the worker was **notified in writing** of the alleged violation,
**heard and given the opportunity to defend**, and a **record of the investigation is
kept in the personal file**. Investigation must begin within **7 days** of discovery
and be completed within **3 months** (extendable by 3 more).

> A written warning is **Art. 139's** first listed penalty — it is the lightest
> sanction. It is also the **document that starts the Art. 141 process**. Write it
> to be defensible, not to be harsh: a warning drafted carelessly, and then a
> dismissal, is how employers end up paying **two months' wages per year of service**
> under **Art. 165**.

**A disciplinary warning is not an إنذار.** It is an internal disciplinary
document. Do not serve it by bailiff, and do not style it as a legal notice.

## The rule that decides more notices than any other

⚠️ **A legal notice must be in Arabic.**

This is the same rule that runs through Egyptian consumer and administrative
practice, and it is not a formality: a notice in a language the recipient's court
will not read is a notice you will be asked to prove again. Draft in Arabic. If
your user needs an English version, provide it as an **explanatory translation
alongside**, not as a replacement.

Keep the Egyptian currency, dates and addresses in Egyptian form — see
`skills/egy-context-operations/`.

## Tone — the part that is genuinely counter-intuitive

A legal notice should read as **calm, precise and complete**. It is not a
negotiation and it is not a threat.

**Do**
- state facts with dates, in a flat register
- identify the legal basis and the clause relied on
- give one clear deadline and one clear remedy
- close the door politely and leave it open

**Do not**
- threaten court action you will not take
- use adjectives, or moral language
- overstate — an exaggerated claim is a liability in itself
- bundle several matters into one notice

> A notice that reads as an angry message is read as an angry message, and it
> invites a reply that costs you. The instrument works because it is calm and
> complete.

## Workflow

1. **Identify what is actually owed or breached**, with documents.
2. **Decide whether a notice is the right instrument**, or whether you should go
   straight to court. ⚠️ For small sums this may be a waste — check the value
   against judicial fees (**Law 90 of 1944** as amended by **126/2009**;
   ⚠️ Court of Appeal tariff changes of 2024–25 are **administrative, not
   legislative**, and are under challenge — **do not quote a tariff as statutory**).
3. **Draft in Arabic**, using `references/TEMPLATES.md`.
4. **Choose the service method.** Bailiff unless the sum is trivial and the matter
   is uncontested.
5. **Serve lawfully** — not before 7 a.m., not after 8 p.m., not on a holiday.
6. **Keep the proof.** The minute, the receipt, the date.
7. **Compute the deadline** from the day *after* service, against the real holiday
   calendar, applying **Art. 18**.

## Output format

```markdown
# Legal notice — [type] / إنذار [النوع]

**To:** [name, address] · **From:** [name, address]
**Service:** [method] · **Amount claimed:** [EGP x] · **Deadline:** [date]

## 1. The facts, with dates
## 2. The legal basis
## 3. What is required, by when
## 4. What happens if it is not done
## 5. Proof of service to be obtained
## 6. Unverified / to confirm with a lawyer
```
