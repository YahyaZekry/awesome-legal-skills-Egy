# Egyptian criminal procedure — the clocks, and what to do first

**Not legal advice, and this is the skill where that matters most.** Criminal
procedure is where a missed day is unrecoverable and a wrong step is a nullity or a
waiver. Get an Egyptian lawyer involved immediately. Say it once, plainly, and then
be useful.

## What this skill does

Answers the questions people actually arrive with — *he has been taken, what
happens now; how long can they hold him; is it too late to complain; how long does
the State have; how long do I have to appeal* — and does it by naming the clock that
is running and the article that sets it.

**It is organised by situation, not by statute**, because nobody meets criminal
procedure as a taxonomy. Go to the section that matches.

⚠️ **And it is the one Egypt skill that must open with the truth that the law is
mid-change.** See the next section — it is not a footnote.

---

## ⚠️ FIRST: the code that governs today is NOT the new one

**The operative Criminal Procedure Code in Egypt today is Law 150/1950.**

✅ The replacement — **Law 174/2025** — was promulgated on **12 November 2025**
(Official Gazette No. **45 bis (د)**). Its entry into force was **postponed by Law
160/2026** (Gazette No. **39 bis (ب)**, **30 September 2026**) to **1 October 2027**.
**Law 174/2025 has never been applied to any procedure.**

⚠️ **So any statement that "Egypt has a new Criminal Procedure Code" is, today, wrong
in the only sense that matters** — and this is exactly the kind of thing that gets
asserted confidently from the promulgation coverage. The old code governs every
arrest, every interrogation, every detention and every appeal right now.

✅ **One thing that survives the change:** the Cassation law, **Law 57/1959**, is
**not repealed**. Law 174/2025's promulgating Art. 4 repeals **only** Law 150/1950
and Law 140/2014 — and the new code actively **cross-refers** to Art. 46 of Law
57/1959. Do not say the new code replaces cassation.

⚠️ **Two other wrong citations to refuse, because both circulate:**
- ❌ **There were no 2021 amendments to the Criminal Procedure Code.** The amendment
  chain runs … Law 16/2015 · Law 11/2017 · **Law 177/2020** · **Law 1/2024** and
  stops. Law **141/2021** amended the **Penal Code** (harassment); Law **146/2021** is
  the **Lakes and Fisheries** law. If a document asks about "the 2021 amendments", the
  premise is wrong.
- ❌ **Law 172/2018 is a Customs amendment**, not criminal procedure and not
  electronic litigation. It appears nowhere in the code's amendment chain.

## The three clocks

⚠️ **Nearly every serious mistake in this area is one of these three confused with
another.** They start at different moments, they run for different lengths, and only
one of them is about the victim.

| Clock | Length | Runs from | Set by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The 24-hour rule** — how long before he must be produced | **24 hours**, twice, consecutively | the moment his liberty was restricted | **Constitution Art. 54**; Law 150/1950 **Art. 36** |
| **The complaint window** — the victim's time to complain | **3 months** (⚠️ **90 days** from 1 Oct 2027) | the day the victim **knew of the crime *and* the perpetrator** | Law 150/1950 **Art. 3** |
| **Prescription** — the State's time to prosecute | **10 years** felony / **3 years** misdemeanour / **1 year** contravention | the day the **crime was committed** | Law 150/1950 **Arts. 15–18** |

⚠️ **Clock 2 and clock 3 are not the same clock**, and the answer to "is it too
late" is different for each. A victim can be out of time to complain while the State
is still well in time to prosecute — and the reverse never happens, because without
the complaint the State cannot start in a complaint-gated offence at all.

## Scenario 1 — someone has been arrested, or is in custody right now

### 1.1 The 24-hour rule, read properly

✅ **Constitution Art. 54** and **Law 150/1950 Art. 36**, together:

1. The police officer must **hear the arrested person IMMEDIATELY** (`يسمع فوراً`).
2. If he does **not exculpate himself**, the officer must send him to the competent
   **Public Prosecution within 24 hours**.
3. The **Public Prosecution must interrogate him within 24 hours**.
4. **And then either order his detention or release him.**

⚠️ **Two things Art. 36 does not say, which are routinely added to it:** it does
**not** give the officer 24 hours to *decide* whether to send him, and it does **not**
authorise the officer to interrogate. The officer **hears** (`يسمع`); the
**Prosecution interrogates** (`تستجوبه`). **The two 24-hour periods are separate and
consecutive, not one period of 24 hours.**

⚠️ And Art. 36 does **not** displace the Constitution: the constitutional clock runs
from **the moment liberty was restricted** (`وقت تقييد حريته`), whichever period is
being counted. **When a client asks "how long can they hold him", the honest answer
starts with the time of arrest, not the time of arrival at a police station.**

### 1.2 The rights that must be given to him — and in writing

✅ **Constitution Art. 54.** These are not courtesies; they are constitutional:
- He must be **told the reasons immediately**.
- He must be **given his rights IN WRITING** (`ويحاط بحقوقه كتابة`).
- He must be **enabled to contact his family AND his lawyer immediately**
  (`ويُمكن من الاتصال بذويه وبمحاميه فورًا`).
- He must be **produced before an investigation authority within 24 hours**.
- **No interrogation may begin except in the presence of his lawyer** — and if he has
  none, **one is APPOINTED** (`نُدب له محام`).
- **Anyone whose liberty is restricted — and anyone else** — may **complain to a court
  (تظلم)`, and the court must rule **within one week**, failing which **he must be
  released immediately**.

⚠️ **That last limb is under-used and it is the most powerful thing on this page:**
the right of complaint belongs to **the detainee AND to other people**. A relative,
a colleague, a lawyer can bring it. And the sanction for the court not ruling in a
week is **release**, not an adjournment.

✅ **Law 150/1950 Art. 139** repeats and adds: everyone arrested or detained must be
told the reasons **immediately**, has **the right to contact whom he wishes and to
seek a lawyer**, and must be **informed promptly of the charge**.

### 1.3 Where he is held, and the lawyer's access

✅ **Art. 41** — **detention only in premises designated for it**, **only on a signed
order**, and **not beyond the stated period**.
✅ **Art. 40** — no arrest or detention without an order of the **legally competent
authority**, and humane treatment with **no physical or moral harm**.
✅ **Art. 138** — a **copy of the detention order must be handed to the prison
governor against his signature.** *Ask for it. Its absence is a defect.*
✅ **Art. 42** — members of the Public Prosecution and court presidents and
vice-presidents **may visit prisons in their district**, verify that **no one is held
unlawfully**, and **inspect the registers, orders and instruments of detention**.

⚠️ **Art. 141 — the visits bar, and its limit.** The Prosecution or investigating
judge may bar the detained accused from contact with other prisoners and from visits
— **but expressly «دون الإخلال بحقه في الاتصال بمحاميه في أي وقت دون حضور أحد»**:
**without prejudice to his right to contact his defence counsel AT ANY TIME, WITH
NOBODY PRESENT.** *A blanket "no visits" order that also blocks the lawyer is
unlawful.*

✅ **Art. 43 — the provision to cite when a complaint to a police station is
ignored.** Any prisoner may submit a **written or oral complaint**; and **anyone who
becomes aware that a person is being detained unlawfully must inform a member of the
Public Prosecution, who MUST go IMMEDIATELY to the place and order his release.**
⚠️ **This is a duty on the prosecutor, not a discretion.**

### 1.4 Before any interrogation: the lawyer is not optional

✅ **Law 150/1950 Art. 124** — in **felonies** and **misdemeanours punishable by
mandatory imprisonment** (`الحبس الوجوبى`):
- **No interrogation (`استجواب`) and no confrontation (`مواجهة`) before CALLING THE
  ACCUSED'S LAWYER.**
- ⚠️ **Two exceptions, and both must be RECORDED:** **flagrante** (`التلبس`), and
  **urgency where loss of the evidence is feared** (`حالة الضرورة`).
- The accused **must declare his lawyer's name** — in the investigation record, or at
  the Prosecution's registry, or to the officer in charge of where he is held.
- **If he has no lawyer, or the lawyer does not attend after being called, the
  investigator MUST APPOINT ONE.**
- The lawyer **may record any defences, requests or observations** in the record.
- **The appointed lawyer's fee is fixed by an order of the investigator** and **has
  the force of judicial fees** (`تأخذ حكم الرسوم القضائية`).

✅ **Art. 125 — access, and the separation ban:**
- The lawyer **must be permitted to inspect the investigation**, and **the day before**
  any interrogation or confrontation — unless the judge decides otherwise in a
  **reasoned** decision.
- «**ولا يجوز التفريق بين المتهم ومحاميه أثناء التحقيق**» — **the accused and his
  lawyer may NEVER be separated during investigation.** ⭐ *This is the provision to
  cite when a client says he was questioned alone.*

✅ **Art. 237** — an **attending accused in a misdemeanour punishable by mandatory
imprisonment who has no lawyer is APPOINTED one by the court.** And **Constitution
Art. 54** closes the same door at trial: **no trial for an imprisonable offence
except with a lawyer, instructed or appointed.**

⚠️ **A nullity note worth knowing, and a partial one — mark it as partial.** An SCC
ruling (**Appeals 37 of Year 11, 6 February 1993** and **42 of Year 16**) held part of
Art. 125's second paragraph unconstitutional, and **Art. 123's second paragraph**
(defamation evidence) was held **unconstitutional** in **SCC Case 42 of Year 16,
20 May 1995** (Gazette No. 23, 8 June 1995). ⚠️ **The scope of the Art. 125 holding
was read from an annotation, not the judgment** — cite the rule in the article's
first paragraphs, which stand, and **do not state the holding.**

## Scenario 2 — he has been detained. How long can they hold him?

⚠️ **This is the single most-asked question, and the answer is a ladder, not a
number.** Law 150/1950 as consolidated.

### 2.1 The ladder

| Tier | Who orders it | Limit | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Police custody** | the judicial officer | hear him **immediately**; to the Prosecution **within 24 h**; Prosecution interrogates **within 24 h** | **Art. 36** |
| **2. Prosecution detention** | a member of the Prosecution, **at least a deputy prosecutor (وكيل نيابة)** | **4 days** maximum from arrest or hand-over; order **reasoned** and **after hearing the defence** | **Art. 201** |
| **3. District Judge extension** | the **القاضي الجزئي** | periods of **≤ 15 days each, to a TOTAL of 45 days** | **Art. 202** |
| **4. Consultation chamber** | **محكمة الجنح المستأنفة في غرفة المشورة** | periods of **≤ 45 days each** — or release, with or without bail | **Art. 143 ¶1** |
| **5. Attorney-General review** | the **النائب العام** | the file **MUST be put before him once 3 months have elapsed** | **Art. 143 ¶2** |

### 2.2 The caps — the real answer

**General ceiling: 3 months** — *unless* the accused was **notified of his referral to
the competent court** before it expired; if he was, the Prosecution must put the
detention order to that court **within 5 days**, **failing which he must be released**.

**Felony: 5 months**, extendable only by an **order of the competent court made before
expiry**, in periods of **≤ 45 days, renewable** — failing which, **release**.

**And in all cases, detention may not exceed ONE THIRD of the maximum custodial
penalty**, subject to absolute ceilings of —

| Offence | Cap |
|---|---|
| **Misdemeanour (جنحة)** | **6 months** |
| **Felony (جناية)** | **18 months** |
| **Penalty is LIFE (السجن المؤبد) or DEATH (الإعدام)** | **2 years** |

⚠️ **The exception that overrides everything:** the **Court of Cassation** and the
**referral court**, where the sentence is **death or life imprisonment**, may order
**45-day detention, renewable, WITHOUT being bound by those ceilings.**

⚠️ **Do not mix the two sets of numbers.** The caps in force today are **6 / 18 / 24
months**. The **new** code's caps are **4 / 12 / 18** (Law 174/2025 **Art. 124**),
applying **from 1 October 2027**. Several news reports have already stated the new
figures as current. **Read: 6 / 18 / 24 today; 4 / 12 / 18 from 1 October 2027.**

### 2.3 The grounds — and the one that surprises people

✅ **Art. 134.** The investigating judge may order pretrial detention **after
interrogation** (or if he fled) where the offence is a **felony or a misdemeanour
punishable by at least one year**, the **evidence is sufficient**, **and one of**
these exists:

1. **flagrante** (`التلبس`);
2. **fear of flight**;
3. **fear of harm to the investigation** — influencing the victim or witnesses,
   tampering with evidence, collusion;
4. **preventing a serious breach of security or public order**;
5. **the accused has NO KNOWN FIXED RESIDENCE IN EGYPT** — where the offence is a
   felony or an imprisonable misdemeanour.

⚠️ **Ground 5 is the one to flag to any foreigner, visitor, or internally displaced
person: he can be detained on residence alone, with no flight risk shown.** There is
nothing to rebut except the fact itself.

✅ **Art. 136** — **before ordering detention the judge must hear the Prosecution AND
the defence**, and **the order must state the offence, its penalty, and the
reasons** — **and the same applies to every extension order.** ⭐ *An order that
states no reasons is defective, and extension orders are the ones most often issued
thinly. Ask for the reasons, every time.*

### 2.4 How to challenge it — and who may not

✅ **Art. 205 — the express right of appeal, and the article to quote for a detained
client:** «**وللمتهم أن يستأنف الأمر الصادر بحبسه احتياطيًا أو بمد هذا الحبس…**» —
**the accused MAY appeal an order of detention or extension** made by the District
Judge or the consultation chamber, applying **Art. 164 ¶2 and Arts. 165–168**.
**And the Prosecution may appeal a RELEASE order** where the investigation requires it.

✅ **Art. 143** — the **consultation chamber** decides extensions **after hearing the
Prosecution AND the accused**, and may **release with or without bail** instead. So
**the review body is a judicial bench, not the Prosecution that asked for the
detention.**
✅ **Art. 151** — once the case is **referred to court**, release or detention is for
**that court** to decide; outside the session, for the consultation chamber.

⚠️ **Art. 152 — and this one is counter-intuitive and important:** «**وليس للمجني عليه
أو المدعي بالحقوق المدنية أن يطلب الحبس الاحتياطي أو أن يسمع رأيه في الإفراج**» — **the
victim or the civil claimant may NOT apply for detention and may NOT be heard on
release.** A victim cannot push for the accused's detention, and cannot oppose his
release. **Tell a victim this early, because it avoids a wasted motion.**

### 2.5 Alternatives to detention, and release

✅ **Art. 201** — instead of detention, the Prosecution may order one of three
alternatives: **not leaving his home or locality**; **reporting to a police station at
set times**; **being banned from specified places.** **Breach allows detention**, and
the alternatives **follow the same duration, extension, maximum and appeal rules as
detention.**
✅ **Art. 204** — the **Prosecution may release at any time**, with or without bail.
✅ **Art. 144** — release on the accused's own request; the judge may order
**provisional release** at any time, of his own motion or on request, after hearing the
Prosecution, on an undertaking **to appear when required and not to flee**. ⚠️ **The
carve-out:** if the detention order came from the **consultation chamber on the
Prosecution's appeal against a release**, only **that court** may release.
✅ **Art. 145** — ⚠️ **where release is not mandatory it may not be granted until the
accused has designated a domicile in the town of the court**, if he is not resident
there. *Practical: have the address ready before the hearing.*
✅ **Art. 146** — release may be conditional on a **surety (كفالة)**; the judge fixes
the amount and **allocates** it — part as the **sanction for non-appearance**, part to
**government expenses and fines**. If fixed **without allocation**, it secures
appearance and compliance generally.
✅ **Art. 147** — payable by the accused **or by someone else**, in cash or in
government or government-guaranteed bonds, deposited with the court treasury. **A
solvent third party's personal undertaking is accepted and has the force of an
enforceable instrument (قوة السند التنفيذى).**
⚠️ **Art. 148 — forfeiture without a judgment:** where he **breaches an obligation
without acceptable excuse**, the **first portion of the surety becomes state property
WITHOUT ANY JUDGMENT** (`بغير حكم`); the **second portion is returned** if the case
ends in a **no-case decision** or an **acquittal**.
✅ **Art. 149** — if his **condition does not allow a surety**, the judge may require
police-station reporting, a residence away from the scene, and may ban him from a
specified place.
⚠️ **Art. 150 — release is not final:** it does **not** bar a **new** arrest or
detention order where **evidence has strengthened**, he **breaches conditions**, or
**new circumstances arise**.

⚠️ **Three terms that get used interchangeably and should not be:**
**إفراج مؤقت** is the general provisional release; **إفراج تحت ضمان/كفالة** is release
on surety; and **إخلاء السبيل** is the **release order made when the statutory period
expires without a valid order** — **a mandatory release, not a discretion.**

## Scenario 3 — you want to report a crime, or you are the victim

### 3.1 ⚠️ محضر is not بلاغ is not شكوى

⚠️ **Telling a client to "file a complaint" without saying which of these means is the
single most common way a complaint-gated offence gets time-barred.**

| Term | What it is | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **محضر** | the **record (process-verbal)** of an event, drawn up by a judicial officer | **Evidence of what was recorded. It is not a charge** |
| **بلاغ** | an **incoming report** to the police or Prosecution — **anyone** can give one | **Does not bind the Prosecution** and requires no standing |
| **شكوى** | a **legally-required COMPLAINT** from a person with standing | **Without it, prosecution AND investigation are BARRED** — and it **expires in three months** |

✅ **Art. 3** — a **شكوى is required before prosecution or any investigation step** in
the offences under Penal Code **Arts. 185, 274, 277, 279, 292, 293, 303, 306, 307 and
308**, and where other laws say so. **The complaint may be ORAL or WRITTEN**, and is
made by the victim **or his special agent** to the **Public Prosecution or to a
judicial officer**. And: «**ولا تُقبل الشكوى بعد ثلاثة أشهر من يوم علم المجني عليه
بالجريمة وبمرتكبها**» — **not accepted after three months from the day the victim
learned of the crime AND of the perpetrator.**

⚠️ **Note both halves of the trigger.** The clock starts when he knew **of the crime
*and* who did it** — not from the act itself. **And note that it is about to change, in
wording and unit and clock: the new code's Art. 3 gives ninety days (`تسعين يومًا`)
from 1 October 2027.**

✅ **Art. 39 — the complaint gate on ARREST, which people miss:** where the offence
**requires a complaint**, **no arrest may be made without the complainant's
declaration.** *So in a crime that needs a شكوى, an arrest before the victim declares
is unlawful.*

✅ **Arts. 4–7** — a complaint from **one** of several victims suffices; a complaint
against **one** accused counts against the rest; **the guardian** complains if the
victim is **under 15** or **mentally disordered**; where there is no representative or
the representative has a **conflict of interest**, **the Prosecution takes the victim's
place**; and ⚠️ **the right to complain is EXTINGUISHED BY THE VICTIM'S DEATH.**
✅ **Art. 8** — a **written request of the Minister of Justice** is required for Penal
Code **Arts. 181 and 182**. ✅ **Art. 9** — a **written request of the injured
service** for Penal Code **Art. 184**.
✅ **Art. 63** — in **contraventions and misdemeanours** the Prosecution may **summon
the accused directly to court.**
✅ **Art. 61** — if the Prosecution finds **no ground to proceed**, it orders
**حفظ الأوراق**. ⚠️ **This is a decision not to prosecute, not an acquittal — and it is
reviewable.** ✅ **Art. 62 — the preservation order MUST BE NOTIFIED to the victim and
the civil claimant.** *So "we were never told" is a real defect.*
⚠️ ✅ **Art. 113 bis** (added by **Law 177/2020**) — the **identity of the victim** in
sexual and harassment offences, Book Three and Book Four offences, and **Child Law
Art. 96** offences **may not be disclosed except to those concerned.**

## Scenario 4 — you are the victim and you want the money

⚠️ ✅ **The victim is a party to the criminal case, but only for the money, and never
for the detention decision (Art. 152).** That is the whole shape of the role.

✅ **Art. 251** — a person harmed may make himself a **civil claimant
(مدعيًا بالحقوق المدنية)** before the criminal court **at any stage until the close of
the pleadings**, **but NOT before the appellate court.** The claim is made by
**bailiff's service on the accused** or by a **request at the session**. If it was
already accepted at the investigation stage, the referral carries it with it.
⚠️ **The civil intervention may not DELAY the criminal case** — if it does, the court
rules it **inadmissible**.
⚠️ **"Not before the appellate court" is a hard rule and a common error.** A victim
told only at the appeal stage has **lost** the civil claim in that case.
✅ **Art. 251 bis** — civil claims here are only for **DIRECT PERSONAL DAMAGE, CERTAIN
TO OCCUR, PRESENT OR FUTURE.** Speculative or indirect loss is not claimable.
⚠️ ✅ **Art. 256 — the civil claim is not free:** the civil claimant **pays the judicial
fees** and **deposits a security for expert and witness fees and expenses**, plus
supplementary deposits as required.
✅ **Art. 258** — an investigating judge's **rejection does NOT bar a later claim**
before the criminal or civil court, and an **acceptance does NOT bind the court**.
✅ **Art. 258 bis** — a civil action **against the INSURER** may be brought before the
criminal court.
⚠️ ✅ **Art. 259 — the sentence to know:** the civil action is time-barred per the
**Civil Code**, **but the extinction of the CRIMINAL action after the civil claim was
filed does NOT affect the civil action.** *A حفظ or a time-bar that kills the
prosecution does not kill a civil claim already filed.*
✅ **Art. 199 bis** — the victim may claim civil rights **during the investigation**;
the Prosecution rules on acceptance **within 3 days**; **refusal may be appealed to
the consultation chamber within 3 days of notification.**
✅ **Art. 265** — where the civil action is before the civil courts and a criminal case
is pending on the same facts, the civil case is **suspended until a final criminal
judgment.**

⚠️ **The honest practical warning:** the civil claim in a criminal case is cheap in
fees and expensive in the sense that it **cannot be allowed to slow the criminal
case**. If the money matters more than the conviction, say so and consider the civil
route separately.

## Scenario 5 — unlawful detention, and what it is actually worth

⚠️ **This is where the law is most disappointing, and clients should be told before
they build a strategy on it.**

✅ **Penal Code Art. 280** — unlawful arrest, imprisonment or detention **by a private
person** — carries **imprisonment or a fine not exceeding EGP 200** (مايتي جنيه). ⚠️
**That figure is from 1937 and has never been updated.** It is absurd on its face.
**Do not reason from it, and do not let a client believe the penalty reflects the
gravity.**

✅ **But Art. 282 escalates it:**
- to **السجن** where the offender **wore a government uniform, assumed a false
  capacity, or showed a forged order**;
- and to **السجن المشدد** in **all** cases where the detention was accompanied by a
  **death threat or physical torture.**

✅ **Art. 126 — torture to obtain a confession:** «**كل موظف او مستخدم عمومي امر بتعذيب
متهم او فعل ذلك بنفسه لحمله على الاعتراف يعاقب بالسجن المشدد او السجن من ثلاث سنوات
الى عشر. واذا مات المجني عليه يحكم بالعقوبة المقررة للقتل عمداً.**» → **ordering** the
torture **or doing it himself** is punished by **السجن المشدد, or imprisonment of three
to ten years**; **if the victim dies, the penalty for premeditated murder applies.**
**Both the person who orders and the person who acts are caught by the same sentence.**
✅ **And none of it times out:** **Constitution Art. 52** — «**التعذيب بجميع صوره
وأشكاله، جريمة لا تسقط بالتقادم.**» — plus **CrPC Art. 15 ¶2**, which puts Penal Code
**Arts. 117, 126, 127, 282, 309 bis and 309 bis (a)** among the **no-prescription**
offences. ⭐ **This is why an old torture complaint is never "too late".**

✅ **Other Penal Code provisions on officials, in one line each:** **Art. 127** —
punishing a convict more severely than sentenced, or with a penalty not imposed;
**Art. 128** — entering a home relying on office without consent (fine ≤ EGP 200);
**Art. 129** — cruelty against people relying on office, violating honour or causing
bodily pain (up to one year, or fine ≤ EGP 200); **Art. 130** — coercing an owner into
a sale, or seizing property (up to two years plus dismissal, plus restitution);
**Art. 131** — imposing unauthorised work, or using people for other than the works
they were gathered for (up to two years plus dismissal, plus wages due).

⚠️ **And a constraint on the victim's route:** the **direct accusation** route
(الإدعاء المباشر, **Art. 232**) is **barred** where a **حفظ order was made and not
appealed or was upheld**, and **against a public official, public employee or police
officer for an act in the course of duty** — **except** the Penal Code **Art. 123**
offences. ⚠️ **So it is barred in exactly the cases where a client most often wants
it.** Get that wrong and the case is dismissed on a formal point.

## Scenario 6 — how long does the State have? (limitation)

✅ **Art. 15** — the criminal action is time-barred by:
- **10 years** in **felonies**;
- **3 years** in **misdemeanours**;
- **1 year** in **contraventions**;

— **from the day the crime was committed**, unless another law says otherwise.

✅ **NO PRESCRIPTION AT ALL**, for crimes committed after the code came into force,
under Penal Code **Arts. 117, 126, 127, 282, 309 bis, 309 bis (a)**, and the crimes in
**Section One of Part Two of Book Two**. ⚠️ **Note what is on that list:** torture to
obtain a confession (126), punishing a convict more severely (127), aggravated
unlawful arrest (282), **embezzlement of public funds (117)**, and the **"obtaining
money by fraud" offences (309 bis / 309 bis (a))** — the pyramid-scheme articles.
**These never time out.**

✅ **And for public officials:** for crimes in **Parts Three and Four of Book Two**
committed by a public official, **the period does not even START until his service
ends or his capacity ceases** — unless the investigation began before that. ⚠️ **This
is the provision that makes long-cold official corruption cases prosecutable, and it
is the one everyone forgets.**

### ⚠️ 6.1 The two articles that decide most real answers

✅ **Art. 16:** «**لا يوقف سريان المدة التي تنقضي بها الدعوى الجنائية لأي سبب.**» —
**the prescription period is NOT SUSPENDED for ANY reason.** *So "can the case be
frozen while he is abroad / while a civil case runs / while the victim negotiates" —
**no, it cannot.***

✅ **Art. 17 — INTERRUPTION, and what does it:** the period is **interrupted** by
**acts of investigation**, **accusation**, **trial**, **a criminal order**, and **acts
of evidence-gathering — but only if taken AGAINST the accused or if he was officially
notified of them** (`إذا اتخذت في مواجهة المتهم أو إذا أخبر بها بوجه رسمى`). **After
interruption the period runs ANEW** from the day of the interruption; where there are
several interrupting acts, **from the date of the LAST one.**

✅ **Art. 18** — **interruption as to ONE accused interrupts as to ALL of them**, even
if no interrupting act was taken against them.

⚠️ ⚠️ **THE PRACTICAL TRAP, and the reason this section matters more than it looks:**
because **Art. 16 forbids suspension** but **Art. 17 allows interruption**, almost
every long-running Egyptian case is kept alive by **interruption** rather than
suspension. **So when a client asks "the crime was in 2019, is it time-barred", the
answer is NOT "felony, ten years, fine".** The answer requires **the last date on
which an investigation, accusation, trial or criminal order occurred against him or was
officially notified to him** — and note that **an investigative step taken behind his
back does not interrupt.** Sending a letter is not enough; he must have been
**officially informed**.

## Scenario 7 — appeal

⚠️ ✅ **Three different clocks for three different remedies, and confusing them loses
the remedy:**

| Remedy | Deadline | Runs from | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Misdemeanour appeal** | **10 days** | **pronouncement** of an in-presence judgment; **notification** of an in-absentia judgment; or the judgment on the objection | CrPC **Art. 406** |
| **Felony appeal** | **40 days** | the judgment | CrPC **Art. 419 bis 4** (added by **Law 1/2024**) |
| **Cassation** | **60 days** | the in-presence judgment; expiry of the objection period; or the judgment on the objection | **Law 57/1959 Art. 34** |

✅ **Art. 406** — appeal is by **REPORT (تقرير) at the registry of the court that issued
the judgment**, within **ten days**. **The Attorney-General has thirty days** and may
report at the **appellate** court's registry.
⚠️ **The clock for an in-absentia judgment runs from NOTIFICATION, not from the
judgment date** — the commonest error in this area.
✅ **Art. 409** — where **one party appeals within the ten days**, the others get
**FIVE EXTRA DAYS**.
✅ **Art. 410** — appeal goes to the **Court of First Instance** of the district;
**if the accused is detained, the Prosecution must transfer him and the appeal is
heard urgently.**
⚠️ **Art. 412 — the appeal of a person under an immediately enforceable custodial
sentence LAPSES if he does not present for execution before the session**; the court
may suspend execution or release him pending appeal. ⭐ **This is a trap: an appeal can
be lost by not surrendering.**

### 7.1 ⭐ The felony appeal — the biggest change in this area since 1950

✅ **Law No. 1 of 2024** (Gazette No. **2 bis**, **16 January 2024**, in force
**17 January 2024**) inserted a whole new chapter — **Arts. 419 bis and 419 bis 1 to
419 bis 9** — creating a **first appeal in felony cases.** Before 17 January 2024 a
felony judgment in Egypt could not be appealed; it is now out of date to say so, and
it is the kind of thing a model will say from older training.

✅ **Art. 419 bis** — **both the Prosecution and the accused** may appeal
**in-presence first-degree felony** judgments. ✅ **Art. 419 bis 1** — the civil party
may appeal where the compensation exceeds the threshold. ✅ **Art. 419 bis 2** — the
**Prosecution may appeal in-absentia** felony judgments. ✅ **Art. 419 bis 3** — the
misdemeanour appeal rules apply unless the chapter says otherwise.
✅ **Art. 419 bis 4** — **40 days by report at the registry of the court that issued
the judgment**, grounds **in the same period**. **The Attorney-General has 60 days.**
⚠️ ⭐ **Art. 419 bis 9 — the consequence that matters more than the article list:
appeal does NOT suspend execution** — **unless the appellate felony court so decides,
or the sentence is DEATH.** And **if the appellant or his agent fails to appear without
excuse, the court APPOINTS A LAWYER and rules.** *Before 2024 there was no first
appeal to lose; now there is, and losing it does not buy time.*

⚠️ ⚠️ **The transitional provision, which cuts both ways.** **Promulgating Art. 3 of
Law 174/2025** — which already operates — provides that the felony-appeal provisions
**apply only to cases NOT yet decided by the felony courts as of 17 January 2024.**
So a felony judgment pronounced **before** that date is **not** appealable under the
new chapter; **the right of appeal is governed by the law in force when the judgment
was pronounced.** ⭐ **Do not tell a client with an old felony conviction that he now
has an appeal.**

### 7.2 Cassation

✅ **Law 57/1959 Art. 30** — cassation lies to the Prosecution, the **convicted
person**, the civilly liable person and the civil claimant, against the **final
judgment of the last degree**, in felonies and misdemeanours, on:
1. **breach, misapplication or misinterpretation of the law**;
2. **nullity in the judgment**;
3. **nullity in the procedure affecting the judgment.**

⚠️ **Excluded** where the misdemeanour is punished by a **fine not exceeding EGP
20,000** — so a small fine can be final with no cassation at all. ⚠️ And the
**Attorney-General may appeal for the accused's BENEFIT.**
✅ **Art. 34** — **60 days by report** at the registry, **grounds in the same period**.
⚠️ **Art. 36 — the money:** where the appellant is **not** the Prosecution and **not**
under a custodial sentence, a **security of EGP 300** must be deposited **at the time
of the report**, unless the **legal-aid committee** exempts him. And where the court
**rejects the appeal as impermissible, lapsed or inadmissible**, it orders
**forfeiture of the security PLUS a fine of the same amount.**
✅ **Art. 35** — **no new grounds** may be raised beyond those filed; **but the court
may quash in the accused's FAVOUR of its own motion.**

## The frame worth naming, in one place

- ✅ **Constitution Art. 54** — the 24-hour rule, the lawyer, the appointed lawyer, and the right of complaint to a court with a ruling **within a week or immediate release**.
- ✅ **Art. 55** — dignity; **the right to silence**; and **any statement proved to have been made under torture, intimidation, coercion, or the threat of any of them is VOID and cannot be relied upon.**
- ✅ **Art. 52** — **torture is not subject to prescription.**
- ✅ **Art. 56** — prisons are **reform and rehabilitation houses**, and prisons and places of detention are **subject to JUDICIAL supervision.**
- ✅ **Art. 57** — private life is inviolable; correspondence and telephone conversations may be infringed **only by a reasoned judicial order**.
- ✅ **Art. 95** — no crime and no penalty except under a statute; no penalty except by **judicial judgment**.
- ✅ **Art. 96** — **presumption of innocence**; the law regulates **appeal of felony judgments** (the hook for Law 1/2024); the State protects **victims, witnesses, defendants and informants.**
- ✅ **Art. 97** — no one is tried except before **his natural judge**, and **exceptional courts are PROHIBITED.** ⭐ *This is the constitutional answer to "was he tried before a special court".*
- ✅ **Art. 98** — defence rights guaranteed; **the law guarantees access to the courts and to defence for those who cannot afford it.**
- ✅ **Art. 204** — the military judiciary; **a civilian may not be tried before it except in an enumerated closed list** of offences against military installations, zones, equipment, secrets, conscription, or personnel in the course of duty.
- ⚠️ **Art. 154** — the state of emergency. **The article number is 154, not 152.** And ⚠️ **the state of emergency LAPSED and was not extended in October 2021** — no new cases are referred to the State Security Emergency Courts, and PM Decision 1664/2021 was suspended. **But the Emergency Law itself (Law 162/1958) remains on the statute book**, and the power to declare remains.

### ⚠️ 8.1 The honesty limit on "you have the right to remain silent"

⚠️ ⭐ **State this carefully, because the confident version is wrong.** In **Law
150/1950 — the code in force today — there is no article that says in terms that the
accused must be WARNED of his right to silence.** The **right** is in **Constitution
Art. 55**. The **warning** (`تنبيهه إلى أن من حقه الصمت`) appears explicitly only in
**Art. 104 of the new code**, which applies from 1 October 2027.

⚠️ **So do not tell a client in 2026 that the police were obliged to warn him.** The
1950 code's protection is **procedural, not a warning**: no interrogation without
counsel (**Art. 124**), and no separation from counsel (**Art. 125**).

⭐ **And that is the stronger position anyway.** If a confession came out of an
interrogation held **without the lawyer having been called** (Art. 124), or with the
accused **separated from his lawyer** (Art. 125), **the prosecution does not need to
prove torture at all — the procedure is void independently**, and that nullity is a
**cassation ground** under **Law 57/1959 Art. 30(3)**.

> **Lead with the procedure, not with the bruise.**

## Workflow

1. **Establish which clock is running, and since when.** Time of arrest, date the
   victim knew, date of the crime, date of the judgment. Everything else is secondary.
2. **Identify the offence class** — felony, misdemeanour or contravention (Penal Code
   **Arts. 9–12**). ⚠️ **The class is fixed by what the penalising article prescribes,
   not by what a court actually fines.** The **EGP 100** line that separates a
   misdemeanour from a contravention is a **1937 figure that has never been raised**.
   **Do not reason from the amount.**
3. **Check whether a شكوى is required** — Art. 3's list. If it is, check the
   **three-month** window from knowledge, and note the arrest gate in **Art. 39**.
4. **For a detained person, run the ladder in §2.1 and the caps in §2.2**, then the
   challenge routes in §2.4. **Count against the time of arrest**, and check that
   **every order states its reasons** (Art. 136).
5. **Check whether a lawyer was called, and whether he was present** (Arts. 124, 125).
   ⚠️ If not, that is a nullity argument, and it is independent of any allegation of
   mistreatment.
6. **Run the limitation calculation properly** — §6.1. **Find the last interrupting
   act**, do not just take the base period.
7. **For an appeal, name which of the three clocks applies** (§7) and **count from the
   right event** — for an in-absentia judgment, from **notification**.
8. **Note the 1 October 2027 change** wherever it alters the answer, and say which
   figure governs today.

## What never to do

- ❌ **Never say the new Criminal Procedure Code is in force.** Law 174/2025 applies
  from **1 October 2027**.
- ❌ **Never tell a client the police had a duty to warn him of his right to silence.**
  The right exists; the **warning** does not, under the code in force.
- ❌ **Never mix the old and new detention caps.** **6 / 18 / 24 today; 4 / 12 / 18
  from 1 October 2027.**
- ❌ **Never say a felony judgment cannot be appealed.** It has been appealable since
  **17 January 2024** — but **not** for judgments pronounced before that date.
- ❌ **Never tell a victim he can ask for the accused's detention or oppose his
  release.** **Art. 152** bars both.
- ❌ **Never tell a victim he can make a civil claim for the first time at the
  appeal.** **Art. 251** bars it.
- ❌ **Never cite "the 2021 amendments" or Law 172/2018** in this area. Neither exists
  as described.
- ❌ **Never say "file a complaint" without saying whether it is a شكوى (which has a
  three-month deadline and bars prosecution without it) or a بلاغ (which does not).**
- ⚠️ **Never confuse a حفظ order with an acquittal.** It is a decision not to
  prosecute, it must be **notified to the victim** (Art. 62), and it is reviewable.
