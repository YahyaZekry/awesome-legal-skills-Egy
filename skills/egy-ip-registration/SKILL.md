# Intellectual property in Egypt — what you can register, and what you cannot

**Not legal advice.** IP is where a non-lawyer can do the easy 80% alone and should pay
for the hard 20%. Registering a mark is a form. Enforcing against a counterfeiter is
not. Say so once.

## What this skill does

Egypt's IP statute is **Law 82/2002** — the **قانون حماية حقوق الملكية الفكرية** — and
it is unusually stable: **Books One and Two have never been amended since 2002.** That
means the trademark, patent, design and trade-secret article numbers you find are still
right, which is rare in Egyptian law and worth knowing.

⚠️ **But the *administration* moved.** Since **7 August 2023** a single body — **EGIPA,
الجهاز المصري للملكية الفكرية** (Law 163/2023) — replaced six ministries, the Patent
Office and the Plant Variety Office. **Office addresses in older guides are stale, and
ITDA's trademark pages now return 404.** Telephone **16528** before travelling anywhere.

---

## ⚠️ Read this first: five things people believe that are wrong

These are stated confidently all over the internet. All five fail against the
statute.

1. ❌ **"Law 82/2002 was amended by Law 131/2003 and Law 5/2010."**
   **Neither law exists.** The complete amendment history is **exactly three laws**:
   **26/2015**, **144/2019**, **178/2020** — confirmed independently by the amendment
   footnotes in the Court of Cassation's consolidated text and by WIPO Lex's own notes.
2. ❌ **"IP law changes constantly, so article numbers drift."**
   **Not for the core.** **Every amendment has hit Book Three (copyright) or Book Four
   (plant varieties).** Patents, utility models, layout designs, trade secrets,
   trademarks, trade names, designs and geographical indications are all on their
   **2002 numbering and 2002 wording**. ⚠️ **Book Three article numbers are not safe** —
   Art. 185 was rewritten in 2020.
3. ❌ **"An IP case worth EGP 5 million goes to the Economic Court's appellate
   circuit."** That was the **2008** figure. **Law 146/2019** raised it to **EGP 10
   million**, and **Law 156/2024** to **EGP 15 million**. And IP is no longer "item 9"
   of the list — it is **item (8)** of the criminal list and **item (7)** of the civil
   list, because Law 146/2019 replaced the list wholesale. **Anything quoting "item 9"
   is quoting repealed text.**
4. ❌ **"ITIDA gives software businesses a free-zone or export exemption from IP or
   tax."** **No such exemption exists in any IP law.** What exists is **ITIDA's Export-IT
   programme** — a **cash rebate** of 10–35% of value-added exports, capped at **EGP 2.5
   million**, for ICT companies that are **>50% Egyptian-owned**, headquartered in Egypt,
   with **annual revenue ≤ EGP 150 million** — and free-zone relief, which lives in
   **Investment Law 72/2017**, not in IP law.
5. ❌ **"There is a 2026 amendment to Law 82/2002 in force."**
   A bill was referred to a joint committee and consultations began in **late April
   2026**. **No promulgation, no law number and no gazette reference exists.** ⚠️ **Do
   not tell a client the penalties changed.**

## The four rights, and which one fits

| What you have | The right | Registered? | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| A **brand name or logo** | **Trademark** — Book Two | ✅ **Yes** — registration is what gives you the right | **10 years, renewable** |
| An **invention** | **Patent** — Book One | ✅ Yes | **20 years from filing in Egypt** |
| A **small mechanical improvement** | **Utility model** — Book One | ✅ Yes | **7 years, NON-RENEWABLE** |
| **Writing, music, film, software, a database** | **Copyright** — Book Three | ❌ **No formality — it arises on creation** | Varies by type; moral rights **perpetual** |
| A **look or shape** | **Industrial design** — Book Two | ✅ Yes | 10 years, renewable |
| A **process or formula you keep quiet** | **Trade secret** — Book One Ch. Three, **Arts. 55–62** | ❌ No filing — secrecy *is* the right | **Until it stops being secret** |

⚠️ **The choice that most often goes wrong: trade secret versus patent.** A patent
publishes your invention in exchange for a 20-year monopoly. A trade secret lasts
indefinitely — **but reverse engineering a product in circulation is expressly lawful
(Art. 59(2))**, and protection ends the moment the information stops being undisclosed.
**If your secret is visible in the product you sell, a trade secret is the wrong
instrument.** If it is a recipe, an algorithm behind a server, or a customer list, it is
the right one.

## Goal 1 — register a trademark

### 1.1 The steps, and the four windows that matter

| Step | Rule | ⚠️ The window |
|---|---|---|
| **File** | **Art. 73** — with the Department; **ER Art. 71** needs the form **plus a power of attorney**; **ER Art. 72** needs **four copies of the mark image** | — |
| ⚠️ **Complete the documents** | **ER Art. 73** — supporting documents within **6 months** or the application **is deemed never to have existed** (`يعتبر الطلب كأن لم يكن`) | **6 months** |
| **Classify** | **Art. 74** — one or more **categories** of goods; use is **limited to the registered categories**. Egypt applies the **Nice Classification** ⚠️ as a **treaty obligation** (in force 18 June 2005), **not** because Art. 74 names it. ER Art. 71 allows **one application covering several classes** | — |
| **Priority** | **Art. 75** — a **6-month** priority right from a first filing in a WTO member or reciprocal country | **6 months** |
| **Examination** | **Arts. 77–79** — refusal is appealable to a **Grievance Committee** (three members, at least one from the **Council of State**) | ⚠️ **30 days** to appeal |
| **Publication** | **Art. 80** — in the **Gazette of Trademarks and Industrial Designs**, issued **monthly in the first week** (ER Art. 82) | — |
| **Opposition** | **Art. 80** — **any interested party** may oppose **in writing within 60 days** of publication | **60 days** |
| ⚠️ **Reply to an opposition** | **ER Art. 90** — you must reply within **30 days** or **the application is deemed abandoned** | **30 days** |
| **Complete registration** | **ER Art. 92** — after a **rejected** opposition | **90 days** |
| **Registration takes effect** | **Art. 83** — **from the FILING date**, not the registration date ⭐ | — |

⭐ **The filing date is what you own.** Because Art. 83 back-dates the registration to
filing, a slow examination does not cost you priority.

⚠️ **And the paperwork cliff is real: miss the 6-month document window and your
application is treated as never having existed** — not refused, *never filed*. Diary it
at the moment you file.

### 1.2 Duration, renewal, and the cliff

**Art. 90** — registration lasts **10 years**, renewable **for identical periods**.
⚠️ **Renewal must be requested during the LAST YEAR of the term** — not after it ends.

**If you miss it:** a **six-month grace period** after expiry, on payment of the fee plus
a supplement **not exceeding EGP 500** — «**failing which the Department shall proceed
to cancel the said mark from the register**».

⚠️ **The mark is struck off, not suspended.** Re-registration afterwards:
- possible **within 3 years** (**Art. 92**, fee **≤ EGP 1,000**),
- **only in favour of the former owner**,
- and it is a **privilege, not a right**.

**And there is a use requirement.** **Art. 91** — a mark **unused for 5 consecutive
years** is **cancellable by the court on the request of any interested party**. ⚠️ So
registering a mark you do not use is not protection; it is a liability you are paying
renewal fees on. **Register what you actually trade under.**

## Goal 2 — protect writing, music, software, film

### 2.1 Protection is automatic. Your DEAL is not.

⚠️ **This is the single most consequential sentence in the whole law for a freelancer,
and it is the one nobody knows.**

✅ **Protection arises on creation with no formality.** That part of the folklore is
true.

⚠️ **But Art. 185, as substituted by Law 178/2020, provides that a disposal of a work is
NOT EFFECTIVE AGAINST THIRD PARTIES until the recordal is completed:**

> «**وفى جميع الأحوال، لا يكون التصرف نافذًا فى حق الغير إلا بعد إتمام القيد**»

**So an assignment or exclusive licence you sign and hand over is not enforceable against
anyone who is not a party to it, until it is entered in the register.** If a producer
buys your rights and does not record the transfer, and you then grant the same rights to
someone else who does record it, **the producer loses — even though he paid you first and
has a signed contract.**

📌 **Tell the client to record the transfer *before* delivering the work, not after.**
And note the fee is capped at **EGP 1,000 per entry** — with applicants **under 21** and
persons covered by the **disability-rights law (10/2018) EXEMPT** from it.

⚠️ **A source conflict worth knowing:** a draft reproduced in an August 2020 news report
showed an **EGP 100** fee for under-21s. **The enacted text says `يُعفى` — exempt — and
contains no EGP 100 limb at all.** The enacted text governs.

⚠️ **The competent office depends on the class of work** — the **Cultural Office** for
literary and artistic works, **ITIDA** for software and databases, the **Information
Office** for others. **Ask which one before queuing.**

⚠️ **There is no separate software chapter.** Software sits **inside Book Three**:
computer programs are **Art. 140(2)** works, databases **Art. 140(3)**. The *department*
is separate; the *law* is copyright.

## Goal 3 — patent or utility model

| Right | Term | Starts | Renewal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Patent** | **20 years** — Art. 9 | **the date of filing in Egypt** | none |
| **Utility model** | **7 years** — Art. 30 | **filing with the Patent Office in Egypt** | ⚠️ **NON-RENEWABLE** |

⚠️ **Both begin at FILING, not at grant** — and neither is extendable. A patent that
takes five years to grant has already spent five of its twenty.

⚠️ **And there is an annuity trap: Art. 31 requires a progressive annual fee from the
second year until expiry**, with a **7% surcharge per year of delay**, and **lapse for
non-payment**. ⚠️ **A granted patent is not a safe asset — it is a subscription.** Diary
it.

✅ **Ask about the reduction — it is not automatic.** The national filing fee is
**EGP 150** for a patent and **EGP 100** for a utility model, **0 for students**, and the
2003 reductions carry into EGIPA's practice (**50% for individuals or establishments with
≤ 10 staff**; students **0–10%**).

⚠️ **Filing abroad:** Egypt is a **PCT** member, so a PCT application can enter the
national phase here. **But the 20 years still runs from the Egyptian filing date**, and
the annuities still fall due.

## Goal 4 — keep a trade secret

✅ **Arts. 55–62** — Law 82/2002 has **its own** undisclosed-information chapter, with an
**unfair-competition list at Art. 58** and a **criminal penalty at Art. 61 (fine EGP
10,000–50,000; on repetition imprisonment up to 2 years + EGP 50,000–100,000)**.

⚠️ **This is not the Commercial Code.** Commercial Code **Arts. 66 and 83** are a
*different, cumulative* regime, not the primary home of the right.

⚠️ **And be honest about the limit: reverse engineering a product in circulation is
expressly LAWFUL (Art. 59(2)).** Trade-secret protection ends the moment the information
stops being undisclosed. **If a competitor can buy your product and read your secret off
it, this is the wrong instrument** — see the choice table above.

## Goal 5 — someone is copying you

### 5.1 The deadline that kills the seizure order

✅ **Every Book of Law 82/2002 has a pre-trial seizure article**, and **each has a
different deadline**. **Miss it and the order lapses**, whatever its value.

| Your right | Article | What can be ordered | ⚠️ **Days to file the case** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Patent / utility model** | **Art. 33** | Conservatory measures against imitating products, per the patent description; goods preserved as they are | **8 days** |
| **Trade secret** | **Art. 62** applying Arts. 33, 35 | As above | **8 days** |
| **Trademark** | **Art. 115** | Description and inventory of machines, products, packaging, invoices, ads; establishing the infringement | **15 days** |
| **Industrial design** | **Art. 135** | Same structure as trademarks | **15 days** |
| **Copyright** | **Art. 179** | Description; **discontinuance** of publication or manufacture; **seizure** of originals, copies and the materials; establishing the infringement; **assessment and seizure of the income** from exploitation | **15 days** |

⚠️ **Two practical barriers, and both cost money:** the copyright seizure requires an
**appropriate security from the requesting party** (Art. 179), and customs requires
**one quarter of the consignment value** (§5.2). **Budget before you petition.**

**Appeals against a seizure order:** **within 30 days** for trademarks and copyright
(Arts. 116, 180); the court may **confirm, revoke, or appoint a custodian**, with income
deposited in the court treasury until the dispute is settled.

### 5.2 ⭐ The thing to tell a client before they decide it is not worth it

✅ **Art. 117 — the court may order destruction even in the event of an ACQUITTAL**
(«**وللمحكمة أن تأمر ببعض أو كل هذه التدابير، حتى في حالة الحكم بالبراءة**»), and may
order **publication of the judgment in newspapers at the infringer's expense**.

📌 **So the value of a criminal complaint is not the sentence — it is the seizure,
confiscation and destruction that follow, plus the closure power.** ⚠️ **In trademark
cases the court may order closure of the enterprise for up to 6 months, and on
repetition closure is MANDATORY (Art. 113).** A criminal complaint is therefore a
**commercial** instrument, not a punitive one. Say that plainly.

✅ **Customs is the route a small business can actually trigger in a week.**
**Customs Law 207/2020 + ER 430/2021**, and the **IP Rights Protection Department**
handles it:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who complains** | The right owner, usufructuary, exploiter, or agents — covering copyright, trademarks, geographical indications, designs, patents and circuit designs |
| **What it reaches** | Goods **not yet released**, and goods **en route to Egyptian ports** |
| ⚠️ **Cost** | A **cash security or bank guarantee equal to 1/4 of the consignment value** |
| ⚠️ **Window** | The suspension lasts **at most 10 working days**, within which you **must take judicial measures** or it falls away |
| **The importer's counter** | An **objection to the Head of Customs within 3 working days**, which **does not release the goods until decided** |
| **Ex officio** | Customs **may suspend on its own initiative** on prima facie evidence |
| **Separate ban** | **Import Regulations 770/2005 Art. 40** bans importing or exporting goods bearing trademarks **without the owner's or agent's approval** |

📌 **Have the court petition drafted BEFORE you file with customs.** 10 working days is
the whole window, and you will spend three of them getting the file to the right desk.

⚠️ **Which officials may seize:** **Arts. 118 and 188** (and Art. 42 for Book One) let the
Minister of Justice designate **law enforcement officers** for this law — and the
designation is **book-specific**. ⚠️ **Ask to see the designation, and ask again if the
officers are from a different administration than the right book.** This is the article
that makes a seizure visit lawful or not.

## The clocks — and the one that is shorter than you think

| Clock | Instrument | Period | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Civil compensation — general | **Civil Code Art. 374** | **15 years** | the default |
| ⚠️ **Unpaid royalties** | **Civil Code Art. 375** | **5 years** | periodic, self-renewing dues — **this is the royalty clock** |
| **Criminal** | **CrPC Art. 15** | **3 years** | ⭐ **Every penalty in Law 82/2002 is a جنحة (misdemeanour)** — so **3 years**, not 10, whatever the fine |
| **Trademark non-use** | **Art. 91** | **5 consecutive years** | then the mark is cancellable on any interested party's request |

⚠️ **Two corrections that decide real answers:**

1. ❌ **The limitation period is in the Criminal Procedure Code (Law 150/1950), NOT the
   Penal Code.** CrPC **Art. 15** — 3 years misdemeanour / 10 felony / 1 year
   contravention — and interruption is under **Art. 17**, on the same terms as any other
   criminal matter.
2. ⚠️ **Prescription is NOT applied by the court of its own motion — Civil Code Art.
   387.** On the defence side, **plead it**; on the claim side, **do not assume the other
   side knows**.

📌 **And a freelancer's practical warning: an uncollected royalty invoice from 2020 is
probably gone already.** The 5-year clock under Art. 375 is the one people get wrong,
because the general rule they remember is 15.

## Fees — and why a quoted figure is worthless

⚠️ **There are four layers, and the number on a website is almost never the total.**

| Layer | Instrument | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **1. The statutory ceiling** | Law 82/2002 itself — **Arts. 31, 73, 90, 92, 122, 185, 186, 187** | An **upper bound in the law** the regulations may not exceed |
| **2. The fee schedule** | **PM Decree 1366/2003** (Books I, II, IV) · **497/2005** (Book III) | The actual fees — ⚠️ **at 2003 / 2005 prices** |
| **3. The Authority's service charge** | **EGIPA Decisions 84, 138, 139, 140, 141 of 2025** | A **separate** charge per service, expressly **"in addition to the fees imposed by law and the stamp dues"** |
| **4. The annual top-up** | **EGIPA Decisions 64 and 65 of 2026** ⚠️ | Fixed additions per service — **sourced only from law-firm client alerts**, not the decision text |
| **+ Stamp duty, always** | The Stamp Law | Every schedule ends with *"stamp value to be added"* |

**The ceilings, which ARE reliable:** Art. 73 trademark fees **not more than EGP 5,000 in
total** · Art. 90 renewal supplement **≤ EGP 500** · Art. 92 re-registration **≤ EGP
1,000** · Art. 31 utility model **≤ EGP 1,000** · Art. 122 design application, up to 50
designs, **≤ EGP 3,000** · Art. 185 recordal **≤ EGP 1,000** · Art. 186 deposit
certificate **≤ EGP 1,000** · Art. 187 establishment licence **≤ EGP 1,000**.

⚠️ ⚠️ **The largest genuine gap, and it should be disclosed rather than papered over:
there is NO published current trademark fee schedule.** The 2025 EGIPA decisions do not
cover trademarks, **ITDA's trademark pages all return 404**, and no EGIPA trademark fee
page was found. **What can be verified is a 2003 base figure, a ceiling, and an EGP 500
surcharge sourced only from client alerts.**

📌 **So give the client a RANGE, the LAYERS, and the DATE you read each one.** A figure
quoted without a date is not information.

## Workflow

1. **Name the right first.** Trademark, patent, utility model, design, copyright or
   trade secret — from the table in §"The four rights". **Getting this wrong wastes
   everything downstream**, and the most common error is filing a patent for something
   whose value is in being secret, or relying on a trade secret for something visible in
   a product.
2. **Check the office before travelling.** EGIPA has replaced six bodies since 2023, the
   Trademark Office has been moving to **101 Kasr El Aini St.**, and **ITDA's trademark
   pages are 404**. **Telephone 16528.**
3. **For a trademark, diary the four windows** — 6 months documents, 30 days opposition
   appeal, 30 days opposition reply, 90 days post-opposition completion — and note that
   **the registration back-dates to filing** (Art. 83).
4. **For a creator or freelancer, check whether any rights were assigned** and whether
   the **Art. 185 recordal was completed**. ⚠️ **If not, the deal does not bind third
   parties.** This is usually urgent and almost always overlooked.
5. **Do not quote a fee without a date** — see §"Fees". Give the ceiling, the layer, and
   the date.
6. **For an infringement, decide which of the two doors you are using** — courts (8-day
   or 15-day post-seizure deadline, plus a security) or customs (1/4 consignment value,
   10 working days). ⚠️ **Draft the court petition before filing with customs.**
7. **Run the limitation calculation properly** — **3 years criminal, 15 years general
   civil, 5 years for royalties**, and remember **the court will not apply it on its own
   motion**.
8. **Flag the 2026 bill as not law** wherever a client has read about it.

## What never to do

- ❌ **Never cite Law 131/2003 or Law 5/2010** as amending Law 82/2002. **They do not
  exist.** The amendments are **26/2015, 144/2019, 178/2020**.
- ❌ **Never say the 2026 IP amendment is in force.** As of **19 October 2026** there is
  **no law number and no gazette reference**.
- ❌ **Never repeat the ITIDA free-zone or export IP exemption.** It does not exist. The
  real relief is the **Export-IT cash rebate** and **Investment Law 72/2017**.
- ❌ **Never say "copyright needs no paperwork" and stop there.** Protection is
  automatic; **Art. 185 makes the DEAL unenforceable against third parties until it is
  recorded.**
- ❌ **Never quote the Economic Court thresholds as EGP 5 million or 10 million**, and
  never say IP is "item 9". It is **EGP 15 million**, finality at **EGP 750,000**, item
  **(8)** criminal and **(7)** civil.
- ❌ **Never give a patent "20 years from grant"** or a utility model "10 years". They are
  **20 years from filing** and **7 years non-renewable from filing**.
- ❌ **Never say the limitation period is in the Penal Code.** It is **CrPC Art. 15**.
- ❌ **Never sell a trade secret for something visible in the product** — **Art. 59(2)
  makes reverse engineering lawful.**
- ❌ **Never treat the trademark renewal grace period as a late-fee problem.** After six
  months, **«the Department shall proceed to cancel the said mark from the register»**.
