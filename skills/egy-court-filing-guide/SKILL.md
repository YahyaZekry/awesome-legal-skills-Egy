# Egyptian courts — where to file, and what it costs

**Not legal advice.** Procedure is exactly where a wrong step costs the client the
case. Get an Egyptian lawyer for anything filed. Say so once.

## The structure

**Court of Cassation (محكمة النقض)** → **Court of Appeal (محكمة الاستئناف)** →
**Court of First Instance (محكمة الابتدائية)** → **District Court (محكمة المواد
الجزئية)**. Alongside: **Execution Courts (محاكم التنفيذ)**, the **judge of summary
matters (قاضي الأمور الوقتية)**, and the **bailiff's office (قلم المحضرين)**.

⚠️ **There is no "Supreme Labour Court."** 🟡 Labour cassation runs through
**specialised circuits inside the Court of Cassation** (**Art. 186**, Labour Law
14/2025), and a **screening-circuit refusal is unappealable**. This is routinely
described online as a separate institution.

## Pecuniary limits — check the current numbers

⚠️ These were **amended twice**, by **Law 191/2020** and then **Law 157/2024**.
Material written before that quotes the old figures.

| Court | First instance | Judgment final |
|---|---|---|
| **District Court** | value **≤ EGP 40,000** | value **≤ EGP 5,000** |
| **Court of First Instance** | all other civil and commercial | value **≤ EGP 100,000** |

Finality is also by subject matter in an enumerated list, irrespective of value.
**An appealable judgment gets one appeal.** Below the threshold, the judgment is
final and the appeal is gone.

## Judicial fees — and the tariff you must not quote

The statute is **Law 90 of 1944** as amended by **126/2009**.

⚠️⚠️ **The Court of Appeal's administrative tariff changes of 2024–25 are NOT
legislation.** They are administrative and are **under challenge in ECESR Case
51843/79**. **Do not quote a Cairo Court of Appeal figure as statutory** — it is
exactly the kind of number that ends up in a filing and gets challenged.

⚠️ Also note a live search trap: the numbers **"3/1972", "3/1973" and "17/1973"**
floating around Egyptian fee discussions are **Bahraini and Kuwaiti**, not
Egyptian.

**Two fee regimes that change the economics:**

- ⚠️ **Employment** — **Labour Law 14/2025 Art. 7**: lawsuits by **workers,
  trainees, apprentices or their beneficiaries are exempt from judicial fees and
  expenses at every stage**; the court may order **immediate self-execution without
  bail**; and they are exempt from **stamp duty**.
- **Consumer** — **CPA decisions are final** and appealable only before the
  **Administrative Court (القضاء الإداري)**.

## Filing and service

**Who serves** — ✅ **Law 13/1968**: the **court bailiff's office (قلم المحضرين)**
serves the statement of claim within **no more than 30 days** of receipt.

**When you may not serve** — ⚠️ **Art. 15**: no service or enforcement procedure may
be carried out **before 7:00 a.m., after 8:00 p.m., or on an official holiday**,
except in necessity with the **written permission of the Judge of Summary Matters**.

**Deadlines** — ✅ **Art. 18**: where the final day of a time limit falls on an
official holiday, **the time limit is extended to the first working day thereafter**.
⚠️ "Working day" means a day that office or court is **actually open** — not the
weekday. **Do not compute it from the weekday alone.**

**Art. 15** — the day of service, or the event starting the limit, is **not
counted**. Time limits in months or years run on the **Gregorian** calendar.

**Appearances** — **15 days** before First Instance and Appeal, **8 days** before
District Courts; reducible in necessity to 3 days and 24 hours.

**Prescription** — ⚠️ **Civil Code Art. 387: the court may NOT apply prescription of
its own motion.** It must be pleaded by the defendant, and he may plead it at any
stage including on appeal. Do not assume a dormant claim is safe, and do not assume
one is dead either.

## Which court, by subject

| Dispute | Where |
|---|---|
| **Consumer Protection Law 181/2018** civil/commercial | **Economic Courts (محاكم ECONEconomic)** |
| Consumer law, administrative | **Administrative Court (القضاء الإداري)** |
| **Employment** | **Specialised labour courts** under **Labour Law 14/2025 Arts. 149–150** — each with its own enforcement department (Decision 4621/2025) |
| **Possession and in rem** | ✅ **Civil Code Art. 50** — the court in whose district **the property lies** |
| **Delivery of immovables** | ✅ **Arts. 43(6) and 50** — **exclusively the District Court** |
| **Urgent / interim** | **Judge of Summary Matters (قاضي الأمور الوقتية)** at the Court of First Instance |
| **Execution** | **Execution Courts (محاكم التنفيذ)** — one execution judge per court district |

⚠️ **Art. 44** — do **not** combine a possession claim with the claim to the right
in the same action.

## Execution — when you have a judgment

The **Judge of Execution (قاضي التنفيذ)** and the **Execution Courts** operate under
**Law 13/1968** and the **Administrative Seizure Law 308 of 1955**
(قانون الحجز الإداري).

The judge is a **stand-alone court with its own statutory jurisdiction**, analogous
to the judge of urgent matters. **Execution Courts are as numerous as District
Courts** — one execution judge per court district.

**Local jurisdiction** — for seizure of **movables and immovables**, the criterion is
the **location of the property**; for seizure of what a **garnishee** owes or holds,
it is the **garnishee's domicile**.

**The bailiff's deputy (مندوب الحاجز)** under Law 308/1955 may: carry out the seizure
notice (**Art. 4**); **break doors and locks, but only in the presence of a judicial
police officer (Art. 5)**; inventory and set the sale day, **not earlier than eight
days** from seizure unless the goods are perishable (**Art. 6**); notify the debtor
(**Art. 7**); seize standing crops — **void if less than 55 days have passed since
ripening (Art. 8)**; seize precious metals and jewellery (**Art. 9**); **take
delivery of seized cash and issue a receipt (Art. 10)**; appoint a custodian
(**Art. 11**), who may **not use, exploit or rent out the seized items (Art. 12**).

🟡 The specific article numbers within Law 13/1968 for the execution judge's powers
are **UNVERIFIED** in our corpus. Do not cite them.

## Electronic litigation

⚠️ **Law 172/2018 is NOT an electronic-litigation law.** It **amends the Customs Law
66/1963**. This is a widespread and persistent error online.

→ For electronic filing, work from the **State Council electronic services** and the
relevant court's own e-filing calendar, and **verify the governing instrument**
rather than assuming one exists. The corpus does not carry a verified e-litigation
statute.

## A practical filing checklist

- [ ] **Which court** — by subject matter, then by value, then by geography
- [ ] **Finality threshold** — will the judgment be appealable?
- [ ] **Limitation** — has the period run, and is prescription pleaded-able?
- [ ] **Fees** — and if it is employment, **exempt**
- [ ] **Evidence** — bank statements, invoices, originals, not screenshots
- [ ] **Arabic** — the pleading is in Arabic
- [ ] **Service** — who serves, and on which days it is lawful
- [ ] **Deadlines** — counted from the day **after**, against the **real holiday
      calendar**
- [ ] **Execution planning** — where the property or the debt actually is
