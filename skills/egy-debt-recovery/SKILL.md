# Egyptian debt recovery — from unpaid paper to collected money

**Not legal advice.** Debt collection in Egypt is procedural: the person with the
right article number and the right clock usually wins, and the person who sleeps on
a deadline loses a real claim. Use this skill to orient, then get an Egyptian lawyer
for anything beyond the first steps.

## What this skill does

Answers the questions people actually arrive with — *he won't pay me, what can I do?
can I freeze his money now? can I take his salary? is my claim already time-barred?
what will this cost? he bounced a cheque — criminal or civil? they garnished my
wages, what's protected?* — and every answer names the article behind it.

**It is organised by situation, not by statute.** Go to the section that matches.

---

## ⚠️ FIRST: no taking, no freezing — except through a سند تنفيذي

✅ **CPC Art. 280**: «لا يجوز التنفيذ الجبري إلا بسند تنفيذي اقتضاء لحق محقق
الوجود ومعين المقدار وحال الأداء» — forced execution exists only on an **executory
instrument** claiming an established, quantified, due right. The executory
instruments: **judgments, orders (الأوامر), notarised documents (المحررات الموثقة),
and certified settlement minutes — plus instruments the law grants the quality**.
No invoice, WhatsApp promise or unpaid cheque is itself an سند تنفيذي.

⭐ **The system's design:** get an *instrument* first (a payment order is the
cheapest), then attach, then sell, then collect. Skipping a step is where
self-help becomes a crime (تبديد, Penal Code 341/342) and where a creditor becomes
a defendant.

## Choose your tool by situation

| You have | First move | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Unpaid commercial invoice + written contract | Demand letter ≥5 days (Art. 58) | أمر أداء (§3) |
| Bounced cheque | Bank statement on the cheque (Art. 518) | Art. 525 attachment + Art. 534 complaint (§4) |
| Debtor's salary/employer known | Garnish at the employer (Art. 325) | 15-day تقرير rule (§5) |
| Debtor owns land | Executive track: تنبيه نزع ملكية (Art. 401) | Registered sale (§7) |
| Nothing provable in writing | Ordinary action + evidence rules | Comm. Code Art. 69 (any proof, commercial) |
| You are the debtor | Answer the تقرير in 15 days | Use 302/303 deposit release (§8) |

## 3. The payment order (أمر الأداء) — CPC Arts. 201–210 ⭐

`As of: 2026-10-28`

The fastest legitimate route from unpaid invoice to execution. Six steps:

1. ✅ **The gate (Art. 201)**: the right is **established in writing**, **due**, and
   everything claimed is money of a determinable amount (or identified movables).
   On commercial paper (cheques/bills/notes) the order runs **only** against the
   drawer/maker/acceptor/avaller — against anyone else, use ordinary rules.
2. ✅ **The demand (Art. 202)**: demand payment in writing with a term of **at least
   five days** — registered mail with proof of delivery, or a **protest**
   (بروتوستو عدم الدفع) which replaces the demand. ⭐ One demand letter can be drafted
   to serve both this and the commercial notice (Art. 58) — `egy-legal-notice`.
3. ✅ **The application (Art. 203)**: to the قاضي المواد الجزئية at the **debtor's
   domicile court**; two identical copies; the debt instrument + proof of the demand
   attached; the debtor's **full name and residence**; the creditor's chosen domicile.
   The order issues within **three days**.
4. ✅ **Refusal (Art. 204)**: if the judge doubts, he refuses the order and sets a
   hearing — it becomes an ordinary suit, not a rejection of the claim.
5. ✅ **Service within three months (Art. 205)**: serve the debtor personally/at
   domicile — **if not served within three months of the order, it is as if it never
   existed**.
6. ✅ **Enforcement (Arts. 206–209)**: the debtor may object within **ten days** of
   service (reasoned, before the same-instance court); the order carries **accelerated
   enforcement** (Art. 209) — no waiting for appeal.

✅ **Art. 210 — the combo**: the same judge may order the **garnishment (حجز لدى
الغير)** with the payment order — an express exception from the general rule that
reserves that for قاضي التنفيذ — provided the creditor submits the application and
the attachment's validity within **eight days of the attachment**, or the attachment
is as if it never was. ⭐ Freeze the receivable on day one, then prove the debt.

## 4. The bounced cheque — the full toolkit

`As of: 2026-10-28`

- ✅ **Get the bank's statement on the cheque itself (Art. 518)** — dated, on the
  cheque, signed by the bank; the bank may not refuse; it may ask up to the **next
  working day**. This statement (or a formal احتجاج) is the dishonour proof for
  everything below. ⭐ Do not leave the bank without it.
- ✅ **Freeze without a bond (Art. 525)**: with the protest or its substitute, the
  holder may place a **conservatory attachment without a bond** on the assets of the
  drawer, any endorser, or the avaller — per the CPC conservatory rules (§5).
- ✅ **The crime (Art. 534)** — imprisonment and/or a fine up to **50,000 EGP** for
  whoever **intentionally** issues a cheque with no payable consideration, withdraws
  the balance after issuing, orders the bank not to pay outside permitted cases, or
  signs/endsorses in bad faith to prevent payment — and the same penalty for handing
  over such a cheque knowingly. **Recidivism within five years → up to 100,000 EGP.**
- ✅ **The صلح that ends it (Art. 534/4)** ⭐: settlement between the parties
  **extinguishes the criminal case even if filed by الادعاء المباشر**, and stops
  sentence execution. So: the criminal case is *leverage*; the money is recovered by
  the civil claim — **Art. 539** lets the holder claim the unpaid value inside the
  criminal case.
- ✅ **The bank's liability (Art. 533)**: an employee who falsely states the balance,
  refuses a covered cheque in bad faith, or withholds the Art. 518 statement —
  fine 3,000–10,000 EGP and **the bank is jointly liable**.
- ✅ **Cheque clocks (Art. 531)**: recourse vs drawer/endorsers — **1 year**;
  vs the drawee bank — **3 years**; obligors among themselves — **1 year** from
  payment/judicial demand; filing suit interrupts; a judgment or the debtor's
  standalone acknowledgment stops the clock. Even after prescription — **Art. 532**
  restitution against the unjustly enriched drawer/endorser.
- ⭐ **Basics that change the plan**: a post-dated cheque is **payable on presentation**
  (Art. 503/2); an "expired" cheque with funds is still payable (Art. 506); multiple
  cheques clear by issue-date order / lowest number / smallest amount (Art. 509);
  the creditor may demand payment by cheque above **100,000 EGP** (Art. 62);
  dispute bank statements within **30 days** (Art. 530).

## 5. Freezing assets first — the conservatory attachments

`As of: 2026-10-28`

### 5.1 On movables — Arts. 316–324

✅ **Art. 316**: attach where you hold a **bill/promissory note signed by a
trader-debtor**, or **wherever you fear losing your security** («خشية فوات
الضمان»). ✅ **Art. 319**: without an سند تنفيذي you need **قاضي التنفيذ's
authorisation** (+ temporary quantification for unquantified debts, decided within
three days). ✅ **Art. 320**: **serve within eight days** or the attachment is as if
it never was; with the judge's order, file the **validity suit within eight days**.

### 5.2 Garnishment at third parties — Arts. 325–352 ⭐

✅ **Art. 325**: any creditor with an **established, due** debt attaches his debtor's
movables **or debts held by third parties** — even deferred/conditional — and every
debt the debtor acquires in the garnishee's hands until the تقرير.
✅ **Art. 328**: no prior notice to the debtor needed — the paper goes to the
**garnishee (المحجوز لديه)**.
✅ **Art. 332** ⭐: notify the creditor's own debtor within **eight days** — or
as if it never was. ✅ **Art. 333**: with the judge's order — validity suit within
eight days.
✅ **Art. 339**: the garnishee must **declare (تقرير)** what he owes within
**fifteen days** of service — with documents. ✅ **Art. 343** ⭐: no/false declaration
→ judgment against the garnishee **for the full amount** + costs and compensation.
✅ **Art. 344**: he pays within **fifteen days of the declaration** once your right is
executory and the **8-day إعلان العزم** (Art. 285) was observed. ✅ **Art. 346**:
otherwise execute **on the garnishee's own assets**.
✅ **Art. 350** ⭐⭐: attachments on **state bodies, local units, public institutions
and their companies expire after three years** unless renewed by notice — repeat
every three years, **or the attachment is void whatever happened**. The trap that
silently kills state-affiliated garnishments.
✅ **Art. 352**: the garnishee dissipating attached items → **Penal Code 341
(التبديد)**.

### 5.3 What can never be attached — Arts. 305–309 ⭐

✅ Beds, clothes and a month's food for the household (305); trade tools and
books, female livestock for subsistence (306 — attachable only for their own
price/maintenance/court-ordered maintenance); court-awarded temporary maintenance
and gifted/willed maintenance monies (307 — except a quarter for maintenance debt);
gifts/wills with a no-attachment condition vs pre-existing creditors (308);
⭐ **wages and salaries: one quarter only** (309 — see §6).

## 6. Wages, interest and the family interplay

`As of: 2026-10-28`

- ✅ **CPC Art. 309**: wage/salary attachment capped at **one quarter (25%)**; in
  competition, **half of the quarter to the court-ordered maintenance claim**, half
  to the rest. ❌ The "20%–50%" figures circulating belong to other Gulf codes —
  never quote them for Egypt.
- ⚠️ **Maintenance (نفقة) has its own priority regime** — Law 1/2000 Art. 76, verified
  in `07-family-law-egypt.md`; the special statute governs maintenance debts.
- ✅ **Interest**: civil/legal interest capped at **4% (5% commercial)** — CC Art. 226;
  commercial delay interest runs **from due date** and its **total cannot exceed the
  principal** — Comm. Code Art. 64; cheque interest runs from presentation at the
  **Central Bank rate** (Art. 522).

## 7. Execution — taking the money

`As of: 2026-10-28`

- ✅ **Service first (Art. 281)**: serve the سند تنفيذي on the debtor personally/at
  domicile, with the payment order and a chosen domicile; execution starts **≥1 day**
  after service. ✅ **Art. 282**: the bailiff must accept payment when offered and
  issue the discharge.
- ✅ **The 8-day notice to third parties (Art. 285)**: a third party pays only after
  the debtor is notified of the intent to execute **≥8 days** beforehand.
- ✅ **Movables (Arts. 353–397)**: the bailiff's محضر fixes the sale date on the spot
  (353); no attachment in the petitioner's presence (355); breaking in needs a
  judicial-police officer (356); attached by listing even without a guard (361);
  ⭐ **attachment void if the sale isn't completed within three months** (375);
  sale ≥8 days after service (376); public auction with immediate payment (384);
  a defaulting bidder is re-sold against and the **محضر البيع is itself an سند
  تنفيذي for the difference** (389).
- ✅ **Real property (Arts. 401–428)**: تنبيه بنزع ملكية → **registered at the الشهر
  العقارى** (402) → the property is **attached by registration** (404) → post-
  registration disposals don't run against attaching creditors (405) → fruits attach
  (406) → the debtor stays guardian (407) → fixed-date leases and مخالصات rules
  (408/409 — cross-ref `05-tenancy-egypt.md`) → sale-conditions list within 90 days
  (414) → mortgagees/rights-holders become parties (417) → sale notice published
  15–30 days ahead (428).
- ✅ **Distribution (Arts. 469–488)**: each attaching creditor is paid **on presenting
  his سند تنفيذي** (470); insufficient proceeds → court-treasury deposit (471) →
  15-day agreement window, then إدارة التنفيذ drafts the provisional list (473–474)
  → objections decided by قاضي التنفيذ immediately (479) → appeal only above the
  statutory disputed amount, **10-day window** (480 ⚠️ figure under the
  191/2020 + 157/2024 amendment chain — never quote the number).

## 8. If you are the debtor

`As of: 2026-10-28`

- ✅ **A payment order is not a final judgment**: object within **ten days** of
  service (Art. 206) — reasoned, or it fails; direct appeal forfeits the objection.
- ✅ **Never ignore a garnishment notice on your employer**: the employer's
  **15-day تقرير** can cost *you* accuracy and the garnishee penalties — make sure
  the employer declares correctly (Arts. 339–343).
- ✅ **Your protected share**: the household necessities (305–306), court-ordered
  maintenance money (307), and **75% of your wages** (309) cannot be attached — the
  employer must hand you the non-attachable share without a judgment (Art. 338) ⭐.
- ✅ **The clean way out**: offer a **substitute deposit** — an amount equal to the
  debt + interest + expenses transfers the attachment from your assets to the money
  (Art. 302; Art. 303 to fix the amount مستعجلاً). ✅ A **certified settlement**
  becomes an سند تنفيذي — get certification if you settle (Art. 280).
- ⚠️ **Do not dissipate attached assets**: تبديد is a crime (Penal Code 341/342;
  CPC Arts. 373, 352) and the cheque-creditor route (534) also criminalises
  post-issuance withdrawal.

## What this skill must never say

- ❌ Any self-help ("take his furniture", "call his bank and block it").
- ❌ "The cheque itself is enough to execute" — it is not an سند تنفيذي.
- ❌ Any wage-garnishment percentage other than **the quarter of Art. 309**.
- ❌ Any current CPC threshold figure (Arts. 41/47/480 chain) — raised by
  Laws 191/2020 and 157/2024; the clerk confirms the number.
- ❌ A limitation period without naming which clock (§9).

## The clocks table ⭐

| Situation | Clock | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Civil personal debt | **15 years** from due | CC Art. 374 ✅ |
| Periodic rights (rents/wages/pensions/interest) | **5 years** | CC Art. 375 ✅ |
| Merchant vs merchant commercial claim | **7 years** from due | Comm. Code Art. 68 ✅ |
| Final judgment (commercial claims) | lapses after **10 years** | Comm. Code Art. 68 ✅ |
| Cheque recourse vs drawer/endorsers | **1 year** | Art. 531/1 ✅ |
| Cheque claim vs the bank | **3 years** | Art. 531/2 ✅ |
| Cheque obligors among themselves | **1 year** | Art. 531/3 ✅ |
| Cheque crime (misdemeanour) | **3 years** | CPCrim 150/1950 Art. 15 ✅ |
| Renewal | debtor's acknowledgment restarts it | CC Art. 385/2 ✅ |
| Civil judgment lapse | **not verified** | 🟡 backlog |
| Payment-order service | within **3 months** of the order | CPC Art. 205 ✅ |
| Garnishment: serve debtor / validity suit | **8 days** | Arts. 332/333 ✅ |
| Garnishee declaration | **15 days** | Art. 339 ✅ |
| State-body attachment | renew every **3 years** | Art. 350 ✅ |
| Movable attachment → sale | **3 months** | Art. 375 ✅ |
