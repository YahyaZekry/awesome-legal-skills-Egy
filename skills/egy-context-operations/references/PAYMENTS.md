# Payments in Egypt — and the evidence you keep

## Cash is still the centre of gravity

✅ **Verified from the CBE's own review (FY 2023/24).** Banknote issue reached
**EGP 1,199.8 billion** at end-March 2024, about **9.3% of GDP**. Currency in
circulation **outside the banking system** was **EGP 1,118.6 billion**.

Denomination mix at end-March 2024: **EGP 200 note 63.2%**, **EGP 100 note 30.7%**,
**EGP 50 note 3.6%**; the EGP 5/10/20 notes together **2.1%**. Average banknote
value **EGP 77.6**.

**What that means practically:** an EGP 200 note doing two-thirds of the work means
everyday transactions are **large-value cash transactions**, not petty cash. Retail
price points are high in EGP, wage packets are paid in notes, and a cash-dominant
economy at this scale coexists with a fast-growing digital layer.

⚠️ Vendor survey (not official): share of consumers paying **cash at POS was 77% in
2020, declining to 72% in 2023**; cash paid in advance online **rose 10pp**. Use as
directional only.

## Cheques — real, and structurally different

**Do not repeat the old geography table.** ✅ The CBE Cheque Clearing House
regulations, as amended by Governor's Decree 13/2019, settle **on the day following
presentation (T+1) regardless of where the drawee bank's branch is**. The old
Cairo T+1 / Delta T+2 / remote T+3 scheme is **abolished**. If a practitioner
resource still shows that table, it predates 2019.

**The single most important cheque rule** (CCH Regulations Art. 5): the
**paper form serves as the legal force**, while the electronic data form is for
**accounting** only. → **The paper cheque is the instrument. The electronic file is a
record. Disputes go to paper.** Do not accept "the system shows it cleared" as
equivalent to holding the instrument.

- EGP cheques: all governorates, **T+1**.
- Instruments for the EGP CCH **must be valued in Egyptian pounds**.
- ✅ **Commercial Code (Law 17/1999), transitional provisions:** in commercial
  matters, **the creditor may demand payment by cheque where the debt exceeds
  EGP 100,000.**

**Foreign-currency cheques — where payment plans break:**
- Covered currencies: **US dollars and euros only.**
- **Two sessions, on consecutive days, Monday to Thursday**, excluding official
  holidays **in Egypt and in the issuing country of the currency**.
- ⚠️ **There is no Friday or Saturday session.** A payment plan built on the
  Egyptian working week fails here.

Cheques are in decline but alive: in **July 2025** the CBE processed **6.040 million
cheques worth EGP 2.56 trillion** — volume −41% YoY, value −29% YoY.

## Bank accounts

- ✅ **IBAN is mandatory in practice** (CBE direction effective 30 June 2020).
  29 characters: `EG` + 2 check digits + 3-digit bank code + 4-digit branch +
  11 digits + account-holder national ID.
- ⚠️ **A foreign buyer can open an account.** A **CBE circular of 7 August 2024**
  states there are **no restrictions** on opening accounts for non-resident foreign
  clients and that refusal exposes the bank to regulatory and reputational risk.
  (Law-firm summary; the circular itself was not retrieved.) **Never tell a foreign
  buyer they cannot open an Egyptian bank account.**
- 🟡 **Some banks are salary-only.** HSBC Egypt's published current account at the
  research date was open to **salary-based customers only**, minimum monthly gross
  salary transfer **EGP 10,000**. → **Do not write "open an Egyptian bank account" as
  a generic step.** Ask what the bank will actually accept.
- Typical non-resident KYC ⚠️ partial: **original passport with valid entry visa**;
  **proof of residence** (electricity/telephone bill, or a tenancy document issued or
  notarised by a public entity); **proof of employment** (HR letter, or tax card if
  self-employed).

## Digital rails

**InstaPay / IPN** ✅ — "the first Payment Service provider licensed by the CBE to
operate over the **Instant Payment Network**", enabling instant transfers between
banks **24/7**. In 2020 the CBE mandated all banks operating in Egypt to support
IPN.

You can transfer to: an **IPA** (Instant Payment Address), a **mobile number**, a
digital-wallet mobile number, a **bank account number or IBAN**, or **any Meeza
card**.

Onboarding: the **mobile number must be registered at your bank**; create an IPN
account; secure it with an **IPN PIN**.

> "Send it to my InstaPay/IPA" is now a normal instruction in Egypt. Ask for the
> counterparty's **IPA** — it is more stable than a phone number.

**Meeza** ✅ — Egypt's **domestic card scheme**, created by the National Payment
Council's **2017 decree** and entrusted by CBE directive to the Egyptian Banks
Association (EBC) to operate. **24/7**, issued by **32 banks**, debit and prepaid,
accepted at ATMs, POS, **government units**, online and in-store.

**Mobile wallets** ✅ — Vodafone Cash, **e&** (the operator rebranded from Etisalat
in **2022**; the CBE's register still lists several wallets under legacy names — use
`e&` for the operator, keep the wallet name as printed on the wallet), Orange Cash,
WE Pay.

## E-commerce acceptance — instalments are mainstream

An Egyptian e-commerce instruction offering only "card or cash on delivery" omits
the **largest segment of Egyptian online purchasing**.

- ✅ **FawryPay** offers cash on delivery; card (Mastercard, Visa, Meeza); digital
  wallets by **QR** or wallet number + mPIN; **payment by reference code** at retail
  POS; **Pay By Link** (SMS, call centre, or shareable link); ATM/IVR/e-banking;
  **instalments**; **recurring card collection**; and refunds including **cash
  refunds by OTP at any Fawry POS**.
- ✅ **Aman** — instalments at ATMs/POS, a POS acceptance solution, and
  bill/instalment payment accepting all bank cards and e-wallets.
- ⚠️ BNPL/consumer-finance brands on the domestic rails are **valU, Halan, Sympl,
  Souhoola, Mogo, Premium Card, Contact Now, Forsa, Klivvr, Tru** — several **not
  individually verified**. **Check the FRA register (`fra.gov.eg`) before naming a
  licensed lender in anything you write.**

## What evidence to keep

| Method | Keep |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | A **signed, dated written receipt (إيصال)** naming payer, payee, amount in **figures and words**, the purpose, and the property or period concerned. ⚠️ For a large amount or a contested lease, **a bank-routed transfer beats any receipt.** |
| **Bank transfer / IBAN** | The **bank confirmation** plus a **statement extract** covering the period. ✅ Arabic practice commentary treats bank receipts and statements as **official documents with high evidentiary weight.** Must show date, payer, payee, amount. |
| **Cheque** | The **original cheque**, plus the payer's statement showing the **debit**, the payee's statement showing the **credit**, and the **rejection slip** if returned. |
| **Mobile wallet / InstaPay** | The **app confirmation or SMS + transaction reference**, plus the payee's **account statement**. ⚠️ **Screenshots alone are weak — always attach the counterparty's bank statement.** |
| **Card** | The **merchant receipt / invoice**, plus the **ETA eInvoice** where the seller is registered. |
| **Anything starting a legal process** | **Proof of service** — registered mail (Egypt Post **Bareedi** for electronic registered mail), a court filing receipt, or a bailiff's act. |

> **A screenshot is not a payment record. A bank statement is.** If the transaction
> is material, tell the user to route it through a bank so the statement exists.

## Foreign currency in a contract

Never hard-code a rate. The pound has moved violently and recently — a float on
**6 March 2024** took it from roughly **30.85** to roughly **49.4** against the USD
within weeks.

Any contract or invoice mixing currencies must state four things:

1. **The currency**
2. **The rate source**
3. **The rate date**
4. **Who bears movement** between the rate date and payment

If nobody is told, the default is contested. Note separately that Civil Code
**Art. 227(1)** caps agreed default interest — anything above the statutory price is
reduced to **7%** with restitution of the excess, which interacts with any penalty
or late-payment clause you write.
