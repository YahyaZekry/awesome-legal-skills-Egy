# English ↔ Arabic working glossary — Egypt

The vocabulary needed to read an Egyptian document, write an instruction to an
Egyptian counterparty, or avoid a translation error that **changes the legal
meaning**. Extracted from the verified corpus; 92 terms.

Deliberately excluded: pleasantries, kinship, food, geography. None of those
change an instruction. What is here changes an instruction.

**Two flags recur:**
- 🔴 **COLLISION** — one Arabic word maps to several English words. Picking the
  wrong sense produces a nonsensical instruction.
- ✅ **NO SINGLE EQUIVALENT** — no clean one-to-one rendering exists; a deliberate
  decision is required and the naive translation is wrong or comic.

**Rendering rule:** keep the Arabic alongside the English whenever the term will
end up in a document. A translated `صورة` that reached an Egyptian counterparty as
"picture" is a document that does not function.

| 40 | ثبت بسند | — | "he proved it by the instrument" | Everyday litigant's phrasing. **No English equivalent** — render as "he established it by the deed / the instrument". | ✅ **YES** |
