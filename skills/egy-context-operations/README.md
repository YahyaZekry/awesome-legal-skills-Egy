The mechanical layer for working in Egypt — what every other Egyptian-law skill builds on. Covers the Egyptian pound and FX risk, national ID / commercial-registry / tax / IBAN identifiers, Egyptian addresses and postal codes, phone and WhatsApp conventions, the official portals that actually work (HTTP-tested, with the false friends called out), electronic signature and electronic seal, cheques and mobile wallets and InstaPay and Meeza, the Egyptian working week and the Art. 18 deadline-extension rule, and a 92-term English–Arabic glossary of the words where naive translation changes the legal meaning.

Built to prevent five specific false beliefs that capable models assert confidently: that Egypt has no postal code (it does), that the CBE runs "CIPS" (that's China), that Egypt's weekend is Friday–Saturday (it's Sunday–Thursday), that the national ID has a published Luhn check digit (it doesn't — that's folklore), and that `gcc.gov.eg` is the Official Gazette (it returns HTTP 200 and is the Cultural Development Fund).
