# Egyptian family law — the routes, the money, and what is NOT law yet

**Not legal advice.** Family litigation decides where children live and how
people survive financially. A wrong step is measured in years. Say it once,
plainly, and then be useful — because most of what a non-lawyer needs here is
to know **which route they are on**, **which clock is running**, and **what a
paper they are about to sign actually gives up**.

## What this skill does

Answers the questions people actually arrive with — *can she marry without a
wali; is my عرفي marriage worth anything; he said "you're divorced", what now;
how do I get out without losing everything; will I get money; who keeps the
children; can he stop paying if I refuse visits; my father died and my brother
took everything* — and it answers them by naming the statute, the article, and
the constitutional ruling where one exists.

**It is organised by situation, not by statute**, because Egyptian family law
is a patchwork of five+ laws nobody experiences as a system. Go to the section
that matches.

⚠️ **And it opens with the two most important structural facts in the whole
area** — the *pending* law, and the confessional structure — because both are
where the confident wrong answers come from.

## ⚠️ FIRST: two things before any scenario

### ⚠️ There is no "new family law". Stop treating the drafts as law

✅ **As of 28 October 2026 there is no new consolidated personal-status law.**
The rules below are the 1920/1929/1985/2000/2004 patchwork. Three government
bills — «قانون الأسرة» (Muslim family law), «قانون الأسرة للمصريين المسيحيين»,
and the «الصندوق التكافلى لدعم الأسرة المصرية» — were approved by Cabinet on
**29 April 2026** and referred to a **joint parliamentary committee** (the two
family bills on **4 May 2026**; the Fund bill on **12 October 2026**). The
Muslim-family bill is reported at **355 substantive articles** in three parts
(الولاية على النفس، الولاية على المال، إجراءات التقاضى). **None has been
debated to a vote; none is enacted; the latest reporting still describes them
as drafts pending discussion.**

- ⚠️ **A pending bill does not suspend the statute.** Advise on the law in
  force; mention the bill only as *what may change*.
- ⚠️ **Do not tell anyone to delay a claim waiting for "the new law".** The
  classical rule is that amendments do not apply retroactively to pending
  cases except by express provision — and no one can name the date or the
  final text.
- ⚠️ **The bill's contents are press-reported, not read.** Specific reported
  provisions (custody at 15 with parental choice afterwards, electronic
  visitation, استضافة schedules, criminalising visitation-denial) are
  **proposals**. The phrase *"the new law criminalises blocking visits"* is
  wrong today — say so.

### ⚠️ The confessional structure — there is no single code

✅ **Egyptian personal status is confessional**, and **Law 1/2000 codifies that
in its promulgating provisions, Art. 3**:

1. **Muslims** → the codified statutes, and where the statutes are silent, the
   **most preponderant opinion of the Hanafi school** (أرجح الأقوال من مذهب
   الإمام أبى حنيفة). This gap-filling clause is why Egyptian family judgments
   read like fiqh.
2. **Non-Muslim Egyptians of one sect and milla** (communities that had milliya
   courts until **31 December 1955**) → **their own shari'a**, within public
   order.
3. **Unified for every Egyptian regardless of religion:** ✅ **inheritance,
   wills and capacity** (Law 77/1943, Law 71/1946) — the SCC's own summary
   calls these the limited area where substantive rules were unified for all.

⚠️ **Three refusals that follow:**

- ❌ **Never cite Law 462/1955 as the current allocation rule.** It was
  **repealed by Law 1/2000's promulgating Art. 4**; cite **promulgating
  Art. 3(2)** instead. 462/1955 survives only for history and case-law.
- ⚠️ **Do not quote the Hanafi-default conditions (custody disqualifications,
  بطلان grounds, the wali's role) with an article number.** They are doctrine
  applied through the default clause, not codified text.
- ⚠️ **For a Christian couple, custody may not be governed by Art. 20 at all**
  — the unification passage covers estates, wills and capacity, and whether
  custody sits inside the confessional area is unresolved. Do not advise on
  cross-confessional custody from this skill.

## Scenario 1 — you are marrying, or documenting a marriage

### 1.1 The 18 rule — where it actually lives, and what its sanction is

✅ **Art. 31 bis of the Civil Status Law (Law 143/1994), added by Law
126/2008:** «لا يجوز توثيق عقد زواج لمن لم يبلغ من الجنسين ثماني عشرة سنة
ميلادية كاملة» — documentation of a marriage contract is prohibited for anyone
under **18 complete calendar years**. It also makes a **pre-marriage medical
examination** a condition of documentation, with the diseases and procedure
fixed by a Health-Minister decision agreed with the Justice Minister.

- ❌ **Get the home right:** this is **not** in the Child Law. The gazette
  heading of Law 126/2008 says «بتعديل بعض أحكام قانون الطفل» and the same law
  touches three statutes — that is why the internet files the rule under the
  Child Law. **Cite Art. 31 bis of Law 143/1994.**
- ❌ **No exception exists in the article** — no guardian-consent exception,
  no judicial dispensation. "She can marry at 16 with her guardian's consent"
  has **no basis in the statute**.
- ⚠️ **The sanction is disciplinary against the registrar only** («ويعاقب
  تأديبياً كل من وثق زواجاً بالمخالفة»). It is weak — and that weakness is why
  the age rule is enforced through other machinery.
- ✅ **The criminal layer is Penal Code Art. 227, and it is narrow:** up to
  **2 years** and/or a fine up to **EGP 300** for a party who lies about age to
  the authority to prove the other has reached the legal age (and the contract
  was documented on the lie); up to **a fine of EGP 500** for the person
  legally empowered to conclude the marriage who **knows** a party is under
  age. ⚠️ **No verified conviction under Art. 227 for an underage marriage was
  found** — do not assert enforcement that was not found.
- ❌ **The "1 year + 50,000–200,000 EGP" penalty everyone quotes is a 2022
  draft bill, never enacted** (committee approved in principle 3 July 2022;
  article-by-article discussion never finished). The "200,000" figure also
  circulates as a *human-trafficking* penalty — a different offence.

### 1.2 What documenting gives you — and the عرفي marriage

✅ **Where marriage and divorce are recorded** (Law 143/1994 Art. 5): by
«أقلام الكتاب بمحاكم الأحوال الشخصية» where **both parties are Egyptian and of
the same religion and sect**; by the **real-estate notary offices (الشهر
العقاري)** where one party is foreign or the two Egyptians differ in religion
or sect. The record must be lodged with the court registry **within 15 days**
(Arts. 30–31). **What توثيق gives you, in one line: an official document** —
and almost everything below turns on having one.

**The عرفي (undocumented) marriage — the single most practically important
doctrine in this area, stated wrongly in both directions.** The verified
position:

- ✅ **It is not void.** Egyptian law does not make registration a condition of
  *validity*; the عرفي contract is a valid marriage with its own internal
  effects.
- ✅ **But its proof is restricted by statute — Law 1/2000 Art. 17:** claims
  **arising out of the marriage contract** (mahr, جهاز, نفقة, قائمة) are **not
  admissible against a denial** unless the marriage is proved by an **official
  document**; **only تطليق/فسخ** claims are admissible where the marriage is
  proved by **any writing at all** (أية كتابة).
- ⭐ **So the honest answer to "what can I do about my عرفي marriage" is:** she
  can **get a divorce** (تطليق/فسخ) proved by any writing, and the child has
  نسب and maintenance routes — but the **money claims usually fail on denial**.
  The wrong advice in both directions: "عرفي gives you nothing" (❌ — divorce
  and the child's نسب are reachable) and "عرفي gives you everything" (❌ — the
  money is usually lost).
- ⚠️ **Do not promise a نسب outcome** — practice varies with the strength of
  the writing (§5 of this file's Scenario on children and the corpus §6).

### 1.3 Mahr, and the قائمة المنقولات

✅ **Law 25/1929 Art. 19 is the whole mahr rule, in three sentences:** (a) in a
dispute over the mahr, **the wife bears the burden of proof** — an undocumented
mahr is usually lost; (b) if she fails, **the husband's oath** decides; (c)
unless his figure is one that **could not plausibly be a mahr for a woman like
her**, in which case the court awards **مهر المثل** — which closes the "I
married her for one pound" escape. **مؤخر الصداق follows the same proof rule.**

- ⚠️ **The قائمة المنقولات** (household-goods inventory annexed to the
  contract) has **no statute of general application**. It works in practice
  as: (civil) a **signed written acknowledgment** — a claim for the items or
  their value; (criminal) prosecutions over it are brought under **Penal Code
  Art. 341 (خيانة الأمانة)**, but the elements (delivery as deposit + intent)
  matter and ⚠️ no verified Cassation ruling applying it to a قائمة was found
  — **do not tell a client that signing or contesting a قائمة produces a
  criminal conviction automatically**.
- ⭐ **A related giant trap:** under Art. 17 of Law 1/2000, a قائمة in an
  **عرفي** marriage is worth very little if the marriage itself is denied — the
  claim "arises out of the marriage contract".

## Scenario 2 — a marriage has broken down: choosing the route

### 2.1 The four routes at a glance

| Route | Who uses it | The price | Revocable? | Appealable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **His طلاق** (unilateral) | the husband | none | usually yes (رجعي) | documentation rules carry penalties |
| **خلع** (Law 1/2000 Art. 20) | the wife | **her own shari'a financial rights + return of the mahr** | ❌ **بائن** | ❌ **not at all** |
| **Judicial تطليق** (Law 25/1929/25/1920) | the wronged spouse, per ground | none or a بدل per fault | ❌ mostly **بائن** — but the **non-maintenance** divorce is رجعي | yes |
| **فسخ/بطلان** (annulment) | either | none | n/a | yes |

⚠️ **Choosing wrongly costs real money.** A wife who takes خلع gives up her own
financial claims; a wife who sues on non-maintenance gets a **revocable**
divorce the husband can undo in the عدة.

### 2.2 His طلاق — and the paperwork that decides everything

✅ **Law 25/1929 Arts. 1–5, read properly, because clients are usually wrong
about them:**

- **Art. 1:** no divorce from an intoxicated or coerced man.
- **Art. 3 ⭐:** «الطلاق المقترن بعدد لفظاً أو إشارة لا يقع إلا واحدة» —
  **"أنت طالق ثلاثاً" is ONE divorce, not three.** This single article decides
  the most common client panic in Egypt. (It does not undo pronouncements on
  *separate occasions*.)
- **Art. 4:** implied forms (كنايات) do not divorce without **intention**.
- **Art. 5:** every divorce is revocable (رجعي) except the third of three,
  a divorce before consummation, a divorce **for consideration**, and whatever
  is made **بائن** by statute — including **خلع** and the judicial divorces
  under Arts. 6, 12, 13, 14.
- ✅ **The documentation duty — Art. 5 bis:** the divorcer must **document the
  divorce within 30 days**; the wife is deemed aware if present, else the
  registrar must **serve her personally**; **the effects run from the
  pronouncement — but if he concealed it from her, her inheritance and other
  financial rights run only from the date she learned of it.**
- ✅ **Proof against a denial — Law 1/2000 Art. 21:** a divorce is **not
  provable except by إشهاد and توثيق**; the registrar must **warn of the risks
  and call each spouse to name an arbitrator** to reconcile them.
- ✅ **Revocation (مراجعة) — Law 1/2000 Art. 22:** not accepted against a
  denying husband unless he **notified her by official paper within 60 days**
  (menstrual عدة) **or 90 days** (monthly عدة) of the documentation — unless
  she is pregnant or has acknowledged the عدة continues. She may prove
  revocation **by any means**.
- ✅ **The penal sanction — Art. 23 bis:** the divorcer: up to **6 months**
  and/or **EGP 200** for breaching the documentation rules; the husband:
  likewise for **false marital-status or residence data** to the registrar
  (the Art. 11 bis duty, §2.6 of the corpus); the registrar: up to
  **1 month** and/or **EGP 50**, removable.
- ⭐ **The money point:** a wife divorced unilaterally is **not** stripped of
  her shari'a financial rights — that is the whole difference from خلع. Her
  checklist is §3 below.

### 2.3 خلع — the price, the words, and the line that protects the children

✅ **Law 1/2000 Art. 20 (read verbatim in the corpus). The five operating
rules:**

1. **The statutory consideration is fixed:** she returns **the mahr** and
   waives **her own shari'a financial rights (المالية الشرعية)**. It is not
   "whatever the parties agreed", and the court will not grant خلع without it.
2. **It is not a fast route.** The court must attempt reconciliation,
   **appoint two arbitrators**, and allow **up to three months** for it.
3. **The required statement is judicial, not moral:** she must **state
   expressly** that she abhors the life with him and fears she cannot keep
   God's limits («تبغض الحياة مع زوجها … وتخشى ألا تقيم حدود الله»). A judge
   who does not hear that statement cannot grant خلع.
4. ⭐ **The consideration cannot touch the children** — Art. 20(3) in terms:
   «ولا يصح أن يكون مقابل الخلع إسقاط حضانة الصغار أو نفقتهم أو أى حق من
   حقوقهم». A خلع deal that trades away custody or child maintenance is **not
   a valid consideration**. Her own rights are waived; **her children's
   survive**.
5. **No appeal at all** — «غير قابل للطعن عليه بأى طريق من طرق الطعن». The
   only family judgment in this area with that absolute finality. It cuts both
   ways: it cannot be appealed against her either, and it cannot be reopened.

⚠️ **Two consequences people get wrong:** (a) **no متعة on خلع** — she
consented, so the Art. 18 bis condition (divorced «دون رضاها ولا بسبب من
قبلها») fails; (b) the divorce is **بائن** — no رجعة.

### 2.4 Judicial تطليق — the grounds, each with its own proof

✅ **الضرر (Law 25/1929 Art. 6):** harm making cohabitation impossible —
**proven**, the judge divorces her **immediately بائناً**; **unproven but
repeated**, the case goes to the arbitration machinery. ⭐ **The fault
allocation decides the wife's money (Art. 11):** husband wholly at fault →
irrevocable divorce **with no prejudice to any marital right**; wife wholly at
fault → divorce **against a suitable consideration she bears**; shared → in
proportion; unknown → without consideration. ⚠️ And the arbitrators' opinion
**does not bind the court** (Law 1/2000 Art. 19: «وللمحكمة أن تأخذ بما انتهى
إليه الحكمان أو بأقوال أيهما أو بغير ذلك»).

✅ **The arbitration machinery (Arts. 7–11):** two arbitrators, **mandate not
exceeding six months** (one extension up to **3 months**), each **sworn**;
failure to report in time = **deemed disagreement**; non-attendance does not
stop it.

✅ **نشوز (Art. 11 bis/2) — read before advising a wife to "just leave":** if
she refuses to obey without right, **her maintenance stops from the date of
the refusal**; she is deemed refusing if she does not return after the
husband's court-officer summons **stating the residence**; her **objection
window is 30 days**, stating the shari'a grounds, failing which the objection
is not heard and the maintenance counts as stopped from the end of the period.
⚠️ **The common wrong advice is "he can't stop maintenance unless he sues for
نشوز" — the statute says maintenance stops on the refusal and is reviewed in
the objection.** And the 1985 reform protects her from the mirror abuse:
**going out to lawful work is not forfeiture** (Law 25/1920 Art. 1), and
leaving the marital home is not forfeiture **in the cases the shari'a
permits**.

✅ **Absence (Art. 12):** absent **a year or more** without acceptable excuse →
she may ask for an irrevocable divorce for harm, **even if he has money she
could spend from**. **Imprisonment (Art. 14):** husband finally sentenced to
**3+ years** → she may ask **one year after his imprisonment**.

✅ **Non-maintenance — the fastest judicial route (Law 25/1920 Arts. 4–6),
⭐ and the trap inside it:** he refuses to maintain: with **visible property**
the judgment is executed against it; with **no visible property and no claimed
insolvency but refusing to pay** — **the judge divorces him immediately**; if
he **claims insolvency and fails to prove it** — immediate divorce; if he
**proves insolvency** — a grace period of **up to one month**, then divorce.
⭐ **BUT the non-maintenance divorce is REVOCABLE (Art. 6):** he may take her
back in the عدة **if he proves solvency and readiness to spend**. **Tell the
client before they celebrate.**

✅ **Defects (Law 25/1920 Arts. 9–11):** a **settled incurable defect**
(عيب مستحكم) — the statute's examples: «الجنون والجذام والبرص» — separation is
**بائن**; but **no separation where the marriage was contracted knowing of the
defect**, or the defect arose and was accepted. ⚠️ **Do not state the
بطلان grounds from memory** — they are classical-fiqh grounds applied through
the promulgating default, not a codified list.

✅ **Bigamy as a ground (Art. 11 bis):** the husband must **declare his marital
status** in the marriage document and **name his existing wives**, whom the
registrar notifies by registered letter; a wife taken over may seek divorce
for material or moral harm **even without a contractual stipulation**, and the
right **lapses one year after she learns of the other marriage** (renewed each
new marriage; a new wife who did not know may also seek divorce).

### 2.5 فسخ / بطلان, briefly

✅ The claim sits before the Family Court with **compulsory expert attendance**
(Law 10/2004 Art. 11); ✅ Law 1/2000 Art. 17 admits the **فسخ** plea proved by
**any writing** — the statutory doorway for terminating an عرفي marriage; ⚠️
the **substantive impediments are Hanafi-default doctrine — do not enumerate
them with article numbers**.

## Scenario 3 — you are divorced: the money checklist

| Item | Article | The rule |
|---|---|---|
| **نفقة العدة** | ✅ Law 25/1920 Art. 2 + **Art. 3 bis** + Law 25/1929 Art. 17 | a **debt from the date of divorce**; both claim and execution **capped**: no execution beyond **1 year from the divorce** (non-nursing) / **2 years 3 months from delivery** (nursing) |
| **متعة** | ✅ Law 25/1929 **Art. 18 bis** (1985) | a wife of a **valid, consummated** marriage, divorced **without her consent and without fault on her part**: «متعة قدرها نفقة سنتين على الأقل», assessed on his circumstances, her conduct, and the marriage's duration; **instalments possible**. ⚠️ **None on خلع, none where she consented** |
| **مؤخر الصداق + جهاز/قائمة** | ✅ Law 25/1929 Art. 19 | burden on the wife; his oath; مهر المثل rescue (§1.3) |
| **Children's maintenance** | ✅ Law 25/1929 Art. 18 bis/2 | **continues on the father** regardless of the divorce route — and **runs from his abstention**, not the judgment |
| **Housing for the children** | ✅ Art. 18 bis/3 | **with two SCC subtractions** — §5.3 |
| **الوصية الواجبة / الأسر البديلة cross-checks** | — | not a divorced-wife item; §6 |

⭐ **The timing asymmetry is the number-one practical fact:** a **divorce
judgment is not enforceable until the cassation periods lapse** (Law 1/2000
Art. 63), but **maintenance, visitation and child-delivery orders are
enforceable at once, by force of law, without security** (Art. 65). **File the
نفقة order first; do not promise the divorce money fast.**

## Scenario 4 — maintenance: the claim, the clock, and the collection ladder

### 4.1 Who owes whom, and from when

✅ **Wife on the husband (Law 25/1920 Art. 1, 1985 text):** from **the date of
the valid contract** (she is entitled **even if wealthy (موسرة)** or of a
different religion), covering **الغذاء والكسوة والسكن ومصاريف العلاج**. It is
**not** due if she refuses herself without right, is prevented by a cause not
attributable to him, or leaves without permission — **with two carve-outs**:
lawful work (§2.4), and leaving in cases the shari'a permits.

✅ **Children on the father (Law 25/1929 Art. 18 bis/2):** if the child has no
money of his own — **the daughter until she marries or earns enough; the son
until he completes 15 able to earn**, and **beyond 15** if he is unable to earn
(disability), or **is pursuing study suited to his peers and aptitudes**, or
**such earning is not available**. The father must also **provide housing** at
the level appropriate to his means. ⭐ **Child maintenance runs from the date
of the father's abstention** («تستحق نفقة الأولاد على أبيهم من تاريخ امتناعه
عن الإنفاق عليهم»).

✅ **The wider family:** parents, relatives, and **أجر الحضانة، أجر الرضاعة،
أجر المسكن** are all inside the same system — named expressly in Law 1/2000
Art. 76 (garnishment) and Penal Code Art. 293 (the offence covers «أجرة حضانة
أو رضاعة أو مسكن»).

### 4.2 Assessment — and the one-year arrears bar

✅ **Law 25/1929 Art. 16(1):** maintenance is assessed **on the husband's
circumstances when it falls due, يسراً أو عسراً**, with a **floor at her
essential needs**. ⚠️ **There is no statutory percentage and no statutory
minimum figure** — the garnishment percentages below are **deduction
ceilings, not the award**.

✅ **Five rules in Law 25/1920 Art. 1 that decide the real case:**

1. maintenance is a **debt** — extinguished only by payment or release;
2. ⭐ **a claim for past maintenance is time-barred beyond one year ending at
   the date the claim is filed** — **file at once; do not let a client assume
   she can claim years of arrears**;
3. the husband's **set-off** is barred below her essential needs;
4. his maintenance debt carries a **privilege over all his property**, ranking
   above other maintenance debts;
5. plus the نشوز and work carve-outs (§2.4).

✅ **The income-hunting article — Law 1/2000 Art. 23:** if income is seriously
disputed, the court **must ask the Public Prosecution to investigate**, and
**notwithstanding bank secrecy (Decree-Law 205/1990) any body must give the PP
the information it holds**. ⭐ **This is the route for "he's a driver with no
income on paper".**

### 4.3 Provisional maintenance (نفقة مؤقتة) — the clock is the strongest right she has

✅ **Law 25/1929 Art. 16(2):** once the cause exists, the judge **must** impose
provisional maintenance for the wife **and her small children** within
**a fortnight at most** of filing, **by an unreasoned, immediately enforceable
order (غير مسبب واجب النفاذ فوراً)**. ✅ **Law 1/2000 Art. 10(4):** during the
divorce case itself the court may issue interim orders on رؤية and
**نفقة وقتية** (up or down), not appealable until final judgment (Art. 10(5)).

⚠️ **Two honest caveats:** (a) **two weeks is the statutory ceiling, not a
promise** — the settlement office (§7.1) and court lists sit in front of it,
so say "as early as the court can", never "money within 14 days"; (b) the
interim order fixes **her essential needs**, not the final standard.

### 4.4 The collection ladder, in order

| # | Route | What it needs | What stops it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Family Insurance Fund pays** (Law 1/2000 Arts. 71–72; Law 11/2004 Art. 3 as replaced by Law 113/2015) | an enforceable maintenance judgment for wife/divorcée/children/parents | the operating cap: **EGP 500/month per beneficiary**, no arrears; the balance «يتم رد فارق القيمة عند التحصيل» |
| 2 | **Automatic deduction from salary/pension** (Art. 73) | the bank's request + executory copy + proof of completed service | the Art. 76 ceilings: **wife 25% (40% if several)**, parents 25%, ≤2 children 35%, combinations **40–50%**, hard cap 50% pro rata; no salary → **Art. 74**: he must deposit in the **first week of each month** after the bank's notice |
| 3 | **Coercive imprisonment (Art. 76 bis, added by Law 91/2000)** | enforceable judgment + **proof he is able to pay** + court order + non-compliance → **up to 30 days**; release on paying **or a guarantor the creditor accepts**; judgment **final** | ability must be proved — and ⭐ **this route is the PRECONDITION of route 4** |
| 4 | **The criminal complaint (Penal Code Art. 293, as replaced by Law 6/2020)** | enforceable judgment + **ability** + **3 months' default after notification** + a complaint | paying, or an acceptable guarantor, defeats the penalty; **صلح** at any stage — with the **Bank's certificate** if the creditor already collected from the Fund |

⭐ **The order matters, and it is the trap:** do the free/mechanical things
first (Fund + deduction), then the court for the 30-day order **with evidence
of ability**, and **only then** the criminal complaint — a complaint filed
first can be defeated by the very article that creates the remedy. ✅ The bank
recovers what it paid **and its expenses** (Art. 75), and it has **priority
stacking**: wife → children → parents → relatives (Art. 77). ✅ **The
counter-offence (Art. 79):** 6 months' imprisonment minimum for obtaining Fund
money on **sham procedures or evidence** — the Fund is a soft target, and the
statute treats it as one.

⚠️ **Three figures that collapse into one sentence if you are careless:**
**EGP 5,000** = the fine ceiling in Art. 293 **as amended in 2020**;
**EGP 500** = the **pre-2020** fine text that still circulates (❌ do not cite
it); **EGP 500/month** = the **Fund's payment cap**. **Never mix them.**

### 4.5 The criminal route in detail

✅ **Art. 293 as it now stands (Law 6/2020, Gazette No. 5 bis, 2 February
2020):** anyone under an **enforceable judgment** to pay نفقة (or
حضانة/رضاعة/مسكن money) who refuses **for 3 months after notification** while
**able** — up to **1 year** and/or a fine **not exceeding EGP 5,000**. The
2020 additions: complaint by **«شكوى أو طلب»**; **suspension of the convict's
government-provided professional services** until he pays the creditor **and
the Bank**; **صلح** recorded at any stage extinguishing the action (but not
effective if the creditor already took the award from the Bank, unless he
produces the Bank's settlement certificate); and the pay-or-guarantee defence
survives. ✅ **Law 11/2004 Art. 3 (Law 113/2015)** lets **Nasser Social Bank**
itself move the Art. 293 action to recover what it paid.

⚠️ **And what the statute does NOT give the husband:** there is **no
provision** letting him stop paying maintenance because the custodian refuses
visits — the statute's remedy for refused visits is the **custody-transfer**
route (§5.2). When a client says "my lawyer told me I can stop paying if she
blocks the visits", the verified answer is: **the statute does not say that.**

## Scenario 5 — children: custody, visitation, the home

### 5.1 Custody — the statutory order, the age, and the choice

✅ **The age rule (Law 25/1929 Art. 20(1), as replaced by Law 4/2005, Gazette
No. 4 bis, 4 March 2005):** «ينتهى حق حضانة النساء ببلوغ الصغير أو الصغيرة سن
الخامسة عشرة» — **women's custody ends at 15 for a girl AND a boy alike.**
Four things follow, each commonly got wrong:

1. the pre-2005 split (**10 for a boy; until marriage for a girl**) is
   **repealed text** — ❌ do not repeat it;
2. **after 15 the child chooses** (يخير) to remain with the custodian;
3. ⭐ **from 15 the custodian receives NO custody wage** (دون أجر حضانة) — the
   money trap hidden in the age rule;
4. **the two end dates differ:** until the **boy reaches majority (الرشد)**
   and until the **girl marries** — not a common 18.

✅ **The order (Art. 20, final paragraph):** **الأم** first, then the female
محارم in a long fixed list (grandmothers, full sisters, half-sisters, aunts…),
**with the maternal side outranking the paternal where two equal female
relatives tie** («مقدما فيه من يلي بالأم على من يلي الأب»); then the male
عصبات by inheritance order; then the male محارم. ⚠️ **The order is statutory;
the outcome is not automatic** — the court must be guided by «مصالح الطفل
الفضلى» (Law 10/2004 Art. 10) and the **experts' reports are compulsory**
(Art. 11). ⚠️ **The disqualifications (remarriage to a non-محرم, apostasy,
misconduct) are Hanafi-default doctrine, not a codified list** — ⚠️ the claim
"a remarried mother loses custody automatically" is **not in the statute
read**; check current Cassation before warning anyone.

✅ **Where custody is documented:** it is a District-court (جزئية) matter
(Law 1/2000 Art. 9(أولاً)); the Family Court absorbed both dockets
(Law 10/2004 Art. 3(1)) — the Art. 9/10 distinction now decides **which
formation of the family court** sits.

### 5.2 رؤية and استضافة — the visit that can never be forced

✅ **Law 25/1929 Art. 20(2), verbatim, is the whole framework:**

- **Both parents** have the visitation right — and **grandparents too**: the
  words «عند عدم وجود الأبوين» were **struck down as unconstitutional**
  (**SCC, Case 37 of judicial year 33, 12 May 2013**, Gazette No. 21 bis,
  26 May 2013). **Grandparents claim رؤية in their own right.**
- If the parties cannot agree, the court fixes the arrangement and a place
  that **does not harm the child psychologically**.
- ⭐ **«ولا ينفذ حكم الرؤية قهراً» — the visitation order CANNOT be enforced
  by force.** The sanction for unjustified refusal is a **warning, then
  temporary transfer of custody** to the next person entitled, for a period
  the judge fixes, by an immediately enforceable judgment. **Never promise a
  parent police-assisted visitation; never let a custodian believe refusal is
  free.**
- ⚠️ **The operational quotas** (parent once/twice weekly; grandparents
  once/twice monthly; other محارم relatives 3–4 times a year; electronic
  contact) come from **MoJ Decision 1150/2010** — ⚠️ quoted only through
  press; **say "the ministry regulation is reported to provide…", never
  quote it as the statute. The statute itself sets no schedule.**
- ⚠️ **استضافة (overnight hosting) is not a term of the statute** — it is
  practice shorthand; the 2026 bills would codify schedules (reported: one
  24-hour stay weekly, school-holiday weeks, Eid days, and a suspension
  sanction if the paying parent falls 3 months behind). **Proposals, not
  law.**

✅ **The verified enforcement asymmetry — carry the two apart:** **ضم/حفظ/
تسليم** (handing over/keeping/receiving the child) **may be executed by
force** (Law 1/2000 Art. 66), with home entry **only per the execution
judge's order**, and the same instrument reusable whenever needed; **رؤية
never by force** (Art. 20(2) of Law 25/1929). ✅ Visits are executed at
venues designated by Justice-Minister decision **unless the parties agree on
another place** (Art. 67). ✅ The fastest child-return route in the statute —
and the least known: **Art. 70**, the **Public Prosecution's reasoned,
immediately enforceable decision** to hand a custodial-age child to the person
whose interest lies with him, pending the custody judgment, issued by **at
least a chief prosecutor**. **In a sudden removal of a child, ask about this
route, not the custody suit's first hearing.**

### 5.3 The children's housing — read the article with two subtractions

✅ **Law 25/1929 Art. 18 bis/3 (1985):** the divorcer must **prepare suitable
housing** for his small children and their custodian; if he fails within the
عدة, they may **stay in the marital home** (if rented, rent-free from him);
where it is not rented, he may take it back **if** he provides them suitable
alternative housing; the judge may instead fix **أجر مسكن (a rent allowance)**.

⚠️ **Two subtractions, both from the SCC (Case 5 of judicial year 8,
6 January 1996, Gazette No. 3, 18 January 1996), printed as notes on the
consolidated text:**

1. **The obligation falls away where the children have present money
   sufficient for housing, or the custodian has a residence — rented or not;**
2. **the article's time limit on the obligation was struck down too.**

⭐ **Consequence for a non-lawyer:** if the mother has her own flat or the
children have money, the "stay in the marital home" mechanism is **unavailable
— the remedy becomes a rent allowance.** Do not tell a client "she keeps the
flat"; the correct line is "she may keep it, or take a rent allowance instead,
depending on what she already has". ✅ The **Public Prosecution** may decide
possessory disputes over the marital home **pending the court's ruling** (last
paragraph).

### 5.4 الولاية — the two guardianships, and the wali question

✅ **الولاية على النفس:** the statute's only direct trace is **Law 1/2000
Art. 9(أولاً)(7)** — the District Court's power to **«الإذن بزواج من لا ولى
له»** (permit the marriage of a woman who has no wali). The substantive wali
rules are **Hanafi-default doctrine** (per which an adult of sound mind may
marry herself) — ⚠️ Dar al-Ifta's statements on this are **fatwas, not law**;
⚠️ and the reported practice (Ma'zouns demanding a wali) is journalism, not a
regulation. **The practical rule:** the law permits an adult woman to marry
without a wali; if the office refuses, the remedy is an **order on petition to
the judge of urgent matters** (Law 1/2000 promulgating Art. 1), not a lawsuit.
✅ **The age rule does not bend for the wali** — Art. 31 bis has no guardian
exception.

✅ **الولاية على المال:** a different regime, same court — the District Court
appoints and supervises the وصي/مشرف/مدير, ends/replaces them, decides
**الغيبة**, authorises the minor to trade, assesses the minor's maintenance
from his own property, and continues guardianship **beyond 21** (Law 1/2000
Art. 9(ثانياً); majority at **21**, litigation capacity in الولاية على النفس
from **15** — Art. 2). ✅ **Law 176/2020 replaced Art. 47**: the PP may
authorise a representative to disburse **up to EGP 10,000 (raisable to
20,000 by the Attorney-General), once every three months**, plus an emergency
paragraph for urgent medical necessity. ⚠️ **The substantive framework is Law
119/1952, not read in the corpus — for a question about a minor's money, read
it directly before advising.**

## Scenario 6 — a death in the family: inheritance and wills

### 6.1 Wills — one-third, and it may go to an heir

✅ **Law 71/1946 Art. 37, verbatim:** a bequest **up to one-third** is valid —
**to an heir or a non-heir** — and takes effect **without the heirs' consent**;
what exceeds one-third is valid but **not effective as to the excess** unless
the heirs consent **after the testator's death**, by persons competent to
donate and knowing what they approve. ❌ **The popular "الوصية لوارث باطلة"
contradicts the statute** — the Egyptian law deliberately departed from it.

✅ **الوصية الواجبة (Law 71/1946 Art. 76):** where the deceased did not
bequeath to the children of a **predeceased child**, those grandchildren take
an **obligatory bequest equal to what their parent would have inherited**,
**capped at one-third**, provided they are **not themselves heirs** and were
not already given that much. ✅ It **ranks above other wills** (Art. 78), is
**extracted first** before distributing the remainder (Cassation), and the
SCC **upheld it**. ⭐ The route: it is claimed **in the same certification
application** as the inheritance (Law 1/2000 Art. 24 names it expressly).

### 6.2 The estate and the shares

✅ **The order of payment (Law 77/1943 Art. 4):** (1) **burial costs** for the
deceased and his dependents, from death to burial; (2) **the debts** (the
widow's مؤخر الصداق sits here — characterised, not quoted); (3) **the will**
within its enforceable limit; then distribution; with no heirs at all, an
acknowledged نسب-claimant first, then the excess bequest, then the **Public
Treasury**.

✅ **Bars and gate rules worth knowing:** the killer of the deceased is barred
(Art. 5, including a false witness whose testimony led to an execution;
excess in self-defence excused); **no inheritance between a Muslim and a
non-Muslim** (Art. 6 — hard and severe for a mixed marriage, and still the
text); the heir must be **alive at the death** (Art. 2; the **حمل مستكن**
inherits, Art. 43); where the order of deaths is unknown, neither inherits
from the other (Art. 3).

✅ **The shares (Art. 8 lists the فرض holders; Arts. 9–21 set them).** The
outline: **husband 1/2 (no descendant) / 1/4 (with); wife 1/4 (none) / 1/8
(with) — plural wives share one fraction; mother 1/6 with a descendant or two
siblings, 1/3 otherwise; father 1/6 with descendants; daughters 1/2 one, 2/3
two-plus; sisters likewise; paternal siblings on the same pattern; father/
grandfather with a daughter: 1/6 **plus the residue** (Art. 21).**

⚠️ **Do NOT tell a person their share from this outline.** Exclusion (الحجب),
the return (الرد), residuary (العصوبة) and the special combinations are
arithmetic over the **full family tree** (Arts. 15–23). ⚠️ **The single most
common arithmetic error: the wife's 1/8 requires a descendant — otherwise it
is 1/4.** Never quote a fraction without the descent condition.

### 6.3 ⭐ The withholding offence — Law 219/2017, and it is NOT in the Penal Code

✅ **Art. 49 of Law 77/1943, added by Law 219/2017** (Gazette No. 52 bis (أ),
30 December 2017), read verbatim in the SCC's own judgment: **imprisonment of
not less than 6 months** and/or a fine of **EGP 20,000–100,000** for anyone who
**wilfully** refuses to hand an heir his shari'a share, **conceals a document
evidencing a share**, or refuses to hand over such a document on demand;
**not less than 1 year** on repetition; **صلح** possible at any stage — even
after the judgment is final — extinguishing the criminal action without
prejudice to civil rights.

- ⚠️ **Three cautions:** it requires **intent (عمدًا)** and possession — it is
  not an automatic "brother goes to prison" rule; the complaint route is
  **ordinary criminal procedure** (corpus file 06); and it sits **in Law
  77/1943, not the Penal Code** — which is exactly why searches for
  "عقوبة حرمان البنت من الميراث في قانون العقوبات" return nothing.
- ✅ **Constitutional status:** the SCC **upheld Art. 49** (a challenge filed
  2021 was rejected — the article protects inheritance as a source of private
  property under Constitution Art. 35).

### 6.4 عرفي marriages and inheritance — the two ways it fails

✅ **The widow's claim "arises out of the marriage contract"**, so **Law 1/2000
Art. 17(2)** blocks it **on denial** without an official document — an **عرفي
widow cannot claim her inheritance where the heirs deny the marriage**. ✅
**A second, independent bar for a divorced wife** — Law 25/1929 Art. 17: her
inheritance claim is not heard on denial where the husband died **more than
a year after the divorce** — but read it with **Art. 5 bis(3)**: where he
**concealed** the divorce, her financial rights run **from the date she
learned of it**. ✅ **The child's route** runs through **نسب** — the evidence
problem of §1.2 and the corpus's parentage rules govern, and documentary
acknowledgements help.

### 6.5 Getting the estate certified — the procedural track

✅ **Law 1/2000 Arts. 24–25:** apply to the competent court with an **official
paper proving the death**, stating the **deceased's last domicile, the heirs,
and the obligatory-will legatees**, **served with notice**; the judge verifies
by reliable testimony and may add administrative enquiries; **a genuine denial
refers the application to the Court of First Instance**; the certificate is
**conclusive evidence** unless a judgment rules otherwise. ✅ **Law 10/2004
Art. 3(3):** the **president of the Family Court** issues the death and
inheritance certificates, and may refer them on a serious dispute. ⚠️ **An
estate claim is not heard on denial if brought more than 33 years after the
right arose** (Law 1/2000 Art. 8(2)) — a long-stop, not a licence.

## Scenario 7 — how a family case actually runs

### 7.1 ⭐ The settlement office comes first — and it is free

✅ **Law 10/2004 annexed Arts. 5–9:** in every District-court district there is
a **family-dispute settlement office (مكتب تسوية المنازعات الأسرية)**, staffed
by legal/social/psychological specialists. **Who must go:** everyone bringing
a settlable personal-status claim — **except** claims where reconciliation is
impossible, **urgent matters (الأوامر الوقتية), execution disputes, and interim
orders**. **The clock:** settlement must conclude **within 15 days** (extendable
only by the parties' agreement). **The outputs:** a settled dispute is recorded
in minutes that have **«قوة السندات واجبة التنفيذ» — the force of an executory
instrument** (a free, fast enforcement title almost every guide misses); an
unsettled one produces minutes + experts' reports sent to the court **within
7 days**. ⭐ **The sanction for skipping it:** the claim is **«لا تقبل»** — but
the court **may instead refer it to the office**, so in practice the omission
costs time, not the case. ⭐ **And the office is free** («بدون رسوم»).

✅ **Urgent matters are not stuck behind the office.** The judge-of-urgent-
matters' **order on petition** (Law 1/2000 promulgating Art. 1) covers, among
five matters: **the Tawthiq/refusal problem** — «التظلم من امتناع الموثق عن
توثيق عقد الزواج أو عدم إعطاء شهادة مثبتة للامتناع» — and **«المنازعات حول
السفر إلى الخارج»**. Law 10/2004 Art. 3(4) gives the power to the **president
of the family court**.

### 7.2 Fees — what is free, what is unpriced

✅ **The free parts:** **maintenance claims and what is in their like (الأجور
والمصروفات) are exempt from all judicial fees at every stage** (Law 1/2000
Art. 3(2)), expressly including **imprisonment-for-non-execution claims**
before the family courts (Law 10/2004 Art. 3(2)); **no lawyer's signature is
required** on a District-court personal-status claim sheet, and a court-
appointed lawyer is paid by the Treasury (Art. 3(1)); the **settlement office
is free**; transferred claims enter **fee-free**.

⚠️ **The unpriced parts — say so rather than guess:** the exemption's words
cover the **maintenance family only**. **مهر، جهاز، دوطة، شبكة، مقابل الخلع،
الميراث** and property claims sit **outside the express words** — their fees
are set by general fee legislation and registry practice, ⚠️ **not priced in
the corpus**. The rule: **never state a figure**; say *"this claim is outside
the statutory exemption; ask the family court's registry (قلم الكتاب) for a
written fee estimate (تقدير الرسوم)"*. ⚠️ **The two experts' fees** — the
largest real cost driver in a contested divorce/custody case — are likewise
**not priced in the statute read**.

✅ **What the Fund charges at the counter** (Law 11/2004 Art. 2, as replaced by
Law 113/2015 — receipts for the Fund, not court fees): marriage **EGP 100**
(husband) + **4** per document extract; divorce/revocation **100** + **9**
per extract; birth certificate **20** first extract + **3** each later;
death-certificate/family-record extracts **4**; national ID **5**. ❌ **Any
pre-2015 table (50/50/20) is superseded.**

### 7.3 Where to file — and the one-file rule

✅ **Local jurisdiction (Law 1/2000 Art. 15(1)):** the **defendant's domicile**
normally — **but for claims by the children, the wife, the parents or the
custodian** (نفقة/أجور، حضانة/رؤية، مهر/جهاز، تطليق/خلع), competence lies with
**either her chosen court or his** — her choice. Estate verification → the
deceased's **last domicile in Egypt** (else where an asset sits);
property guardianship → the wali's/minor's domicile.

✅ **The one-file rule (Law 10/2004 annexed Art. 12):** the family court that
receives the **first** claim between the spouses is competent, to the exclusion
of others, for **all** later claims between them arising out of the marriage
or divorce — and a **family file (ملف للأسرة)** is opened into which everything
after is deposited. ⭐ Practical: file a later claim in a different court and
you risk a transfer order.

### 7.4 ⭐ One court, one judgment — the linked-claims rule

✅ **Law 1/2000 Art. 10(2)–(5):** where a **طلاق/تطليق/تفريق جسماني** claim is
pending, that court rules **alone**, at first instance, on **maintenance,
custody, حفظ، رؤية، ضم، الانتقال، مسكن الحضانة** — and the other courts
**must refer** those claims to it «حتى يصدر فيها حكم قطعى واحد». ⭐ **Tell the
client: bring the money and children claims INSIDE the divorce case** — filed
separately they will be referred, costing months.

### 7.5 The two reconciliations, the arbitrators, and the appeal question

✅ **The statutory reason a divorce cannot be fast — Law 1/2000 Art. 18:** in
طلاق/تطليق claims the court may not rule before a **genuine** reconciliation
attempt; **where there is a child, it must offer صلح twice, with 30–60 days
between the sessions** — plus the arbitrators of Art. 19 (each spouse names
one; failure to name = the court names; their opinion **does not bind the
court**). ⚠️ **Do not promise a fast decree, and do not let the client
conclude the judge is delaying on purpose.**

✅ **Appeal (Law 1/2000 Arts. 56–62):** appeal to the family **appeal
circuits**; the **PP may always appeal**; the appellate court may issue
**interim enforceable orders** on رؤية/نفقة pending its judgment (Art. 59);
**60 days** is the appeal period for a person **with no domicile in Egypt**
(Art. 61 — the only figure in the article; ⚠️ do not state the domestic
period); Art. 62 opens cassation in appeal-court judgments — **but Law 10/2004
Art. 14 closes it** for the specialised family appeal circuits, «مع عدم
الإخلال بأحكام المادة (250)» of the CPC. ⚠️ **The two annexed laws conflict;
the later, specific rule (finality) governs in principle, subject to whatever
Art. 250 preserves — advise finality and have a lawyer check Art. 250 before
filing any cassation.**

## The frame worth naming, in one place

### ⚠️ The honesty limits

1. **Practice is not statute.** The wali demand at the counter, the visitation
   quotas, the Fund's EGP 500 cap, the police recovering children — each is
   reported practice, ministerial decision, bank page or press, not the text
   an article contains. **Name the source of every sentence.**
2. **A fatwa is not a law.** Dar al-Ifta's opinions on the wali, divorce and
   custody are **fatwas**; they are useful to explain *why the law is as it
   is*, and they are **not** citable as the rule.
3. **An expert report is not a verdict.** The social/psychological experts'
   reports are compulsory in the big claims — and the court is **not bound**
   by them. **Do not predict outcomes.**
4. **The shares need a tree.** Any inheritance question answered without the
   full family tree is answered wrongly.

## Workflow

1. **Establish the confession** — the spouses' religion(s) and sect, because
   the confessional structure (§FIRST) decides which rules govern at all.
2. **Establish which route the case is on** — his طلاق, خلع, judicial تطليق on
   which ground, or فسخ — and **check its money consequences** (§2.1) before
   anything else.
3. **Check the paper** — is there an **official document**? If عرفي, run the
   Art. 17 consequences: divorce provable by any writing; money claims fail on
   denial; نسب with the corpus's limits.
4. **For maintenance: find the dates** — contract/abstention/divorce — apply
   the **one-year arrears bar** and the assessment rule, then the **provisional
   order**, then the **collection ladder in its order** (Fund → deduction →
   30-day imprisonment → criminal complaint last).
5. **For children: run the Art. 20 order and the 15-year rule**, then
   visitation's **no-force** rule and the **custody-transfer sanction**, then
   the housing subtractions.
6. **For an estate: the Art. 4 payment order**, the bars, and — only with the
   **full family tree** — the shares; check the **الوصية الواجبة**; if
   someone is withholding, name **Law 77/1943 Art. 49** (not the Penal Code).
7. **For any case about to be filed: the settlement office first** unless it
   is urgent; **file everything inside the first claim's court**; and if a
   divorce is pending, **bring the maintenance/custody/visitation claims
   inside it**.
8. **Before any figure:** is it one of the verified ones? If not —
   **the registry's written estimate, and no number from you**.

## What never to do

- ❌ **Never say "the new family law" applies, is passed, or will apply to a
  pending case.** It is three bills in committee, none enacted.
- ❌ **Never cite Law 462/1955 as the current rule for non-Muslims.** Cite
  **Law 1/2000 promulgating Art. 3(2)**.
- ❌ **Never cite the Child Law for the marriage age.** It is **Art. 31 bis of
  Law 143/1994** (added by Law 126/2008), sanctioned **disciplinary**.
- ❌ **Never quote the "200,000 EGP" child-marriage penalty as law.** It is a
  **2022 draft**, never enacted.
- ❌ **Never merge the two age rules.** Documentation age **18/18**
  (Art. 31 bis); claim-admissibility ages **wife 16 / husband 18 at filing**
  (Law 1/2000 Art. 17(1)).
- ❌ **Never promise custody to a parent** — the order is statutory, the
  outcome turns on the child's best interest and compulsory expert reports.
- ❌ **Never say "custody after 15 goes to the father".** **The child
  chooses** — and from 15 there is no custody wage.
- ❌ **Never promise a forced visit.** **«ولا ينفذ حكم الرؤية قهراً»** — the
  sanction is temporary transfer of custody. (But **ضم/تسليم may be by
  force**, per the execution judge.)
- ❌ **Never tell a wife an عرفي marriage gives her everything or nothing.**
  Divorce and نسب yes; money on denial no.
- ❌ **Never claim years of arrears.** **One year**, ending at the filing date.
- ❌ **Never file the Art. 293 criminal complaint before exhausting Art. 76
  bis.** The precondition defeats the complaint.
- ❌ **Never quote the EGP 500 fine for Art. 293.** It is **EGP 5,000** since
  2020 — and 500/month is the **Fund cap**, a different thing.
- ❌ **Never say "she keeps the flat"** or that children's housing is
  absolute — **two SCC subtractions** apply.
- ❌ **Never say a will to an heir is void.** **One-third is valid to anyone**;
  only the excess needs the heirs' consent after death.
- ❌ **Never cite the Penal Code for the inheritance-withholding offence.** It
  is **Art. 49 of Law 77/1943** (Law 219/2017).
- ❌ **Never state a DNA-based paternity promise.** ❌ **There is no DNA
  statute** — the report is an expert opinion the court weighs.
- ❌ **Never say a family case is free** — only the **maintenance family** is
  fee-exempt; quote no other fee figure.
