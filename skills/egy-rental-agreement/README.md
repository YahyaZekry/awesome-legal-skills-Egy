Draft, review and end a residential lease in Egypt, and handle rent disputes.

Leads with what almost nobody has written up: **Law 164/2025 and Law 165/2025 were promulgated 4 August 2025** (Gazette 31 bis, in force 5 August 2025) and they **end the old regime on a statutory clock** — old-system residential leases after **7 years (≈ 5 Aug 2032)**, non-residential natural-person leases after 5 — and **repeal Laws 49/1977, 136/1981 and 6/1997 outright** in 2032. Anyone telling you an إيجار قديم lease is permanent is out of date. **Art. 6** of Law 164/2025 also creates Egypt's **first mandatory rent index**: **+15% annually by statute**, on top of a 20×/10×/5× reset, and it is **not subject to contract**.

The provision that catches almost every landlord: **Civil Code Art. 598** — a lease ends automatically at the end of its term with no notice needed — but **Arts. 599(1) and 600** mean that if the lessor serves **no notice** and the tenant stays, the fixed term converts into an **indefinite periodic lease**. Not serving notice doesn't preserve your position; it destroys it.

Also carries: **key money is banned** for old-system leases (Law 49/1977 Art. 26) with criminal sanctions and a reporting amnesty; deposits are **capped at 2 months** and recoverable by set-off without suing; the **closed eviction list** at Law 136/1981 Art. 18 which **expressly excludes expiry of the agreed term**; the new aggressive 2025 ground that a tenant **owns another usable unit**; and the registration trap — **Law 114/1946 Art. 12 bis** makes the **date of an unregistered registrable instrument unprovable**, with notarisation as the cheap fix via Law 137/2006.
