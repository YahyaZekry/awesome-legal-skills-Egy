Compliance audit and fix for an online shop or website selling to consumers in Egypt, under Consumer Protection Law No. 181 of 2018 (as amended by Law 20 of 2024), its Executive Regulations (PM Decree 822 of 2019), and Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 with its Executive Regulations (MCIT Decision 816 of 2025).

Leads with the rule that catches almost every foreign seller: **Art. 5 requires advertisements, invoices, receipts, contracts and electronic records to be in Arabic** — multilingual is fine, Arabic-only is not. Then works through the eleven mandatory pre-contract disclosures for distance contracts (Art. 37), the 7-working-day order-correction window (Art. 38), the 14-day returns and the delay rule under which **your own late delivery converts shipping costs from the customer to you** (Art. 40), the six statutory exceptions to the withdrawal right (Art. 41), and **Art. 28, which makes any contract term reducing your obligations void regardless of what it says**.

Also states the things commentary gets wrong: there is **no adequacy list and no SCC route** for cross-border data transfers in the enacted text, and children's data is **categorically** sensitive. Penalties run to EGP 2,000,000, double on recidivism, and to life imprisonment where a consumer dies.
