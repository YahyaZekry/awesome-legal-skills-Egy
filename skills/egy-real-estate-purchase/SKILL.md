# Egyptian real-estate purchase — the registry, not the paper

**Not legal advice.** A property purchase is the largest single transaction most
people ever make, and Egyptian law makes the difference between "I signed" and "I own"
the entire game. Use this skill to orient, then get an Egyptian lawyer and a licensed
broker for anything beyond checking.

## What this skill does

Answers the questions people actually arrive with — *I signed the contract, is the
flat mine? what must I check before paying? what does registration cost? who pays the
2.5%? can a foreigner buy? is this desert/agricultural/Sinai land? the unit has a
crack, what are my rights?* — and every answer names the article or the official
figure behind it.

**It is organised by situation, not by statute**, because nobody meets real-estate
law as a taxonomy. Go to the section that matches.

---

## ⚠️ FIRST: signing a contract does not make you the owner

✅ **Ownership of real property in Egypt passes only by registration (الشهر/القيد) at
the Real-Estate Registry** — not by signing, not by handing over keys, not by paying
in full.

- An unregistered sale contract **binds the two parties personally** — but it
  transfers **nothing in rem** against anyone else (الغير). Cassation **680/64 ق** and
  **3037/59 ق**: the unregistered sale transfers the *benefits* between the parties,
  not ownership as against third parties.
- ✅ The State and the utilities will not deal with an unregistered buyer:
  **Law 186/2020 added Art. 35 مكرر to Law 114/1946** — government bodies, utilities
  and public institutions **refuse unregistered buyers** (electricity, water, gas
  connections and more).
- ⭐ **So: "I bought the flat" in Egypt means "I registered it."** If the seller
  proposes to leave it unregistered "to save money", the saving is not yours — it is
  a permanent defect in your title.

## The three traps that cost people their homes

### 1. وعد البيع (the promise of sale)

❌ **"وعد البيع ينقل الملكية" — it does not.** A promise of sale is a **تعهد بفعل**
(an obligation to act), not a transfer (Council of State heritage judgment 3856).
If the seller later refuses, your path is: sue → obtain a final judgment → register
the judgment. Law 114/1946 gives the mechanics: **Art. 9(4)** (registration by
judgment), **Art. 17** (a **5-year** window to register the judgment — miss it and the
judgment loses its registration power), and the possession route below.

### 2. التوكيل بالبيع (the POA used as a deed)

- ❌ **"أي توكيل يصلح لنقل الملكية"** — only a POA that **expressly authorises sale**
  does anything here; a general or administrative POA does not.
- ⚠️ Cassation (طعن **7910 لسنة 91 ق**, reported 6 March 2026): a POA authorising the
  agent to sell **to himself or others** with intent to complete the sale itself ranks
  as a **sale contract**. That is a two-edged instrument — understand it before
  signing one either as buyer or seller.

### 3. الشقق على الورق (unregistered apartments, esp. off-plan)

⚠️ Off-plan and unregistered units are the most common Egyptian purchase — and the
most exposed. The buyer holds a contract against a developer, and everything depends
on what the developer can and will register. Verify **who holds the land title, in
what form, and with what mortgage or lien on it** before any money moves.

## Before you pay: the checklist

`As of: 2026-10-28`

1. **Registry extraction (كشف قيد) for the exact unit** — from the
   الشهر العقارى office. Confirm: registered owner's name matches the seller's ID;
   the property's description (area, floor, boundaries) matches what you saw; no
   mortgages, liens, court seals or prior registered sales on the record.
2. **Identity**: seller's national ID; if married, know that **spousal consent issues
   exist in family-law practice** — raise it with the lawyer.
3. **Land type** (§6 below): ordinary urban, new-cities, desert, agricultural or
   Sinai — the rules differ drastically.
4. **The price and how it is paid** — `04-practical-operations-egypt.md` §7.2 for
   paying safely (no cash at a café; registered instruments; the Art. 62 rule that
   a creditor may demand payment by cheque above 100,000 EGP).
5. **Taxes and fees** (§Fees and taxes below) — agree in the contract **who pays what**.
6. **Defects and warranty terms in writing** (§Defects below).
7. ⚠️ **A penalty clause for late registration/delivery**, and a **registration
   deadline in the contract itself** — "the seller will register later" without a date
   and a penalty is where flats stay unregistered forever.

## Registration — the real mechanics and fees

`As of: 2026-10-28`

- ✅ **Ordinary route**: contract → registration at the الشهر العقارى. Fees are
  **area-tiered: 500 / 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000 EGP** for residential units, with a
  **maximum of 3,900 EGP**, per the Ministry of Justice's official statements
  (Ahram Gate, 12 May 2022) and the official real-estate platform (EREP, 2024).
  Decision timeline ≤ **37 days**.
- ❌ **"تسجيل شقة يكلّف 500 جنيه"** — the ≤500 EGP fee is statutory **only** for the
  special route below; a flat-500 claim for ordinary registration is wrong, and so is
  any "3–3.5% of value" claim.
- ✅ **The possession route — Law 9/2022 Art. 10 مكررا**: applicants with **five
  years' actual possession** plus an **عرفي deed** may register through the special
  committee, with a **fee ≤ 500 EGP** for that route (Law 9/2022 Art. 23 مكررا).
  Law 9/2022 entered into force **7 May 2022**.
- ⚠️ **The Art. 35 مكرر consequence (Law 186/2020)**: plan the utilities transfers
  *around* registration, not before it.

## Fees and taxes — who pays what

`As of: 2026-10-28`

- ✅ **The 2.5% property-disposition tax — Law 151/2026 Art. 43** (in force
  **29 July 2026**): **2.5% on the gross value of the property disposition**, and it
  applies **even to unregistered contracts** — **heirs included**. Payment window:
  **60 days**. Exclusions include: forced sale (بيع جبري), expropriation (نزع
  ملكية), gift/will **to parents/spouse/descendants** (هبة/وصية للأصول/الزوج/الفروع),
  and a share given *in kind* on condition of non-disposal for **five years**.
  ⭐ This tax catches unregistered sales and inheritances — "we didn't register, so no
  tax" is wrong.
- ✅ **Annual property tax — Law 3/2026**: the residential self-use exemption is
  **100,000 EGP of net annual rental value** (❌ the 24,000 figure is stale; ❌ 50,000
  was the draft-stage number). On-time filing discounts of **25%/10%**, an amnesty,
  and a **70% settlement** facility.
- ❌ **"لا توجد ضريبة على بيع عقار"** — false; and "the 2.5% is new in 2026" is wrong
  in the sense that matters: the *rate's current statutory home* is Law 151/2026
  (§5 of the corpus for the amendment history).

## Foreign buyers

`As of: 2026-10-28`

✅ **Law 230/1996** (verified via GAFI and Andersen's English summary): non-Egyptians
may own up to **two properties** for family residence, each **≤ 4,000 m²**; the
property should be **built (5-year rule)** and resale generally waits **five years**
from acquisition; **Prime-Minister-level consent** applies; the transaction runs
through the **GAFI gate** (مكتب تملك غير المصريين — 🟡 procedure internals, see the
corpus backlog).

## Restricted land — where the contract may be void outright

`As of: 2026-10-28`

- ✅ **Agricultural land**: purchase by non-farmers is banned — **Law 15/1963** (Art. 1)
  as amended by **Law 104/1985**; still standing.
- ✅ **Desert lands**: **Law 143/1981** regime, amended by **Law 11/2024**. ⚠️ Two laws
  numbered 143 exist — 143/**1981** (desert) and 143/**1994** (civil status). There is
  **no "Law 143/2017 registration law"** — that instrument is a phantom (corpus 09
  corrections #1).
- ✅ **Sinai**: **Decree-Law 14/2012** + its amendment chain (**95/2015**,
  **Law 172/2020**, **Decree 128/2022**, ministerial circular **33/2022**) — contracts
  in Sinai are **void absolutely without the required approvals**. ⭐ No cure, no
  waiver, no "we'll regularise later": without the approvals there is no deal.

## Defects — what you can actually claim

`As of: 2026-10-28`

- ✅ **CC Arts. 449/452**: the buyer must notify and sue within **one year** of
  discovering the latent defect — except **fraud (غش)**, which keeps the claim alive
  beyond the year.
- ✅ **CC Art. 651 — the ten-year structural guarantee**: the builder/designer/supervisor
  guarantees **collapse and structural defects for ten years** (Cassation
  **4503/61 ق**, 11 January 1998). ⭐ Cracks and structural faults are a **651**
  conversation, not a small-defect one.

## Scam patterns this skill exists to catch

- The seller is not the registered owner (bought with a وعد بيع from someone else).
- The "landlord" selling a third party's property with a forged POA.
- The same flat sold to several buyers via unregistered وعد بيع contracts.
- Desert/agricultural/Sinai land sold with "papers" that are not registration deeds.
- Off-plan units where the land is mortgaged to the developer's bank.
- ⚠️ **Every one of these is visible in the registry extraction and the land-type
  check — the two steps people skip.**

## What this skill must never say

- ❌ "You signed, so you own it."
- ❌ Any flat registration fee ("500 EGP for everything").
- ❌ "No tax on property sales."
- ❌ Any figure for the CPC/property-tax thresholds without the as-of caveat — the
  exemption is **100,000 EGP** (Law 3/2026), everything else 🟡.
- ❌ A Sinai contract described as merely "risky" — without approvals it is **void**.
