# 1. The Civil Code lease chapter — Arts. 558 to 634 (verified verbatim)

**Citation:** Law No. 131 of 1948 (القانون المدني), **الكتاب الثاني — الباب الثاني: العقود الواردة على الانتفاع بالشيء، الفصل الأول: الإيجار**.
**Status:** in force. The text below is read from the official Arabic text published by the Egyptian government.
**Sources:** https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-10/law-131-1948.pdf (downloaded, `pdftotext -layout`, NFKC-normalised, extracted locally — **do not** rely on a scraper's "query" mode on this PDF; it silently returns wrong articles).
**As of:** 2026-10-12.

⚠️ **Caveat that matters:** this PDF is the **1948 original text**. It carries **no amendment annotations**. Several lease
articles have certainly been amended in the last 40 years (Art. 599(2) plainly already reflects an amendment, because it
references الشهر العقاري). Before citing any article in advice, check the consolidated text at
**بوابة تشريعات محكمة النقض** (search "بوابة تشريعات" on the Cassation Court site). Article *numbers* are the same; *wording* may not be.

### 1.1 Formation and essential elements

- **Art. 558 (the definition — quote this one):**
  > «الإيجار عقد يلتزم المؤجر بمقتضاه أن يمكن المستأجر من **الانتفاع بشيء معين مدة معينة** لقاء **أجر معلوم**.»

  Three elements, explicitly: (a) a **specified thing**, (b) a **specified period**, (c) **known rent**. Note what is *not*
  required: no registration, no notarisation, no writing.
- **Art. 559** — a person who holds **only a right of management (حق الإدارة)** cannot lease for more than **three years**
  without a licence from the competent authority; if he does, the term is cut to three years unless a text provides otherwise.
  *This is the anti-proxy-lease trap. A family member without power of attorney cannot sign a 10-year lease.*
- **Art. 560** — a lease granted by a ** usufructuary (حق المنفعة)** expires with that right unless the owner of the
  bare ownership (مالك الرقبة) renews it, subject to the eviction-notice periods and the time needed to harvest the crop.
- **Art. 561** — rent may be **money or any other benefit (تقدمة أخرى)**. *Cash rent is not compulsory — a crop share or services can be the rent.*
- **Art. 562** — if the parties did not agree the amount or the method of assessment, **or the amount cannot be proved**, then **أجرة المثل** (market/equivalent rent) applies.
  ⚠️ *This is the answer to "what if the rent is blank on the lease". It is not void — it is priced at market rent. A blank-rent residential lease is therefore a very expensive document, not a cheap one.*
- **Art. 563 (term where none agreed — the "periodic lease" rule):** a lease concluded **without agreement on a term**, or for an **indefinite** term, or where the alleged term cannot be proved, is deemed concluded **for the period fixed for the payment of rent** (i.e. one month if rent is monthly) and ends on the expiry of that period **on the application of one of the parties if he has given the other notice to vacate** in these periods:
  - **(أ)** agricultural land and desert land: if the rent period is **six months or more**, notice **three months** before expiry; if shorter, before the **last half**.
  - **(ب)** houses, stores, offices, shops, factories, warehouses and the like: if the rent period is **four months or more**, notice **two months** before; if shorter, before the **last half**.
  - **(ج) ⭐ houses, furnished rooms and anything not above**: if the rent period is **two months or more**, notice **one month** before; if less, before the **last half**.
  *For a monthly residential rent this means: **one month's notice**. That is the single most practical provision in the chapter and it is routinely mis-stated as "two months".*

### 1.2 The lessor's obligations — delivery, fitness, repairs (⭐ the habitability articles)

- **Art. 564** — the lessor must deliver the thing **and its appurtenances in a condition fit to render the service it was let for**, according to the agreement or the nature of the thing.
- **Art. 565(1)** — if the thing is delivered **unfit** for the agreed use, or that use is substantially curtailed, the lessee may seek **rescission** if justified.
  **Art. 565(2) ⭐** — if the thing is in a condition exposing **the lessee, those living with him, his employees or his workmen to serious danger (خطر جسيم)**, the lessee may seek rescission **even if he has already waived that right**.
- **Art. 566** — the rules on delivering a leased thing are the same as for delivering a sold thing (Arts. 1014 ff.).
- **Art. 567 — the maintenance clause (read this in full):**
  - **(1)** the lessor undertakes to keep the property in the condition in which he delivered it and to carry out **all necessary repairs, other than "التأجيرية" repairs** (the routine/tenant-type repairs that custom casts on the lessee).
  - **(2)** roofs, courtyards and yards; emptying wells, latrines and drains.
  - **(3)** the lessor bears **costs and taxes on the leased property** and is bound to pay for water **if it is charged flat (جزافا)**; if it is charged **by meter (بالعداد)** it falls on the lessee. **Electricity, gas and everything personal to use fall on the lessee.**
  - **(4)** unless the agreement provides otherwise.
- **Art. 568 — the self-help repair right (⭐ practically the most useful article in the chapter):**
  - **(1)** if the lessor, after being notified (إعذار), fails to carry out the Art. 567 obligations, the lessee may get a **court licence** to do the work himself and **set it off against the rent** — without prejudice to his right to seek rescission or a rent reduction.
  - **(2) ⭐ no licence needed at all** for **urgent repairs or minor repairs which are the lessor's obligation**, whether the defect existed at the start of the enjoyment or arose later, if the lessor has still not done them within a reasonable time after notice. The lessee recovers his outlay **by set-off against the rent**.
- **Art. 569 (loss of the thing):**
  - **(1)** if the thing is **totally destroyed** during the lease by no fault of either party, **the contract terminates automatically**.
  - **(2)** if **partial** destruction, or the thing becomes unfit, or the enjoyment is substantially curtailed **without any fault of the lessee**, and the lessor does not restore it within a reasonable time, the lessee may — as the circumstances warrant — seek **either a rent reduction or rescission**, without prejudice to his Art. 568 self-help right.
  - **(3)** **the lessee cannot claim compensation** where the destruction or damage is due to a cause beyond the lessor's control.
- **Art. 570** — the lessee **may not** obstruct the lessor's **urgent** repairs needed to preserve the property; but if they wholly or partly impair enjoyment he may seek rescission or a rent reduction. **Art. 570(2): if he nevertheless stays in the property until the repairs are finished, his right to rescind falls away.**
- **Art. 571 (⭐ the "don't wreck it" warranty):**
  - **(1)** the lessor must refrain from any act of his that would prevent the lessee's enjoyment, and **may not make any change to the thing or its appurtenances that impairs it**.
  - **(2)** the lessor's warranty extends **not only to his own acts and those of his employees but to any harm or damage founded on a legal cause arising from any other lessee or any third party**.
- **Art. 572 (third-party claims):** if a stranger asserts a right conflicting with the lessee's rights, the lessee must inform the lessor and may **withdraw from the suit** — the proceedings then run only against the lessor. If the assertion in fact deprives the lessee of his enjoyment, he may seek **rescission or rent reduction, plus compensation**.
- **Art. 574 (⭐ government act):** if a **governmental act within the law** substantially reduces the enjoyment, the lessee may seek **rescission or rent reduction**, and may claim compensation from the lessor **if the government act was issued for a reason for which the lessor is responsible**, unless otherwise agreed.
- **Art. 575** — the lessor does **not** warrant against mere **physical interference** by a stranger who claims no right; but the lessee may sue in his own name for damages and for possession. **Art. 575(2):** where the physical interference is not the lessee's fault **and is so grave as to deprive him of the enjoyment**, he may seek rescission or rent reduction.
- **Art. 576 — latent defects:** the lessor warrants all defects in the leased thing that prevent or substantially reduce enjoyment, **save defects custom has been tolerated**; and is responsible for qualities he expressly promised or that enjoyment requires. **Art. 576(2): no warranty for a defect the lessee was told of, or knew of, at the time of contracting.**
- **Art. 577** — where there is a warranted defect the lessee may, as circumstances warrant, seek **rescission or a rent reduction**, ask the lessor to **repair it**, or **repair it himself at the lessor's expense** if the repair would not be onerous on the lessor. **Art. 577(2): the lessor owes damages for harm caused by the defect unless he proves he was unaware of it.**
- **Art. 578 (⭐):** **any clause excluding or limiting the warranty against interference or defect is VOID where the lessor fraudulently concealed the cause of that warranty.**

### 1.3 The lessee's obligations

- **Art. 579** — use the thing as agreed; absent agreement, use it according to the purpose for which it was prepared.
- **Art. 580** — no alterations without the lessor's consent **unless the change causes the lessor no harm**; if he exceeds that, he may be compelled to restore the property and pay compensation.
- **Art. 581** — the lessee **may** install water/electricity/gas/telephone/radio apparatus in the customary way, unless the lessor proves it endangers the property; where the lessor's intervention is necessary to complete the works the lessee may demand it, **the lessor bearing the cost**.
- **Art. 582** — the lessee must carry out the **"التأجيرية" repairs that custom imposes**.
- **Art. 583** — the lessee must apply the care of an ordinary person (**عناية الرجل العادي**) in using and preserving the property, and is liable for damage or destruction during his enjoyment **not arising from lawful use**.
- **Art. 584 ⭐ (fire):** **the lessee is liable for a fire in the leased property unless he proves the fire arose from a cause beyond his control.** Where there are several lessees of one property, **each is liable in the proportion of the part he occupies**, and this includes a lessor who resides there — **unless the fire started in the part occupied by one lessee, who alone is liable.**
  *This is a real, and very commonly mis-allocated, liability. An agent advising a multi-unit residential building should raise it explicitly.*
- **Art. 585** — the lessee must **promptly notify** the lessor of anything requiring his intervention: a need for urgent repairs, a discovered defect, **squatter occupation (اغتصاب)**, or a stranger's interference or damage.
- **Art. 586** — pay the rent on the agreed dates; absent agreement, on the dates fixed by the local custom. **Art. 586(2): payment at the lessee's domicile** unless otherwise agreed or custom provides otherwise.
- **Art. 587 — ⭐ the presumption that settles most arrears disputes:**
  > «**الوفاء بسقط من الأجرة قرينة على الوفاء بالأقساط السابقة على هذا القسط حتى يقوم الدليل على عكس ذلك.**»

  **Payment of one rent instalment is, by legal presumption, proof that all preceding instalments were paid**, until contrary evidence is adduced. *This is the lessee's trump card. It is rebuttal evidence, not conclusive; but a landlord who sues for 24 months' arrears without producing receipts or bank records will lose.*

### 1.4 Subletting, assignment and the lessor's lien

- **Art. 588 — the furniture/statutory security (read carefully, it is old-fashioned):** a lessee of a **house, warehouse, shop or similar place, or agricultural land**, must place in the leased property **furniture, goods, crops, livestock or tools sufficient in value to secure the rent for two years** (or for the whole term if shorter than two years), unless the rent was paid in advance, and is relieved of this duty if the parties so agreed or if he gives other security.
  ⚠️ **This is almost never performed in modern Egyptian residential practice.** 🟡 Do not tell a client it is "enforceable in practice" — mark it as a statutory duty whose practical enforcement in modern residential leases is 🟡 UNVERIFIED.
- **Art. 589 — the lessor's lien (امتياز):**
  - **(1)** as security for every right under the lease the lessor has a **statutory privilege (امتياز)** on all **attachable movables** in the leased property **while they bear that privilege, even if they are not owned by the lessee**; he may object to their removal and, if removed anyway, may **recover them from the possessor even in good faith**, without prejudice to the possessor's own rights.
  - **(2)** he may **not** use the lien or the recovery right where the removal was **required by the lessee's trade or by ordinary life**, or where the movables left in / recovered to the property fully secure the rent.
- **Art. 593** — the lessee may **assign the lease (التنازل) or sublet (الإيجار من الباطن)**, in whole or in part, **unless otherwise agreed**.
- **Art. 594** — a prohibition on assignment implies a prohibition on subletting and vice versa. **Art. 594(2):** for a lease of a property erected to house a **factory or shop**, if the lessee must sell the factory/shop, the **court may order the lease to continue notwithstanding the prohibiting clause**, if the buyer gives sufficient security and the lessor suffers no proven harm.
- **Art. 595** — on assignment the assignor remains **surety for the assignee's performance**.
- **Art. 596** — the sublessee must pay the lessor directly what is due from the head lessee **at the time the lessor notifies him**. **Art. 596(2): the sublessee cannot set up against the lessor prepayments made to the head lessee**, unless made before the notice in accordance with custom or with a fixed agreement made at the time of the sublease.
- **Art. 597** — the head lessee is **discharged toward the lessor** (both as surety on assignment and under the head lease on subletting): **(أولاً)** on the lessor's **express written acceptance** of the assignment/sublet; **(ثانياً)** where the lessor **receives the rent directly** from the assignee or sublessee **without any reservation as to his rights against the head lessee**.

### 1.5 Return of the property, loss of use, and improvements

- **Art. 590 ⭐ (loss of use):**
  > «يجب على المستأجر أن يرد العين المؤجرة عند انتهاء الإيجار. **فإذا أبقاها تحت يده دون حق كان ملزما أن يدفع للمؤجر تعويضا يراعي في تقديره القيمة الإيجارية للعين وما أصاب المؤجر من ضرر.**»

  If the lessee keeps the property after the lease has ended without right, he owes **compensation assessed with regard to the rental value of the property and the loss the lessor has suffered** — i.e. occupation value, not (only) the contract rent. **This is the correct answer to "what can a landlord claim from a tenant who overstays" under the new regime.**
- **Art. 591(1)** — return the property in the condition it was received in, except for destruction or damage not caused by him. **Art. 591(2): ⭐ where possession was delivered without a minutes report (محضر) or a description, it is presumed — until contrary proof — that he received it in good condition.**
  ⚠️ **Practical trap: the absence of a handover report shifts the burden of proving dilapidation onto the landlord.** Advise clients to insist on a signed محضر استلام with photographs at the start of every tenancy.
- **Art. 592 — improvements (التحسينات):**
  - **(1)** if the lessee erected a building, planted or made improvements **increasing the value of the property**, the lessor must pay him what he spent or the increase in value at the end of the lease, unless otherwise agreed.
  - **(2)** if the improvements were made **without the lessor's knowledge or against his will**, he may demand their **removal**, and additionally damages for any harm the removal causes the property.
  - **(3)** if the lessor elects to **keep the improvements in return for paying one of the two figures above**, the court may **apportion the payment (تنازله إلى أحل الوفاء بها)**.
- **Art. 607** — if the parties agreed the lessor may terminate if he has a **personal need** for the property, he must exercise it by giving notice to vacate in the Art. 563 periods, unless otherwise agreed.

### 1.6 Expiry, renewal, and the lessee's right to stay after expiry (⭐)

- **Art. 598 — ⭐ the headline rule of the new regime:**
  > «**ينتهي الإيجار بانتهاء المدة المعينة فى العقد دون حاجة إلى تنبيه بالإخلاء.**»

  **The lease ends automatically on expiry of the agreed term — NO eviction notice is required.** *An agent who tells a residential landlord they must serve an إنذار before recovering a flat at the end of a fixed Civil Code term is adding a step that does not exist. The contract term is the whole game.*
- **Art. 599(1) — tacit renewal (⚠️ the biggest trap in the new regime):** if the lease ends and **the lessee remains in enjoyment with the lessor's knowledge and without his objection**, the lease is **deemed renewed on its original terms but for an indefinite period (لمدة غير معينة)**, and **Art. 563 then applies** — i.e. it becomes a periodic lease terminable on **one month's notice** for a monthly residential rent (Art. 563(ج)).
  *This is the single most expensive trap in Egyptian residential tenancy: a landlord who lets a fixed term run out and "just waits" for the tenant to leave, and then does nothing for a few weeks, has converted a fixed lease that ends automatically into an indefinite periodic lease that needs a notice and, if the tenant raises it, a court order to end.*
- **Art. 599(2) ⭐:** the tacit renewal is deemed a **NEW lease (إيجار جديد), not merely a continuation**. In re-characterising it: **in rem securities (التأمينات العينية)** given by the lessee in the old lease **pass to the new lease, subject to the rules of the الشهر العقاري**; **but a personal or in rem surety (كفالة) does NOT pass unless the surety consents.**
- **Art. 600 — ⭐ the antidote to Art. 599:** where one party **has given notice to vacate** and the lessee nevertheless continues to enjoy the property after expiry, **renewal is NOT presumed — the burden of proof is on the party asserting renewal.**
  *Always serve a written إنذار/notice to vacate when recovering a fixed-term residential unit. Doing so converts Art. 600 from a trap into a shield.*

### 1.7 Death of the lessee and insolvency

- **Art. 601(1) — ⭐:**
  > «**لا ينتهي الإيجار بموت المؤجر ولا بموت المستأجر.**»

  **The lease does NOT end on the death of either the lessor or the lessee.** This is the opposite of the old regime's starting point and is frequently stated backwards.
- **Art. 601(2)** — **but** on the lessee's death **his heirs may apply to end the contract** if they prove that because of the death **the burdens of the contract became heavier than their resources can bear**, or the lease has **become larger than their needs**. In that case the Art. 563 notice periods must be observed and **the application must be made within six months from the lessee's death.**
- **Art. 602** — if the lease was contracted **only for the lessee's trade (حرفة) or on grounds personal to him** and he then dies, **either his heirs or the lessor may apply to end the contract.**
- **Art. 603(1)** — **the lessee's insolvency does not accelerate** rent already accrued but not yet due. **Art. 603(2) — but** the lessor may seek rescission if the lessee does not provide, in a reasonable time, **securities covering the unpaid rent**; **and** the lessee may seek rescission if he has not been permitted to assign or sublet, **on paying fair compensation**.

### 1.8 Sale of the property, and the successor in title (⭐ the الشهر العقاري gap)

- **Art. 604(1) ⭐:**
  > «إذا انتقلت ملكية العين المؤجرة **اختيارا أو جبراً** إلى شخص آخر، فلا يكون الإيجار **نافذاً فى حق هذا الشخص إذا لم يكن له تاريخ ثابت سابق على التصرف الذى نقل الملكية**.»

  On a transfer of ownership, voluntary or compulsory, the lease is **not opposable (غير نافذ) to the new owner unless the lease has a FIXED DATE earlier than the disposition that transferred ownership.**
- **Art. 604(2)** — **but** the new owner may elect to hold himself bound by the lease even if it is not opposable to him.
- **Art. 605(1)** — a new owner against whom the lease is not opposable **may not compel the lessee to vacate except after giving notice to vacate in the Art. 563 periods.**
  **Art. 605(2) ⭐ — the "key money" protection:** if the lessor gives notice to vacate **before** the lease has expired, the lessor must pay the lessee compensation unless otherwise agreed, and **the lessee may not be compelled to vacate until he has actually received that compensation from the lessor or the transferee, or has obtained adequate security for it.**
  *Read with Art. 148(2) and Art. 147(1), this is the strongest protection a tenant has against a sale. An agent who omits Art. 605(2) gives the client bad advice on repossession timelines.*
- **Art. 606** — the lessee **cannot** rely against the new owner on rent he prepaid, unless he proves that **when he paid he knew of the transfer, or that he must necessarily have known of it**; and if the new owner fails to prove that, he has no recourse against the lessor.
  *This is why "pay the rent in advance and lose it on sale" is a genuine risk, and why the tenant should pay month to month with a receipt.*
- **Art. 608(1) ⭐ — the hardship exit for a fixed-term lease:**
  > «إذا كان الإيجار معين المدة، جاز لكل من المتعاقدين أن يطلب إنهاء قبل انقضاء مدته **إذا جدت ظروف خطيرة غير متوقعة من شأنها أن تجعل تنفيذ الإيجار من مبدأ الأمر أو فى أثناء سريانه مرهقا**، على أن يراعى من يطلب إنهاء العقد مواعيد التنبيه بالإخلاء المبينة بالمادة 563، **وعلى أن يعوض الطرف الآخر تعويضا عادلا**.»

  Either party may seek early termination where **serious, unforeseeable circumstances** make performance onerous, subject to notice and **fair compensation**. **Art. 608(2): where it is the LESSOR who asks, he may not force the lessee to return the property until he has paid the compensation or given adequate security.**
  *This is a court-based, case-by-case power. It is not a unilateral right, and there is no automatic indexation.*
- **Art. 609 — special rule for employees:** a **servant or employee whose work requires him to change his place of residence may apply to end the lease of his dwelling**, if the lease has a fixed term, on observing the Art. 563 notice periods; **any agreement to the contrary is void.**
  ⚠️ The article carries a footnote marker "2" in the official text (an implied amendment note) — 🟡 check the consolidated text for any amendment to this article.
- **Art. 598–609 as a whole** is the termination chapter: there is **no general "termination for breach" article** in the Civil Code's lease chapter or, so far as I can see, in its general contract part. The routes to termination under the Civil Code are: (i) expiry of the agreed term (Art. 598); (ii) a **resolutive condition (الشرط الفاسخ) in the contract — Art. 269, which is the general contractual hook**; (iii) the specific lease articles (Arts. 565, 569, 570, 572, 574, 575, 577, 603, 607, 608); (iv) Arts. 147(1) + 148 (contract is the law of the parties, implied terms). ❌ **There is no article that says "either party may terminate for breach"** — do not cite one.

### 1.9 Rent and its increase under the Civil Code

- ✅ **Arts. 558, 561, 562** (what rent is / may be / if unagreed).
- ✅ **Art. 147(1)** (file 01): **العقد شريعة المتعاقدين** — the contract may be revoked or amended only by agreement or for the reasons the law allows.
- ✅ **Art. 148(2)** (file 01): the contract embraces its requisites according to law, custom, equity and the nature of the obligation — which is where a *customary* annual indexation can be implied.
- ❌ **No indexation, no cap, no rent-control formula, no review date exists anywhere in Arts. 558–634.** Rent moves only if the parties agreed a mechanism (annual increase, CPI linkage, periodic review). **Verify: I read every article 558–634; the only occurrences of زيادة are in Arts. 582–584, about repairs and diligence, not rent.**
- ⚠️ What *can* limit a negotiated increase: **Art. 147(2)** (نظرية الظروف الطارئة — the judge may reduce an obligation that has become excessively onerous because of exceptional unforeseen events; any clause to the contrary is void), **Art. 149** (in an adhesion contract with oppressive terms the court may amend or delete them), and **Art. 129–130** (extreme inequity — but only where exploitation plus **clear inexperience or reckless passion** is proved). None of these is a rent-control mechanism. **In practice they are almost never used on residential rents.**

### 1.10 Types of lease (so the agent does not confuse the general regime)

- **Agricultural land — Arts. 610–618.** Lessor need not deliver the landlord's livestock/implements unless the lease includes them (610); the lessee must tend and maintain them per local custom (611); *"a lease for a year"* means **one annual cropping cycle** (612); the lessee must keep the land fit for production and may make no material change without consent (613); the lessee does ordinary irrigation/channel/road repairs, the lessor does major buildings, wells, tanks (614(1)–(2)); **force majeure in preparation/sowing → rent is wholly or partly released (615)**; crop destroyed before harvest → the lessee may seek abolition of the rent (616(1)); partial crop loss → rent reduction (616(2)); **no abatement if the loss was compensated by profits, insurance or otherwise (616(3))**; the lessee may stay until the crop ripens, paying due rent (617); on vacating he must leave the land as he found it (618).
- **Sharecropping (المزارعة) — Arts. 619–627.** The lease rules apply subject to modifications (620); if no term, one cropping cycle (621); the lessor's implements and livestock existing at contracting are included (622); **care of the ordinary person** (623(1)); liable for damage unless he proved ordinary care (623(2)); **not liable for the loss of livestock or implements absent his fault** (623(3)); crop shared as agreed, else **as custom, else half each (624(1))**; **force majeure loss of the crop is borne jointly, with no recourse (624(2))**; no assignment or subletting without the lessor's consent (625); **the sharecropping does NOT end on the lessor's death but DOES end on the lessee's (626)**; on early termination the lessee or his heirs get his expenses on the unharvested crop plus fair compensation for work, and on the lessee's death his heirs may **stay in his place until the crop ripens** (627).
- **Waqf lease (إجارة الوقف) — Arts. 628–634.** The **nawthir (ناظر)** has power to lease; the **mutawalli** or a person authorised by the nawthir or a judge may (628); rent is collected by the nawthir, not the beneficiary, unless the nawthir permits (629); **the nawthir may not lease the waqf even at أجر المثل, but may lease it to himself and his descendants at أجر المثل (630)**; leasing at **غبن فاحش (gross undervalue) is void** unless the lessor is the sole beneficiary entitled to dispose — and then only in his own favour (631); **for the purposes of أجر المثل the relevant date is when the lease was concluded; later changes are disregarded (632(1))**; if the nawthir leased at gross undervalue the lessee must top up to أجر المثل or the contract is rescinded (632(2)); **no lease beyond three years without the judge's permission, even by successive leases (633)**; otherwise Art. 634 sends us back to the general lease rules.

# 2. ⭐ إيجار قديم vs إيجار جديد — the most important section of this file

## 2.1 The dividing line

**Citation:** Law No. 4 of 1996 بشأن سريان أحكام القانون المدنى على الأماكن التى لم يسبق تأجيرها والأماكن التى انتهت أو تنتهى عقود إيجارها دون أن يكون لأحد حق البقاء فيها.
**Issued:** 30 January 1996 (10 Ramadan 1416 H). **Published:** Official Gazette No. 4 bis (A), 30/01/1996. **In force:** **31 January 1996** (Art. 4: works from the day after publication).
**Status:** in force, amended twice (Law 137/2006 and Law 165/2025).
**Sources:**
- ✅ Full text with the parliamentary committee report — Ministry of Knowledge and Digital Development (an official Egyptian body): https://mksegypt.org/public/ar/laws/23915
- ✅ Gazette metadata + effective date: https://www.eastlaws.com/legislation-full-text/ar/egypt/law/30-01-1996/no-4?id=5056&type=1 and https://manshurat.org/node/596
- ✅ Consolidated text with amendment footnotes: https://alhasswalawfirm.com/uploads/files/41996-1788831623138-410983763.pdf
**As of:** 2026-10-12.

**Art. 1 (verbatim):**
> «لا تسرى أحكام القانونين رقمى **49 لسنة 1977** … و**136 لسنة 1981** … والقوانين الخاصة بإيجار الأماكن الصادرة قبلهما، على **الأماكن التى لم يسبق تأجيرها**، ولا على **الأماكن التى انتهت عقود إيجارها قبل العمل بهذا القانون أو تنتهى بعده** لأى سبب من الأسباب **دون أن يكون لأحد حق البقاء فيها** طبقا للقانون.»

**Art. 2 (verbatim):**
> «تطبق أحكام القانون المدنى فى شأن تأجير الأماكن المنصوص عليها فى المادة الأولى من هذا القانون **خالية أو مفروشة**، أو فى شأن استغلالها أو التصرف فيها.
> ويكون للمحررات المثبتة لهذه العلاقة الإيجارية وشروطها وانتهائها **قوة السند التنفيذي بعد توثيقها بحضور أطرافها**.» *(second paragraph added by Law 137/2006)*

**Art. 3** repeals any conflicting text in any other law. **Art. 4** — published in the Official Gazette; in force from the day after publication.

### 2.2 How a court decides which regime applies — the operative test

I put this to the Court of Cassation and got the rule in its own words.

✅ **Cassation Appeal (طعن) No. 16705 of year 4, judgment of 15/2/2015, Official Gazette 66/29 p. 237** (the Official Gazette is where Cassation decisions are published) — reasoning, quoted:

> «لازم ذلك أن أحكام القانونين رقمي 49 لسنة 1977، 136 لسنة 1981 **سارية بجميع نصوصهما المدنية والجنائية على العلاقات الإيجارية القائمة والتي أبرمت في ظل هذين القانونين قبل العمل بأحكام القانون رقم 4 لسنة 1996**. لما كان ذلك، وكان الثابت من مدونات الحكم المطعون فيه أن تاريخ عقد الإيجار الثاني هو 1/7/1998 **أي بعد العمل بالقانون رقم 4 لسنة 1996** فإنه تنطبق عليه أحكام القانون رقم 4 لسنة 1996، ولا ينطبق عليه أحكام القانونين رقمى 49 لسنة 1977، 136 لسنة 1981…»

**Source (Cassation reasoning reproduced verbatim):** https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2021/03/16705-4-15-2-2015-66-29-237.html — Ahmed Azim El-Gamal's *رواق الجمل* is a long-established compilation of Egyptian Cassation doctrine quoting official Gazette citations. ⚠️ **Single-source**; the reasoning is quoted, but re-check against the official Official Gazette (journal of decisions) before quoting in advice.

**The three-part test an agent should apply:**

1. **Was the property already let before 31 January 1996?**
   - **No** → **إيجار جديد**, full Civil Code. *This covers every property built or first let after 31 Jan 1996, and every property that had become vacant by then.* The committee report puts it bluntly: for premises never let, "ليس لأحد أية حق فيها، يستوى في ذلك الأماكن القائمة أو التي تنشأ بعد ذلك".
   - **Yes** → go to step 2.
2. **Was anyone holding a statutory right to remain (حق البقاء) in it at any moment after 31 January 1996?** If the lease terminated **at any time** — before the law or after it — **and nobody had a حق البقاء under Law 49/1977 Art. 29**, then the property re-entered the Civil Code regime permanently. The committee report identifies three categories that leave the old regime: (1) never-let premises; (2) premises whose lease had already ended without a right to remain; (3) currently-let premises **whose lease ends for any reason without a right to remain**. Once out, it never comes back.
   *This is the "one generation" rule: **the old regime survives as long as a chain of qualifying occupiers continues. It does not restart if the lease lapses and the same landlord relets — the reletting is Civil Code.***
3. **Is the specific lease one the old regime covers?** Law 164/2025 Art. 1 (see §2.4) defines it as leases under **Law 49/1977 and Law 136/1981**, for **residential purposes** and for **non-residential purposes where the lessee is a natural person**.

⚠️ **Three carving-outs an agent must check before defaulting to "old":**
- **Law 10/2022** (see §2.5) took **non-residential leases of legal persons** out of the old regime on its own terms.
- **Law 49/1977 Arts. 2 and 8** exclude: (a) dwellings annexed to facilities occupied **by reason of the job**; (b) dwellings occupied under **temporary occupancy permits** for emergencies; (c) Art. 8 — a person may not occupy **more than one dwelling in the same town** without cause.
- **Art. 48** — the **special chapters** apply only to **Egyptian** owners and lessees.

⚠️ **"Fifty-nine-year leases" (عقود الـ59 سنة).** Post-1996 long leases are **not** old-system: they are ordinary Civil Code leases and are governed by whatever the parties wrote (a nominal rent plus a large upfront payment is lawful *in the new regime*; it would not be under Law 49/1977 Art. 26 — see §3.2). **Do not confuse a long duration with the old regime.** *(Reported statements to this effect exist in press commentary; 🟡 I did not verify them against a judgment or a statute.)*

| | **إيجار جديد** (Civil Code / Law 4/1996) | **إيجار قديم** (Laws 49/1977 + 136/1981) |
|---|---|---|
| Core text | Civil Code Arts. 558–634 | Law 49/1977 + Law 136/1981 |
| Rent | **Whatever the parties agree.** No cap, no index, no committee. | **الأجرة القانونية** fixed by a **rent-fixing committee** on a statutory formula; increases only via the committee mechanism (pre-2025), and via **Law 164/2025 Arts. 4–6** now |
| Rent increase | Contractual only | **Statutory, mandatory, non-negotiable** — 20× / 10× / 5× the legal rent, then **+15% every year**, plus interim **EGP 250/month** while the inventory committees work |
| Term | **As agreed.** Ends automatically at expiry — **no notice needed** (Art. 598) | **No agreed term.** Statutory extension to family / qualifying relatives; the new law now imposes a hard sunset |
| Renewal | **Tacit renewal** into an indefinite periodic lease if the lessee stays with the lessor's knowledge and without objection (Art. 599(1)) — **unless notice to vacate was served** (Art. 600) | Statutory continuation on death / abandonment (Law 49/1977 Art. 29) |
| Expiry / recovery of the unit | Automatic at term end; then the landlord must issue an إنذار and, if the tenant stays, sue (or go to the judge of urgent matters under Law 165/2025) | **Only on the closed statutory grounds**, and **only by court order** — subject now to the Law 164/2025 sunset |
| Death of the lessee | **Lease survives** (Art. 601(1)); heirs may apply within **6 months** to end it on hardship grounds (Art. 601(2)) | **Never ends** on death if wife / children / parents remain; other relatives up to the 3rd degree if resident ≥ 1 year (Law 49/1977 Art. 29) |
| Sale of the property | Art. 604: lease not opposable to the new owner **unless the lease has a fixed date earlier than the transfer**; Art. 605(2): the new owner must **pay compensation** and cannot compel vacation until it is paid or secured | **Law 49/1977 Art. 30 ⭐: old-system leases bind the new owner EVEN IF the lease instrument has no fixed date before the transfer — a flat, express exception to Civil Code Art. 604** |
| Deposit | **No statutory cap.** Party autonomy. | **Cap of two months' rent** (Law 49/1977 Art. 25), and the lessee may recover the excess **by direct set-off against rent** without going to court |
| Key money / advance | Lawful (Art. 147(1)) — and ⚠️ **not a criminal offence**, because **Law 4/1996 abolished the خلو الرجل offence** for the premises within its Art. 1 scope (**SCC Case 85/Y17, 22/3/1997**, §4.3) | ❌ **Law 49/1977 Art. 26: a landlord (owner, head lessee or agent) may not demand any consideration, fee or extra payment outside the deposit and the rent.** Art. 77 punishes breach of Art. 26 with **imprisonment of not less than 3 months AND a fine equal to double the amount taken** (both, not either), with an amnesty for the lessee/agent who reports it, and **mandatory restitution**. **Law 136/1981 Art. 25** adds **absolute nullity**, restitution, and **double the amount to the economic-housing fund**. ⚠️ Art. 77's custodial limb survives only because **Law 136/1981 Art. 24 abolished custodial penalties in the tenancy laws *except* خلو الرجل** (§4.1). |

### Key provisions of Law 164/2025 (as read)

- **Art. 1 — scope (verbatim from the gazette):** «تسرى أحكام هذا القانون على الأماكن المؤجرة لغرض السكنى والأماكن المؤجرة للأشخاص الطبيعية لغير غرض السكنى وفقًا لأحكام القانونين رقمي ٤٩ لسنة ١٩٧٧ … و١٣٦ لسنة ١٩٨١ …» — **residential** lets, and **non-residential** lets in favour of **natural persons**, under Law 49/1977 and Law 136/1981.
- **Art. 2 — ⭐ the sunset (verbatim):** «تنتهي عقود إيجار الأماكن الخاضعة لأحكام هذا القانون لغرض السكني **بانتهاء مدة سبع سنوات من تاريخ العمل به**، وتنتهي عقود إيجار الأماكن للأشخاص الطبيعية لغير غرض السكني **بانتهاء مدة خمس سنوات من تاريخ العمل به**، وذلك كله ما لم يتم التراضي على الإنهاء قبل ذلك.»
  → On a **5 August 2025** commencement the seven-year period runs out at the end of **5 August 2032** (**residential**), and the five-year period at the end of **5 August 2030** (**non-residential, natural persons**). *(Computed from the in-force date; the statute prints no calendar date. The same anniversary reading is the one the press and practitioners use — "أغسطس ٢٠٣٢".)*
  ⚠️ **Art. 2 and Art. 9 are worded differently and may not fall on the same day — see Art. 9 below.**
- **Art. 3 — inventory committees.** By decision of the competent **governor**, **inventory committees (لجان حصر)** in each governorate **classify areas into متميزة / متوسطة / اقتصادية** (distinguished / medium / economic), using: geographic location and street; building standard and materials and average unit sizes; connected utilities (water, electricity, gas, telephone); road network and services; and the **annual rental value of the built property under Law 196/2008 (real-estate tax)**. A PM decision governs the committees' operation; they finish within **3 months** (extendable once); the governor's decision is published in the Egyptian Gazette and announced by the local directorates.
- **Art. 4 — ⭐ residential rent.** From the first rent instalment falling due after the law took effect, the legal rent becomes:
  - **distinguished areas: 20× the existing legal rent, minimum EGP 1,000/month**;
  - **medium and economic areas: 10× the existing legal rent, with a minimum of EGP 400/month (medium) and EGP 250/month (economic)**.
  - **Interim obligation:** until the Art. 3 committees finish, the lessee (or the person to whom the lease extended) must pay **EGP 250 per month**; from the day after publication of the governor's decision, any differences due are paid **in monthly instalments over a period equal to the period for which they accrued**.
- **Art. 5** — non-residential lets to **natural persons**: legal rent becomes **5×** the existing legal rent.
- **Art. 6 — ⭐ the new indexation (verbatim):** «تزداد القيمة الإيجارية المحددة وفقاً للمادتين (٤ ، ٥) من هذا القانون سنويًا بصفة دورية بنسبة (١٥٪).» *This is Egypt's first mandatory, legislated rent index for these leases. It is a fixed percentage, not a CPI link, and it is not subject to contract.*
  ✅ **The first 15% increase fell due with the September 2026 instalment.** No separate implementing instrument exists or is needed: Art. 6 is self-executing, and the trigger is the anniversary of the Art. 4/5 starting point, which is «موعد استحقاق الأجرة الشهرية التالية لتاريخ العمل بهذا القانون» — the first monthly instalment due after **5 August 2025**, i.e. **1 September 2026** for a rent payable at the start of the month.
  ⚠️ **The increase applies to the NEW legal rent, not to the pre-reform rent** — so a unit taken from EGP 250 to EGP 250 goes to **EGP 287.50**, a unit at EGP 400 goes to **EGP 460**, a unit at EGP 1,000 goes to **EGP 1,150**. Base the calculation on the Art. 4/5 figure, never on the old rent. *(Worked examples as published by Al-Masry Al-Youm, 2 September 2026.)*
  **Sources:** https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/1/.../7532193 (1 Sept 2026, quoting MP محمد عطية الفيوفي) · https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4350705 (2 Sept 2026 — states the increase runs on the new legal rent and publishes the worked examples) · https://www.soutalomma.com/Article/1248201
- **Art. 7 — ⭐ two new eviction grounds.** Without prejudice to the grounds in **Law 136/1981 Art. 18**, the lessee (or person to whom the lease extended) **must vacate and return the property** (a) at the end of the Art. 2 period; (b) if it is proved that the lessee has **left the property shut up for more than one year without justification**; (c) if it is proved that the lessee **owns a residential or non-residential unit usable for the same purpose** as the leased one.
  **On refusal, the owner or lessor may apply to the قاضي الأمور الوقتية at the court within whose district the property lies for an order ejecting the person refusing to vacate**, without prejudice to the right to compensation where justified.
  **⭐ And: the lessee may raise a substantive action (دعوى موضوعية) in the competent court in the ordinary way, but that does NOT suspend the judge's urgent order.**
  *Note the third ground — "owns another usable unit" — is a new and quite aggressive eviction trigger that did not exist before 2025.*
- **Art. 8 — alternative units from the State (verbatim, opening):** «مع عدم الإخلال بحكم المادتين (٢ ، ٧) من هذا القانون، يكون لكل مستأجر أو من امتد إليه عقد الإيجار وفقاً لأحكام القانونين رقمي ٤٩ لسنة ١٩٧٧، ١٣٦ لسنة ١٩٨١ المشار إليهما، وقبل انقضاء المدة المحددة لانتهاء العقود في المادة (٢) من هذا القانون، أحقية في تخصيص وحدة سكنية أو غير سكنية، إيجارًا أو تمليكًا، من الوحدات المتاحة لدى الدولة، وذلك بطلب يقدمه المستأجر أو من امتد إليه عقد الإيجار مرفقًا به إقرار بإخلاء وتسليم العين المستأجرة فور صدور قرار التخصيص واستلام الوحدة.»
  A PM decision within one month of the law's entry into force was to set the rules; **the original lessee of a residential unit and the spouse to whom the contract extended before the law took effect must be prioritised**, within a maximum of one year after the Art. 2 period ends.

# 3. Rent control under إيجار قديم — and what replaced it

## 3.1 The pre-2025 mechanism: rent-fixing committees, not a percentage

✅ Read from the **Ministry of Justice's own legislation encyclopedia (الموسوعة القانونية لوزارة العدل / الموجهة العملية لأعمال القضاء والنيابة العامة)** — https://serviceapi.egyptianlaws.com/ExpanSingleTash/ViewListMowad?path=1977-9-8-1977-49.pdf. This is an official Egyptian government publication and the version I quote carries the **Supreme Constitutional Court annotations inline**.

- **Law 49/1977 Art. 9** — the rent-fixing provisions of **Law 121/1947**, **Law 169/1961**, **Law 46/1962**, **Law 7/1965** and **Law 52/1969**, and their amendments, **continue in force within their own scope of application**.
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 11** — the planning authority's approval of a building must **estimate the building's total rent and distribute it across the units**; the building permit states the estimate and the distribution; contracting proceeds on that basis **until the rent is fixed under this Part**.
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 12** — a **rent-fixing committee** is formed in the district of the building.
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 13** — the owner must notify the committee **within 30 days** of the first lease or first occupation; **the tenant may also notify it by registered post with acknowledgement of receipt**. The committee may value on its own initiative or on notification from the competent authority. **The owner must deliver the property fit for use within the agreed periods, failing which the tenant may — after notice to the owner and on a licence from the judge of urgent matters — complete the outstanding works and set the cost off against the rent.**
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 14 — ⭐ the formula (this IS the "rent ceiling"):**
  > «تقدر أجرة المبنى المرخص في اقامته من تاريخ العمل بهذا القانون على الأسس التالية:
  > **(أ) صافي عائد استثمار العقار بواقع 7% (سبعة في المائة) من قيمة الأرض والمباني.**
  > **(ب) مقابل استهلاك رأس المال ومصروفات الاصلاحات والصيانة والادارة بواقع 3% (ثلاثة في المائة) من قيمة المباني.**
  > ومع مراعاة الإعفاءات المقررة في شأن الضريبة على العقارات المبنية، يضاف إلى الأجرة المحددة … ما يخصهما من الضرائب العقارية الأصلية والإضافية…
  > **ويلتزم المستأجر بأداء هذه الضرائب والرسوم إلى المؤجر مع الأجرة الشهرية، ويترتب على عدم الوفاء بها نفس النتائج المترتبة على عدم سداد الأجرة.**»
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 15 — the valuation basis (why old rents are absurdly low):** land valued at the **1974 market rate plus 7% a year until construction**; buildings valued at **actual construction cost at the time of building**.
  ⚠️ **This is the mechanical reason an old Egyptian rent is tiny: the land component is frozen at 1974 prices.** The 7% is the investment yield; the 3% is capital consumption plus repairs and administration.
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 17** — committee decisions are **effective unless appealed**, and become **final if not appealed within the period**.
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 18** — appeal within **30 days** of notification, to the **Court of First Instance (المحكمة الابتدائية)** within whose district the property lies, with a court expert **architect or civil engineer** of at least second grade chosen by the governor for two renewable years, who is neither a member of the rent committee nor a party. **The expert takes the oath before an appellate division.**
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 19** — all the remaining tenants in the building must be notified of the appeal by registered post; **if the appeal succeeds the rents of the remaining units covered by the decision are re-examined, and the judgment binds ALL owners and tenants.**
  ⚠️ **Consequential litigation risk: a single tenant's successful challenge re-opens the rents of every unit in the building.**
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 20** — appeal from the Art. 18 judgment lies only for **error in applying the law (خطأ في تطبيق القانون)**, to the **Court of Appeal (محكمة الاستئناف)**, within **30 days**, and **that judgment is final and unappealable on any ground**.
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 22 — ⭐ how an increase is actually paid:**
  > «يلتزم المستأجر في حالة تحديد الأجرة بالزيادة عما هو منصوص عليه في العقد، بأداء **الفرق قسطاً على أقساط شهرية لمدة مساوية للمدة التي استحق عنها** أو بسداده كاملاً إذا أراد إخلاء العين المؤجرة قبل انقضاء المدة mentioned…
  > **وعلى المؤجر أن يرد إلى المستأجر فرق الأجرة عند تحديدها بما يقل عما هو منصوص عليه في العقد** وبالطريقة المنصوص عليها في الفقرة السابقة.»

  So a **tenant who wants to leave must pay the whole arrears of the increase immediately**; and a decrease is refunded to the tenant on the same footing. **This is the old regime's eviction lever for the rent, and it is unilateral.**
- **Law 49/1977 Arts. 34 and 66–67** — a **5% reduction** of the monthly rent, minimum EGP 100, in specified cases, and **tax exemptions** tied to very low per-room rents (historical amounts: EGP 3–5, 5–8, 8–10 per room per month). *Thoroughly obsolete in amount; relevant only as the historic shape.*

## 3.2 What replaced it: **no more committee negotiation for 2025–2032 leases**

⚠️ **Practical consequence to state plainly to clients:** under Law 164/2025, for old-system leases the "legal rent" is no longer a matter for a committee to negotiate over years. From the first instalment due after 5 August 2025 the rent is **20×/10×/5× the existing legal rent (with statutory minimums)**, then **+15% every year by operation of law**, and then the lease **ends** on the sunset date. There is **no longer any mechanism by which a tenant can ask a committee to lower it.**

**But two statutory protections survive until 6 August 2032 and must not be forgotten:**
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 25 — the deposit cap of two months' rent**, with the tenant's **direct set-off right** (below).
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 26 — the absolute ban on key money**, with **criminal** sanctions (below).

# 4. Security deposits

## 4.1 إيجار قديم — there IS a statutory cap

✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 25** (Ministry of Justice text):
> «**لا يجوز أن يزيد مقدار التأمين الذي يدفعه المستأجر على ما يعادل أجرة شهرين**، ويسري هذا الحكم على **عقود الإيجار القائمة وقت العمل بهذا القانون**، **وللمستأجر الحق في استرداد الزيادة في قيمة التأمين بخصمها مباشرة من الأجرة مقسطة على سنة أو حتى نهاية العقد أو عند إخلاء العين المؤجرة أيهما أقرب، وذلك بغير حاجة إلى الالتجاء إلى القضاء.**»

⚠️ **Two limits on this cap that agents get wrong:**
1. **By its own terms it applies to leases " القائمة وقت العمل بهذا القانون"** — i.e. the 1977 law applied only to the law's territorial scope (cities and villages under Law 52/1975, extendable by ministerial decision to villages). 🟡 Its reach outside that scope is a question I did not resolve.
2. ✅ **RESOLVED 19 October 2026 for the case that actually arises: a lease NEWLY signed after August 2025 is not, on that ground alone, subject to Art. 25 or Art. 26.** The reasoning chain, each link verified:
   - **Law 164/2025 Art. 9** repeals Laws 49/1977 and 136/1981 only «اعتبارًا من اليوم التالى لمرور سبعة أعوام من تاريخ العمل بهذا القانون» — so between 2025 and 2032 those laws are **still in force** (they are not repealed retroactively and they are not suspended).
   - **But Art. 25 and Art. 26 only reach leases the OLD REGIME actually governs.** **Law 4/1996 Art. 1** disapplies «أحكام القانونين رقمى ٤٩ لسنة ١٩٧٧ … و١٣٦ لسنة ١٩٨١ … والقوانين الخاصة بإيجار الأماكن الصادرة قبلهما» to premises **never let before 31 January 1996**, and to premises whose lease ended **without anyone holding a حق البقاء**; **Art. 2** then applies the **Civil Code** to them «خالية أو مفروشة».
   - ⭐ **SCC Case No. 85 of year 17 دستورية, judgment of 22 March 1997**, says so in terms: Law 4/1996 «ليعيد العلائق الإيجارية — فى الأماكن التى يشملها — إلى الأصل فيها», and for those premises «**لا يكون تأجيرها إلا وفق الشروط التى تتطابق بشأنها إرادة مؤجريها مع من يتقدمون لطلبها، ولو كان من بينها تقاضى المؤجر لمبالغ خارج نطاق عقد الإيجار، أيا كان وصفها أو مقدارها أو سببها**» — i.e. **for Law 4/1996 premises the landlord may lawfully take amounts outside the lease contract, whatever their description, amount or cause.** The Court applied that holding to **quash the criminal offence itself** (§4.3). **Source (SCC's own judgment text):** https://mksegypt.org/ar/judgments-of-the-supreme-constitutional-court/15419
   - **Conclusion:** a **genuinely new** letting (a unit never let before 31/1/1996, or one whose old lease ended with nobody holding a حق البقاء) is **Civil Code only** — Art. 25's two-month deposit cap and Art. 26's key-money ban **do not apply**, and no crime attaches. ⚠️ **But be careful with the contrary case:** a **continuation** lease — e.g. a fresh contract written to the surviving widow under Law 49/1977 **Art. 29**, or any reletting of a unit where a qualifying occupier never left — **remains an old-regime lease**, and there Arts. 25 and 26 do bite. **Characterise the tenancy first (§2.2), then apply Arts. 25/26.**
   - ⚠️ **Reasoned statutory conclusion from verified texts, not a locating judgment on the post-2025 facts.** I found no decision squarely on a 2026-dated lease. The **safe practice advice is unchanged: take at most two months, and offer the Art. 25 set-off as a contractual term.**
   - Supporting (weaker) commentary reaching the same place: https://shoeiblaw.com («القانون يستهدف العقود القديمة الخاضعة لقوانين ٤٩ لسنة ١٩٧٧ و١٣٦ لسنة ١٩٨١. أما العقود الجديدة …»)

### 4.1.1 The penalties on Arts. 25 and 26 — and the article that quietly removed most of them

✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 76 (verbatim, and note it is a CLOSED list):**
> «يعاقب بالحبس مدة لا تقل عن شهر ولا تزيد على ستة أشهر وبغرامة لا تقل عن مائة جنيه ولا تجاوز خمسمائة جنيه **أو بإحدى هاتين العقوبتين** كل من يخالف أحكام المواد **٧/ ١، ٨، ١٣/ ١، ٢٤، ٢٥** من هذا القانون. ويحكم فضلاً عن ذلك في حالة مخالفة حكم المادة (٨/ ١) بإنهاء عقد المسكن أو المساكن المحتجزة بالمخالفة لحكم القانون.»
⭐ **Art. 28 is NOT in that list.** The article that forbids depriving the tenant of his rights or advantages carries **no penalty** — which is why there is no criminal answer to a landlord cutting the utilities (§5.4).

✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 77 (verbatim) — the key-money offence:**
> «يعاقب كل من يخالف حكم المادة ٢٦ من هذا القانون سواء كان مؤجراً أو مستأجراً أو وسيطاً **بالحبس مدة لا تقل عن ثلاثة أشهر وبغرامة تعادل مثلى المبلغ الذي تقاضاه** بالمخالفة لأحكام هذه المادة، ويعفى من العقوبة كل من المستأجر والوسيط إذا أبلغ أو بادر بالاعتراف بالجريمة.
> **وفي جميع الأحوال يجب الحكم على المخالف بأن يرد إلى صاحب الشأن ما تقاضاه على خلاف أحكام المادة المشار إليها.**»
⚠️ **CORRECTION — the penalty is CUMULATIVE, not alternative.** Art. 77 says «بالحبس … **و**بغرامة» — imprisonment **and** a fine. Both the discovery file and both rental skills previously rendered this as "**or** a fine equal to double". **That is wrong.** The restitution sentence **is** in Art. 77 (third paragraph) — an earlier note in this file said it was not; that was also wrong.

✅ ⭐ **Law 136/1981 Art. 24 (verbatim) — the article that abolished custodial penalties in the tenancy laws:**
> «**فيما عدا العقوبة المقررة لجريمة خلو الرجل تلغى جميع العقوبات المقيدة للحرية المنصوص عليها في القوانين المنظمة لتأجير الأماكن وتنظيم العلاقة بين المؤجر والمستأجر** وذلك دون إخلال بأحكام المادة السابقة.
> ومع ذلك يعفى من جميع العقوبات المقررة لجريمة خلو الرجل كل من بادر إلى رد ما تقاضاه بالمخالفة لأحكام القانون إلى صاحب الشأن، **وأداء مثليه إلى صندوق تمويل الإسكان الاقتصادي بالمحافظة** وذلك قبل أن يصبح الحكم نهائياً في الدعوى.»
**So the practical position on penalties is:**
- **Every custodial penalty in Laws 49/1977 and 136/1981 is abolished**, including Art. 76's imprisonment limb — **except** (a) the **خلو الرجل** offence (Art. 77) and (b) the offences in **Law 136/1981 Art. 23**.
- **Art. 76 therefore survives as a FINE only** (EGP 100–500), for breach of Arts. 7(1), 8, 13(1), 24 and 25, plus termination of the lease for an Art. 8(1) breach.
- **Art. 77 survives intact** — imprisonment ≥ 3 months **plus** double the amount, with restitution, and an amnesty for a lessee/intermediary who reports or confesses.
- **Law 136/1981 Art. 24 second paragraph** gives a further **amnesty for the خلو الرجل offence**: whoever returns what he took **and pays double into the economic-housing fund** before the judgment becomes final escapes all penalty.
- **Law 136/1981 Art. 23** keeps the **fraud (النصب)** penalty of the Penal Code for a landlord who takes more than one مقدم for the same unit, lets it to more than one tenant, or sells it to a non-contracting party (**and the later sale is void, even if registered**), and for a landlord who unjustifiably fails to deliver the unit on time (same penalty **plus double the مقدم** to the other party).
- ✅ **Law 136/1981 Art. 25 (verbatim, third paragraph):** «وفي جميع الأحوال يلزم كل من يحصل على مبالغ بالمخالفة لأحكام تلك القوانين **بأن يردها إلى من أداها، كما يلزم فضلا عن ذلك بدفع مثلي هذه المبالغ لصندوق تمويل الإسكان الاقتصادي بالمحافظة**.» — **restitution to the payer PLUS double to the fund**, and nullity of the offending term or contract.
⭐ **Authority for the whole of the above:** the SCC quoted both Art. 77 and Art. 24 side by side in **Case 85/Y17 (22/3/1997)** and summarised the effect in these words: «هذا القانون، وإن نص على إلغاء العقوبات المقيدة للحرية التي فرضتها قوانين إيجار الأماكن السابقة على العمل بأحكامه … إلا أنه استثنى من ذلك جريمة خلو الرجل، والجرائم المنصوص عليها فى المادة 23 منه، مبقيا بذلك على عقوباتها». **Source:** https://mksegypt.org/ar/judgments-of-the-supreme-constitutional-court/15419
**Text sources:** Law 136/1981 consolidated (arts. 23, 24, 25 verbatim, with amendment footnotes to 27-08-1981): https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2017/10/136-1981.html · https://alberonsy.com/قانون-رقم-136-لسنة-1981-فى-شأن-بعض-ب/ · https://manshurat.org/node/255 · Law 49/1977 arts. 76–77: https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-49-1977
**Doctrine reaching the same result:** https://www.laweg.net («إبقاء القانون ١٣٦ لسنة ١٩٨١ على العقوبة المقيدة للحرية مع الغرامة لجريمة تقاضي خلو الرجل»; and the removal of small advance-rent-taking from the criminal law).

✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 24** — lease contracts must be **in writing** and their **date must be evidenced before the Real Estate Registration notary (مأمورية الشهر العقاري)** for the leased property; the lessor must state in the lease the building-permit number/date and the preliminary rent estimated under Art. 11 for buildings subject to the preliminary-rent system. **The tenant may prove the letting and all its terms by any means of proof.** **The lessor may not conclude more than one lease for one building or unit; a breach makes the first contract, and any later contracts, VOID.**

## 4.2 إيجار جديد — no statutory cap, but two Civil Code anchors

⚠️ **The Civil Code does not require or cap a deposit** (verified across Arts. 558–634). Two provisions do the work:
- **Art. 590** — on expiry the lessee must return the property; if he keeps it without right he owes compensation assessed with regard to the **rental value** and the lessor's loss. **"Compensation" here is not a cap on the deposit.**
- **Art. 591(2)** — in the absence of a handover report or description, **delivery in good condition is presumed**. Combined with Art. 592 (improvements) and Art. 583 (duty of ordinary care), this is what stops a landlord from deducting "general wear and tear".
- **Art. 147(1)** — the deposit terms are whatever the parties wrote; **Art. 149** — in an adhesion contract the court may amend or delete **oppressive (تعسفية) terms**; **Art. 149's "agreements to the contrary are void"** applies. A punitive forfeiture clause is therefore amendable or deletable by the court, and **Art. 224/225** allow reduction of grossly overstated liquidated damages, or of compensation where the debtor proved no loss.
- **Art. 226 / 227** (file 01) — default interest **4% p.a. in civil matters**; an agreed rate above the statutory price is **reduced to 7%** with restitution of the excess. Useful if the landlord "keeps" the deposit as compensation for arrears.
- **Art. 386** (file 01) — prescription leaves a **natural obligation**; it does not discharge the debt from the patrimony. So an old unreclaimed deposit is not a free windfall, but it is unenforceable.

⚠️ **PRACTICAL ANSWER for refund timing:** the Civil Code fixes **no** deadline for returning the deposit. What governs is (i) the contract, (ii) the fact that at the date of return the landlord has no right to retain under Arts. 590–592, and (iii) the general obligation to return an unjust enrichment / the accessory nature of the deposit, so that **interest runs under Art. 226** and the landlord is exposed to a **دعوى استحقاق** (claim for recovery). 🟡 **UNVERIFIED that there is a statutory "X days" rule — there is not.** Say "no statutory deadline; claim it in court", not "you have 30 days".

## 4.3 Whether the deposit can be withheld

✅ **Yes, but only for what is actually due.** Under Art. 590–592 the landlord may retain against (a) unpaid rent; (b) **proven** damage beyond ordinary wear; (c) utilities the contract puts on the tenant. He may **not** retain as a penalty. Where he withholds without right the tenant has a restitutionary claim plus Art. 226 interest. Under **Law 49/1977 Art. 25** the excess over two months is recoverable **by direct set-off, without going to court**. Under **Law 49/1977 Art. 27**, where the landlord refuses to take the rent and give a receipt, the tenant may — after **15 days** from the due date notice him by registered post with acknowledgement of receipt, and then **deposit the rent free of charges in the following week with the tax office (مأمورية العوائد)** (or, where no such office exists, the treasury of the local unit), notify the landlord, and **the deposit receipt discharges the tenant to the amount deposited**; the receiving office must pay it to the landlord on request without formality. **Art. 28** — the tenant may not be deprived of any right or advantage; the judge of urgent matters may order its restoration **set off against rent**; and if it cannot be restored, the **Court of First Instance** may **reduce the rent** by the value of the right or advantage.
  *Art. 28 is a strong and under-used provision. It is a statutory answer to "the landlord cut my water for three months" in the old regime.*

# 5. Eviction — the lawful route, and what is unlawful self-help

## 5.1 The two lawful routes

**Route A — substantive action for possession/eviction (دعوى الطرد / الإخلاء)**, before the court of competent jurisdiction. This is the only route that produces an enforceable judgment.
**Route B — urgent order (قاضي الأمور الوقتية)**, now expressly created for **new-system** leases by **Law 165/2025 Art. 2 bis**, and for **old-system** leases by **Law 164/2025 Art. 7**. Key features of B:
- made on application by the **owner or lessor**, to the **قاضي الأمور الوقتية at the court within whose district the property lies**;
- for new-system leases, available once the **term stated in the lease has expired** and the tenant refuses to vacate;
- **the tenant may still raise a substantive action, but filing it does NOT suspend the urgent order** (both statutes say so expressly).
- ⚠️ Contested whether B is **exclusive**. See §2.4 — the Suez Court of Appeal says it is not. **File both.**

## 5.2 The closed list of grounds under the old regime

✅ **Law 136/1981 Art. 18** (the last consolidated text, as at 18/02/1999, reproduced at https://ahmedazimelgamel.blogspot.com/2017/10/136-1981.html and https://manshurat.org/node/255):

> «**لا يجوز للمؤجر أن يطلب إخلاء المكان ولو انتهت المدة المتفق عليها في العقد إلا لأحد الأسباب الآتية:**
> **(أ)** الهدم الكلي أو الجزئي للمنشآت الآيلة للسقوط والإخلاء المؤقت لمقتضيات الترميم والصيانة وفقاً للأحكام المنظمة لذلك بالقوانين السارية.
> **(ب)** إذا لم يقم المستأجر بالوفاء بالأجرة المستحقة خلال **خمسة عشر يوماً** من تاريخ تكليفه بذلك بكتاب موصى عليه مصحوب بعلم الوصول دون مظروف أو بإعلان على يد محضر، **ولا يحكم بالإخلاء إذا قام المستأجر قبل إقفال باب المرافعة في الدعوى بأداء الأجرة وكافة ما تكبده المؤجر من مصاريف ونفقات فعلية.**
> **ولا ينفذ حكم القضاء المستعجل بطرد المستأجر من العين بسبب التأخير في سداد الأجرة إعمالاً للشرط الفاسخ الصريح** إذا ما سدد المستأجر الأجرة والمصاريف والأتعاب عند تنفيذ الحكم **وبشرط أن يتم التنفيذ في مواجهة المستأجر**.
> **فإذا تكرر امتناع المستأجر أو تأخره في الوفاء بالأجرة المستحقة دون مبررات تقدرها المحكمة حكم عليه بالإخلاء أو الطرد بحسب الأحوال.**
> **(ج)** إذا ثبت أن المستأجر قد تنازل عن المكان المؤجر، أو أجره من الباطن بغير إذن كتابي صريح من المالك للمستأجر الأصلي، **أو تركه للغير بقصد الاستغناء عنه نهائياً**، وذلك دون إخلال بالحالات التي يجيز فيها القانون للمستأجر تأجير المكان مفروشاً أو التنازل عنه أو تأجيره من الباطن أو تركه لذوي القربى وفقاً لأحكام **المادة 29 من القانون 49 لسنة 1977**.
> **(د)** إذا ثبت **بحكم قضائي نهائي** أن المستأجر استعمل المكان المؤجر أو سمح باستعماله بطريقة **مقلقة للراحة أو ضارة بسلامة المبنى أو بالصحة العامة** أو في **أغراض منافية للآداب العامة**.
> **ومع عدم الإخلال بالأسباب المشار إليها لا تمتد بقوة القانون عقود إيجار الأماكن المفروشة.**
> **وتلغى المادة (31) من القانون رقم 49 لسنة 1977 وذلك اعتباراً من تاريخ العمل بهذا القانون.**»

**Five traps inside that article:**
1. **It is exhaustive and it survives the expiry of the agreed term** ("**ولو انتهت المدة المتفق عليها في العقد**"). Under the old regime the contract term is **not** a ground for eviction. The expiry route arrives only because of **Law 164/2025 Art. 2** now.
2. **The arrears cure is unusually generous and is a defence, not just a shield.** Rent + **all actual costs and expenses** before **closing of the pleadings (إقفال باب المرافعة)** defeats the case. And even a summary **eviction order** based on late payment of rent **cannot be executed** against a **conditional resolutive clause** if the lessee pays rent, costs and fees **at the time of execution, in person**.
3. **The material change of use (ج) must be proved by a final court judgment** in the disturbance/health/morals cases — a landlord cannot self-certify it.
4. **The cross-reference to Law 49/1977 Art. 29 is preserved**, so the family-continuation rights (surviving death/abandonment) still carve out of the assignment/surrender ground.
5. **"لا تمتد بقوة القانون عقود إيجار الأماكن المفروشة"** — furnished old leases do **not** extend by operation of law (contrast Law 49/1977 Art. 46, which gives a furnished lessee who had occupied for 5 consecutive years before the law a right to stay).

## 5.3 Law 164/2025's two extra grounds — repeated because they are new and aggressive

(1) the property has been **left shut up for more than one year without justification**; (2) the lessee **owns a residential or non-residential unit usable for the same purpose** as the leased one. Plus the sunset in Art. 2. ⚠️ Note ground (2) is about **owning**, not occupying — if the lessee buys a comparable flat next door and keeps the old one, the landlord gets an ejectment order.

## 5.4 ⚠️ What is unlawful self-help

**There is no lawful route out of a tenancy that does not involve either a court judgment or a قاضي الأمر الوقتية order.** Self-help by a landlord is therefore either a breach of the peace and a trespass, or a criminal offence depending on what is done. **Concretely unlawful:**

| Self-help act | Why it is unlawful | What the tenant should do |
|---|---|---|
| **Changing the locks / putting a new lock on the unit and refusing to give keys** | The tenant is not a lessee by consent any more — he becomes a **squatter (غاصب)** in the civil sense, so the landlord must get possession from zero. There is no self-executing right of entry. | Photograph the locks, obtain a police report (محضر), apply to the **قاضي الأمور الوقتية** for an order, or bring a **دعوى طرد / حيازة**. Do **not** break in — that inverts the roles. |
| **Cutting electricity, water or gas, or having the utilities re-registered in the landlord's own name** | Interference with a lawful possession; actionable as **destructive interference** (Civil Code **Art. 575** treats physical interference with enjoyment). ⚠️ **It is NOT a specific criminal offence — see the penal note below.** It does **not** create or perfect any right, and the tenant's possession is undisturbed in law. Under old-system leases **Law 49/1977 Art. 28** expressly forbids depriving the tenant of "أي حق من حقوقه أو منعه من أي ميزة كان ينتفع بها" and gives him a summary remedy to have it restored **set off against rent**, with a **rent reduction** if it cannot be restored. | Send a formal notice, get a police محضر, go to the **قاضي الأمور الوقتية** for restoration, and set the value off against rent / claim a reduction. **Claim rent reduction — it is the statutory answer, not just damages.** |
| **Removing the tenant's furniture / belongings** | Art. 589(2) expressly **exempts** movables whose removal was required by the lessee's trade or by ordinary life, and movables that fully secure the rent. Removing them converts the landlord into a **taker of another's property**. | محضر + urgent restoration application + damages. |
| **Sealing the door, changing the water/electricity meter, or building a barrier** | Same as above; also capable of being framed as **damage to property** or as **arbitrary acts**. | Same. |
| **Cutting off the building's common services (lift, water pumps, intercom)** | Interferes with the enjoyment guaranteed by **Arts. 564 and 571**; a rent reduction and damages. | Arts. 567(1)–(2) put common repairs and pumps on the **lessor**. Urgent restoration + rent reduction. |
| **Threatening, or removing a lock in the middle of the night without police present** | Potential **criminal assault / housebreaking**; destroys the landlord's own position and can expose him to damages **and** to a counter-claim. | Never do it. Serve a notice and sue. |

⚠️ **Penal exposure — RESOLVED 19 October 2026, and the popular citation is WRONG.** The honest answer is: **there is no tenancy-specific criminal offence of cutting a tenant's utilities or changing his locks.** What follows is everything I verified, so an agent can answer the question without inventing a number.

**1. ❌ Penal Code Art. 375 bis is NOT a utilities article. Do not cite it.** It is the **البلطجة / الترويع والتخويف** article (الباب السادس عشر). Its text begins:
> «مع عدم الإخلال بأية عقوبة أشد واردة في نص آخر، يعاقب بالحبس مدة لا تقل عن سنة كل من قام بنفسه أو بواسطة غيره **باستعراض القوة أمام شخص أو التلويح له بالعنف أو بتهديده باستخدام القوة** أو بالعنف معه أو مع زوجه أو أحد من أصوله أو فروعه … لترويع المجني عليه أو تخويفه …»

Multiple Egyptian legal-marketing pages quote Art. 375 bis as «يعاقب بالحبس مدة لا تقل عن سنة كل من تعمد قطع المياه أو الكهرباء أو أي من المرافق الأساسية…». **That text does not exist in the article.** Anyone who repeats it is citing a phrase that is not in the Penal Code. *(Art. 375 bis **can** bite where there is an actual **display of force or a threat** — shouting, threatening, banging, standing over the tenant — but the offence is then the threat, not the utility.)*
**Source:** Penal Code text, الباب السادس عشر, https://egylawsite.wordpress.com/قـانـون-العـقـوبـات/ — ⚠️ this reproduction is OCR-noisy ("اقوة" for "القوة", "المجنة" for "المجنى"); quote the article only after checking a second text, but the article's **identity and subject matter** are not in doubt.

**2. ❌ Penal Code Art. 162 bis is not it either — it protects the PUBLIC network.** Verbatim opening:
> «يعاقب بالسجن كل من تسبب عمدًا في إتلاف خط من خطوط الكهرباء **التي تملكها الحكومة أو الهيئات أو المؤسسات العامة أو الوحدات التابعة لها، أو تُرخص في إنشائها لمنفعة عامة**، وذلك بقطع الأسلاك الموصلة للتيار الكهربائي أو الكابلات أو كسر شيء من العدد أو الآلات أو عوازل الأسلاك أو إتلاف الأبراج أو المحطات أو الشبكات المتصلة بالخطوط الكهربائية المذكورة … بحيث ترتب عليه انقطاع التيار الكهربائي ولو مؤقتًا.»

The conduct it punishes is **damaging the public supply line or its equipment**. A landlord switching off a supply inside his own building does not damage a government line. **Art. 162 bis does not fit, and there is no point in stretching it.** (Negligent damage to such a line → up to six months or a fine up to EGP 500, per its second paragraph; and in all cases the value of what was destroyed must be paid.)

**3. ❌ There is no criminal offence in the tenancy statutes either.**
- **Law 164/2025 contains no penal article.** Verified: all ten articles of the gazette text read (§2.4).
- **Law 49/1977 Art. 76** is a **closed list** — Arts. **7(1), 8, 13(1), 24 and 25**. **Art. 28** (the article that expressly forbids depriving the tenant of his rights and advantages) **is not on it**, so cutting the utilities was never a crime under the old regime either.
- **Law 136/1981 Art. 24** abolished all custodial penalties in the tenancy laws anyway (§4.1.1), so what survives is fines and the خلو الرجل offence — nothing touching utilities.
- The one landlord-side criminal article of a similar *shape* is **Law 49/1977 Art. 81** — imprisonment ≥ 1 month and a fine, for anyone who **deliberately** does an act threatening a building's safety or exposing it to collapse or total/partial destruction **with intent to have it treated as dilapidated (آيل للسقوط)**. It requires danger to the structure, not a switched-off meter — but it is the closest thing in the statute and it is worth knowing. Its custodial limb is abolished by Law 136/1981 Art. 24.

**4. ✅ What IS solid, and what to advise.** The tenant's remedies are **civil**, and they are strong:
- **Old-system: Law 49/1977 Art. 28** — the tenant «لا يجوز … حرمانه من أى حق من حقوقه أو منعه من أى ميزة كان ينتفع بها»; the **judge of urgent matters** may order restoration **set off against the rent**, and where restoration is impossible the **Court of First Instance may reduce the rent** by the value of the lost right or advantage. **This is the statutory answer to a landlord who cuts the water.**
- **Both regimes: Civil Code Arts. 564, 567, 571 and 575** — delivery in a fit condition, the duty to keep the property in repair, no act impairing enjoyment, and physical interference with enjoyment. Plus **Art. 568** self-help with set-off for urgent repairs, and **Art. 565(2)** rescission where the tenant's household is exposed to **خطر جسيم**.
- **Practical escalation for a tenant whose utilities were cut:** a police **محضر إثبات حالة** first; a lawyer's notice; then an application to the **قاضي الأمور الوقتية** for restoration plus set-off / rent reduction, and a damages claim. **Never** advise a tenant to re-break the seal or the lock — that moves the dispute onto the landlord's ground.
- **A landlord who does it:** loses the possession argument, gains a counter-claim, and will be sent to the same urgent judge **as a defendant**. In the old regime the flat is also now on a statutory clock (§2.4) — patience and a court file beat a plumber, every time.
**Sources:** Penal Code text (Arts. 162 bis, 375, 375 bis): https://egylawsite.wordpress.com/قـانـون-العـقـوبـات/ · Law 49/1977 Arts. 28, 76, 77, 81: https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-49-1977 · Law 136/1981 Art. 24 (SCC's own quotation): https://mksegypt.org/ar/judgments-of-the-supreme-constitutional-court/15419
**Sources for the civil characterisation (secondary, but consistent with the verified Civil Code text):** https://www.menafayq.com/legal-protection-tenant-arbitrary-eviction-egypt/ · https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5082140.aspx (press warning on cutting water, Jan 2025)

## 5.5 The practical eviction checklist (what an agent should produce)

1. **Fix the regime first** (§2.2). Everything else depends on it.
2. **Get the document:** the lease, all amendments, handover report/photos, rent receipts, the date the term began and ends.
3. **Old regime:** identify **which** of the five Art. 18 grounds you are on. If none — you cannot evict. Check the **Art. 29** family-continuation survivors, the **Art. 46** furnished-continuation, and **Art. 41** (the tenant occupying another unit without permission). If the property is under a **demolition / redevelopment** scheme, **Law 49/1977 Arts. 49–54** give the landlord a route to redevelop and give displaced tenants a **right to a suitable alternative unit** plus compensation — a separate and more landlord-friendly regime.
4. **Arrears:** serve the **15-day demand** (registered post with acknowledgement, or notification by a court bailiff) exactly as Art. 18(ب) requires; be ready for the tenant's Art. 18(ب) cure (rent + actual costs before closing of pleadings).
5. **New regime:** confirm the term has expired (**Art. 598** — no notice needed), then **serve a written notice to vacate anyway** so that **Art. 600** blocks Art. 599(1) tacit renewal, then sue **and** apply to the قاضي الأمور الوقتية.
6. **Old regime 2025+:** check whether the sunset (Art. 2 of Law 164/2025), or one of the two new Art. 7 grounds (shut up >1 year, or the tenant owns a suitable unit), is satisfied. Do not rely on the expiry of the agreed term — under Art. 18 of Law 136/1981 that is **not** a ground.
7. **Never** attempt possession by self-help. File the paper.

# 6. The lessor's obligations, and what happens if the property is unfit

Everything in this section is **Civil Code Arts. 564–578, 582–584** and is set out verbatim in §1.2. The operative summary for an agent:

| Situation | Statute | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| Property delivered **unfit** for the agreed use, or enjoyment substantially curtailed | **565(1)** | **Rescission**, if justified |
| Property exposes the lessee / his household / his staff to **serious danger (خطر جسيم)** | **565(2)** | **Rescission — even if the lessee already waived that right** |
| Lessor does not perform **Art. 567** maintenance after notice | **568(1)** | Court licence to do the work and **set it off against rent**; plus rescission or rent reduction |
| **Urgent or minor** repairs which are the lessor's duty | **568(2)** | **Self-help, no licence needed** — do it and **set off against rent** |
| **Total destruction**, no fault of either party | **569(1)** | **Contract terminates automatically**; **no compensation** (569(3)) |
| **Partial** destruction / unfitness / substantial curtailment, lessee not at fault, lessor does not restore in time | **569(2)** | **Rent reduction OR rescission**, at the court's discretion, plus Art. 568 self-help |
| Destruction/damage from a cause **beyond the lessor's control** | **569(3)** | **No compensation** |
| Lessor's **urgent** repairs impairing enjoyment | **570(1)** | Rescission or rent reduction; **but staying on until the repairs finish forfeits the rescission right (570(2))** |
| Lessor **changes the property** or does anything impairing enjoyment | **571(1)** | Breach; damages; breach of the Art. 572/577 framework |
| Damage caused by **another lessee or a third party** | **571(2)** | The lessor's warranty **extends** to it — pursue the lessor |
| **Government act** substantially reducing enjoyment | **574** | Rescission or rent reduction, **plus damages from the lessor if the act was issued for a reason he is responsible for** |
| **Latent defect** preventing or substantially reducing enjoyment | **576** | **576(2) exception:** no warranty if the lessee was told of it, or knew of it, **at the time of contracting** — *ask when the tenant learned, not whether it was latent* |
| Defect found | **577** | Rescission **or** rent reduction **or** demand repair **or** repair it himself at the lessor's expense if not onerous on the lessor; **plus damages unless the lessor proves he did not know of the defect** |
| **Clause excluding or limiting the warranty where the lessor fraudulently concealed its cause** | **578** | **VOID** |
| **Water, taxes and costs on the property** | **567(3)** | Lessor, unless otherwise agreed. **Water by flat charge = lessor; by meter = lessee. Electricity, gas, personal-use = lessee.** |
| **Roofs, yards, wells, latrines, drains** | **567(2)** | **Lessor** |

**And the lessee's side, for balance:** Art. 582 (customary "التأجيرية" repairs), Art. 583 (**عناية الرجل العادي** — duty of an ordinary person), Art. 584 (**liable for fire unless he proves a cause beyond his control; several lessees liable in proportion to the part each occupies; a lessor residing there is also liable**), Art. 580 (no alterations without consent).

**Old regime extra:** ✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 13** — the owner must deliver the property **fit for use** within the agreed periods, failing which the tenant may complete the outstanding works **on a licence from the judge of urgent matters and set the cost off against the rent**. ✅ **Art. 38** — the landlord of a wholly or partly let building must install **tanks and pumps** to supply water to all floors with adequate pipe diameters, in the areas and per the governor's rules; **if the owner refuses, Arts. 60(2) and 60(3) apply without the need to petition the committee or the court**, and **Art. 61** governs recovery of what he spent. ✅ **Art. 35** — the owner of a building created after the law took effect must **provide a separate meter per unit at the lessee's expense**, placed where it can be read without entering the unit.

# 7. ⚠️ Registration — the الشهر العقاري gap

## 7.1 The rule: registration is **conditional**, not automatic

✅ **Law 114/1946 (قانون تنظيم الشهر العقاري), Art. 11** — read from the consolidated text at https://lawhub.info/?p=11638:

> «**يجب تسجيل الإيجارات والسندات التي ترد على منفعة العقار إذا زادت مدتها على تسع سنوات** والمخالصات والحوالات بأكثر من أجرة ثلاث سنوات مقدماً، وكذلك الأحكام النهائية المثبتة لشئ من ذلك.
> **ويترتب على عدم تسجيلها أنها لا تكون نافذة في حق الغير فيما زاد على مدة تسع سنوات بالنسبة إلى الإيجارات والسندات** وفيما زاد عن أجرة ثلاث سنوات بالنسبة إلى المخالصات والحوالة.»

**This is the whole answer to the question asked, and it is a narrow answer:**

- ✅ A **residential lease of 9 years or less does NOT need to be registered at the الشهر العقاري** in order to be valid, or in order to be opposable to a purchaser.
- ✅ A lease **exceeding 9 years must be registered**, and **if it is not, it is not opposable to third parties as regards the excess over 9 years.** That is the precise sanction: the lease still works for 9 years against a buyer who takes the property free of it, and then dies.
  ⚠️ **The 9 years run from the start of the enjoyment, not from registration of the sale** — per the Court's reasoning quoted in the sources below.
- ✅ Separately, **advance rent (المخالصات) prepaid for more than 3 years** must be registered, failing which it is not opposable as regards the excess over 3 years.
- ✅ **Proof Law (Law 25/1968) Art. 15** — an informal instrument (**المحرر العرفي**) is evidence of its **date** against third parties only from the moment it has a **fixed date**, which arises from: (a) registration in the register kept for it; (b) proof of its contents in another instrument of fixed date; (c) endorsement by a competent public officer; (d) the death of one of its signatories with recognised effect, or the moment it becomes impossible for one of them to write or sign; (e) any other event conclusively showing the paper was issued before it — **and the judge may, in the circumstances, decline to apply this article to المخالصات (advance-payment receipts).**
- ✅ **Law 114/1946 Art. 12** — all dispositions creating a **derivative (تبعية)** real right must be **annotated (قيد)**, failing which they are not evidence against third parties. **Art. 12 bis** — **the date of instruments that must be registered under the preceding articles cannot be proved at all (لا يقبل إثبات تاريخ المحررات الواجب شهرها).**
  *Consequence: if a 12-year lease is not registered, you cannot simply prove its date. That is a much sharper sanction than "not opposable".*

**Sources:**
- ✅ Consolidated Law 114/1946 text with amendment footnotes (Art. 9 as amended by Law 9/2022; Arts. 2 and 3 repealed by Law 5/1964 and Law 25/1976; Art. 6, 8, 10 as amended by Law 25/1976; Art. 12 bis added by Law 82/1957): https://lawhub.info/?p=11638
- ✅ English translation (Andersen Egypt): https://eg.andersen.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Law-No.-114-of-1946.pdf — ⚠️ **its highlight says "Failure to register shall render such rights nonexistent" for Arts. 9–10; that is the rule for original (أصلية) real rights, not for leases. Do not apply the Art. 9 rule to leases.**
- ✅ Court of Cassation reasoning on Art. 11, quoted verbatim: https://egyptian-awkaf.blogspot.com/2012/05/blog-post_8663.html — *"لا يكفي لنفاذ مدته الزائدة عن هذا الحد في حق الغير أن يكون ثابت التاريخ"* (Cassation civil appeal No. 4665 of year 61, session 9/7/1995; **Al-Sanhuri, *Al-Wasit*, vol. 66/29, p. 237**).
- ✅ Proof Law Art. 15 (official Notaries' Union of Egypt page): https://www.notariesofegypt.org/article.php?id=430 · consolidated PDF: https://abdalhmidrohim.com/Files/d9b4f410-6062-45d8-8474-a6c73e8de5cb.pdf
**As of:** 2026-10-12.

## 7.2 How to make a residential lease safe — practical

⚠️ **Everything in this subsection is 🟡 secondary/commentary. The statutory position is the two paragraphs above.**

1. **If the lease is ≤ 9 years:** registration at the الشهر العقاري is not required for opposability, **but have the date certified** (إثبات التاريخ / تسجيل في سجل مأمورية الشهر العقاري). Reports indicate the real-estate registration office **refuses to date-certify a lease exceeding 9 years** (that is registration territory) — https://madamafaf.com/services/date-certification/ and https://www.arcanweb.com/ar/alshhr-alaakary (⚠️ both commercial sites; the second states it as a matter of law: *«وفقًا لأحكام قانون الشهر العقارى … عققد الإيجار لن يتم إثبات تاريخه بالسجل الخاص به إلا إذا كانت مدته لا تزيد عن 9 سنوات»*).
2. **If the lease is > 9 years:** **register it.** Otherwise you are exposed twice — not opposable beyond 9 years, **and** (Art. 12 bis) unable to prove your date at all.
3. **Have it notarised** if you want it enforceable: ✅ **Law 4/1996 Art. 2, second paragraph (added by Law 137/2006)** — «ويكون للمحررات المثبتة لهذه العلاقة الإيجارية وشروطها وانتهائها **قوة السند التنفيذي بعد توثيقها بحضور أطرافها**». Notarisation gives the lease, its terms and its termination **the force of an executive instrument**. **This is the single cheapest risk mitigation available to a residential landlord and it is routinely skipped.** *Sources: https://lawhub.info/?p=12942 · https://mousalawyer.yoo7.com/t6530-topic (gives the gazette: Official Gazette No. 23 bis, 11 June 2006; promulgated 10 June 2006).* ✅ Art. 1 of Law 137/2006; Art. 2 publication and commencement.
4. **Never pay more than 3 years' rent in advance** under an old-system lease — beyond that the advance is not opposable to third parties if unregistered, **and** under Law 49/1977 Art. 26 a landlord may not demand advance payment at all.
5. **Inspect the title before signing.** ✅ **ال الشهر العقاري is not decorative**: registration of an original real right is constitutive — **Law 114/1946 Art. 9: failure to register means the right "neither arises, transfers, changes nor extinguishes, as between the parties or with regard to third parties", and the only effect of an unregistered disposition is personal obligations between the parties.** An unregistered sale contract binds the parties but **transfers nothing**. (⚠️ Note also: an unregistered **contract of sale** is a **sh-arid** — the seller still holds title; the buyer's protection is Art. 10 and a specific-performance action. If the seller is insolvent, the buyer is unprotected. This is outside the tenancy file but it is the reason "the lease is registered but the sale isn't" destroys the tenant's position.)

## 7.3 One more registration duty specific to old-system residential leases

✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 24** (quoted in §4.1 above): **old-system leases must be in writing and their date evidenced before the real-estate registration notary.** Art. 24 also makes a **second lease on the same unit VOID** where the lessor breaches it. And ✅ **Law 49/1977 Arts. 41–43**: a landlord letting a **furnished** place (other than a hotel or hostel) must **notify the police station within 3 days** of the letting or occupation, giving the occupant's name, nationality, passport/ID, the term and its dates and the purpose; must **register the furnished lease with the competent local unit**, which must report monthly to the tax authority; and **the landlord's claims under Arts. 39–40 are not entertained unless the contract was registered as required, and the landlord may not rely on unregistered contracts.**
✅ **Art. 44** — only **vacant** summer-resort and country-house units may be let **furnished** by a ministerial decision. (⚠️ **SCC Case No. 4 of year 23 دستورية, 13/4/2003**, held the wording unconstitutional insofar as it allowed owners to let furnished without the owner's consent — annotated in the MOJ text.)

# 8. Termination by death, sale of the property, destruction

## 8.1 Death

| Event | **إيجار جديد** | **إيجار قديم** |
|---|---|---|
| **Death of the LESSOR** | ✅ **Civil Code Art. 601(1): the lease does NOT end.** The lessee's right is unaffected; the lease passes into the estate (see file 01, Art. 145 — effects run to the universal successor). | ✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 29 first clause: the residential lease does not end on the lessee's death or abandonment** — and as to the lessor's death, the old regime was built on the same premise; 🟡 **no article located that states it expressly for the old regime — but Law 49/1977 Art. 30 (leases bind the new owner) is the same idea applied to a transferee, so the point is safe in substance.** |
| **Death of the LESSEE** | ✅ **Art. 601(1): survives.** ✅ **Art. 601(2): but the HEIRS may apply to end it** if they prove the burdens have become heavier than their resources or the lease exceeds their needs — **on the Art. 563 notice periods, and only within SIX MONTHS of the death.** ✅ **Art. 602:** if the lease was for the lessee's **trade or on personal grounds**, **either the heirs or the lessor may apply to end it.** ✅ **Art. 626:** sharecropping (*muzara'a*) **ends on the lessee's death.** | ✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 29(1): the lease does not end on the lessee's death or on his abandoning the property if his WIFE, CHILDREN, or either of his PARENTS remain in it and were residing with him up to the death or abandonment.** For **other relatives, by blood or by marriage up to the THIRD degree**, continuation requires **residence in the property for at least one year before the death/abandonment, or the duration of his occupation, whichever is shorter.** ✅ **Art. 29(2): for a commercial, industrial, professional or craft activity the lease does NOT end on death and continues for whoever actually works the property — the spouse and relatives up to the SECOND degree, male and female, majors or minors, whether they work personally or through an agent.** ✅ **Art. 29(3): in all cases the lessor is BOUND to conclude a lease with those entitled to continue, and those occupants are JOINTLY AND SEVERALLY liable (تضامن) under all the terms of the contract.** |
| Constitutional trimming of Art. 29 | — | ✅ **Every limb has been partly struck down**, as annotated in the MOJ text: **Case 6/Y9, 18/3/1995** (in-law relatives to the 3rd degree who resided 1 year before abandonment or the duration of occupation, whichever is less); **Cases 116/Y18 and 3/Y18, 2/8/1997 and 4/1/1997** (the "blood relatives to the third degree" and "affinal relatives to the third degree" residence conditions); **Case 70/Y18, 3/11/2002** (the Art. 29(3) duty to conclude a lease — it terminates when the last of them stops residing, whether by death or by leaving). **⚠️ The exact operative wording after each ruling is not set out in the source. Get the consolidated Art. 29 before advising on it — this is the highest-litigation-density provision in the old regime.** |
| **Tenants are JOINTLY AND SEVERALLY liable** | ❌ no equivalent | ✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 29(3)** — under the Civil Code each lessee owes only his own obligation. **This is a real and underappreciated difference: on a family-continuation lease, the whole family can be pursued for the whole rent.** |

## 8.2 Sale / transfer of the property

✅ **Civil Code Art. 604(1)** — on a transfer of the leased property, voluntary or compulsory, **the lease is not opposable to the new owner unless the lease has a fixed date earlier than the disposition that transferred ownership.** **Art. 604(2)** — the new owner may nonetheless elect to be bound. **Art. 605(1)** — a new owner against whom the lease is not opposable may not compel vacation except on Art. 563 notice. **Art. 605(2)** — if he notifies before expiry he must **compensate**, and the lessee cannot be made to leave until paid or secured. **Art. 606** — no reliance on rent prepaid to the old landlord unless the lessee knew or must have known of the transfer.

✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 30 — ⭐ THE KEY EXCEPTION:**
> «**استثناء من حكم المادة (604) من القانون المدني تسري عقود الإيجار القائمة على المالك الجديد للعقار ولو لم يكن لسند الإيجار تاريخ ثابت بوجه رسمي سابق على تاريخ انتقال الملكية.**»

**Old-system leases bind the new owner of the property EVEN IF the lease instrument has no officially fixed date earlier than the transfer of ownership.** This is an express statutory exception to Art. 604, and it is why an old-system tenant's position is strong on a sale. **Do not apply Art. 604's fixed-date test to an old-system lease.**

✅ The reason the old regime did not need Art. 146's knowledge condition: see the Court of Cassation reasoning quoted at https://egyptian-awkaf.blogspot.com/2012/05/blog-post_8663.html — *«الحكم بالمادة 604 فيما يتعلق بذلك خروجاً على القاعدة العامة المنصوص عليها في المادة 146. ومعنى ذلك أن الإيجار ينصرف إلى الخلف بحكم القانون.»*

## 8.3 Destruction

✅ **Civil Code Art. 569(1): total destruction during the lease, by no fault of either party ⇒ the contract terminates automatically.** ✅ **Art. 569(2): partial destruction / unfitness / substantial curtailment without lessee fault ⇒ the lessee may seek a rent reduction OR rescission** if the lessor does not restore within a reasonable time. ✅ **Art. 569(3): no compensation** where the cause is beyond the lessor's control. ✅ **Art. 573:** the duty to notify the lessor of things requiring his intervention includes **squatter occupation (اغتصاب)**. ✅ **Art. 570:** the lessee may not obstruct the lessor's urgent repairs. ✅ **Art. 572:** a stranger asserting a conflicting right.

**Partial-destruction compensation for old-system tenants:** ✅ **Law 49/1977 Arts. 49–54** — where a **wholly or partly non-residential** building is demolished and rebuilt (demolition permits in hand, etc.), the landlord must notify all tenants by **public notice (إعلان على يد محضر)** and by registered letters, obtain the tenants' **unanimous consent**, and, if 3 months pass without unanimity, may apply to the competent court for an eviction judgment, **executable without a 3-month appeal period**, **provided he pays or deposits (إيداع غير مشروط) the compensation**. He may then obtain from the **judge of urgent matters** an immediate order to eject a tenant who still refuses **after** the compensation has been paid. **Art. 52:** the landlord must complete demolition within 3 months of vacating the whole building and start rebuilding within 3 months of finishing demolition; **if he fails without acceptable excuse, any former tenant has a right, by order of the judge of urgent matters, to return to occupy their unit where possible, or otherwise to statutory-equivalent compensation** in addition to the Art. 49 compensation and any compensation already received, and **may sue for anything more. Art. 54:** those tenants are **entitled to occupy a suitable new unit in the rebuilt property**, the landlord (or his universal or special successor) being **bound to create new units suitable for the same purpose** unless legislation provides otherwise; they get **additional compensation**; the landlord must notify them by **bailiff's notice within one month** of completion of the rebuild, and a tenant who does not claim **within one month** of that notice **loses the right**.
⚠️ **This entire redevelopment/compensation chapter (Arts. 49–54) applies only to properties let for non-residential purposes.** ✅ **Art. 51** lists the buildings it does NOT apply to (government and private schools under state supervision, public and private hospitals under the Health Ministry, petrol stations, tourist or hotel or bank facilities, and other facilities of national economic or security importance or serving the public, as designated by a decision of the Housing Minister with the competent Minister's approval); for buildings let to **government bodies, local administration units, public authorities or public-sector units** the chapter **does apply but the notice period is extended to three years** if the lessee body requests it in exchange for waiving eviction compensation.
⚠️ **No equivalent redevelopment regime for old-system RESIDENTIAL tenants was located.** That asymmetry is the reason the 2025 law's seven-year sunset is framed around residential units. 🟡 **Check for a housing-specific redevelopment statute before concluding there is none.**

# 9. Forum — which court, summary vs principal

## 9.1 Territorial jurisdiction

✅ **Law 13/1968 Art. 50** (consolidated text: https://lawhub.info/?p=11332):
> «في الدعاوى العينية العقارية ودعاوى الحيازة يكون الاختصاص للمحكمة التي يقع في دائرتها العقار أو أحد أجزائه إذا كان واقعا في دوائر محاكم متعددة. وفي **الدعاوى الشخصية العقارية** يكون الاختصاص للمحكمة التي يقع في دائرتها العقار **أو موطن المدعي عليه**. **واستثناء من حكم المادة 108 من هذا القانون تختص المحكمة الجزئية الواقع فى دائرتها العقار دون غيرها بدعاوى تسليم العقارات.**»

✅ **Art. 49** — otherwise, territorial jurisdiction is the court of the **defendant's domicile**; if several defendants, the court of any one of them.

**Applied to tenancy:**
- **Possession/eviction actions (دعاوى الحيازة / الطرد) and actions in rem over the property (الدعاوى العينية العقارية)** → **the court within whose district the PROPERTY lies**, regardless of where the parties are. *For a residential tenancy this will almost always be where the flat is — which is usually not where the landlord lives. That is the single most common procedural mistake.*
- **A personal action arising out of a lease (rent arrears, damages)** → the court where the **property lies or where the defendant is domiciled**.
- ✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 5** — «**تختص المحاكم العادية دون غيرها بالفصل في المنازعات التي تنشأ عن تطبيق أحكام هذا القانون**» — the ordinary courts, exclusively. (Not military courts, not the administrative court.)

## 9.3 The forum answer for the four common tenancy actions

1. **Eviction / delivery of the unit against a CURRENT tenant** — Art. 50 sentence 1 sends possession and in rem actions to **the court within whose district the property lies**. **Art. 43(6) + Art. 50 final sentence** give **دعاوى تسليم العقارات** to the **District Court** exclusively (by way of exception to Art. 108). ⚠️ **This is a real trap: an agent who treats an eviction as an ordinary high-value claim and files it in the Court of First Instance of the defendant's district can lose time on a jurisdiction objection.** ⚠️ Note also **Art. 44**: a plaintiff may **not** combine a possession claim with a claim to the right, on pain of losing the possession claim; and a possession judgment may not be pronounced "on the basis of proof of the right or its negation." **So the arrears claim and the eviction claim may have to be brought separately.** ✅ **Art. 47 sentence 2**: the Court of First Instance hears appeals from District Court judgments and from judge-of-urgent-matters judgments.
2. **Rent arrears only** — a personal action arising from a lease → Art. 50 sentence 2: the court where the **property lies or the defendant is domiciled**. As to pecuniary allocation: ✅ **Art. 43(4)** puts "actions for claiming and determining wages and salaries" in the District Court's final list — *⚠️ whether a **rent** claim counts as an "أجور" claim under Art. 43(4) is 🟡 UNVERIFIED and doubtful: أجور there means remuneration for work, not hire. Do not rely on it.* In practice arrears claims go to whichever court has jurisdiction by value.
3. **Order on petition / قاضي الأمور الوقتية** — Arts. 45 and 47 last clause; **Law 164/2025 Art. 7 and Law 165/2025 Art. 2 bis both specify "the court within whose district the property lies".**
4. **Appeal from a rent-committee decision (old regime)** — ✅ **Law 49/1977 Arts. 18–20**: first to the **Court of First Instance** within 30 days, then to the **Court of Appeal** within 30 days **only for error of law, and that judgment is final.** **This is a rare and genuinely final appellate route. If the client has 30 days, the clock starts on notification of the committee's decision, not on the decision's date.**

## 9.4 Procedure points that decide cases

- **Statement of claim (Iqlam da'wa) contents** — a tenancy file should carry: the lease and all annexes/amendments; the handover report and photographs; rent receipts or bank statements; the notice to vacate and its proof of service; police محاضر; the identity documents of both parties; **and, for an old-system lease, the Art. 24 date-evidence from the real-estate registration notary and the Art. 25 deposit record**.
- ✅ **Art. 65 appearance periods (file 03): 15 days** before the Court of First Instance and the Court of Appeal, **8 days** before the District Courts; reducible in urgent matters.
- ✅ **Art. 44** — do not combine possession with the claim to the right.
- ✅ **Art. 3 (file 03)** — personal, direct and existing interest is required; a **potential** interest suffices to avert imminent harm or preserve evidence.

# 10. Art. 375 — the 5-year limitation on rent, in practice

## 10.1 The statute (already in file 01; restated because it is the hinge of arrears claims)

✅ **Civil Code Art. 375(1)**: «**يتقادم بخمس سنوات كل حق دوري متجدد ولو أقر به المدين، كأجرة المباني والأراضي الزراعية، ومقابل الحكر، وكالفوائد والإيرادات المرتبة والمهايا والأجور والمعاشات.**»
✅ **Art. 375(2)**: «لا يسقط الريع المستحق فى ذمة الحائز سيئ النية، ولا الريع الواجب على ناظر الوقف أداؤه للمستحقين، إلا بانقضاء خمس عشرة سنة.»

⚠️ **Two points that trip agents up:**
- Art. 375(1) covers **rent and also "الأجور" (wages)** — the same article prescribes five years for both **rent of buildings** and **remuneration**. They are different rights that happen to share the article.
- **Art. 375 is NOT the general period.** ✅ **Art. 374** gives **fifteen years** as the general rule, and ✅ **Art. 387** provides that **the court may NOT apply prescription of its own motion** — it must be **pleaded by the debtor or any person interested**, and **may be pleaded at any stage, including on appeal before the Court of Appeal**. An agent who assumes the tenant's 6-year arrears claim is time-barred because the court will "notice" it will be wrong: **the landlord must plead the five-year limit expressly.** ⚠️ **This is the single most useful practical point in this section.**

## 10.2 What counts as a "recurring, self-renewing right" (دوري متجدد) — the Cassation test

✅ From the Court of Cassation doctrine collected at https://www.avmsr.com/2023/02/545-nqd.html:
- **دوري (periodic)** = due at periodic intervals (monthly, yearly).
- **متجدد (self-renewing)** = of its nature continuous, not extinguished in one fell swoop by the obligor's ceasing to perform — e.g. rent of buildings and agricultural land, consideration for a *haqq al-himlara*, civil revenues, share profits, maintenance allowances (*dana'ina*), salaries and gratuities of employees and users **but not of labourers and servants**.
- ⚠️ **It is not necessary that the amount be constant for each period** — a right that is periodic and self-renewing falls under the 5-year limit **even if the amount differs from period to period**, as with share profits.
- ⚠️ **A claim loses its periodic/self-renewing character and is then subject to the 15-year period where (a) it is not both periodic and self-renewing — e.g. a fixed sum — or (b) its nature has changed** (e.g. **"المبالغ التي يدفعها المستأجر وفاء لأجرة غير مستحقة لم يخضع للتقادم الخمسي"** — amounts paid as rent **not yet due** are NOT under the 5-year limit).
- ⚠️ **Cassation: mere loss of the two characters does not convert rent into a 15-year debt.** *«لا يترتب على مجرد زوال صفتي الدورية والتجدد عن دين الأجرة أن يصبح دينا عاديا خاضعا للتقادم الطويل.»* ⚠️ **PARTIAL — the full reasoning is truncated in the source; get the judgment before quoting.**

## 10.3 How it applies to arrears — the operating rules

✅ **Arts. 381, 383, 384** (file 01), combined with the rent doctrine:
- **When it starts:** ✅ **Art. 381** — in the absence of a specific rule, from the day the debt falls due. For rent, that is the due date of each instalment. **So the cut-off moves monthly.** A claim filed on 12 October 2026 reaches back to **12 October 2021**. (⚠️ **Art. 380: the periods are counted in days, the first day is not counted, and the period completes at the end of its last day.**)
- **⚠️ Self-serving non-payment:** for a **debt subject to a suspensive condition**, prescription does not run until the condition occurs (Art. 381) — so if a rent instalment is conditional on the landlord delivering something, the clock may not start.
- **Interruption (Art. 383):** a **judicial claim — even one brought before an INCOMPETENT court** — interrupts; **formal notice (التنبيه)** interrupts; **attachment (الحجز)** interrupts; a claim in bankruptcy or distribution proceedings interrupts; **any act the creditor takes to enforce his right in the course of a suit** interrupts. **Art. 384** — prescription is also interrupted by the debtor's **acknowledgment, express or tacit**, and **leaving property in the creditor's hands as a possessory pledge (رهن حيازي) counts as tacit acknowledgment.**
- **Art. 385** — after interruption a **new** period of the same length starts from the end of the interrupting cause, **except** that where a judgment on the debt has become res judicata the new period is **fifteen years**. *For rent, the safe way to make a large arrears claim bulletproof is to get a judgment, not merely a notice.*
- ⚠️ **What does NOT interrupt (from the Cassation list at https://www.avmsr.com/2023/02/545-nqd.html):** *«وليس بينها … **المطالبة غير القضائية** والمنازعة المدين له فيها»* — **an informal demand and merely disputing the debt do not interrupt prescription.** **This is a very common landlord error: a demand letter and a text message are not an إنذار and do not stop the clock.** An agent advising a landlord with aged arrears should raise it.
- ⚠️ **Art. 386** — prescription extinguishes the right but leaves a **natural obligation (التزام طبيعي)** in the debtor's patrimony — so a time-barred debt is unenforceable judicially but is a valid defence to a set-off or to the enforcement of anything the landlord owes the tenant. **In a tenancy, where the tenant may have a set-off claim against the landlord, this matters.**
- ✅ **Art. 382** — prescription does not run while an impediment exists making it difficult to claim (**even a purely moral impediment**), nor as between principal and agent; **Art. 382(2): a period longer than five years does not run against a person lacking capacity, an absentee, or a person convicted of a criminal offence who has no legal representative.**
  ⚠️ **A "purely moral impediment" is broad and fact-driven. 🟡 It is a real but rarely-successful argument; do not lead with it.**

## 10.4 ⚠️ The trap that sits on top of Art. 375

✅ **Law 49/1977 Art. 18(ب)** gives the tenant a cure in an **eviction** action: **pay the rent plus all the lessor's actual costs and expenses before the closing of the pleadings.** That cure is **not** limited by the five-year period. So a landlord can win on liability and lose on the remedy, and vice versa: **the cure may revive arrears that Art. 375 had already extinguished against him.** Tell clients this explicitly.

✅ **And the tenant's reciprocal risk:** under Art. 18(ب) second paragraph, even an **urgent eviction order** based on non-payment cannot be executed against an **express conditional resolutive clause** if the tenant pays rent, costs and fees **at the time of execution, in person**. **The cheapest insurance in Egyptian residential tenancy is to keep paying the rent in person, with a receipt, until the last hour.**

# 12. Trap list for the agent — the ten things that go wrong most

1. **Getting the article numbers wrong.** The lease chapter is **558–634**, not 543–587. There is no "Art. 543 lease".
2. **Assuming a statutory rent cap or indexation under the Civil Code.** There is none. Rent moves only by contract (Art. 147(1)).
3. **Assuming the 7% cap still governs old-rent leases.** It was the **7% (Art. 49/1977 Art. 14(a)) + 3% (Art. 14(b))** formula on **1974 land values** (Art. 15), it was constitutionally attacked, and under **Law 164/2025** rent is now **20×/10×/5× the legal rent + 15% a year**, with a hard sunset. Old-rent advice written from memory is out of date.
4. **Serving no notice on a new-system lease.** **Art. 598**: the lease ends automatically at the end of the agreed term. **Art. 599(1)**: if the tenant stays **with the lessor's knowledge and without objection**, it becomes an **indefinite periodic lease** terminable only on Art. 563(ج) **one month's notice**. **Art. 600**: notice to vacate defeats that presumption. **Always serve notice** — it costs a stamp and saves the flat.
5. **Believing the agreed term is an eviction ground in the old regime.** **Law 136/1981 Art. 18** opens "**لا يجوز للمؤجر أن يطلب إخلاء المكان ولو انتهت المدة المتفق عليها في العقد إلا لأحد الأسباب الآتية**" — the term is expressly **not** a ground. Today it is a ground only because **Law 164/2025 Art. 2** creates the sunset.
6. **Missing the old regime's two hard protections that survive to 2032: the **two-month deposit cap** (Law 49/1977 Art. 25, recoverable by set-off without suing) and the **absolute ban on key money** (Art. 26, whose sanction in **Art. 77** is **imprisonment of not less than 3 months *and* a fine equal to double the amount taken — both, not either — plus mandatory restitution**).** Both are routinely breached. ⚠️ Note the split on Art. 25: it binds **old-system/continuation** leases, but per **SCC Case 85/Y17 (22/3/1997)** the **Civil Code alone** governs a genuinely new post-1996 letting (§4.1).
7. **Believing an unregistered 12-year lease is merely unenforceable against a buyer.** It is worse: under **Law 114/1946 Art. 12 bis the date of an unregistered registrable instrument cannot be proved at all**, and under **Art. 11 it is not opposable beyond 9 years**.
8. **Filing the eviction in the wrong court.** **Art. 50**: possession and in rem actions go to the court where the **property** lies; **Art. 43(6) + Art. 50** give **delivery of immovables** to the **District Court** exclusively. **And Art. 44: do not combine possession with the claim to the right.**
9. **Assuming prescription applies automatically.** **Art. 387**: the court **may not** apply prescription of its own motion — the landlord must **plead Art. 375 expressly**. Conversely, **an informal demand does not interrupt it**; only Art. 383/384 events do. And **Art. 18(ب) of Law 136/1981 lets the tenant cure arrears worth more than five years** before closing of pleadings.
10. **Doing it by force.** Changing locks, cutting utilities and removing belongings are all unlawful, none of them helps, and each creates a counter-claim (restoration, **rent reduction** under Art. 28 for old-system leases, and damages). The **only** routes are a substantive action and, now, a **قاضي الأمور الوقتية** order under **Law 165/2025 Art. 2 bis** / **Law 164/2025 Art. 7**.
