# Egyptian Real-Estate Purchase (Buying Property) — Verified Reference Corpus
# شراء العقار في مصر — المرجع التشريعي

**Purpose:** a citation-safe reference for an AI agent helping a **non-lawyer in Egypt** buy (or sell, or inherit,
or resolve a promised sale of) a property — the legal nature of unregistered sales, the title check, the contract,
registration at the الشهر العقارى and its real fees, the 2.5% transfer tax and the annual property tax, foreign-buyer
and restricted-land rules, and the warranty a buyer actually has against defects and collapse.
Every article number below was read out of a retrieved primary or official-secondary text. **Nothing is asserted from memory.**
**As-of date for the whole file: 2026-10-28.**

⚠️ **Each section carries its own `As of:` line — that is the date *that section* was last read against a source.**
Everything in this file was first built on **2026-10-28**.

⚠️⚠️ **THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT THING IN THIS FILE:** in Egypt, **ownership of real property does not pass by signing
a contract — it passes by registration (الشهر/القيد) at the Real-Estate Registry.** An unregistered sale contract binds
the two parties personally but transfers **nothing in rem**, and the State and the utilities will not deal with an
unregistered buyer. An agent that says "you signed, so the flat is yours" is wrong. **§1 is the section to read first.**

## ⚠️ CORRECTIONS TABLE — read this before citing anything in this file

These are claims or beliefs that this research checked and **overturned or narrowed**. If an AI output contradicts this table, trust the table.

1. ❌ **"قانون تسجيل العقارات رقم 143 لسنة 2017"** — no such instrument could be located. Multiple targeted searches
   (manshurat.org, lawhub.info, official and press coverage of the registration reform) surfaced **no** primary or
   secondary source naming a Law 143/2017 on real-estate registration. The registration reform is carried by
   **Law 186/2020** and **Law 9/2022**, both amending **Law 114/1946** (§4). The confusion vector is real: **two laws
   numbered 143 exist** (Law 143/**1981** on desert lands and Law 143/**1994** on civil status), and something between
   them produced the phantom "143/2017 registration law". Do not cite it. If a user insists it exists, say what is
   above, not a rejection.
2. ⚠️ **Art. 35 مكرر of Law 114/1946 was added by Law 186/2020, not by Law 9/2022.** Practitioner summaries conflate
   the two amendment laws. The gazette text of Law 186/2020 (manshurat PDF) is the verified origin (§1.3).
3. ❌ **"تسجيل شقة يكلّف 500 جنيه"** — the ≤500 EGP fee is statutory only for the **Art. 23 مكررا special route**
   (possession-based applications, §4.3). General registration fees are **area-tiered 500 → 2,000 EGP** for residential
   units with a **maximum of 3,900 EGP**, per the Minister of Justice's official statements (Ahram Gate, 12 May 2022)
   and the official real-estate platform (EREP, 2024) (§4.5). Both a flat-500 claim and a "3–3.5% of value" claim are
   wrong as of 2026.
4. ❌ **"لا توجد ضريبة على بيع عقار" / "the 2.5% is new in 2026"** — a **2.5% tax on the gross value of property
   dispositions exists** and applies **even to unregistered contracts**, heirs included. Its current statutory home is
   **Art. 43 of income-tax Law 91/2005 as replaced by Law 151/2026** (in force 29 July 2026). What Law 151/2026 did
   **not** do is create a gains-based capital-gains tax on property — that remains absent (§5).
5. ❌ **"حد الإعفاء من الضريبة العقارية 24,000 جنيه"** — stale. The residential self-use exemption is **100,000 EGP
   net annual rental value** under **Art. 18/1(d) as replaced by Law 3/2026** (signed 2 April 2026). The 24,000 figure
   survives on stale official example pages (rta.gov.eg); the 50,000 figure was the **draft-stage** number reported by
   the Cabinet in May 2025 and was not the enacted text (§5.5).
6. ❌ **"وعد البيع ينقل الملكية"** — it does not. A promise of sale generates a **تعهد بفعل** (obligation to act), not a
   transfer (Council of State heritage judgment 3856, cc.gov.eg; §1.4). Enforcement path = sue, obtain a final
   judgment, register the judgment (Arts. 9(4), 17, 35 مكرر).
7. ❌ **"أي توكيل يصلح لنقل الملكية"** — the POA must **expressly include authority to sell**; a general/administrative
   POA does not authorize a sale transfer. And per Cassation (طعن 7910 لسنة 91 ق, reported 6 March 2026), a POA that
   authorizes the agent to sell **to himself or others** with intent to complete the sale itself ranks as a **sale
   contract** — a two-edged instrument buyers and sellers must understand (§2.2).
8. ❌ **"الأجانب لا يملكون عقارات في مصر"** — false as a blanket statement: **Law 230/1996** allows non-Egyptians to
   own up to **two properties** for family residence, each ≤4,000 m², under stated conditions (GAFI; Andersen English
   translation). The true restrictions are specific: **agricultural land (Law 15/1963), desert-land regime (Law
   143/1981 as amended 2024), and the Sinai regime (Decree-Law 14/2012 as amended)** (§6).
9. ❌ **"عقد سيناء عادي والحالة تمام"** — in Sinai, disposal without the required sovereign approvals **renders the
   contract void absolutely (يقع باطلاً مطلقاً)** — even between Egyptians — per منشور فني 33/2022 implementing
   Presidential Decree 128/2022 (§6.4).
10. ⚠️ **"ضمان عشر سنوات يقع على المطور"** — Civil Code **Art. 651** binds the **architect and the contractor**
    jointly for ten years against collapse. Reaching the *developer company* as such requires corporate-attribution
    analysis not settled here — surface the rule as written (§7.2).
11. ⚠️ **Origin of the 2.5% transfer tax** — Art. 43 of the income-tax law **existed before 2026** (Law 151/2026 *replaced*
    its text), so the tax predates 2026. The exact originating amendment law (commonly attributed to Law 108/2014)
    could **not** be verified against a retrieved text — do not cite an origin year; cite the current text.

# 1. ⭐ The rule everything hangs on: an unregistered sale transfers nothing

`As of: 2026-10-28`

## 1.1 The constitutive rule (Law 114/1946 Arts. 9–10) — verified core, held in corpus 05

- **Law 114/1946 Art. 9** — registration is **constitutive** for original real rights over real property:
  an unregistered sale **transfers nothing in rem**; it creates **personal obligations only** between the parties.
  Full verified text: `05-tenancy-egypt.md` §7.2 (registry chapter). Do not re-derive it here.
- **Law 114/1946 Art. 10** — the specific-performance action the unregistered buyer has. Cross-ref same section.
- **Law 114/1946 Art. 17** — judgments creating real rights must be registered and endorsed within
  **five years** of finality (the provision also carries the older 1 May 1976 anchor for pending cases —
  historical, not operative for new purchases).
  ✅ mohamymasr.com («وجوب تسجيل الحكم والتأشير به خلال خمس سنوات — المادة 17 من الشهر العقارى») and
  egyls.com («التأشير بمنطوق الحكم الصادر بالصحة والنفاذ خلال خمس سنوات من تاريخ صيرورته نهائياً أو من تاريخ
  ١ / ٥ / ١٩٧٦ أيهما أطول»). **Practical consequence ⭐:** a buyer who wins a registration judgment and sleeps on
  it beyond five years loses the window — file the judgment promptly.
- egyls.com, on the two-articles reading (Arts. 9 and 17 together): «مؤدى نص المادتين ٩، ١٧ من القانون ١١٤ لسنة
  ١٩٤٦ أن ملكية العقار المبيع لا تنتقل إلا بتسجيله».

## 1.2 What the parties DO get from an unregistered sale — the Cassation split

- **Cassation, Appeal 680/64 ق** (the widely-cited ruling, surfaced via the real-estate registrars' association
  clarification carried by Veto Gate, 2021): an unregistered sale **transfers the benefits, the fruits, and the
  procedural rights (الثمرات والدعاوى) between the parties themselves**, but **does not transfer ownership
  as against third parties (الغير)**. This is why an unregistered buyer can collect rent from a sub-tenant and
  defend his possession against his own seller — while remaining exposed to the seller's creditors, a later
  registered purchaser, and the State.
- **Cassation 3037/59 ق (1991)**: same line — the contract concluded is real between the parties; the **real
  right passes only upon registration**.
- **Council of State heritage judgment (doc 3856, cc.gov.eg)** on وعد بالبيع: «الوعد بالبيع ليس له أثر عقد البيع ولا
  ينقل الملك، وغاية ما فى الأمر انه يترتب عليه تعهد بفعل أمر من جانب الواعدين» — a **promise of sale is a promise
  to act, not a sale**; it generates no in-rem effect and does not transfer ownership.
- **Practical split for the agent's output ⭐:**
  | Right | Unregistered buyer | Registered buyer |
  |---|---|---|
  | Possession vs the seller | yes (personal + defended) | yes |
  | Rent/fruits between the parties | yes (680/64 ق) | yes |
  | Oppose the seller's creditors | **no** | yes |
  | Oppose a later registered purchaser | **no** | yes |
  | Transfer utilities / deal with State bodies | **no** (Art. 35 مكرر) | yes |
  | Sell onward by registered deed | **no** | yes |

## 1.3 ⭐ Art. 35 مكرر (added by Law 186/2020): the State and the utilities will not deal with an unregistered buyer

Law 186/2020 (Gazette No. 36 مكرر (ب), 5 September 2020; in force six months after publication) added Art. 35 مكرر
to Law 114/1946, in two parts ✅ (gazette text via manshurat.org PDF; corroborated by mnasserlaw.com, Feb 2026):

1. **Judgment → registration, fast-tracked:** where the title relied on is a **final judgment** establishing or
   creating, transferring, declaring, modifying or extinguishing an original real right, أمين المكتب must give the
   request a **preliminary (وقتي) number** as a شهر or قيد in a dedicated register, after payment of the prescribed
   fee; the preliminary number **converts to final** with all the effects of registration, absent an objection or
   once an objection fails. Objections go to **قاضى الأمور الوقتية within one month** of publication in a daily
   newspaper at the applicant's expense; the judge decides **within seven days**, reasoned, and the decision is
   final.
2. **The utilities/State-bodies rule ⭐⭐:** «وعلى شركات الكهرباء والمياه والغاز وغيرها من الشركات والجهات والوزارات
   والمصالح الحكومية عدم نقل المرافق والخدمات، أو اتخاذ أى إجراء مع صاحب الشأن يتعلق بالعقار إلا بعد تقديم السند
   الذى يحمل رقم الشهر أو القيد» — **electricity, water and gas companies and every government body may not
   transfer services or take any action concerning the property except upon presentation of a deed carrying the
   registration number.**

**Why this is the most practical rule in the file:** the unregistered buyer cannot even put the electricity meter
in his name. Anyone telling a client "the عرفي contract is enough for day-to-day life" is contradicted by the
statute itself. It is also the ordinary buyer's strongest practical lever to force registration.

## 1.4 وعد بالبيع — the promise, and how the promised buyer actually enforces it

- The promise of sale is a **تعهد بفعل** (§1.2, cc.gov.eg 3856). It does not transfer ownership and does not itself
  become registrable.
- The promised buyer's real remedies:
  1. **Sue for registration/specific performance** — an action to compel the seller to execute the transfer; on a
     **final judgment**, register the judgment under **Art. 9(4)** (below) with the **Art. 35 مكرر** fast-track
     (preliminary number → final absent objection).
  2. **Terminate + damages** where performance becomes impossible or the seller's breach repudiates the contract
     (general obligations law; article numbers for that route live in the general-contracts material, not here —
     describe the two branches without an article number).
- **Law 114/1946 Art. 9(4)** (as replaced by Law 9/2022) ✅ — alberonsy full text:
  «ويجوز لمن حصل لصالحه أو مع آخرين على حكم نهائى مثبت لحق من هذه الحقوق أن يطلب قصر التسجيل على القدر الذى قضى له
  به ... وسواء كان ذلك شائعاً أو مفرزًا، على حسب الأحوال» — the judgment-holder may register exactly what was
  adjudged, common or partitioned.
  Art. 9(6) excludes this for **مقايضة** (exchange) judgments — noted for completeness.
- **Mandatory registration of final judgments** is the settled rule the heritage judgment states; do not advise a
  client that "the judgment itself is enough" — §1.5's five-year window applies.

## 1.5 The five-year judgment window (Art. 17) — repeat-and-flag

Judgments: register and endorse within **five years of finality** (§1.1). ⭐ Agents should treat "we won the case"
as the **start** of a deadline, not the end of the matter.

# 2. Before you pay anything — the title check

`As of: 2026-10-28`

## 2.1 What to demand and inspect

- **A registered deed (سند ملكية مشهر)** where one exists: ask for the deed and verify the seller's identity
  against it; then verify the property's description. Where registration is being sought from scratch, the
  statutory application contents define what "good papers" look like — **Law 114/1946 Art. 22** (as replaced by
  Law 9/2022) ✅: (1) identity, capacity and authority data for each party (final judgments being registered are
  exempt from this item); (2) **an official digital map showing the property/unit data and coordinates, or any
  other official document carrying the same data**; (3) **the legal title (سند) for the application**; (4) a
  declaration by the applicant of existing rights/encumbrances on the property, if any — all per the executive
  regulations.
- **Encumbrance check:** the declaration item (4) exists precisely because rights on the property (leases, usufruct,
  attachments, mortgages) ride with it. Ask for the كشف قيد on the property from the registry; treat an
  "everything is clean" verbal assurance as worth nothing.
- **The عرفي reality ⚠️:** a very large share of Egyptian housing stock is held under unregistered (عرفي) titles.
  That does **not** make the purchase safe — it makes the buyer an **Art. 10 مكررا / Art. 23 مكررا** registration
  applicant (§4.3) whose five-year possession clock, deed, and good faith must actually hold. Where the seller has
  no registered deed and no five-year story, the "purchase" is exposure, not ownership.
- **Villages / أراضى فضاء exclusions ⭐:** the possession-based special route **does not apply** to properties
  falling under **Civil Code Art. 970** (state-owned and public-juristic-person property that cannot be acquired by
  prescription) nor to **أراضى فضاء (vacant land)** — Art. 23 مكررا last paragraph ✅. A promise of "register the
  bare plot" via the fast route is therefore false on its face.

## 2.2 Who may lawfully sign — the POA rules

- **The sale authority must be explicit.** A general or administrative POA does not authorize the agent to sell
  the principal's property; the POA must expressly grant sale power. ⚠️ (settled principle, reported across
  practitioner sources incl. egylaws 2026 commentary; state it as a rule without inventing an article number).
- **Cassation, طعن 7910 لسنة 91 ق** (reported by elwatannews, 6 March 2026) ⭐: where a POA is issued **with the
  purpose of actually completing a sale** and empowers the agent to sell **to himself or to others**, it is not a
  mere agency — it **rises to the level of a sale contract of معاوضة**, standing or falling with the contract.
  Two consequences: (a) such a POA can itself function as the sale instrument; (b) **a POA to sell to oneself is a
  self-dealing instrument** — agents and buyers must say out loud which of the two they hold.
- **Forgery risk ⭐:** presenting a **forged عرفي instrument** for registration is a crime —
  **Law 114/1946 Art. 36 مكررا** (as replaced by Law 9/2022): imprisonment **not less than one year** and a fine
  **not exceeding 50,000 EGP**, in addition to any severer penalty; the head of the مأمورية or أمين المكتب must
  seize the forged instrument and refer the matter to the prosecution ✅ (alberonsy text; parlmany.news report of
  the same figures).
- **Inherited property ⭐:** heirs selling before the estate is registered is a classic dispute magnet. The estate
  registration route (Arts. 48–49, §4.4) exists precisely for this; the buyer of inherited land must ask
  **إشهاد شرعي + سند ملكية المورث** (or the 23 مكررا fallback) — not a handshake from "one of the heirs".

## 2.3 Units bought from developers / new cities — what is verifiable and what is not

- ⚠️ **PARTIAL:** units inside **new urban communities (هيئة المجتمعات العمرانية الجديدة)** and developer schemes
  are commonly sold on **initial (مبدئي) contracts, allotment (مخصص/صك مخصصة) documents, or وعد بيع** forms, with
  individual registration historically unavailable until the developer's own title and partition are sorted.
  El-Balad (30 August 2025) reports the Housing Minister's decision granting an **additional six-month window**
  (from announcement) for owners to submit transfer/تنازل requests for residential, professional, administrative
  units and shops and lands **inside New Urban Communities Authority cities** — evidence that these disposals run
  through an administrative window, not ordinary registry practice.
- 🟡 **UNVERIFIED — do not cite:** a dedicated statute regulating pre-completion sale of units (the kind of
  "developer-regulation law" some markets have). Searched; not found in primary text. Describe developer purchases
  structurally: **verify the developer's own title on the land, the contract's assignment/transfer mechanics, and
  whether the unit's registration is contemplated at delivery — anything else is promise, not ownership.**
- ⭐ For any developer deal, the two questions that matter: **(1) whose name is the land in, and (2) does the
  contract bind the developer to register the unit (with dates and consequences).** If either answer is soft, the
  buyer is buying litigation risk.

## 2.4 The buyer's own registration obligation — chain not required ⭐

Law 9/2022's reform is reported across official and practitioner sources as having **abolished the requirement of
proving the ownership chain (تسلسل الملكية) as a condition of registration** and **severed registration from
proof of payment of the 2.5% disposition tax** ✅ (sadanykhalifa.com practitioner summary; elwatan/Ahram/youm7
coverage of the Minister's launch statements, 7 May 2022; the Facebook explainer posts list both points).
Consequence for the buyer ⭐: a "seller can't prove his seller's seller" objection is **no longer a registration
killer**; the possession route and the documentary route can carry the registration themselves. (The statutory
anchors are Art. 10 مكررا and Art. 23 مكررا, §4.3.)

# 3. The contract

`As of: 2026-10-28`

## 3.1 No statutory form requirement for the sale's validity — but evidence is everything

- Egyptian law does not require a specific form for the **validity** of a sale; the sale is concluded by offer and
  acceptance. **The absence of form is exactly why unregistered/عرفي contracts are ubiquitous — and why
  everything turns on what can be proved and what can be registered.** (General-contracts framing; the
  constitutive rule is §1.1.)
- For **registration**, the instrument must satisfy **Art. 22** contents (§2.1): party data, **official digital
  map/coordinates**, title, declaration of encumbrances. A contract drafted without a property description that
  can be mapped will fail at the registry even if valid between the parties. ⭐ Draft the contract with the
  registry in mind, not the printer.

## 3.2 عرفي contracts — certification at the مأمورية, and the forgery line

- **Law 114/1946 Art. 28** (as replaced by Law 9/2022) ✅: after the مأمورية reviews the instrument and endorses its
  eligibility for registration (التأشير بصلاحيته للشهر), the instrument is **notarised (توثيق) or its signatures
  certified (تصديق) as the case may be, including for عرفي instruments** — dedicated books are kept at each
  مأمورية for exactly this; the file then goes up to the مكتب within **the next day at most**, and the مكتب must
  complete registration formalities within **seven days** at most.
  → **⭐ عرفي contracts are not dead-end paper:** the reform built a certification lane for them, and the
  possession route (§4.3) gives the عرفي holder a registration claim after five years.
- **Art. 36 مكررا** (§2.2): forged عرفي instruments presented for registration → ≥1 year prison + ≤50,000 EGP fine.
- **Art. 22 مكررا** ✅: a registration request is **not accepted** unless complete under Art. 22 and accompanied by
  the **draft of the instrument to be registered**; the regulations set the completion procedure and deadlines.
- **Art. 33** ✅: multiple requests on one property are handled **in the order of recording** in the requests
  register; no later request proceeds before the earlier one is decided — a *pars pro toto* protection for the
  first-in-time buyer ⭐.

## 3.3 The checklist a good purchase contract answers

Practitioner-grade (non-statutory) checklist — no article numbers; each item exists to survive one of the rules
above:

1. **Parties & authority:** full identity data; if by agent, the **POA expressly covering sale** (and by whom —
   §2.2), with the POA's registration details; for heirs, the إشهاد and all-heirs posture (§2.2, §4.4).
2. **Property:** description that satisfies Art. 22 — area, boundaries, the **official map/coordinates** data, and
   whether common areas are included.
3. **Price & payment:** total, installments and dates, **and the payment instrument** (bank transfer to the
   seller's own account — evidence discipline, cross-ref `04` §7.2 for cheques).
4. **Delivery:** date, state, fixtures.
5. **Registration covenant:** who bears the registration steps, costs, and the **deadline**; a date after which
   delay itself is breach ⭐ (this clause is what converts Art. 35 مكرر leverage into a case).
6. **Encumbrances declaration:** consistency with the Art. 22(4) declaration.
7. **Utilities:** recognition that transfer of meters requires the registered deed (Art. 35 مكرر) — so the
   registration covenant and delivery are coordinated.
8. **Dispute & termination branches:** what happens on default — registration suit vs rescission+damages (§1.4).

# 4. ⭐ Registration — procedure, fees, timelines (Law 9/2022 in force 7 May 2022)

`As of: 2026-10-28`

## 4.1 What the 2022 reform changed — the honest list

Law 9/2022 (Gazette No. 9 مكرر (أ), 6 March 2022; **in force from 7 May 2022** — Art. 5 sets operation from the day
after 60 days from publication; the Minister of Justice launched the amended system on 7 May 2022 per Ahram Gate
and youm7) did the following, each from the law's own text ✅ (alberonsy full text):

- **Electronic applications** become possible (Art. 21: applications on the Ministry-of-Justice form to the
  مأمورية of the property's locality, "as may be submitted electronically per the executive regulations").
- **Possession becomes registrable** (new Art. 10 مكررا, §4.3).
- **The ≤500 EGP committee route** for applications not resting on registrable instruments (Art. 23 مكررا, §4.3).
- **Art. 35 مكرر's judgment fast-track** had already been created in 2020 (Law 186/2020, §1.3).
- **Reported reforms ⚠️** (official statements and practitioner coverage, not independent statutory text): abolition
  of the ownership-chain requirement (تسلسل الملكية) as a registration condition; severing registration from proof
  of payment of the 2.5% disposition tax; registration decision ceiling of **37 days**; ownership application
  recorded in **3 minutes** (Ahram Gate 12 May 2022; youm7 7 May 2022; sadanykhalifa.com).
- **Repealed** by Art. 3 of Law 9/2022: Arts. 9(5), 24, 26, 29, 34 of Law 114/1946 — mechanical, listed for
  completeness so no agent resurrects them.

## 4.2 The ordinary route — steps and statutory clocks

From the law's text ✅:

1. **Apply** to the مأمورية of the property's locality (Art. 21), on the MOJ form, electronic possible.
2. **Completeness gate** (Art. 22 مكررا): Art. 22 data + draft instrument; otherwise no قيد.
3. **Review & eligibility endorsement** (Art. 28): the مأمورية reviews and endorses eligibility for registration
   (صلاحية للشهر); for عرفي instruments, **notarisation/certification happens in the dedicated books** at the
   مأمورية; file to the مكتب **next day at most**; registration formalities completed within **7 days** of arrival.
4. **Priority** (Art. 33): requests on one property proceed strictly by order of recording; no later request
   proceeds before the earlier is decided.
5. **Objection/preliminary-number lane** (Art. 35): where the مأمورية sees cause to withhold or reject (استيفاء
   بيان / رفض), the applicant may re-present within **10 days**, obtaining a **preliminary number** after fee
   payment and (for عرفي instruments) certification, posting **security of 0.5% of the obligation value capped at
   1,000 EGP** (refunded if the number stands); **قاضى الأمور الوقتية** of the court of first instance decides,
   reasoned, **within 7 days**, keeping or cancelling the number — decision final.
6. **Foreign/Sinai gates:** where the application requires review by **مكتب تملك غير المصريين** (foreign-ownership
   office) or **الجهاز الوطنى لتنمية شبه جزيرة سيناء**, no preliminary number issues before that approval ⚠️
   (mnasserlaw procedural description — practitioner secondary; treat the existence of the gates as verified via
   the Sinai circular §6.4 and the GAFI foreign-ownership framework §6.1, the office's internal mechanics 🟡).

## 4.3 ⭐⭐ The possession route (Arts. 10 مكررا + 23 مكررا) — the reform's centrepiece

**Art. 10 مكررا (new, Law 9/2022)** ✅:

> «يجوز أن تشهر الوقائع التى من شأنها إنشاء حق من الحقوق العينية العقارية الأصلية أو نقله أو تغييره أو زواله أو
> تقريره بطريق التسجيل، ويعد من هذه الوقائع فى تطبيق أحكام هذه المادة الحيازة المكسبة للملكية وفقًا لأحكام المادتين
> (968 و969) من القانون المدنى أو الحيازة المصحوبة بسند، ولو كان عرفيًا، لمدة خمس سنوات تبدأ من تاريخ نشوء الحق إذا
> كانت بحسن نية حتى التسجيل. ويترتب على عدم التسجيل عدم الإحتجاج بالحقوق المشار إليها قبل الغير.»

Three registrable fact-families: (a) **acquisitive possession** under Civil Code Arts. 968/969; (b) **possession
supported by a deed — even an عرفي one — for five years** from the right's creation, **good faith until
registration**; (c) the general "facts creating/transferring/… original real rights" formula. Non-registration →
the right is **not opposable to third parties**.

**Art. 23 مكررا (new)** ✅ — the procedure when the application rests on those facts (or on original ownership not
resting on an Art. 23 registrable instrument):

- The مأمورية investigates whether the facts' conditions are met and refers the application, with her opinion,
  to the مكتب within **30 days** of filing.
- A **three-member committee** at the مكتب (أمين المكتب presiding + the two most senior assistant secretaries or
  legal members) examines the application and any objections and issues a **reasoned decision within 7 days** of
  receiving the file.
- **Fee: "رسم محدد لا يزيد على خمسمائة جنيه" plus publication and travel expenses** — the executive regulations
  set the fee tiers (فئات هذا الرسم).
- **Excluded:** properties under **Civil Code Art. 970** and **أراضى فضاء**; nothing prejudices the parties' right
  to litigate the substance of the right.

**The Civil Code anchors, verified** (lawer-eg full-text copy; treat as statute text with source URL):

- **Art. 968:** possession by a non-owner of a thing (movable or immovable) or of a real right over a thing →
  acquisition of ownership/right if possession continues **uninterrupted for fifteen years**.
- **Art. 969:** where possession over an immovable/real right is **good-faith and based on a valid cause (سبب
  صحيح)**, the acquisitive period is **five years**; good faith is required only at the moment of receipt; a valid
  cause is a deed issued by a non-owner, **and it must be registered according to law** (as the Code stands).
- **Art. 970:** inheritance rights are only acquired by prescription after **33 years**; **state-owned and
  public-juristic-person property, economic-unit property, public-sector bodies' property, and charitable waqf
  cannot be owned by prescription nor can any real right over them be acquired by prescription**; ministerial
  removal of encroachment.
- ⭐ **How the pieces fit:** the Civil Code's own five-year prescription requires a *registered* deed (Art. 969(3));
  **Law 9/2022 Art. 10 مكررا is the legislative bridge that lets an عرفي deed + five years' good-faith possession
  reach registration** through the administrative committee route — no lawsuit required. This is the single most
  consequential buyer-side reform in the file.
- **The Minister's launch statement** (Ahram Gate, 12 May 2022) matches: any holder who **possessed for five
  years** with proof of residence (عرفي contract + a gas/electricity/water bill) is registered "immediately"; and
  for **heirs**, possession since **fifteen years** triggers transfer even without supporting documents — the
  press-level gloss on Arts. 10 مكررا/23 مكررا and the Art. 968 line. Cite the statute for the rule and the press
  statement only for its launch.

## 4.4 Heirs — registering the estate before or while selling (Arts. 48–50)

- **Art. 48** ✅: the request to register a **right of inheritance** goes to the مأمورية of the property's
  locality, signed by the heir-applicant (or his representative/interested party), containing the deceased's and
  heirs' data plus the Art. 22 items (2)–(4).
- **Art. 49** ✅: attach (1) **الإشهاد الشرعي / the judgment / other documents proving the inheritance right**;
  (2) **the deceased's title deed** — applying Art. 23's rules; **if the deceased's deed cannot be produced, the
  Art. 23 مكررا route applies** (the investigation-committee lane).
- **Art. 50** ✅: Arts. 25 and 27 of Law 114/1946 apply to the request (fees/publication mechanics — instrument
  verified; contents of those two articles not re-read here ⚠️).
- ⭐ For a **buyer of inherited property**: the seller-heirs must either show a registered estate right or walk the
  23 مكررا lane; "the other heirs will sign later" is where these deals die. Buying from **one** heir requires
  clarity on the others' positions (co-ownership) — handled in family/estate practice (cross-ref `07`).

# 5. ⭐ The money: the 2.5% transfer tax and the annual property tax

`As of: 2026-10-28`

## 5.1 Art. 43 of the income-tax law (Law 91/2005) — current text per Law 151/2026

**Law 151/2026** ("بتعديل بعض أحكام قانون الضريبة على الدخل الصادر بالقانون رقم 91 لسنة 2005"; Gazette No. 30 مكرر
(أ), **28 July 2026**; Art. 7: in force from the day after publication → **29 July 2026**) replaced Art. 43 in full.
The operative text (ETA official PDF, read in full):

- **Tax:** 2.5%, **no reduction**, on the **total value of the disposition** in built properties or land for
  building, **except villages (عدا القرى)**.
- **Scope, regardless of:** number of dispositions; disposition as-is or after erecting structures; whole property,
  part, or a residential unit; structures on land owned by the taxpayer or by another; **whether the disposition
  contracts are registered (مشهرة) or unregistered (غير مشهرة)** ⭐⭐.
- **Heirs ⭐:** the paragraph applies to **an heir's disposition of inherited** built properties/lands (as-is or
  after building for his private use). (The *inheritance itself* is not the taxed event — the heir's *later
  disposal* is.)
- **Not "احتراف":** these dispositions are not deemed a professional activity unless the authority proves
  commercial intent under Art. 19(7).
- **Value:** the total value is what the disposition contract states; the authority bears the burden of proof to
  depart from it.
- **Exclusion ⭐:** contributing the property as **in-kind shares** in a joint-stock company, provided the
  corresponding shares are not disposed of for **five years**.
- **Payment:** within **60 days** of the disposition date; delay damages per Art. 110 from the day after the term.
- **Deemed dispositions:** sale; **will (وصية); donation (تبرع) or هبة to anyone other than ascendants, spouse, or
  descendants**; creating a **حق انتفاع** on the property; **leasing it for more than fifty years**.
- **Not subject:** **compulsory sales (البيع الجبري), administrative or judicial**; expropriation for public
  benefit or zoning (التحسين); donations/hiba to the Government, local units, public legal persons, or
  public-benefit projects.
- **Credit:** tax paid under this article is **deductible** from the total taxes due where Art. 19(7) applies.

## 5.2 What Law 151/2026 did NOT do — and what it did elsewhere

- **No gains-based capital-gains tax on real estate exists** under Art. 43: it is a flat tax on **gross disposition
  value**, indifferent to gain or loss. Any agent output describing "capital gains tax on selling your flat at X%"
  is wrong as of this file's date; the correct statement is **2.5% of gross value**.
- The **capital-gains provisions Law 151/2026 added/replaced concern shares and securities**, not property:
  **Art. 46 مكررا (3)** imposes tax on gains from disposing of unlisted shares/holdings (payable within 60 days);
  **Art. 46 مكررا (4)** defines those gains (disposal price minus acquisition cost after brokerage commission; an
  inflation-credit equal to the CBE credit rate per year held, for holdings ≥3 years); and the replaced **Art.
  50/7** brings **listed-securities capital gains** into charge with no loss-offset, while the transitional Arts.
  (4–5) handle the 2023-era exemption revocation for listed shares. ⚠️ Securities detail is **outside this file's
  scope** — cite only the shares-vs-property distinction, never the securities rates, from here.
- **Origin ⚠️:** Art. 43 existed before 2026 (the law *replaced* its text), so the 2.5% predates 2026; the
  originating amendment is not verified (Corrections #11).

## 5.3 Registration is severed from the tax ⭐

Since Law 9/2022, the registry **no longer requires proof of 2.5% tax payment as a condition of registration**
(§2.4, §4.1). The tax remains owed **to the Tax Authority on its own 60-day clock** (§5.1). Practical statement for
users: **"registering and paying the tax are two separate obligations with two separate offices."**

## 5.4 Who pays — the statute's word and practice

- The statutory text obliges **"المتصرف"** (the disposer) to pay within 60 days ✅ (Art. 43).
- 🟡 **UNVERIFIED — needs primary-source check:** the tax administration's administrative allocation between seller
  and buyer in ordinary private sales (practice reportedly varies; contracts commonly allocate it to the buyer by
  agreement). State the statutory anchor ("المتصرف" — the disposer, i.e. the seller side), note that the parties
  may allocate by contract, and mark the authority's practical practice as unverified. Do not tell a user "the
  buyer always pays by law."
- Note the family carve-out ⭐: تبرع/هبة **between ascendants/spouse/descendants is not a deemed disposition** — but
  a **donation to anyone else IS taxed** at 2.5% of the property's value (as the deemed-disposition clause) — so
  informal "transfers" to relatives outside the exempt circle are taxable events.

## 5.5 The annual property tax (Law 196/2008) — current after Law 3/2026

**Law 3/2026** (amending Law 196/2008; signed 14 Shawwal 1447 = **2 April 2026**; in force from the day after
publication) — full text via the state printing house (alamiria.com) ✅:

- **Rate & base (unchanged by 3/2026, verified on rta.gov.eg — official):** tax = **10%** of the net annual rental
  value, after deducting **30%** (residential use) or **32%** (non-residential) from the annual rental value.
  rta's worked example: rental value 36,000 → net 25,200 (×70%) → minus exemption → ×10%. ⚠️ **rta's example page
  still shows the old 24,000 exemption — the page is stale on that figure** (Corrections #5).
- **Art. 18/1(d) (replaced) ⭐⭐:** the exemption covers **the taxpayer's main family residence unit (taxpayer +
  spouse + minor children) where net annual rental value is below 100,000 EGP**; the excess is taxed; the Cabinet
  may raise the threshold. (Draft-stage reporting in May 2025 said 50,000 — the enacted text says **100,000**.)
- **Art. 4/1 (replaced):** rental value assessed for **five-year cycles**; re-assessment must begin within the last
  1–3 years of the cycle; the service must publish the **indicative price map ≥90 days** before the new valuation
  applies.
- **Art. 14 (replaced):** quinquennial declarations (in the second half of the year before the cycle) + annual
  declarations (by **end of December**) for new properties, additions, changed use, or lifted exemptions; electronic
  or paper; **hotel managers and owners' associations must report** owner data; **utilities and government bodies
  must supply data** to the service.
- **Art. 14 مكررا (new) ⭐:** on-time, complete filers get an annual discount of **25% (residential)** or **10%
  (non-residential)** off the tax; the Minister may add an up-to-**5%** prompt-payment discount scheme.
- **Art. 16 (replaced):** objection to assessment within **60 days** of notification, with a **50 EGP** deposit
  (refunded if the objection succeeds).
- **Art. 17/4 (replaced):** the committee decides within **30 days**; decision final; **collection is not suspended**
  by court challenge.
- **Transitional amnesty ⭐ (Arts. 3–4):** late-payment surcharges waived for compliant taxpayers (or those paying
  within six months of the law's operation, extendable once); and **all past tax is waived on properties never
  previously assessed/notified**, provided the owner files a declaration within **one year** of the law's operation.
- **Art. 5:** pending property-tax disputes may be settled by paying **70%** of the disputed tax; that extinguishes
  the liability and ends the case.
- **Art. 29 مكررا (new):** debt write-off cases (deceased leaving no visible estate, no attachable assets, closed
  bankruptcy, ten-year absence).

**What a buyer must know ⭐:** (1) the tax rides with the property — an "exempt" flat stays exempt only while it is
**one unit, main family residence, under the threshold**; a second flat is taxed; (2) the **25% on-time discount**
(§ above) makes timely filing cheap and late filing expensive; (3) the exemption threshold is on **net rental
value**, not market price — rent-model numbers, not sale price.

# 6. Foreign buyers and restricted land

`As of: 2026-10-28`

## 6.1 Non-Egyptians — Law 230/1996 (residential)

Verified from the **GAFI official compilation** ("Land and Real Estate Ownership Laws" PDF, gafi.gov.eg) and
**Andersen Egypt's English translation** (eg.andersen.com, Law No. 230 of 1996 PDF) ✅. Law 230/1996 superseded Law
56/1988 (14 July 1996). The conditions:

1. Ownership limited to **two real-estate properties** in Egypt serving as accommodation for the owner **and his
   family (spouses and minors)**, **in addition to** the right to own real estate needed for activities licensed by
   the Egyptian government.
2. **Each property ≤4,000 m².**
3. The property is **not a historical site (اثرية)**.
4. **Vacant property must be built upon within five years** from registration of ownership.
5. The non-Egyptian **may sell only after five years from registration**, unless with the **Prime Minister's
   consent**.
- Exemptions from conditions (1)–(2) require **Prime Minister approval**; ownership in **tourist areas and new
  communities** is subject to **Cabinet-set conditions**.
- **Companies:** the non-Egyptian ownership restrictions do not apply to **companies with Egyptian-majority
  capital** (GAFI overview; the law's corporate carve-out ⚠️ — the GAFI summary states the carve-out; read the
  article text before quoting its number).
- **Inheritance is excluded from the law's scope** (GAFI/Andersen) — an inherited property does not count against
  the two-property limit via this law; ⚠️ inheritance mechanics live in `07`.
- 🟡 **Desert-land and agricultural overlays for companies** interact with §6.2–§6.3 — the overlays govern, not
  Law 230/1996.

## 6.2 Agricultural land — the absolute ban (Law 15/1963)

- **Law 15/1963 Art. 1** ✅ (lawyeregypt full text): «يحظر على الأجانب سواء أكانوا أشخاصاً طبيعيين أم اعتباريين تملك
  الأراضى الزراعية وما فى حكمها من الأراضى القابلة للزراعة والبور والصحراوية فى الجمهورية...» — a ban on foreigners,
  natural or legal, owning agricultural land and the like (arable, barr, and desert therein).
- Amended by **Law 104/1985** (Gazette 4 July 1985 — alberonsy) ⚠️ (amendment verified as instrument; its operative
  text not re-read here).
- **Still standing:** the Supreme Administrative Court applied the law (طعن 4011/33, 24 April 1990, mksegypt.org) ✅;
  the Supreme Constitutional Court rejected constitutional challenges (reported case page, ahmed-elhattab) ✅; and a
  Council of State General Assembly position reported in 2026 states Law 15/1963 **still** bans foreign ownership of
  agricultural land ⚠️ (the assembly position surfaces via a press/social carry — cite the instruments, not the
  social post).
- ⭐ For the agent: **agricultural land is simply out of reach for a foreign buyer**; a "company workaround" must
  survive this law and the desert-land regime, and neither is waived by a notary's enthusiasm.

## 6.3 Desert lands (Law 143/1981 — NOT the phantom "143/2017")

- Law 143/**1981** governs desert-land allocation/ownership; amended by **Law 11/2024** (24 February 2024), which
  replaced **Arts. 11(2) and 12(2)**: exceptions for investors under **Investment Law 72/2017**; for Egyptian-owned

## 6.4 ⭐⭐ Sinai — the regime where contracts die

- **Framework:** Decree-Law **14/2012** on integrated development of the Sinai Peninsula (manshurat full text;
  lawhub.info/eg/?p=12824; SDA official site) ✅; amended by Decree-Law **95/2015** (Ahram full text) ✅ and **Law
  172/2020** (manshurat relations page) ⚠️.
- **Presidential Decree 128/2022:** excepted **Sharm El-Sheikh, Dahab, and the Aqaba-Gulf tourism sector** (South
  Sinai) from the Sinai-development law's provisions.
- **منشور فني 33/2022 (Real-Estate Registry technical circular)** ✅ (alraeesnews full-text report): disposing of
  Sinai lands/properties/units **requires the approvals of the Ministries of Defence and Interior and the General
  Intelligence**; **any contract concluded in violation "يقع باطلاً مطلقاً" — is void absolutely**, whether the
  parties are Egyptian or foreign ⭐⭐; for pre-law واضعو اليد claims, the **National Authority for Sinai
  Development** plus those same approvals.
- **GLA & Co analysis (2023) ⚠️ (firm secondary):** under the Sinai law, land/asset ownership is generally
  restricted to Egyptians **with Egyptian parents**; non-Egyptians and dual nationals operate via **usufruct ≤75
  years** (including renewals) with SDA-board + three-ministry approvals. The "Egyptian parents" refinement is the
  firm's reading — treat as guidance pending the article text (🟡 for that specific condition).
- **Agent rule ⭐:** for any Sinai transaction, the first question is the **approval posture** (Decree 128/2022 zone
  vs the rest of Sinai). A "bargain" priced without approvals is a void contract with money spent — say it before
  the user pays.

## 6.5 The foreign-ownership gate at the registry

The registry applies the **مكتب تملك غير المصريين** review for non-Egyptian purchasers (and the Sinai authority
gate for Sinai properties) before issuing preliminary numbers (§4.2 step 6; mnasserlaw procedural description).
⭐ A foreign buyer's file should be built **for that gate from day one** — approvals before money.

# 7. Defects, construction, and what the law actually guarantees

`As of: 2026-10-28`

## 7.1 Hidden defects — Civil Code Arts. 449 and 452 (via Cassation 4503/61 ق, 11 January 1998)

The Cassation text quotes the articles ✅ (ahmedazimelgamel full-text reproduction of the judgment):

- **Art. 449(1):** «إذا تسلم المشترى المبيع وجب عليه التحقق من حالته بمجرد أن يتمكن من ذلك وفقاً للمألوف فى التعامل
  فإذا كشف عيباً يضمنه البائع وجب عليه أن يخطره به خلال مدة معقولة فإن لم يفعل اعتبر قابلاً للمبيع» — on delivery,
  the buyer must inspect as soon as he can, in the usual course; on discovering a defect he must **notify the
  seller within a reasonable period**, else the thing is deemed accepted (قابلاً للمبيع).
- **Art. 452:** the warranty claim lapses **one year from delivery**, even if the defect was discovered later,
  unless the seller undertook a longer warranty — **except** where the seller **deliberately concealed the defect
  (تعمد إخفاء العيب غشاً)**: then the seller cannot plead the one-year bar ⭐.
- Cassation 4503/61 ق holds these as rules of result (claim extinguished by silence or by the year, absent غش).
- **Agent rule ⭐:** photograph/inventory at delivery; **notify in writing within days**, not months; the one-year
  clock is short and unforgiving.

## 7.2 ⭐ Art. 651 — the ten-year structural guarantee (architect + contractor, jointly)

- **Art. 651(1)** (quoted in Cassation 4503/61 ق) ✅: «يضمن المهندس المعمارى والمقاول متضامنين ما يحدث خلال عشر
  سنوات من تهدم كلى أو جزئى فيما شيداه من مبانٍ أو أقاماه من منشآت ثابتة أخرى...» — the **architect and the
  contractor are jointly liable (متضامنين)** for **total or partial collapse** occurring within **ten years** of
  what they built.
- **Art. 651(2):** the guarantee extends to defects in the structure **threatening its solidity and safety**.
- **Art. 651(3):** the ten years run **from delivery of the work**.
- Cassation 4503/61 ق characterises the obligation as an **obligation of result** (the building remaining sound and
  structurally safe for ten years after delivery) and holds that an employer is not liable for the contractor's act
  nor jointly absent supervision/employee relationship — **solidarity is not presumed**.
- ⚠️ Reaching a **developer company** (as distinct from its engineer/contractor) for collapse damage requires the
  corporate route (liability of the company for its obligations, attribution of the building works) — do not
  compress "the developer guarantees ten years" as if Art. 651 said that; say what the article says and flag the
  developer-attribution step.
- **Practical translation for a flat buyer ⭐:** cracks/collapse within ten years of the building's delivery →
  claim against **the engineer and the contractor jointly** (and, in developer deals, against the developer as the
  contracting employer — flag, don't assert). Keep proof of the delivery date; it starts the clock.

## 7.3 What the buyer does with a defect — the action map

1. **Hidden defect at delivery window** → Art. 449/452 warranty route (rescission or price reduction per general
   sale-warranty mechanics — the *forms* of the warranty remedy live in the sale chapter; the deadlines here are
   the verified part).
2. **Structural/collapse issue** → Art. 651 route against engineer/contractor (ten years from delivery).
3. **Both clocks are independent** ⭐ — a buyer can be out of time on Art. 452 and safely inside Art. 651.

# 8. ⚠️ What wrong AI outputs say — the myth table

`As of: 2026-10-28`

| Myth | The verified position |
|---|---|
| "You signed the contract, the flat is yours" | Ownership passes **by registration** (Law 114/1946 Arts. 9/10; Cassation 680/64 ق, 3037/59 ق) — §1 |
| "وعد البيع gives me ownership" | It is a **تعهد بفعل**; enforcement = suit → final judgment → register it (§1.4) |
| "The عرفي contract is enough — nobody registers in Egypt" | The State/utilities must refuse unregistered buyers (**Art. 35 مكرر**); possession route exists but has conditions (5 years, good faith, deed; excluded: state property & أراضى فضاء) — §1.3, §4.3 |
| "Registration costs 500 EGP" | ≤500 EGP is the **special-route** cap; general residential tiers are 500–2,000 EGP, max 3,900 (official statements) — §4.5 |
| "Registration takes years" | The reform's stated ceiling is **37 days** (official statements, 2022) — §4.5 |
| "Selling a flat is tax-free" | **2.5% on gross value**, applies to **unregistered contracts and heirs' disposals** (Art. 43 as replaced by Law 151/2026, in force 29 July 2026) — §5.1 |
| "There's a capital gains tax on property now" | No — Law 151/2026's gains provisions concern **shares/securities**; property is the flat 2.5% — §5.2 |
| "Registration fee includes the 2.5% tax" | They are **separate obligations to separate authorities** since Law 9/2022 — §5.3 |
| "Property-tax exemption is 24,000 (or 50,000) EGP" | Enacted: **100,000 EGP** net annual rental value, main residence only (Law 3/2026 Art. 18/1(d)) — §5.5 |
| "Any POA lets me sell/buy for the owner" | Sale power must be **express**; a POA empowering sale to self/others with completion intent ranks as a sale contract (Cassation 7910/91 ق) — §2.2 |
| "Heirs can sign and sell freely" | Estate registration (Arts. 48–49) or the 23 مكررا lane comes first; one heir ≠ all heirs — §2.2, §4.4 |
| "Foreigners can't own property in Egypt" | Law 230/1996 permits 2 family-residence properties ≤4,000 m² under conditions; real bars: agricultural land (15/1963), desert regime (143/1981+11/2024), Sinai regime — §6 |
| "That Sinai deal is fine, it's just paperwork" | Disposal without sovereign approvals → **contract void absolutely** (منشور فني 33/2022 under Decree 128/2022) — §6.4 |
| "Ten-year warranty = the developer pays" | Art. 651 binds **architect & contractor jointly** for collapse within 10 years of delivery; developer attribution is a further step — §7.2 |
| "Defects? sue anytime" | Art. 449 notice duty + **Art. 452 one-year bar** (absent seller's غش) — §7.1 |
