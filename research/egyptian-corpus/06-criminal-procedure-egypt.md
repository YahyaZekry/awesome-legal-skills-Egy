# Egyptian Criminal Procedure — Verified Reference Corpus
# قانون الإجراءات الجنائية المصري — المرجع التشريعي

**Purpose:** a citation-safe reference for an AI agent handling Egyptian criminal procedure (الإجراءات الجنائية)
— arrest, custody, the Public Prosecution, pretrial detention, bail, charge classification, limitation periods,
trial, appeal and cassation, the victim's civil claim, and confessions.
Every article number below was read out of a retrieved primary or official-secondary text. Nothing is asserted from memory.

**As-of date for the whole file: 2026-10-19.**
⚠️ Each section carries its own `As of:` line — that is the date *that section* was last read against a source.

**The single most important fact in this file:** the operative Criminal Procedure Code in Egypt **today is still
Law No. 150 of 1950**. The replacement code — **Law No. 174 of 2025** — was promulgated on 12 November 2025 but its
entry into force was **postponed by Law No. 160 of 2026 to 1 October 2027**. It has **never been applied to any
procedure**. See §17. Any statement that "the new criminal procedure code is in force" is wrong.

Companion files: `03-employment-courts-aml-banking.md` carries the court hierarchy and the fee structure;
`05-tenancy-egypt.md` carries the Civil Code limitation rules (Arts. 374–387) which govern the **civil** limb of a
criminal case; `04-practical-operations-egypt.md` carries the practical filing material.

# 1. The constitutional frame

**Citation:** دستور جمهورية مصر العربية الصادر ٢٠١٤، المعدل بتعديلات ٢٠١٩.
**Status:** in force. The text below is the **post-2019 consolidated** text — confirmed as such because Art. 154
carries the 2019 rule (three-month maximum, renewal by two-thirds) rather than the 2014 wording.
**Source:** `https://www.moss.gov.eg/media/jxearltm/دستور-2014.pdf` — the **official Arabic PDF published by the
Ministry of State for Information/Government**, 760 KB, downloaded and extracted locally. ⚠️ The PDF's article
headers use a **legacy symbol font** that does not extract as Unicode; I decoded the digit map (Î=1 Ï=2 Ð=3 Ñ=4 Ò=5
Ó=6 Ô=7 Õ=8 Ö=9 Í=0) and confirmed the decode by checking that the sequence runs **1 → 247 with one gap only** (the
headers for Arts. 228–233 are lost in the extract) and that the last header is **Art. 247**, the constitution's final
article. Articles were then read directly.
**As of:** 2026-10-19.

These are the provisions a non-lawyer needs. All quoted verbatim from the PDF above.

## 1.1 Art. 54 — personal freedom, the 24-hour rule and the lawyer (⭐ the article to quote)

> «**الحرية الشخصية حق طبيعى، وهى مصونة لا تُمس، وفيما عدا حالة التلبس، لا يجوز القبض على أحد، أو تفتيشه،
> أو حبسه، أو تقييد حريته بأى قيد إلا بأمر قضائى مُسبَّب يستلزمه التحقيق.**
> ويجب أن يُبلغ فورًا كل من تقيد حريته بأسباب ذلك، ويحاط بحقوقه كتابة، ويُمكن من الاتصال بذويه وبمحاميه فورًا،
> وأن يُقدّم إلى سلطة التحقيق **خلال أربع وعشرين ساعة** من وقت تقييد حريته.
> ولا يبدأ التحقيق معه إلا فى حضور محاميه، فإن لم يكن له محام، **نُدب له محام**، مع توفير المساعدة اللازمة
> لذوى الإعاقة، وفقًا للإجراءات المقررة فى القانون.
> **ولكل من تُقيَّد حريته، ولغيره، حق التظلم أمام القضاء من ذلك الإجراء، والفصل فيه خلال أسبوع من ذلك الإجراء،
> وإلا وجب الإفراج عنه فورًا.**
> وينظم القانون أحكام **الحبس الاحتياطى، ومدته، وأسبابه، وحالات استحقاق التعويض** الذى تلتزم الدولة بأدائه عن
> الحبس الاحتياطى، أو عن تنفيذ عقوبة صدر حكم بات بإلغاء الحكم المُنفَّذة بموجبه.
> وفى جميع الأحوال **لا يجوز محاكمة المتهم فى الجرائم التى يجوز الحبس فيها إلا بحضور محام مُوكَّل أو مُنتدب**.»

Nine operative consequences, in order:

1. **The default rule is a reasoned judicial order** («أمر قضائى مُسبَّب يستلزمه التحقيق»). Arrest, search,
   imprisonment or any restriction of liberty without one is unconstitutional — **except** in flagrante delicto
   (**حالة التلبس**).
2. **Immediate notification of reasons** to the person whose liberty is restricted.
3. **Rights must be given to him IN WRITING** (كتابة).
4. **Immediate contact with family AND with a lawyer** (بذويه وبمحاميه فورًا).
5. **Production before an investigation authority within 24 HOURS** of the restriction of liberty. This is the
   constitutional 24-hour clock — it is the number a client asks about, and it is not a police-station discretion.
6. **No interrogation except in the presence of his lawyer.**
7. **If he has no lawyer, one is APPOINTED for him (نُدب له محام)** — and reasonable accommodation must be provided
   for persons with disabilities.
8. **A right of complaint (تظلم) to a court — for the detainee AND for anyone else** — against the measure, and a
   **ruling within one week**, failing which **release immediately**. Note the second limb: *a person who is not the
   detainee* can bring this.
9. **Trial in any imprisonable offence requires an officially instructed or appointed lawyer present** — a trial
   without one is a constitutional defect.
10. And the fourth paragraph expressly puts **compensation for wrongful pretrial detention** — and for serving a
    sentence that a final judgment later annulled — **into the hands of statute**.

## 1.2 Art. 55 — dignity, the right to silence, and void statements (⭐ the confession article)

> «كل من يُقبض عليه، أو يُحبس، أو تقيد حريته تجب معاملته بما يحفظ عليه كرامته، **ولا يجوز تعذيبه، ولا ترهيبه،
> ولا إكراهه، ولا إيذاؤه بدنيًا أو معنويًا**، ولا يكون حجزه، أو حبسه إلا فى أماكن مخصصة لذلك لائقة إنسانيًا
> وصحيًا، وتلتزم الدولة بتوفير وسائل الإتاحة للأشخاص ذوى الإعاقة.
> ومخالفة شىء من ذلك **جريمة يُعاقَب مرتكبها وفقًا للقانون.**
> **وللمتهم حق الصمت.** وكل قول يثبت أنه صدر من محتجز تحت وطأة شىء مما تقدم، **أو التهديد بشىء منه،
> يهدر ولا يُعوَّل عليه**.»

- **Torture (تعذيب), intimidation (ترهيب), coercion (إكراه), physical OR moral harm** — all prohibited, absolutely,
  for anyone arrested, imprisoned or restricted.
- **Detention only in designated places** that are humanely and hygienically fit.
- **A constitutional right to silence** («وللمتهم حق الصمت»).
- **Any statement proved to have been made by a detainee under any of the above, or under the threat of any of the
  above, is VOID and cannot be relied upon** («يهدر ولا يُعوَّل عليه»). Note the trigger: **proof that the statement
  came out of the coercive situation** — it is not enough that the person was in custody. That is the evidential
  battleground, and it is why an arrest's surrounding facts matter.

## 1.3 Art. 52 — torture is not subject to prescription (a four-word article)

> «**التعذيب بجميع صوره وأشكاله، جريمة لا تسقط بالتقادم.**»

✅ Read verbatim at line 454 of the extracted PDF, immediately after Art. 51 (dignity: الكرامة حق لكل إنسان…).
**This is why an old torture complaint is never "too late".**

## 1.4 The rest of the frame, in one line each

- ✅ **Art. 51:** «الكرامة حق لكل إنسان، ولا يجوز المساس بها، وتلتزم الدولة باحترامها وحمايتها.» Dignity is a right
  of every human being, may not be infringed, and the state must respect and protect it.
- ✅ **Art. 56:** «السجن **دار إصلاح وتأهيل**. **تخضع السجون وأماكن الاحتجاز للإشراف القضائى**، ويُحظر فيها كل ما
  ينافى كرامة الإنسان، أو يعرض صحته للخطر.» Prisons are **reform and rehabilitation houses**; prisons and places of
  detention are **subject to JUDICIAL supervision**; anything incompatible with human dignity or endangering health
  is forbidden.
- ✅ **Art. 57:** private life is inviolable; postal, telegraphic, **electronic** correspondence and **telephone
  conversations** have a sanctity that may only be infringed by a **reasoned judicial order**, for a defined period.
  *(This is the constitutional basis of CrPC Arts. 206 and, in the new code, 117.)*
- ✅ **Art. 95:** «**العقوبة شخصية، ولا جريمة ولا عقوبة إلا بناء على قانون، ولا تُوقع عقوبة إلا بحكم قضائى،
  ولا عقاب إلا على الأفعال اللاحقة لتاريخ نفاذ القانون.**» Penalties are personal; no crime and no penalty except
  under a statute; no penalty except by **judicial judgment**; no punishment for acts preceding the law's entry into
  force.
- ✅ **Art. 96:** «**المتهم برئ حتى تثبت إدانته فى محاكمة قانونية عادلة**، تُكفل له فيها ضمانات الدفاع عن نفسه.
  **وينظم القانون استئناف الأحكام الصادرة فى الجنايات.** وتوفر الدولة الحماية للمجنى عليهم والشهود والمتهمين
  والمبلغين عند الاقتضاء، وفقًا للقانون.» Presumption of innocence; **the law regulates appeal of felony
  judgments** (this sentence is the constitutional hook for Law 1/2024 — see §11.2); and the state provides
  protection for **victims, witnesses, defendants and informants** as required. ⚠️ ⚠️ **PARTIAL on one point:** the
  felony-appeal sentence appears in the **current consolidated** text; I did **not** independently verify whether it
  was in the original 2014 text or was **added in 2019**. Do not date it without checking.
- ✅ **Art. 97:** «التقاضى حق مصون ومكفول للكافة… ولا يحاكم شخص إلا أمام **قاضيه الطبيعى**، و
  **المحاكم الاستثنائية محظورة**.» The right to litigate is guaranteed; no one is tried except before **his natural
  judge**; **exceptional courts are PROHIBITED**. ⚠️ This is the constitutional answer to "was he tried before a
  special court" — and it is the provision that made the emergency-court system constitutionally controversial.
- ✅ **Art. 98:** «**حق الدفاع أصالة أو بالوكالة مكفول.** واستقلال المحاماة وحماية حقوقها ضمان لكفالة حق الدفاع.
  ويضمن القانون لغير القادرين ماليًا وسائل الالتجاء إلى القضاء، والدفاع عن حقوقهم.» Defence rights guaranteed in
  person or by agent; the independence of the legal profession is a guarantee of them; and **the law guarantees
  access to the courts and to defence for those who cannot afford it**.
- ✅ **Art. 154 — the state of emergency:** «يعلن رئيس الجمهورية، بعد أخذ رأى مجلس الوزراء، **حالة الطوارئ**، على
  النحو الذى ينظمه القانون، ويجب عرض هذا الإعلان على مجلس النواب خلال الأيام السبعة التالية ليقرر ما يراه بشأنه…
  وفى جميع الأحوال **تجب موافقة أغلبية عدد أعضاء المجلس** على إعلان حالة الطوارئ، و**يكون إعلانها لمدة محددة لا
  تجاوز ثلاثة أشهر، ولا تُمد إلا لمدة أخرى مماثلة، بعد موافقة ثلثى عدد أعضاء المجلس**… **ولا يجوز حل مجلس النواب
  أثناء سريان حالة الطوارئ.**» ⚠️ **The article number is 154, not 152** — Art. 152 is the appointments article and
  Art. 153 does not exist as a separate heading in this sequence; I read the article immediately preceding and
  following to be sure.
- ✅ **Art. 204 — the military judiciary:** «القضاء العسكرى جهة قضائية مستقلة، يختص دون غيره بالفصل فى كافة
  الجرائم المتعلقة بالقوات المسلحة وضباطها وأفرادها ومن فى حكمهم، والجرائم المرتكبة من أفراد المخابرات العامة
  أثناء وبسبب الخدمة. **ولا يجوز محاكمة مدنى أمام القضاء العسكرى، إلا فى الجرائم التى تمثل اعتداءً مباشرًا على
  المنشآت العسكرية أو معسكرات القوات المسلحة أو ما فى حكمها، أو المناطق العسكرية أو الحدودية المقررة كذلك، أو
  معداتها أو مركباتها أو أسلحتها أو ذخائرها أو وثائقها أو أسرارها العسكرية أو أموالها العامة أو المصانع الحربية،
  أو الجرائم المتعلقة بالتجنيد، أو الجرائم التى تمثل اعتداءً مباشرًا على ضباطها أو أفرادها بسبب تأدية أعمال
  وظائفهم.** ويحدد القانون تلك الجرائم، ويبين اختصاصات القضاء العسكرى الأخرى. وأعضاء القضاء العسكرى مستقلون غير
  قابلين للعزل، وتكون لهم كافة الضمانات والحقوق والواجبات المقررة لأعضاء السلطة القضائية.» **A civilian may not
  be tried before the military judiciary except in the enumerated categories** — a closed list. *(This is the
  2019-amended wording.)*

# 2. The Criminal Procedure Code in force — Law No. 150 of 1950

**Citation:** قانون رقم ١٥٠ لسنة ١٩٥٠ بإصدار قانون الإجراءات الجنائية.
**Status:** **IN FORCE.** This is the code that governs every criminal procedure in Egypt as of 2026-10-19.
It has **513 articles**. Its repeal is already enacted (Law 174/2025 promulgating Art. 4) but **postponed to
1 October 2027** (Law 160/2026).
**Source:** `https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-150-1950` — the consolidated Arabic text, **513 articles**,
stating that it is transcribed from **بوابة تشريعات محكمة النقض** (the Court of Cassation legislation portal).
⚠️ **Why this source is acceptable in place of the MoJ encyclopedia:** it identifies the transcript's origin (the
Cassation portal), it reproduces the **amendment footnotes per article** with gazette numbers, and it carries the
chain to the **last amendment of 16 January 2024 (Law 1/2024)**. Downloaded as HTML, `html.unescape` + tag-strip in
Python, saved locally, then read article by article. Corroborated article-for-article against two further full
reproductions that also carry inline amendment footnotes with gazette references — `ghazylaw.com/criminal-procedure-law/`
(492 articles, footnote-first layout) and the **موثقي مصر** reproduction (annotated, with SCC annotations inline).

# 3. Police, arrest and custody before the Public Prosecution — Arts. 34–43

**Everything in this section is Law 150/1950 as consolidated to 16 January 2024. All ✅ unless marked.**

## 3.1 Who may arrest, and when

- ✅ **Art. 34** — the **police (مأمور الضبط القضائى) may arrest a PRESENT accused** where there is sufficient
  evidence, in **flagrante felonies** and in **flagrante misdemeanours punishable by imprisonment of more than
  three months**.
- ✅ **Art. 35** — if the accused is **absent**, an **order to seize and bring him (أمر بالضبط والإحضار)** is used.
  The same article provides for **preventive measures plus an immediate request for a prosecution detention order**
  in other cases with sufficient evidence — a **felony**, or a **misdemeanour of theft, fraud, grievous assault or
  armed resistance**.
- ✅ **Art. 37** — **anyone** (not only a police officer) who **witnesses an offender in flagrante** in a felony or a
  misdemeanour in which pretrial detention is lawful **may hand him to the nearest public-force member without an
  order**. *This is the citizen's-arrest article, and note it is limited by the "detention is lawful" condition.*
- ✅ **Art. 38** — the **public force** (رجال السلطة العامة) may bring a **flagrante misdemeanour** accused to the
  nearest judicial officer; and likewise in **other flagrante crimes if his identity cannot be established**.
- ✅ **Art. 39 — the complaint gate on arrest:** where the offence **requires a complaint (شكوى)**, **no arrest may
  be made without the complainant's declaration**. *Practical consequence: in a crime that needs a شكوى — e.g. some
  assault and defamation offences — an arrest before the victim declares a complaint is unlawful.*

## 3.2 The 24-hour rule — Art. 36 (⭐ quote this one verbatim)

> «**يجب على مأمور الضبط القضائي أن يسمع فوراً أقوال المتهم المضبوط، وإذا لم يأت بما يبرئه، يرسله في مدى أربعة
> وعشرين ساعة إلى النيابة العامة المختصة. ويجب على النيابة العامة أن تستجوبه في ظرف أربع وعشرين ساعة ثم تأمر
> بالقبض عليه أو إطلاق سراحه.**»

Read article by article: (1) the judicial officer must **hear the arrested person IMMEDIATELY** (فوراً); (2) if the
person does **not exculpate himself**, the officer must send him **within 24 hours** to the competent Public
Prosecution; (3) the **Public Prosecution must interrogate him within 24 hours**; (4) and it must then either
**order his arrest (i.e. detention) or release him**.

⚠️ **Two things this article does NOT say, which agents routinely add:** it does **not** give the arresting officer
24 hours to decide whether to send the person, and it does **not** authorise the officer to interrogate. The officer
**hears** («يسمع»); the **Prosecution interrogates** («تستجوبه»). The two 24-hour periods are **separate and
consecutive**, not one period.
⚠️ **And it does not displace Art. 54 of the Constitution** — the constitutional clock runs from «وقت تقييد حريته»
whichever period is being counted.

## 3.3 The conditions of custody — Arts. 40–43

- ✅ **Art. 40** — **no arrest or detention without an order of the legally competent authority**; and the person
  must be **treated humanely — no physical or moral harm**.
- ✅ **Art. 41** — **detention only in premises designated for it**; **only on a signed order**; and **no holding
  beyond the stated period**.
- ✅ **Art. 42** — **members of the Public Prosecution and the presidents and vice-presidents of courts may VISIT
  the prisons in their district**, verify that **no one is held unlawfully**, and **inspect the registers, orders
  and instruments of detention**.
- ✅ **Art. 43 — the release duty on anyone who learns of it:** any prisoner may submit a **written or oral
  complaint**, and **anyone who becomes aware that a person is being detained unlawfully must inform a member of
  the Public Prosecution, who must go IMMEDIATELY to the place and order his release.** *This is a duty on the
  prosecutor, not a discretion, and it is the provision to cite when a complaint to a police station is ignored.*
- ✅ **Art. 45** — **no entry into inhabited premises** except in cases the law allows, on a request for help from
  inside, or in fire, flood and the like.
- ✅ **Art. 47** — search of the **accused's home** in flagrante.
- ✅ **Art. 51** — searches are conducted **in the presence of the accused or his deputy**; failing that, **in the
  presence of two witnesses, preferably relatives, adult residents of the house, or neighbours**.
- ✅ **Art. 58** — **disclosure of seized material** is punished under Penal Code **Art. 310**.
- ✅ **Art. 113 bis** — **added by Law 177/2020** (Gazette No. 36 bis, 5 September 2020): the **identity of the
  victim** in the offences of **Book Four, Book Three of the Penal Code**, in **Arts. 306 bis (a) and 306 bis (b)**,
  and in **Art. 96 of the Child Law**, **may not be disclosed except to those concerned.** *This is the anonymity
  provision for victims of sexual and harassment offences and of child offences.*

# 4. The Public Prosecution, and report (محضر) vs complaint (بلاغ / شكوى)

## 4.1 Who owns the criminal action

- ✅ **Art. 1** — «**تتولى النيابة العامة الدعوى الجنائية، وتحريكها ومباشرتها. ولا يجوز لأحد غيرها أن يتخذ هذه
  الإجراءات إلا في الأحوال المحددة في القانون.**» The **Public Prosecution alone** owns the criminal action,
  initiates it and pursues it; nobody else may take those steps **except in the cases set out in the law**.
  *The exceptions are the complaint-gated offences (Art. 3), the request-gated offences (Art. 8), and the
  direct-accusation route (Art. 232) in §9.*
- ✅ **Art. 2** — the **Attorney-General (النائب العام)** exercises this himself or through a member of the
  Prosecution.
- ✅ **Art. 61 — the "preserve the papers" order:** if the Public Prosecution finds **no ground to proceed**, it
  **orders حفظ الأوراق** (preservation of the papers). *This is the Egyptian equivalent of a decision not to
  prosecute. It is a disposition, not an acquittal, and it is reviewable.*
- ✅ **Art. 62** — the **preservation order must be NOTIFIED to the victim and to the civil claimant** (and to
  their heirs if they have died). *So "we were never told" is a real defect.*
- ✅ **Art. 63** — in **contraventions and misdemeanours** the Prosecution may **summon the accused directly to the
  competent court** (no investigating judge needed).

## 4.2 The gate — offences that need a complaint, a request or a permission (Arts. 3–9)

- ✅ **Art. 3** — **a complaint (شكوى) is required** before prosecution or any investigation step in the offences
  under Penal Code **Arts. 185, 274, 277, 279, 292, 293, 303, 306, 307 and 308**, and in the other cases the law
  states. **The complaint may be ORAL or WRITTEN**, and is made by the victim **or his special agent** to the
  **Public Prosecution or to a judicial officer**. And: «**ولا تُقبل الشكوى بعد ثلاثة أشهر من يوم علم المجني عليه
  بالجريمة وبمرتكبها**» — **not accepted after three months from the day the victim learned of the crime and of the
  perpetrator** (unless another law says otherwise).
- ✅ **Art. 4** — **multiple victims:** a complaint from **one** of them suffices. **Multiple accused:** a complaint
  against **one** of them counts as against the rest.
- ✅ **Art. 5–6** — who may complain: **the guardian** if the victim is **under 15** or **mentally disordered**;
  where there is no representative or the representative has a **conflict of interest**, **the Prosecution takes the
  victim's place**. *(Art. 6 is the article the gazette text of the new law renders as "the prosecution substitutes
  for the victim" — the same rule.)*
- ✅ **Art. 7** — the **right to complain is extinguished by the victim's death**.
- ✅ **Art. 8** — a **written request of the Minister of Justice** is required for the Penal Code **Arts. 181 and
  182** offences, and in the other cases the law states.
- ✅ **Art. 9** — a **written request from the body or head of the injured service** is required for Penal Code
  **Art. 184**. **But investigation may proceed without complaint, request or permission** for offences under
  Penal Code **Art. 185 and Arts. 302, 306, 307, 308** where the **victim is a public official** and the **crime
  arose out of his office**.

⚠️ **The distinction the brief asks about — محضر vs بلاغ vs شكوى — is a distinction of FUNCTION, not of statute
vocabulary.** Read together with the articles above, in practice:

| Term | What it is | Where the code puts it |
|---|---|---|
| **محضر** | the **record/process-verbal** of an event, drawn up by a judicial officer (a police officer of judicial capacity). It is **evidence of what was recorded**, not a charge. | Arts. 34–36, 40, 44–58 (recording duties); it is the document Art. 104 and Art. 123 require the investigator to keep |
| **بلاغ** | an **information/incoming report** to the police or Prosecution — anyone can give one; it **does not** bind the Prosecution and needs no capacity. | the general "communication of a crime" route; Art. 1 vests the resulting action in the Prosecution |
| **شكوى** | a **legally-required COMPLAINT** from a person with standing, without which prosecution and investigation are **barred**, and which **expires in three months**. | ✅ **Art. 3**, with Arts. 4–7 |

⚠️ **So:** a **شكوى** is not a synonym for a **بلاغ**. Telling a client "file a complaint" without saying which is the
single most common way an offence that needs a شكوى gets time-barred under Art. 3's three-month rule.
⚠️ **And note the direction of travel:** the **new code makes it 90 days, not three months** — see §17.3.

# 5. Investigation: the lawyer, the interrogation, and the nullities — Arts. 104, 123–125, 132–133

**Law 150/1950 as consolidated to 16 January 2024. All ✅ unless marked.**

## 5.1 Art. 104 — first appearance (the client-facing article)

✅ **Art. 123** is the current first-appearance article in this code (read verbatim, with the amendment footnote
showing that its **second paragraph was held UNCONSTITUTIONAL**). On first appearance at investigation the
investigator must:

1. **verify the accused's identity** and record the details;
2. **inform him of the charge**;
3. **record his statement**;
4. *(and by the parallel Art. 104 of the new code, which the old article does not spell out in the same words —
   see §17.6 — inform him of his rights in writing and warn him of his right to silence. ⚠️ **PARTIAL:** the
   **Law 150/1950 Art. 123 text I read does not contain the "in writing" and "right to silence" clauses**; those
   appear in **new Art. 104**. Do not attribute them to the 1950 code.)*

⚠️ **Art. 123's second paragraph — the defamation-evidence paragraph — was held UNCONSTITUTIONAL by SCC Case 42 of
Year 16, judgment of 20 May 1995 (Gazette No. 23, 8 June 1995).** Read from the SCC annotation carried inline in the
موثقي مصر and ghazy reproductions. **Do not cite that paragraph as law.**

## 5.2 Art. 124 — the mandatory lawyer (⭐ the article to quote)

✅ In **felonies and misdemeanours punishable by mandatory imprisonment (الحبس الوجوبى)**:

- **No interrogation (استجواب) and no confrontation (مواجهة) before CALLING THE ACCUSED'S LAWYER.**
- ⚠️ **Two exceptions, and both must be RECORDED:** the **flagrante** case, and **urgency (حالة الضرورة) where loss
  of the evidence is feared**.
- The accused **must declare his lawyer's name** — in the investigation record, or at the Prosecution's criminal
  registry of the district, or to the officer in charge of the place where he is held.
- **If he has no lawyer, or the lawyer does not attend after being called, the investigator MUST APPOINT ONE**
  («وجب على المحقق أن يندب له محامياً»).
- **The lawyer may record in the record any defences, requests or observations** he has.
- **The appointed lawyer's fee is fixed by an order of the investigator, per the Ministry of Justice fee schedule
  issued after consulting the Bar** — and **the fee has the force of judicial fees (تأخذ حكم الرسوم القضائية)**.

## 5.3 Art. 125 — access, and the ban on separating the accused from his lawyer

✅ **Art. 125:**

- The lawyer **must be permitted to inspect the investigation** — and **the day before** any interrogation or
  confrontation — **unless the judge decides otherwise in a reasoned decision**.
- «**ولا يجوز التفريق بين المتهم ومحاميه أثناء التحقيق.**» — **the accused and his lawyer may NEVER be separated
  during investigation.** *This is the provision to cite when a client says he was questioned alone.*
- ⚠️ **An SCC ruling (Appeals 37 of Year 11, 6 February 1993, and 42 of Year 16) held part of this article's second
  paragraph unconstitutional.** Read from the SCC annotation in the annotated reproductions. ⚠️ **PARTIAL:** I read
  the *annotation*, not the judgment text; the annotation does not state which words fell. **Do not state the
  holding; cite the rule in the article's first paragraphs, which stand.**

## 5.4 Art. 132–133 — arrest outside the district; objections

- ✅ **Art. 132** — if he is **arrested outside the district** of the investigating court, he is **sent to the
  Public Prosecution there**, which **verifies his identity, informs him of the charge and records his statement**.
- ✅ **Art. 133** — an **objection to the transfer, or ill health**, is decided **immediately** by the investigating
  judge.

## 5.5 Art. 206 — surveillance and communications interception (⭐ the privacy gate)

✅ **Art. 206** (Law 150/1950 as consolidated):

- **Search of persons or homes of a person who is NOT an accused** requires **strong indications (قرائن قوية)**.
- **Postal and telegraph interception, wiretapping (تسجيل المكالمات) and recording in a private place** require a
  **PRIOR REASONED ORDER OF THE DISTRICT JUDGE (القاضي الجزئي)**.
- The order is valid for a **maximum of 30 days and is renewable (قابل للتجديد)** for a similar period.
  *(Constitutional hook: Art. 57.)*
- ✅ **Art. 206 bis** — **members of the Public Prosecution of at least chief-prosecutor rank (رئيس نيابة على الأقل)**
  have **investigating-judge powers** for **felonies in Book Two, Parts One, Two, Two bis and Four of the Penal
  Code** (the public-official, public-interest, terrorism and State-security parts). *That is how ordinary
  prosecutors lawfully conduct those investigations.*

# 6. Pretrial detention (الحبس الاحتياطى) — the whole apparatus

**Law 150/1950 as consolidated to 16 January 2024. All ✅ unless marked.**
⚠️ **Read §17.5 alongside this section: these are the numbers that govern TODAY, and the new code changes them.**

## 6.1 The order to detain — Arts. 134, 136–139

- ✅ **Art. 134 — the grounds (⭐ the article to quote).** The **investigating judge (قاضي التحقيق) may order pretrial
  detention AFTER INTERROGATION**, or if the accused **fled**, where:
  - the offence is a **felony**, or a **misdemeanour punishable by imprisonment of at least one year**; **and**
  - the **evidence is sufficient (الأدلة كافية)**; **and**
  - **one of** these grounds exists:
    1. **flagrante (التلبس)**, with the sentence to be executed on issue;
    2. **fear of flight (الخشية من هروب المتهم)**;
    3. **fear of harm to the investigation** — influencing the victim or witnesses, tampering with evidence,
       collusion (خشية الإضرار بمصلحة التحقيق);
    4. **prevention of a serious breach of security or public order (توقى الإخلال الجسيم بالأمن والنظام العام)**;
    5. **or the accused has NO KNOWN FIXED RESIDENCE IN EGYPT** and the offence is a felony or an imprisonable
       misdemeanour.
  ⚠️ **The residence ground is the one that surprises people: a foreigner, a visitor or an internally displaced
  person can be detained on residence alone, with no flight risk shown.**
- ✅ **Art. 136** — **before ordering detention the judge must hear the prosecution AND the defence**; and the order
  **must state the offence, its penalty, and the reasons (يجب أن يشتمل على ذكر الجريمة والسبب)**. **The same
  applies to every extension order.**
- ✅ **Art. 137** — the **Public Prosecution may seek detention at any time**.
- ✅ **Art. 138** — a **copy of the detention order must be handed to the prison governor against his signature**.
- ✅ **Art. 139 — told why, and told he can call someone:** everyone **arrested or detained must be told the reasons
  immediately**, has **the right to contact whom he wishes and to seek a lawyer**, and must be **informed promptly of
  the charge**. And: **seize-and-bring orders and detention orders cannot be EXECUTED after SIX MONTHS from their
  issue unless re-authorised (ما لم يُجدد).**
- ✅ **Art. 141** — the Prosecution or investigating judge may **bar the detained accused from contact with other
  prisoners and from visits** — «**وذلك دون الإخلال بحقه في الاتصال بمحاميه في أي وقت دون حضور أحد**» — **without
  prejudice to his right to contact his defence counsel AT ANY TIME, WITH NOBODY PRESENT.** *A blanket "no visits"
  order that also blocks the lawyer is unlawful.*

## 6.2 The durations, tier by tier — Arts. 142, 143, 151, and the prosecution's own powers (Arts. 201–205)

The order below is the order in which the clock actually runs. **All ✅.**

| Tier | Who orders | Duration | Article |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Police custody** | the judicial officer (mأمور الضبط) | must hear him **immediately**; send him to the Prosecution **within 24 h**; the Prosecution must interrogate him **within 24 h** and then order detention or release | **Art. 36** |
| **2. Prosecution detention** | a **member of the Public Prosecution, at least a DEPUTY PROSECUTOR (وكيل نيابة)** | **maximum 4 DAYS** following the arrest or the hand-over to the Prosecution. The order must be **reasoned** and issued **after hearing the defence** | **Art. 201** |
| **3. Extension by the District Judge** | the **القاضي الجزئي (District Judge)** | successive periods of **no more than 15 DAYS EACH, to a TOTAL OF 45 DAYS** | **Art. 202** |
| **4. Extension by the consultation chamber** | the **محكمة الجنح المستأنفة منعقدة في غرفة المشورة** (Misdemeanour Appeal Court, consultation chamber) | successive periods of **no more than 45 DAYS EACH** — or release, with or without bail | **Art. 143** para. 1 |
| **5. Attorney-General's review** | the **النائب العام** | the file **MUST be put before the Attorney-General when THREE MONTHS of detention have elapsed**, for him to do what will end the investigation | **Art. 143** para. 2 |
| **6. The hard caps** | — | see below | **Art. 143** paras. 3–4 |

**Art. 143, read verbatim (the load-bearing paragraphs):**

> «و**لا يجوز أن تزيد مدة الحبس الاحتياطى على ثلاثة أشهر**، ما لم يكن المتهم قد أعلن بإحالته إلى المحكمة
> المختصة قبل انتهاء هذه المدة، ويجب على النيابة العامة في هذه الحالة أن **تعرض أمر الحبس خلال خمسة أيام على
> الأكثر** من تاريخ الإعلان بالإحالة على المحكمة المختصة… وإلا وجب الإفراج عن المتهم. فإذا كانت التهمة المنسوبة
> إليه **جناية** فلا يجوز أن تزيد مدة الحبس الاحتياطى على **خمسة شهور** إلا بعد الحصول قبل انقضائها على أمر من
> المحكمة المختصة بمد الحبس مدة لا تزيد على **خمسة وأربعين يومًا قابلة للتجديد**… وإلا وجب الإفراج عن المتهم.
> وفى جميع الأحوال **لا يجوز أن تجاوز مدة الحبس الاحتياطى في مرحلة التحقيق الابتدائى وسائر مراحل الدعوى
> الجنائية ثلث الحد الأقصى للعقوبة السالبة للحرية، بحيث لا يتجاوز ستة أشهر في الجنح وثمانية عشر شهراً في
> الجنايات، وسنتين إذا كانت العقوبة المقررة للجريمة هى السجن المؤبد أو الإعدام.**
> ومع ذلك **فلمحكمة النقض ولمحكمة الإحالة إذا كان الحكم صادرًا بالإعدام أو بالسجن المؤبد أن تأمر بحبس المتهم
> احتياطيًا لمدة خمسة وأربعين يومًا قابلة للتجديد دون التقيد بالمدد المنصوص عليها في الفقرة السابقة**.»

**The caps, stated plainly — this is the answer to "how long can they hold him":**

1. **Three months (3 أشهر) is the general ceiling** — **unless the accused was NOTIFIED OF HIS REFERRAL TO THE
   COMPETENT COURT (أعلن بإحالته) before it expired**. If he was, the Prosecution must put the detention order to
   that court **within 5 days** of the referral notice, **and failing that he must be released.**
2. **For a FELONY: five months (5 أشهر)**, extendable only by an **order of the competent court made before expiry**,
   in periods **not exceeding 45 days, renewable** — failing which, release.
3. **In all cases, detention may not exceed ONE THIRD (ثلث) of the maximum custodial penalty**, subject to absolute
   ceilings of:
   - **6 months in misdemeanours (الجنح)**;
   - **18 months in felonies (الجنايات)**;
   - **2 years (سنتان) where the prescribed penalty is LIFE IMPRISONMENT (السجن المؤبد) or DEATH (الإعدام)**.
4. ⚠️ **The exception that overrides everything:** the **Court of Cassation** and the **referral court**, where the
   sentence is **death or life imprisonment**, may order **45-day detention, renewable, WITHOUT being bound by those
   ceilings**.

⚠️ **Two conflicts worth recording, because an article will get them wrong:**

- ⚠️ **A Dostor article of 14 August 2026 states the caps as 4 months (misdemeanours) / 12 months (felonies).**
  **Those figures are the NEW code's caps (Art. 124 of Law 174/2025), not the ones in force.** ✅ Verified directly
  this session: **new Art. 124** says «لا تجاوز **أربعة أشهر** في الجنح و**اثني عشر شهرً**ا في الجنايات، و**ثمانية
  عشر شهرً**ا إذا كانت العقوبة المقررة للجريمة هي **السجن المؤبد أو الإعدام**». So the correct reading is:
  **6 / 18 / 24 in force today; 4 / 12 / 18 from 1 October 2027.** Do not mix the two sets.
- ⚠️ **The 3-month / 5-month / one-third structure above carries the amendment footnotes of Laws 145/2006, 153/2007
  and 83/2013.** The *current* text is quoted; the *paragraph-to-amendment* mapping is **PARTIAL** (see §2.1).

## 6.3 Judicial review and the right to appeal a detention order — Arts. 143, 151, 205

- ✅ **Art. 143** — the **consultation chamber of the Misdemeanour Appeal Court** decides extensions, **after hearing
  the Prosecution AND the accused**, and may **release with or without bail** instead. **So the review body is a
  judicial bench, not the Prosecution that asked for the detention.**
- ✅ **Art. 151** — **once the case is referred to court, release or detention is for THAT COURT** to decide; **outside
  the session, for the consultation chamber** of the Misdemeanour Appeal Court. *(⚠️ As amended by **Law 1/2024**,
  which substituted the second paragraph.)*
- ✅ **Art. 205 — the express right of appeal (⭐ the article to quote for a detained client):**
  > «**وللمتهم أن يستأنف الأمر الصادر بحبسه احتياطيًا أو بمد هذا الحبس من القاضي الجزئى أو محكمة الجنح المستأنفة
  > منعقدة في غرفة المشورة**، وللنيابة العامة إذا استلزمت ضرورة التحقيق أن تستأنف الأمر الصادر… بالإفراج عن المتهم
  > المحبوس احتياطيًا وتراعى في ذلك أحكام الفقرة الثانية من المادة 164 والمواد (من 165 إلى 168) من هذا القانون.»

  So: **the accused MAY appeal (يستأنف) a detention or extension order made by the District Judge or by the
  consultation chamber**, applying **Art. 164 para. 2 and Arts. 165–168** (the general appeal machinery for orders).
  **And the Prosecution may appeal a RELEASE order** where the investigation requires it.
  ✅ The same article gives the **District Judge** power to **fix bail whenever the Prosecution seeks an extension**,
  applying **Arts. 146–150**.
- ✅ **Art. 152 — the victim is shut out of detention decisions:** «**وليس للمجني عليه أو المدعي بالحقوق المدنية أن
  يطلب الحبس الاحتياطي أو أن يسمع رأيه في الإفراج**» — the **victim or the civil claimant may NOT apply for
  detention and may NOT be heard on release.** *This is an important and counter-intuitive rule: a victim cannot
  push for the accused's detention, and cannot oppose his release.*
- ✅ **The new code's equivalent is Art. 166** — «**يجوز للمتهم أن يستأنف الأمر الصادر بحبسه احتياطيًا أو بمد مدة الحبس**، وللنيابة العامة إذا استلزمت ضرورة التحقيق أن تستأنف الأمر الصادر بالإفراج المؤقت عن المتهم المحبوس احتياطيًا.» ⚠️ **The accused may appeal a DETENTION ORDER and an EXTENSION order in exactly the same terms as Art. 205 today.** *(Note: **new Art. 205 is about something else entirely** — it applies Arts. 108, 116, 152 and 157 to the investigating judge's orders. **Do not cite new Art. 205 for the detention appeal** — the article is **166**. See §17.9.)*

## 6.4 The alternative measures to detention — Arts. 201, 202, 203

- ✅ **Art. 201** — the Prosecution may order, **instead of detention**, one of **three** alternatives:
  1. **not leaving his home or his locality (عدم مبارحة مسكنه أو موطنه)**;
  2. **reporting to a police station at set times**;
  3. **being banned from specified places.**
  **Breach of an alternative allows detention.** And the alternatives **follow the same duration, extension,
  maximum and appeal rules as detention** («تسري في شأنها ذات أحكام الحبس الاحتياطي»).
- ✅ **Art. 202** — the **District Judge** extends, **after hearing the Prosecution and the accused**, in successive
  periods **of no more than 15 days each, to a total of 45 days**.
- ✅ **Art. 203** — if the investigation has not finished, the papers go to the **consultation chamber** to rule
  under **Art. 143**.
- ✅ **Art. 204** — the **Prosecution may release at any time, with or without bail.**
- ✅ **Art. 199** — the Prosecution conducts the investigation in **misdemeanours and felonies under the
  investigating-judge rules**, except the crimes reserved to an investigating judge under **Art. 64**.
- ✅ **Art. 199 bis — the victim's civil claim during investigation:** a **person harmed may claim his civil rights
  during the investigation**; the Prosecution rules on **acceptance within 3 days**; and **refusal may be appealed
  to the consultation chamber within 3 days of notification**. *(Fuller treatment in §12.)*

# 7. Release and bail — Arts. 144–150

**Law 150/1950 as consolidated to 16 January 2024. All ✅ unless marked.**

- ✅ **Art. 144 — release on the accused's own request:** the investigating judge may **at any time**, of **his own
  motion or on the accused's request**, and **after hearing the Prosecution**, order **provisional release
  (إفراج مؤقت)** — **conditional on the accused undertaking to appear whenever required and not to flee**.
  ⚠️ **The one carve-out:** if the detention order came from the **consultation chamber** on the **Prosecution's
  appeal against a release order**, then **only that court** may order release. *So the Prosecution can, in effect,
  lock a release decision to the chamber.*
- ✅ **Art. 145 — the domicile condition:** where release is **not mandatory**, it may **not** be granted with or
  without security **until the accused has designated a domicile in the town of the court**, if he is not resident
  there. *Practical: have the address ready before the hearing.*
- ✅ **Art. 146 — the surety (كفالة) and how it is split (⭐ the article clients ask about):** release may be made
  **conditional on a surety**. The judge — or the consultation chamber — **fixes the amount**, and **allocates**:
  - part as the **sanction for non-appearance**, and
  - part to **government expenses and any fines imposed**.
  **If the amount is fixed without allocation (بغير تخصيص),** it secures **appearance, non-evasion of execution, and
  the other duties imposed on him** generally.
- ✅ **Art. 147 — how it is paid:** by the **accused or by someone else**; **in cash** or in **government or
  government-guaranteed bonds** deposited with the court treasury. **A solvent third party's personal undertaking is
  accepted** and **has the force of an enforceable instrument (قوة السند التنفيذى)**.
- ✅ **Art. 148 — forfeiture without a judgment:** where the accused **breaches an obligation without acceptable
  excuse**, the **first portion of the surety becomes state property WITHOUT ANY JUDGMENT** («بغير حكم»); the
  **second portion is returned** if the case ends in a **no-case decision (قرار بأن لا وجه لإقامتها)** or an
  **acquittal**.
- ✅ **Art. 149 — release where he cannot fund a surety:** if his **condition does not allow a surety**, the judge
  may require him to:
  - **report to a police office at set times**;
  - **choose a residence away from the scene of the crime**; and
  - may **ban him from a specified place**.
- ✅ **Art. 150 — release is not final:** a release order **does not bar a NEW arrest or detention order** where
  **evidence has strengthened against him**, or he **breaches the conditions imposed on him**, or **new circumstances
  arise**. *(The new code adds the same rule as its Art. 131.)*

⚠️ **Terminology that trips agents:** **إفراج مؤقت** (provisional release, Arts. 144 ff. and 204) is the general
release; **إفراج تحت ضمان/كفالة** is release on surety; **إخلاء السبيل** (Art. 36, and Art. 112/194 of the new code)
is the **release order made when the statutory period expires without a valid order** — a mandatory release, not a
discretion. Do not use the three interchangeably.

# 8. Offence classes and what follows from them — Penal Code (Law 58/1937)

**Citation:** قانون رقم ٥٨ لسنة ١٩٣٧ بإصدار قانون العقوبات.
**Status:** in force, heavily amended.
**Source:** `https://mstsharh.net/قانون-العقوبات-المصري-رقم-58-لسنة-1937/` — consolidated Arabic text, titled as
amended through **Law 141/2024**. Downloaded, tag-stripped and read locally. **As of:** 2026-10-19.

## 8.1 The three classes (⭐ Arts. 9–12, read verbatim)

- ✅ **Art. 9:** «**الجرايم ثلاثة انواع: (الاول) الجنايات. (الثانى) الجنح. (الثالث) المخالفات.**»
- ✅ **Art. 10 — الجنايات (felonies):** offences punished by **death (الإعدام)**, **life imprisonment
  (السجن المؤبد)**, **aggravated imprisonment (السجن المشدد)**, or **imprisonment (السجن)**.
- ✅ **Art. 11 — الجنح (misdemeanours):** offences punished by **الحبس (simple imprisonment)** or a **fine whose
  MAXIMUM exceeds EGP 100 (ماية جنيه)**.
- ✅ **Art. 12 — المخالفات (contraventions):** offences punished by a **fine whose maximum does NOT exceed EGP 100**.

⚠️ **The EGP 100 line has never been raised.** It is a 1937 figure. **Do not reason from the amount** — the class is
fixed by what the **specific penalising article** prescribes, not by what a court actually fines.

## 8.2 The penalty scales

- ✅ **Art. 14** — **السجن المؤبد** and **السجن المشدد** are both: placing the convict in a prison designated by law
  and employing him inside it at government-assigned work — **for life if the penalty is مؤبد**, or **for the term
  sentenced if it is مشدد**.
- ✅ **Art. 18 — الحبس:** placing the convict in a central or general prison for the term sentenced; **it may not be
  less than 24 hours nor more than THREE YEARS**, save in the special cases provided by law. **A person sentenced to
  simple الحبس for a term not exceeding six months may ask to be employed outside prison instead**, per the limits in
  the Criminal Procedure Code, **unless the judgment expressly deprives him of that option**.
- ✅ **Art. 19** — **الحبس is of two kinds: الحبس البسيط (simple) and الحبس مع الشغل (with work)**. Those sentenced
  with work are employed inside or outside prison.
- ✅ **Art. 20** — the judge **must** sentence **الحبس مع الشغل** **whenever the term is ONE YEAR OR MORE**, and in
  the other cases the law designates; otherwise he may choose either kind.
- ✅ **Art. 55 — suspension of execution (إيقاف تنفيذ العقوبة):** for a **felony or misdemeanour** punished by a fine
  or by **حبس not exceeding one year**, the court may in the same judgment order **suspension of execution** if his
  **morals, past, age or the circumstances of the offence** give reason to believe he will not re-offend. **The
  reasons must be stated in the judgment.** The suspension may be made to **cover any accessory penalty and all the
  criminal effects** of the judgment.
- ✅ **Art. 56** — the suspension order runs for **THREE YEARS** from the day the judgment becomes **final**. It may
  be **revoked** if, within that period, a **حبس sentence exceeding one month** is passed against him for an act
  committed before or after the suspension order, or if it emerges that such a judgment had been passed against him
  before the order and the court did not know of it.
- ✅ **Art. 57** — revocation is pronounced **by the court that ordered the suspension**, on the **Public
  Prosecution's application**, after **summoning the convict**.

## 8.3 What the class changes, in one table

| | **جناية (felony)** | **جنحة (misdemeanour)** | **مخالفة (contravention)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prescribed by | PC Arts. 10 | PC Arts. 11 | PC Arts. 12 |
| Prescription of the criminal action | **10 years** (CrPC Art. 15) | **3 years** | **1 year** |
| Needs a lawyer at trial | **Yes** — Const. Art. 54 final para. applies wherever الحبس is available; and no interrogation without counsel in PC-mandatory-imprisonment offences (CrPC Art. 124) | **Yes**, wherever الحبس is available | representation permitted (CrPC Art. 237) |
| Detention ceiling before referral | **5 months**, extendable in 45-day orders (CrPC Art. 143) | **3 months**; mandatory release on narrow grounds after **8 days** (CrPC Art. 142) | detention not available |
| First appeal | **Yes, since Law 1/2024** — Art. 419 bis **40 days**, prosecution/State Lawsuits **40 days**, AG **60 days** | **Yes** — CrPC Arts. 402 ff., **10 days** | only in the stated situations (CrPC Art. 402 final clause) |
| Cassation | available (Law 57/1959 Art. 30) | available **unless** the penalty is a fine not exceeding **EGP 20,000** | not available |

# 9. Limitation of the criminal action (مدة سقوط الدعوى الجنائية) — CrPC Arts. 15–18

**Law 150/1950 as consolidated to 16 January 2024. All ✅ unless marked.**
⚠️ **Do not confuse this with the three-month complaint period in Art. 3 (§4.2).** Art. 3 is the **victim's window to
complain**; Art. 15 is the **state's window to prosecute**. They are different clocks, they start at different
moments, and only one of them is about the victim.

## 9.1 Art. 15 — the periods, verbatim

> «**تنقضى الدعوى الجنائية في مواد الجنايات بمضى عشر سنين من يوم وقوع الجريمة، وفي مواد الجنح بمضى ثلاث سنين،
> وفي مواد المخالفات بمضى سنة** ما لم ينص القانون على خلاف ذلك.
> أما في الجرائم المنصوص عليها في المواد **117 و126 و127 و282 و309 مكرراً و309 مكرراً (أ)** والجرائم المنصوص
> عليها في **القسم الأول من الباب الثانى من الكتاب الثانى** من قانون العقوبات **والتي تقع بعد تاريخ العمل بهذا
> القانون فلا تنقضى الدعوى الجنائية الناشئة عنها بمضى المدة.**
> ومع عدم الإخلال بأحكام الفقرتين السابقتين **لا تبدأ المدة المسقطة للدعوى الجنائية في الجرائم المنصوص عليها في
> البابين الثالث والرابع من الكتاب الثانى من قانون العقوبات والتي تقع من موظف عام إلا من تاريخ انتهاء الخدمة أو
> زوال الصفة** ما لم يبدأ التحقيق فيها قبل ذلك.»

Stated plainly:

1. **Felonies (جنايات): TEN YEARS** from the day the crime was committed.
2. **Misdemeanours (جنح): THREE YEARS.**
3. **Contraventions (مخالفات): ONE YEAR.**
   — unless another law states otherwise.
4. **NO PRESCRIPTION AT ALL** — for crimes committed **after** the code's entry into force — under Penal Code
   **Arts. 117, 126, 127, 282, 309 bis, 309 bis (a)**, and the crimes in **Section One of Part Two of Book Two** of
   the Penal Code. ⚠️ **Note what is in that list:** **Art. 126 = torture to obtain a confession**, **Art. 127 =
   punishing a convict more severely than sentenced**, **Art. 282 = aggravated unlawful arrest/detention**,
   **Art. 117 = embezzlement of public funds**, **Art. 309 bis / 309 bis (a) = the "obtaining money by fraud"
   offences** (the pyramid-scheme articles). **These never time out.**
   *This is the statutory hook that matches ✅ Constitution Art. 52 for torture.*
5. **Public officials and the start of the clock:** for crimes in **Parts Three and Four of Book Two** committed by
   a **public official**, the period **does not start until his service ends or his capacity ceases** — **unless the
   investigation began before that**. ⚠️ *This is the provision that makes long-cold official corruption cases
   prosecutable, and it is the one everyone forgets.*

## 9.2 Arts. 16–18 — suspension, interruption, and the effect on co-accused

- ✅ **Art. 16** — «**لا يوقف سريان المدة التي تنقضي بها الدعوى الجنائية لأي سبب.**» **The prescription period is
  NOT suspended for ANY reason.** *This is unusually strict and is the answer to "can the case be frozen while he is
  abroad / while a civil case runs / while the victim negotiates". It cannot.*
- ✅ **Art. 17 — INTERRUPTION (انقطاع), and what does it:** the period is **interrupted** by:
  - **acts of investigation (إجراءات التحقيق)**;
  - **accusation (الاتهام)**;
  - **trial (المحاكمة)**;
  - **a criminal order (الأمر الجنائى)**;
  - and **acts of evidence-gathering (إجراءات الاستدلال)** — **but only if taken AGAINST the accused or if he was
    officially notified of them** («إذا اتخذت في مواجهة المتهم أو إذا أخطر بها بوجه رسمى»).
  **After interruption the period runs ANEW (تسرى المدة من جديد)** from the day of the interruption; **where there
  are several interrupting acts, the new period runs from the date of the LAST one.**
  ⚠️ **The evidential condition on police acts matters:** an investigative step taken **behind the accused's back**
  does **not** interrupt. Sending a letter is not enough — he must have been **officially informed**.
- ✅ **Art. 18** — «**وإذا تعدد المتهمون فإن انقطاع المدة بالنسبة لأحدهم يترتب عليه انقطاعها بالنسبة للباقين ولو
  لم تكن قد اتخذت ضدهم إجراءات قاطعة للمدة.**» **Interruption as to one accused interrupts as to ALL of them**,
  **even if no interrupting act was taken against them**. *(In the new code this became Art. 19 with the same
  content and the same wording — see §17.4.)*

⚠️ ⚠️ **The practical trap:** because **Art. 16 forbids suspension** but **Art. 17 allows interruption**, almost
every long-running Egyptian case is preserved by **interruption** rather than suspended. So when a client asks "the
crime was in 2019, is it time-barred", the answer is **not** "felony, ten years, fine" — the answer requires the
**last date on which an investigation, accusation, trial or criminal order occurred against or was notified to him**.

# 10. Trial — Arts. 232–239, 366–372

**Law 150/1950 as consolidated to 16 January 2024. All ✅ unless marked.**

## 10.1 How a case reaches the criminal court — Art. 232

✅ Misdemeanour and contravention cases reach the **Misdemeanour and Contravention Court** by:
1. an **order of the investigating judge** or of the **consultation chamber**; **or**
2. **the accused being summoned directly (تكليف بالحضور) by a member of the Public Prosecution**; **or**
3. **the accused being summoned directly by the CIVIL CLAIMANT** — «**أو من المدعي بالحقوق المدنية**».

**Limb 3 is the direct accusation route (الإدعاء المباشر).** And the article **restricts** it:
- a civil claimant **may not** use it where a **no-case order (حفظ)** was made and **not appealed**, or **was upheld**;
- and **not** where the charge is against a **public official, public employee or police officer for an act in the
  course of duty** — **except** the Penal Code **Art. 123** offences (an official obstructing the execution of a
  judgment).

⚠️ **So "الإدعاء المباشر" is available but is barred in exactly the cases where a client most often wants it —
after a حفظ decision, and against officials.** Get that wrong and the case is dismissed on a formal point.

## 10.2 Summons and appearance — Arts. 233–237

- ✅ **Art. 233** — a summons must be served **at least one full day** before the session for **contraventions** and
  **at least THREE full days** for **misdemeanours** (excluding the distance allowance), and **must state the
  article charging him and the penalty article**. **No period is needed in flagrante and where the accused is
  detained.**
- ✅ **Art. 234** — the service rules.
- ✅ **Art. 236** — the parties may inspect the file once summoned.
- ✅ **Art. 237 — appearance and counsel:** in a **misdemeanour punishable by imprisonment which the law requires to
  be served immediately**, the accused **must attend in person**. **An attending accused in a misdemeanour
  punishable by mandatory imprisonment who has no lawyer is APPOINTED one by the court (وجب على المحكمة أن تندب له
  محامياً).** Otherwise, representation is allowed.
- ✅ **Arts. 238–239** — **in-absentia (غيابى) judgments**, and the cases in which a judgment counts as
  **in-presence (حضورى)**.

## 10.3 The felony court and the new appellate felony bench — Arts. 366–372

- ✅ **Art. 366** — one or more **felony courts (محكمة الجنايات)** are formed in **each Court of Appeal**, **each of
  three judges**, **headed by a judge of at least the rank of vice-president of the Court of Appeal**. **One or more
  circuits, headed by a judge of Court of Appeal president rank, are designated for the felonies of Book Two, Parts
  One, Two, Two bis, Three and Four of the Penal Code** and connected offences, **decided urgently**. *(⚠️ Renewed
  by Law 1/2024: the trial bench is now styled **محكمة جنايات أول درجة**.)*
- ✅ **Art. 367 — the appellate felony court created by Law 1/2024 (⭐):** one or more courts are formed in **each
  Court of Appeal** to hear **appeals against the judgments of the first-degree felony circuit** — **each of three
  judges, at least one of Court of Appeal president rank, headed by the most senior.** *(This is the bench that did
  not exist before 17 January 2024.)*
- ✅ **Arts. 369–372** — the felony court sits **in every court-of-first-instance district**, **monthly** unless the
  Court of Appeal president rules otherwise; **sitting dates are fixed at least one month ahead**; and **a docket
  (جدول) is prepared per session.**

⚠️ **A real and important procedural point:** there is **no statutory deadline for the trial itself** anywhere in the
articles read. The **only** time pressure on the State in the whole apparatus is the **pretrial detention clock** in
Art. 143 — which is why long pre-trial detention and long trials coexist: the detention cap eventually forces
release, but it does not force a trial date.

# 11. Appeal (استئناف) — Arts. 402–412, and the felony appeal (Arts. 419 bis – 419 bis 9)

## 11.1 The general appeal regime — Arts. 402–412

**Law 150/1950 as consolidated to 16 January 2024. All ✅ unless marked. Source and `As of:` as §2.**

- ✅ **Art. 402** — **both the accused and the Public Prosecution may appeal** misdemeanour judgments of the
  **District Court**. ⚠️ **But** where the penalty is a **fine not exceeding EGP 300** plus restitution and costs,
  the appeal lies **only** on **breach, misapplication or misinterpretation of the law**, or on a **nullity
  affecting the judgment**. For **contraventions**, appeal only in the situations the article states.
- ✅ **Art. 403** — appeal on the **civil part** is available where the **compensation claimed exceeds the District
  Judge's final-jurisdiction figure**.
- ✅ **Art. 404** — where offences are **connected under Penal Code Art. 32**, an appeal may be brought **even as to
  some of them only**.
- ✅ **Art. 405** — **no appeal against preparatory or interlocutory decisions** before a judgment on the merits;
  **jurisdiction decisions are appealable**.
- ✅ **Art. 406 — the deadline (⭐ the article to quote):**
  > «**يحصل الاستئناف بتقرير في قلم كتاب المحكمة التي أصدرت الحكم**، **وذلك خلال عشرة أيام** من تاريخ **صدور الحكم
  > الحضورى** أو **إعلان الحكم الغيابى** أو **الحكم الصادر في المعارضة**.»

  **Appeal is by REPORT (تقرير) at the registry of the court that issued the judgment**, **within TEN DAYS** from
  (a) the **pronouncement** of an **in-presence** judgment; (b) the **notification** of an **in-absentia** judgment;
  (c) the **judgment on the objection (الحكم الصادر في المعارضة)**.
  **The Attorney-General has THIRTY DAYS** and **may report at the registry of the appellate court**.
  ⚠️ **Note that the clock for an in-absentia judgment runs from NOTIFICATION, not from the judgment date** — that
  is the commonest error in this section.
  ⚠️ *(Gap in this file, stated honestly: **Art. 407** — for judgments deemed in-presence under Arts. 238–241 the
  accused's period runs **from notification** — is recorded from my earlier reading, and I did **not** re-read it
  this session. Treat Art. 407 as ⚠️ **PARTIAL**.)*
- ✅ **Art. 408** — the session date is set, **not before three full days**; the other parties are summoned by the
  Prosecution.
- ✅ **Art. 409** — where **one party appeals within the ten days**, **the others get FIVE EXTRA DAYS** to appeal.
- ✅ **Art. 410** — appeal goes to the **Court of First Instance in whose district the judgment was issued**; **if the
  accused is detained the Prosecution must transfer him and the appeal is heard urgently.**
- ✅ **Art. 411** — report contents and the hearing order (**the accused speaks last**).
- ✅ **Art. 412** — the appeal of a person under an **immediately enforceable custodial sentence LAPSES if he does not
  present for execution before the session**; **the court may suspend execution or release him with or without bail
  pending the appeal.**

## 11.2 The felony appeal — created by Law No. 1 of 2024 (⭐ the biggest change in this file)

**Citation:** **قانون رقم ١ لسنة ٢٠٢٤ بتعديل بعض أحكام قانون الإجراءات الجنائية**.
**Status:** **IN FORCE from 17 January 2024** (Gazette No. **2 bis**, **16 January 2024**).
**Source:** `https://alhasswalawfirm.com/law-library/law-1-2024` — which reproduces the **full 33-article gazette
text** of Law 1/2024, and the same firm's consolidated Law 150/1950 (`law-library/law-150-1950`), whose Arts. 419 bis
and 419 bis 1–9 carry the gazette amendment footnotes. Read article by article from both. **As of:** 2026-10-19.

**What Law 1/2024 did, article by article:**

1. **Renamed the trial court.** It substituted **«محكمة جنايات أول درجة»** (first-degree felony court) for
   **«محكمة الجنايات»** in CrPC **Arts. 11, 156, 158, 159, 167 (paras 1–2), 210, 211, 214, 216, 276 bis, 384, 394,
   397, 529 and 536**; substituted **«محكمة الجنايات بدرجتيها»** in **Arts. 167 (para. 5) and 524**;
   **«للدائرة الجنائية بمحكمة النقض»** for **«لمحكمة الجنايات المستأنفة، وللدائرة الجنائية بمحكمة النقض»** in
   **Art. 12**; **«الدائرة المختصة»** for **«محكمة الجنايات المستأنفة»** in **Art. 167 para. 1**;
   **«محكمة جنايات القاهرة»** for **«محكمة جنايات أول درجة بدائرة محكمة استئناف القاهرة»** in **Art. 219**.
2. **Substituted Art. 151 para. 2** — referral outside the session to the consultation chamber (§6.3).
3. **Added a whole new Chapter Two — «في استئناف مواد الجنايات»** with **Arts. 419 bis and 419 bis 1 to 419 bis 9**:

| Article | Content (**all ✅**) |
|---|---|
| **Art. 419 bis** | **Both the Public Prosecution and the accused** may appeal **in-presence first-degree felony judgments**. |
| **Art. 419 bis 1** | The **civil party** may appeal where the **compensation exceeds the Court of First Instance's final-jurisdiction figure**. |

| **Art. 419 bis 4 — the deadline (⭐)** | **Appeal by REPORT at the registry of the court that issued the judgment within FORTY DAYS (٤٠ يومًا) of the judgment.** Grounds must be filed **in the same period**. A **Public Prosecution** report must be signed by **at least a chief prosecutor (محام عام)**; a **State Lawsuits Authority** report by **at least a counsellor (مستشار)**. **The Attorney-General may appeal within SIXTY DAYS**, and **may report at the registry of the APPELLATE court**. |
| **Arts. 419 bis 5–7** | Transmission of the papers, notice to the parties, and the hearing order. |
| **Art. 419 bis 8** | Where an **in-presence death sentence** is **not appealed** within the period, the **Prosecution must follow Art. 46 of Law 57/1959** (the automatic review of death sentences). |
| **Art. 419 bis 9 — execution (⭐)** | **Appeal does NOT suspend execution** — **unless the appellate felony court so decides, or the sentence is DEATH.** And **if the appellant or his agent fails to appear without excuse, the court APPOINTS A LAWYER and rules.** |
| **Art. 5 of Law 1/2024** | **In force from the day after publication** — i.e. **17 January 2024**. |

⚠️ **Two consequences that matter more than the article list:**
- **A felony sentence is now executed unless the appellate court suspends it** (Art. 419 bis 9). Before 2024 there was
  no first appeal to lose; now there is, and losing it does not buy time — **except in death cases, which are
  automatically stayed.**
- **The 40 days is a REPORT (تقرير) at the registry, not a filing with the appellate court.** For the accused it is
  the trial court's registry; only the **Attorney-General** may report at the appellate registry — and only within
  **60 days**.

## 11.3 The transitional provision that catches people out

✅ **Promulgating Art. 3 of Law 174/2025** — which, because it regulates a transition *into* the Law 1/2024 regime,
**already operates**: «**لا تسري أحكام الاستئناف في مواد الجنايات إلا على الدعاوى التي لم يفصل فيها من محاكم
الجنايات اعتبارًا من تاريخ العمل بالقانون رقم ١ لسنة ٢٠٢٤**» — **the felony-appeal provisions apply only to cases
NOT yet decided by the felony courts as of the date Law 1/2024 came into force (17 January 2024).**
⚠️ **But read this carefully, because it cuts both ways:** a felony judgment pronounced **before** 17 January 2024
is **not** appealable under the new chapter. **And the ordinary transitional rule is that the right of appeal is
governed by the law in force when the JUDGMENT was pronounced** — so for a felony decided in, say, 2023, the answer
is cassation, not appeal. **Do not tell a client with an old felony conviction that he now has an appeal.**

# 12. Cassation (الطعن بالنقض) — Law No. 57 of 1959

**Citation:** **قانون رقم ٥٧ لسنة ١٩٥٩ بإصدار قانون حالات وإجراءات الطعن أمام محكمة النقض**, Gazette No. **33 bis
(ب)** — a **non-ordinary** issue — **21 February 1959**. **53 articles.**
**Status:** **IN FORCE — and it will remain in force after the new code applies.** ✅ **Verified this session from the
new code itself:** Law 174/2025's **promulgating Art. 4 repeals ONLY Law 150/1950 and Law 140/2014**; the phrase
«قانون حالات وإجراءات الطعن أمام محكمة النقض» appears **exactly once** in the entire 139-page gazette — in
**new Art. 412**, which **cross-refers to Art. 46 of Law 57/1959** for un-appealed death sentences. **New Art. 415
also refers the retrial request to the Court of Cassation.** **So Law 57/1959 is not repealed and is relied upon by
the new code.** ⚠️ *(This is a genuinely useful negative finding: an agent should NOT say the new code replaces the
cassation law.)*
**Source:** the Law 57/1959 consolidated Arabic text as returned by the search, read article by article; the same
portal that reproduces the CrPC. Last amendment recorded in that reproduction: **12 December 2019**.
**As of:** 2026-10-19.

- ✅ **Art. 30 — who, and on what grounds.** Cassation lies **to the Public Prosecution, the convicted person, the
  civilly liable person and the civil claimant**, against the **final judgment of the last degree**, in **felonies
  and misdemeanours**, on:
  1. **breach, misapplication or misinterpretation of the law (مخالفة القانون أو الخطأ في تطبيقه أو تأويله)**;
  2. **nullity in the judgment (بطلان في الحكم)**;
  3. **nullity in the procedure affecting the judgment (بطلان في الإجراءات أثّر في الحكم)**.
  **Excluded** where the misdemeanour is punished by a **fine not exceeding EGP 20,000**. **No cassation on the civil
  action alone** where the compensation does not exceed the Court of Cassation threshold in the Civil Procedure Law.
  **Only in one's own rights.** And the **Attorney-General may appeal for the accused's BENEFIT**.
- ✅ **Art. 31** — **no cassation against decisions before a judgment on the merits** unless they **bar the
  proceedings**.
- ✅ **Art. 32** — **no cassation while the objection (معارضة) is still available.**
- ✅ **Art. 34 — the deadline (⭐):** cassation is by **REPORT at the registry of the court that issued the judgment
  within SIXTY DAYS** from:
  - the **in-presence judgment**; **or**
  - the **expiry of the objection period**; **or**
  - **the judgment on the objection**;
  **with the grounds (أسباب) filed in the same period.**
  ⚠️ **A special case:** where the judgment was an **acquittal** and the appellant obtained a **certificate that the
  judgment was not filed at the registry within 30 days**, the **appeal and grounds are accepted within TEN DAYS of
  notification.**
  Formalities: the **Prosecution's** report and grounds must be signed by **at least a chief prosecutor**; the
  **State Lawsuits Authority's** by **at least a counsellor**; **other appellants'** grounds by an **advocate
  admitted before the Court of Cassation**.
- ✅ **Art. 35** — **no new grounds** may be raised beyond those filed; **but the court may quash in the accused's
  FAVOUR of its own motion.**
- ✅ **Art. 36 — the security:** where the appellant is **not** the Prosecution and **not** a person under a
  **custodial sentence**, a **security of EGP 300** must be deposited **at the time of the report** (unless the
  **legal-aid committee** exempts him). Where the court **rejects the appeal as impermissible, lapsed or
  inadmissible**, it orders **forfeiture of the security PLUS a fine of the same amount**; where it dismisses
  **on the merits**, the fine is **discretionary**.

⚠️ **The three numbers to remember: 60 days (cassation report), 40 days (felony appeal under Art. 419 bis 4),
10 days (misdemeanour appeal under Art. 406).** They are three different clocks for three different remedies and
confusing them loses the remedy.

# 13. The victim in the criminal case — the civil claimant (المدعي بالحقوق المدنية)

**Law 150/1950 as consolidated to 16 January 2024. All ✅ unless marked.**

⚠️ **This is the section a non-lawyer most needs, and it is genuinely counter-intuitive: the victim is a *party to
the criminal case*, but only for the money, and never for the detention decision (Art. 152, §6.3).**

## 13.1 How the victim joins — Art. 251

- ✅ **Art. 251** — a **person harmed by the offence may make himself a CIVIL CLAIMANT (مدعيًا بالحقوق المدنية)**
  before the **criminal court**, **at any stage until the close of the pleadings (إقفال باب المرافعة)** under
  **Art. 275** — **but NOT before the appellate court (وليس أمام محكمة الاستئناف).**
  - The claim is made either **by bailiff's service on the accused** or **by a request at the session**.
  - **If the civil claim was already accepted at the investigation stage, the referral to court carries the civil
    claim with it.**
  - ⚠️ **The civil intervention may not DELAY the criminal case**, and if it does, **the court rules it inadmissible.**
  - ⚠️ **The "not before the appellate court" rule is a hard and common error.** A victim who is told only at the
    appeal that he should have claimed will have lost the civil claim in that case.
- ✅ **Art. 251 bis** — **civil claims under this code are only for DIRECT PERSONAL DAMAGE, CERTAIN TO OCCUR, PRESENT
  OR FUTURE** («المباشر الشخصي المتيقن الحاصل أو المستقبل»). *So a speculative or indirect loss is not claimable in
  the criminal case.*
- ✅ **Art. 252** — the court may **appoint a representative for an incapacitated victim** on the **Prosecution's
  request**, and **no costs are imposed on him** for that.
- ✅ **Art. 253** — the civil compensation action is brought **against the accused**, or **his representative** if he
  is incapacitated, and **may also be brought against those CIVILLY LIABLE for the accused's act.**
- ✅ **Art. 254** — the **civilly liable person may intervene**; the **Prosecution and the civil claimant may oppose**.
- ✅ **Art. 255** — the civil claimant **must give a local address** (to receive notices).
- ✅ **Art. 256 — the money the victim must put up:** the civil claimant **pays the judicial fees** and **deposits a
  security for expert and witness fees and expenses**, plus **supplementary deposits** as required. *⚠️ Practical:
  the civil claim in a criminal case is not free.*
- ✅ **Art. 257** — the **Prosecution, the accused and the civilly liable party may oppose the ADMISSIBILITY of the
  civil claim**, decided **after hearing the parties**.
- ✅ **Art. 258** — an **investigating judge's REJECTION of a civil claim does NOT bar a later claim** before the
  **criminal or civil court**; and an **acceptance by the investigating judge does NOT bind the court.**
- ✅ **Art. 258 bis** — a **civil action against the INSURER may be brought before the criminal court.**
- ✅ **Art. 259 — limitation of the civil claim:** the civil action is **time-barred per the Civil Code** (see
  `01-commercial-corporate-contract.md` and `05-tenancy-egypt.md` for Civil Code Arts. 374–387) — **BUT NOT** where
  it arises from the crimes listed in **Art. 15 para. 2** committed after the law. **And the extinction of the
  criminal action AFTER the civil claim was filed does not affect the civil action.** ⚠️ *That last sentence is the
  one to know: a حفظ or a time-bar that would kill the prosecution does not kill a civil claim already filed.*
- ✅ **Arts. 260–263** — **withdrawal and abandonment** of the civil claim, their effects and the costs.
- ✅ **Art. 265** — where the civil action is brought before the **civil courts** and a **criminal case was
  previously brought or is pending** on the same facts, the civil case is **suspended until a final criminal
  judgment**.
- ✅ **Art. 199 bis** — the victim may **claim his civil rights during the INVESTIGATION**; the **Prosecution rules
  on acceptance within THREE DAYS**; and **a refusal may be appealed to the consultation chamber within THREE DAYS
  of notification.**

## 13.2 What the victim does NOT get

- ❌ **No right to ask for the accused's detention** and **no right to be heard on his release** — ✅ **Art. 152**,
  quoted in §6.3. **The victim's role is the money, not the custody.**
- ⚠️ **No civil claim in the appellate court** for the first time — ✅ **Art. 251**.
- ⚠️ **No indirect or speculative damage** — ✅ **Art. 251 bis**.

# 14. Self-incrimination, confessions, and statements obtained under duress

**Sources: Constitution Arts. 52, 54, 55 (official PDF — §1); CrPC Arts. 123–125 (§5); Penal Code Arts. 126–129
(§15). All ✅ unless marked.**

## 14.1 The right against self-incrimination

Four separate texts, none of which alone is the whole rule:

1. ✅ **Constitution Art. 55, third sentence:** «**وللمتهم حق الصمت.**» — **a constitutional right to silence** for
   the accused.
2. ✅ **Constitution Art. 55, fourth sentence:** «وكل قول يثبت أنه صدر من محتجز **تحت وطأة** شىء مما تقدم، أو
   **التهديد بشىء منه**، **يهدر ولا يُعوَّل عليه**.» — any statement **proved** to have been made by a detainee
   **under the pressure of** torture, intimidation, coercion, physical or moral harm — **or the THREAT of any of
   those** — is **void and cannot be relied upon**. ⚠️ **Note the words: "يثبت" — shown/proved. The article creates a
   rule of exclusion, not an automatic presumption. That is why the *circumstances* of the arrest are the case.**
3. ✅ **Constitution Art. 54, third sentence:** «**ولا يبدأ التحقيق معه إلا فى حضور محاميه، فإن لم يكن له محام، نُدب
   له محام**» — no investigation begins without his lawyer present; if he has none, one is **appointed**.
4. ✅ **CrPC Art. 124 (§5.2)** — no interrogation or confrontation in a felony or a mandatory-imprisonment
   misdemeanour **before the accused's lawyer is CALLED**; if none, the investigator **must appoint one**.
   And ✅ **CrPC Art. 125:** «**ولا يجوز التفريق بين المتهم ومحاميه أثناء التحقيق**» — the accused and his lawyer
   **may never be separated** during investigation.

⚠️ ⚠️ **The honest limit on all of this, stated so an agent does not over-promise:** I found **no article in
Law 150/1950 that says in terms "the accused must be warned of his right to silence."** The **right** is in
**Constitution Art. 55**; the **warning (تنبيهه إلى أن من حقه الصمت)** appears explicitly only in **Art. 104 of the
NEW code** — see §17.6. **Do not tell a client in 2026 that the 1950 code required a silence warning.**
*(The 1950 code's protection is procedural — no interrogation without counsel, no separation — not a warning.)*

## 14.2 The consequences of a coerced confession, in the three places they bite

| Where | Effect | Text |
|---|---|---|
| **Admissibility** | the statement is **void and cannot be relied upon** | ✅ **Const. Art. 55** |
| **Criminal liability of the torturer** | **aggravated imprisonment or 3–10 years**; **the premeditated-murder penalty if the victim dies**; **no prescription at all** | ✅ **Penal Code Art. 126** + ✅ **Const. Art. 52** + ✅ **CrPC Art. 15 para. 2** |
| **Nullity of the procedure** | the interrogation is a **nullity** because it was held without counsel or after the accused was separated from his counsel — and it is also an **independent ground of cassation** | ✅ **CrPC Arts. 124, 125**; ✅ **Law 57/1959 Art. 30(3)** («بطلان في الإجراءات أثّر في الحكم») |

⚠️ **Practical reading of the three together, and it is the strongest available position:** if a confession was
obtained in an interrogation held **without the lawyer having been called** (Art. 124) or with the **accused
separated from his lawyer** (Art. 125), the prosecution does **not** need to prove torture at all — **the procedure
is void independently**, and that nullity is a cassation ground. **Lead with the procedure, not with the bruise.**

# 15. Penal exposure for unlawful arrest and private detention — Penal Code Arts. 126–131, 280–282

**Source:** Penal Code (Law 58/1937) consolidated Arabic text — `https://mstsharh.net/قانون-العقوبات-المصري-رقم-58-لسنة-1937/`,
downloaded, tag-stripped, **read verbatim article by article**. **As of:** 2026-10-19.

## 15.1 Unlawful arrest, imprisonment and detention by a private person — Arts. 280–282

- ✅ **Art. 280** — «**كل من قبض على اى شخص او حبسه او حجزه بدون امر احد الحكام المختصين بذلك وفي غير الاحوال التي
  تصرح فيها القوانين واللوايح بالقبض على ذوي الشبهة يعاقب بالحبس او بغرامة لا تتجاوز مايتي جنيه مصري.**»
  → **imprisonment (الحبس) OR a fine not exceeding EGP 200 (مايتي جنيه مصري).** ⚠️ **The alternative — "or" — is
  the point, and EGP 200 is a 1937 figure that has never been raised.**
- ✅ **Art. 281** — «**يعاقب ايضا بالحبس مدة لا تزيد على سنتين كل شخص اعار محلا للحبس او الحجز غير الجايزين مع
  علمه بذلك.**» → **up to TWO YEARS' imprisonment** for **lending a place for the unlawful imprisonment or
  detention, KNOWING that.** *This is the article to cite when someone offers premises for a private "holding".*
- ✅ **Art. 282 — the escalation (⭐):** «إذا حصل القبض في الحالة المبينة بالمادة 280 من شخص **تزيا بدون وجه حق
  بزي مستخدمي الحكومة او اتصف بصفة كاذبة او ابرز امراً مزوراً مدعياً صدوره من طرف الحكومة** يعاقب بالسجن،
  ويحكم في جميع الاحوال **بالسجن المشدد على من قبض على شخص بدون وجه حق وهدده بالقتل او عذبه بالتعذيبات
  البدنية**.»
  → Two escalations:
  1. **Imprisonment (السجن)** — where the offender **unlawfully wore a government employee's uniform**, or
     **claimed a false capacity**, or **produced a forged order claiming it came from the Government.**
  2. **AGGRAVATED IMPRISONMENT (السجن المشدد) in ALL cases** — where the person unlawfully arrested was
     **threatened with DEATH** or **subjected to PHYSICAL TORTURE.**
  ⚠️ **So a private kidnapper/security-guard case with a threat or a beating is not a EGP 200 matter — it is a
  السجن المشدد matter, which is a felony-class penalty and it does not prescribe (Art. 15 para. 2).**

## 15.2 Abuse of authority — the official side

- ✅ **Art. 126 — torture to obtain a confession (⭐):** «**كل موظف او مستخدم عمومي امر بتعذيب متهم او فعل ذلك بنفسه
  لحمله على الاعتراف يعاقب بالسجن المشدد او السجن من ثلاث سنوات الى عشر. واذا مات المجني عليه يحكم بالعقوبة
  المقررة للقتل عمداً.**» → **Ordering** the torture of a suspect **or doing it himself** to make him confess is
  punished by **السجن المشدد, or imprisonment of THREE TO TEN YEARS**; **if the victim dies, the penalty for
  premeditated murder applies.**
  ⚠️ **Both the person who ORDERS and the person who ACTS are caught by the same sentence, and both are felonies —
  so no prescription (Art. 15 para. 2) and no Constitution Art. 52 limitation.**
  ⚠️ **⚠️ PARTIAL:** the wider *aggravated* limb torturers are sometimes said to fall under (Officials' Criminal
  Liability / Penal Code **Art. 282**'s aggravated limb) is quoted above in its own place. **I read Art. 126 and
  Art. 282 in full; I did not read PC Art. 282's amendment chain.** Cite the words, not the history.
- ✅ **Art. 127** — «يعاقب بالسجن كل موظف عام وكل شخص مكلف بخدمة عامة امر بعقاب المحكوم عليه او عاقبه بنفسه بأشد
  من العقوبة المحكوم بها عليه قانوناً او بعقوبة لم يحكم بها عليه.» → **السجن** for a public official or person
  charged with a public service who **orders the punishment of a sentenced person, or punishes him himself, more
  severely than the sentence, or with a penalty not imposed.**
- ✅ **Art. 128** — **imprisonment or a fine not exceeding EGP 200** for a public official or public employee, **or
  any person charged with a public service**, who **enters a person's home relying on his office without consent**,
  outside the cases the law permits or without observing the rules laid down in it.
- ✅ **Art. 129** — **imprisonment up to ONE YEAR, or a fine not exceeding EGP 200**, for a public official, public
  employee, or person charged with a public service who **uses cruelty against people relying on his office** in a
  way that **violates their honour or causes them bodily pain.**
- ✅ **Art. 130** — **up to TWO YEARS' imprisonment plus dismissal (العزل)**, plus restitution of the property or
  its value, for a public official or employee who, **relying on the authority of his office, buys** a movable or
  immovable **by coercion of the owner**, or seizes it wrongfully, or **coerces the owner to sell it to another**.
- ✅ **Art. 131** — **up to TWO YEARS plus dismissal**, plus the value of the wages due, for a public official who
  **imposes on people work not authorised by law** or **uses people in works other than those for which they were
  gathered under the law**.
- ✅ **Art. 123** — **imprisonment plus dismissal (الحبس والعزل)** for a public official who **uses the authority of
  his office to stop the execution of government orders or of statutory or regulatory judgments**, or to **delay
  collection of public money or fees**, or to **stop execution of a judgment or order** of a court or competent
  authority; and the same for a public official who **deliberately refuses to execute a judgment or order after
  EIGHT DAYS from being notified by a bailiff (بعد مضي ثمانية ايام من انذاره على يد محضر)**, where execution falls
  within his competence.
  ⚠️ **Art. 123 is the exception carved out of Art. 232 (the direct-accusation bar for officials) — see §10.1. It is
  the only official-offence route a civil claimant can use directly.**

# 16. The Emergency Law, exceptional courts, and what is left of them

**Citation:** **قرار رئيس الجمهورية بالقانون رقم ١٦٢ لسنة ١٩٥٨ بشأن حالة الطوارئ** — Presidential Decree-Law No. 162
of 1958 on the state of emergency, **27 September 1958**.
**Status:** ⚠️ **the LAW is still on the statute book**; ⚠️ **the STATE OF EMERGENCY itself lapsed and was NOT
extended in October 2021.**
**Source:** the Arabic text as a PDF on the **ILO NATLEX** database —
`https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/111245/EGY111245.pdf` — downloaded and extracted locally.
**As of:** 2026-10-19.

## 16.1 The constitutional footing — Art. 154 (⚠️ not Art. 152)

✅ **Constitution Art. 154**, quoted in full in §1.4: the President declares the state of emergency **after
consulting the Cabinet**; the declaration **must be put to the House of Representatives within the following seven
days**; the House must approve by **an absolute majority of its members**; the declaration is **for a specified
period not exceeding THREE MONTHS**, renewable only for a similar period **and only with a two-thirds majority**;
and **the House may not be dissolved while the state of emergency is in force.**
⚠️ **The article number is 154.** Several secondary sources and at least one earlier note in this project said 152.
**Art. 152 is the presidential-appointments article.**

## 16.2 Status of the emergency regime

⚠️ **PARTIAL — read the caveat.** What I can state:

- ✅ **Law 162/1958 exists and governs the declaration**, and its constitutional counterpart is Art. 154.
- ⚠️ **The state of emergency lapsed and was not extended in October 2021.** Consequently **no new cases are referred
  to the State Security Emergency Courts (محاكم أمن الدولة طوارئ)**, **pending cases continue with appeal rights**,
  and **PM Decision 1664/2021 on referring offences to the emergency courts was suspended**.
  ⚠️ **This finding is from the project's earlier research on the reported non-extension, not from an article of
  Law 162/1958 that I re-read this session.** Treat the *dates and the mechanism* as ⚠️ **PARTIAL**; treat only the
  bare proposition "the emergency law remains enacted" as ✅.
- 🟡 **Article numbers within Law 162/1958** — I downloaded the NATLEX PDF but **did not read the articles this
  session**. **Do not cite an article of Law 162/1958.**

## 16.3 The constitutional bar on exceptional courts — and how it is worked around

✅ **Constitution Art. 97, final clause:** «**ولا يحاكم شخص إلا أمام قاضيه الطبيعى، والمحاكم الاستثنائية محظورة.**»
— no one is tried except before his **natural judge**, and **exceptional courts are prohibited.**
⚠️ **The honest reading, which an agent must not soften:** the bar is real and is the constitutional hook any defence
uses; **but Egypt's emergency and terrorism legislation has repeatedly been justified on the footing that a court
with a statutory basis and a defined subject-matter is not an "exceptional court" within Art. 97.** **Do not tell a
client that a case can never be heard by a security court.** Say: the constitutional argument is available, and the
controlling texts must be read for the specific case.
✅ **Constitution Art. 204** (quoted in §1.4) is a **closed list** of the situations in which a **civilian** may be
tried before the **military judiciary**. Anything outside it is a constitutional objection.

## 16.4 What is NOT established here — say so rather than guess

🟡 **The continued operation, jurisdiction and current docket of the State Security Courts (محاكم أمن الدولة) that are
not the emergency courts** — I did **not** verify the statute establishing them, their current membership, or whether
their subject-matter has been folded into the ordinary felony circuits. **Do not name them as an available forum.**
🟡 **Whether any terrorism-specific chamber or circuit is currently operating** — the felonies of **Book Two, Parts
One, Two, Two bis, Three and Four** of the Penal Code have a **designated circuit** under ✅ **CrPC Art. 366**
(§10.3), which is an ordinary **محكمة جنايات أول درجة** circuit with a designated bench, **not an exceptional
court**. **That is the verifiable answer to "where does a terrorism case go" — a designated circuit of the ordinary
felony court.**

## 17.3 Criminal action and the complaint — new Arts. 1–11 ✅ read verbatim

- ✅ **New Art. 1** — «**تتولى النيابة العامة التحقيق، وتحريك، ومباشرة الدعوى الجنائية**، ولا تتخذ هذه الإجراءات من
  غيرها إلا في الأحوال المحددة في القانون. **ولا يجوز ترك الدعوى الجنائية، أو وقفها، أو تعطيل سيرها إلا في
  الأحوال المحددة في القانون.**»
- ✅ **New Art. 2** — the **Attorney-General** exercises it **himself or through a member of the Prosecution**.
- ✅ **New Art. 3 — the complaint clock changes:** the same core list (**PC Arts. 185, 274, 277, 279, 292, 293, 303,
  306, 307, 308** and other cases the law states), the complaint may be **oral or written**, by the victim **or his
  special agent**, to the **Prosecution or a judicial officer** — and then: «**ولا تقبل الشكوى بعد تسعين يومًا من
  يوم علم المجني عليه بالجريمة وبمرتكبها**» — **NOT accepted after NINETY DAYS from the day the victim learned of
  the crime and of the perpetrator.** ⚠️ **Same length as today's three months; different unit and textually a
  different clock. Do not present the change as an extension — present it as a restatement.**
- ✅ **New Art. 4** — multiple victims: one suffices; multiple accused: a complaint against one counts against the
  rest. *(Identical to Art. 4 in force.)*
- ✅ **New Art. 7** — «**ينقضي الحق في الشكوى بموت المجني عليه**، وإذا حدث الموت **بعد تقديم الشكوى فلا يؤثر على
  سير الدعوى الجنائية**.» ⚠️ **A real change:** the right to complain dies with the victim, **but a death AFTER the
  complaint does not affect the case.**
- ✅ **New Art. 8** — **written request of the Minister of Justice** for Penal Code **Arts. 181 and 182.**
- ✅ **New Art. 9** — **no criminal action** in the **Penal Code Art. 116 bis (a)** offences … *(the article continues
  into text the proxy extraction garbled; the opening is clear and the rest is not — **PARTIAL**).*
- ✅ **New Art. 11** — investigation steps require the **complaint, request or permission** where the law makes them a
  condition; **the exception is now narrower and expressly enumerated**: investigation may proceed without them in
  offences under **PC Arts. 185, 303, 306, 307, 308** where the victim is a **public official**, a person with a
  **public representative capacity**, or **charged with a public service**, and the crime was committed **because of**
  that office, representation or service. ⚠️ **Compare Art. 9 in force, which lists Art. 185, 302, 306, 307, 308 —
  so the new article drops 302 and adds 303.** **Do not apply the old list to the new code.**
- ✅ **New Art. 15** — the **felony court in both degrees, or the Court of Cassation**, may itself institute a criminal
  action where acts tend to **breach its orders, the respect due to it, or influence its adjudication or its
  witnesses**, in connection with a request or case before it.

## 17.4 Limitation of the criminal action — new Arts. 16–19 ✅ read verbatim

- ✅ **New Art. 16** — the criminal action **is extinguished by the accused's death, by lapse of time, by a final
  judgment, by general amnesty, by settlement or reconciliation (الصلح أو التصالح), or by the other causes the law
  states.** **The accused's death during the case does not prevent a confiscation judgment** in the case stated in
  **Penal Code Art. 30 para. 2**; and **extinction of the criminal action after it was brought does not prevent a
  restitution judgment, or a judgment of the financial penalties in Parts Three and Four of Book Two of the Penal
  Code.** ⚠️ **That last clause is why a حفظ or a time-bar does not automatically wipe the money.**
- ✅ **New Art. 17 — the periods are UNCHANGED, the no-prescription list GROWS:**
  «تنقضي الدعوى الجنائية في مواد الجنايات بمضي **عشر سنين**، وفي مواد الجنح بمضي **ثلاث سنين**، وفي مواد المخالفات
  بمضي **سنة**…
  واستثناءً من حكم الفقرة الأولى، لا تنقضي بمضي المدة الدعوى الجنائية الناشئة عن الجرائم المنصوص عليها في المواد
  **١١٧، ١٢٦، ١٢٧، ١٦١ مكررًا، ٢٨٠، ٢٨١، ٢٨٢، ٣٠٩ مكررًا، ٣٠٩ مكررًا (أ)**، والجرائم المنصوص عليها في **الباب
  الأول** و**القسم الأول من الباب الثاني** من الكتاب الثاني من قانون العقوبات.»
  🚨 **The additions are the story: `161 bis`, `280`, `281` and `282` join the no-prescription list, and `Book Two
  Part One` joins `Part Two Section One`.** **Consequence: unlawful arrest (PC 280), lending premises for unlawful
  detention (PC 281) and aggravated unlawful arrest with a death threat or physical torture (PC 282) will NO LONGER
  PRESCRIBE once the new code applies.** ⚠️ *Today, under Art. 15, only **282** is in the list — 280 and 281 are not.*
- ✅ **New Art. 18** — «**لا يوقف سريان المدة التي تنقضي بها الدعوى الجنائية لأي سبب.**» **No suspension for any
  reason** — identical to Art. 16 in force.
- ✅ **New Art. 19** — **interruption** by **acts of investigation, accusation, trial, the criminal order, or acts of
  evidence-gathering taken against the accused or officially notified to him**; the period runs anew from the day of
  interruption, and **from the date of the LAST act** where there are several; and **interruption as to one accused
  interrupts as to the rest** even if no interrupting act was taken against them. *(Substantively identical to
  Arts. 17–18 in force, merged into one article.)*

## 17.5 Pretrial detention under the new code — new Arts. 112–124 ✅ read verbatim

**This is the part an agent will be asked about, and the numbers are DIFFERENT from today's.**

- ✅ **New Art. 112** — the Prosecution **must interrogate an arrested accused IMMEDIATELY**; if that is impossible he
  is **deposited in a reform-and-rehabilitation centre or a detention place until interrogated, and the deposit may
  NOT exceed 24 HOURS**; after that the centre's manager **must send him to the Prosecution for immediate
  interrogation, failing which a release order must issue.** ⚠️ **New exception:** where interrogation is impossible
  **because his instructed or appointed lawyer is absent** in a **detainable offence**, the Prosecution may order his
  deposit until interrogation **in the presence of a lawyer** — **and the deposit then follows the same rules as
  pretrial detention as to grounds, procedure, duration, extension and appeal.**
- ✅ **New Art. 113 — the Prosecution's own order (⭐):** where **the evidence is sufficient after interrogation** and
  the offence is a **felony** or a **misdemeanour punished by imprisonment of at least one year**, a prosecution
  member **of at least deputy-prosecutor rank (وكيل نيابة على الأقل)** may, **after hearing the defence**, issue a
  **REASONED order** detaining him **for a maximum of FOUR DAYS** following the arrest or hand-over, **if one of**
  these grounds applies:
  1. the offence is **flagrante and the sentence must be executed on issue**;
  2. **fear of flight**;
  3. **fear of harm to the investigation** — influencing the victim or witnesses, tampering with physical evidence
     or indications, or **making agreements with the other offenders to change the truth or efface its traces**;
  4. **prevention of a serious breach of security and public order** that may follow from the gravity of the crime.
  And: **in all cases he may be detained if he has no known fixed residence in Egypt** and the offence is a felony
  or an imprisonable misdemeanour. *(Essentially Art. 134 in force, with the residence ground added as an express
  unlimited ground.)*
- ✅ **New Art. 114 — SEVEN alternatives to detention (was three):**
  1. **not leaving his home or his locality**;
  2. **reporting to a police station at set times**;
  3. **being banned from specified places**;
  4. 🆕 **not leaving a defined GEOGRAPHICAL AREA without the Prosecution's permission**;
  5. 🆕 **refraining from receiving, meeting or contacting specified persons in any way**;
  6. 🆕 🆕 **a TEMPORARY BAN on possessing or keeping FIREARMS and their ammunition, and SURRENDERING them to the
     police station of his residence's district**;
  7. 🆕 🆕 🆕 **the use of TECHNICAL MEANS to TRACK the accused** where the operational conditions exist, **by a
     decision of the Minister of Justice in co-ordination with the Ministers of Interior and Communications.**
  ⚠️ **Items 6 and 7 are new species of restriction in Egyptian criminal procedure and item 7 is electronic
  monitoring in all but name.**
- ✅ **New Art. 117** — prosecution members of **at least chief-prosecutor rank** have **investigating-judge powers**
  in felony investigations — **excluding** the felonies of **Part Three of Book Two** of the Penal Code — **as to the
  DURATION OF PRETRIAL DETENTION**; and they additionally have the **powers of the Misdemeanour Appeal Court sitting
  in the consultation chamber (under new Art. 123)** for the crimes of **Book Two, Part One and Part Two Section
  One** — **subject to the detention not exceeding FIFTEEN DAYS at a time.**
  *(⚠️ Note the exclusion: **Part Three of Book Two is carved out** of the prosecutor's detention-extension power.
  And new Art. 117 also contains the **communications-interception** power — social-media content not publicly
  available, e-mail, textual/audio/visual messages on phones and devices, seizing the media carrying them, and
  **recordings of conversations held in a private place** — **renewable** by a similar order, and the constitutional
  hook is Art. 57.)*
- ✅ **New Art. 118** — a **copy of the detention order must be handed to the manager of the reform centre or
  detention place against signature on the original.** *(Same as Art. 138 in force.)*
- ✅ **New Art. 119 — a NEW protection (⭐):** «**لا يجوز للقائم على إدارة مركز الإصلاح والتأهيل أو أماكن الاحتجاز أن
  يسمح لأحد من رجال السلطة العامة أو مأموري الضبط القضائي بأن يتصل بنفسه أو بواسطة غيره بالمحبوس احتياطيًا داخل ذلك
  المركز أو المكان إلا بإذن كتابي من النيابة العامة**، وعليه أن يدون في الدفتر المعد لذلك اسم الشخص الذي سُمح له
  ووقت المقابلة وتاريخ ومضمون الإذن، **ويقع باطلًا كل إجراء يخالف ذلك**.» — **no public-force member or judicial
  officer may have contact with the detained accused without the Prosecution's WRITTEN permission**, the register
  must record who, when and what, and **any step taken in breach is VOID.** ⚠️ **There is no equivalent article in
  Law 150/1950.** This is the single most significant addition to the detention regime.
- ✅ **New Art. 120** — the Prosecution may at all times order **no contact with other detainees and no visits** —
  «**وذلك دون الإخلال بحق المتهم في الاتصال دائمًا بالمدافع عنه دون حضور أحد**» — **without prejudice to the accused's
  right to contact his defence counsel AT ALL TIMES, WITH NOBODY PRESENT.** *(Same substance as Art. 141 in force.)*
- ✅ **New Art. 121 — the extension tier (⭐):** if the Prosecution wants to extend, **before the FOUR DAYS of Art. 113
  expire** it **must put the papers before the DISTRICT JUDGE (القاضي الجزئي)**, who issues a **REASONED order after
  hearing the Prosecution and the accused**, either **releasing him** or **extending detention for successive
  periods of no more than FIFTEEN DAYS EACH, with a TOTAL of no more than FORTY-FIVE DAYS.**
  And ✅ **the misdemeanour rule carried over:** «**وفي مواد الجنح يجب الإفراج حتمًا عن المتهم المقبوض عليه بعد مرور
  ثمانية أيام من تاريخ استجوابه إذا كان له محل إقامة معروف في مصر وكان الحد الأقصى للعقوبة المقررة قانونًا لا يتجاوز
  سنة واحدة ولم يكن عائدًا أو سبق الحكم عليه بالحبس أكثر من سنة.**» — **MANDATORY release in misdemeanours after
  EIGHT DAYS from the interrogation** if he has a **known residence in Egypt**, the **statutory maximum penalty does
  not exceed one year**, and he is **not a repeat offender previously sentenced to imprisonment of more than a year.**
  *(Same as Art. 142 in force.)*
- ✅ **New Art. 122** — an **alternative-measure order** is **effective for the TEN DAYS following the start of its
  execution**, and the **same rules as pretrial detention apply** to its duration, maximum and appeal.
- ✅ **New Art. 123 — the consultation chamber, and a NEW 90-day Attorney-General review:** where the Prosecution
  wants to extend **beyond the Arts. 121/122 limits**, or in the **new Art. 117 para. 3** cases, it **must put the
  papers before the Misdemeanour Appeal Court sitting in the consultation chamber** before the detention expires, for
  a **reasoned order after hearing the Prosecution and the accused** — either **extending for successive periods of
  no more than FORTY-FIVE DAYS EACH** where the investigation so requires, or **releasing** him, or **ending the
  measure**. And 🆕 **«ومع ذلك يتعين عرض الأمر على النائب العام كلما انقضى تسعون يومًا على حبس المتهم بجناية
  احتياطيًا أو مده»** — **the order MUST be put before the ATTORNEY-GENERAL every time NINETY DAYS elapse on the
  detention of a person on a FELONY charge**, for him to take the steps he considers capable of finishing the
  investigation. ✅ *Same 90-day AG review as Art. 143 para. 2 in force, but **expressly limited to felonies** — the
  article in force says «ثلاثة شهور» without that word.*
- ✅ **New Art. 124 — THE CAPS, AND THEY ARE LOWER (⭐ the single most-cited change):** read verbatim —
  «**لا يجوز أن تزيد مدة الحبس الاحتياطي أو التدبير على ثلاثة أشهر في مواد الجنح** ما لم يكن المتهم قد أعلن بإحالته
  إلى المحكمة المختصة قبل انتهاء هذه المدة، ويجب على النيابة العامة في هذه الحالة أن **تعرض أمر الحبس أو التدبير
  خلال خمسة أيام على الأكثر** من تاريخ الإعلان بالإحالة إلى المحكمة المختصة وفقًا للأحكام الفقرة الأولى من المادة
  **١٣٢** من هذا القانون… **وإلا وجب الإفراج عن المتهم**… **فإذا كانت التهمة المنسوبة إليه جناية فلا يجوز أن تزيد
  مدة الحبس الاحتياطي أو التدبير على خمسة أشهر** إلا بعد الحصول قبل انقضائها على **أمر من المحكمة المختصة بمد الحبس
  أو التدبير مدة لا تزيد على خمسة وأربعين يومًا قابلة للتجديد**… وإلا وجب الإفراج…
  **وفي جميع الأحوال، لا يجوز أن تجاوز مدة الحبس الاحتياطي في مرحلة التحقيق الابتدائي وسائر مراحل الدعوى الجنائية
  ثلث الحد الأقصى للعقوبة السالبة للحرية، بحيث لا تجاوز أربعة أشهر في الجنح واثني عشر شهرًا في الجنايات، وثمانية
  عشر شهرًا إذا كانت العقوبة المقررة للجريمة هي السجن المؤبد أو الإعدام، ويجوز لمحكمة الجنايات المستأنفة ولمحكمة
  النقض إذا كان الحكم صادرًا بالإعدام أو السجن المؤبد أن تأمر بحبس المتهم احتياطيًا لمدة خمسة وأربعين يومًا قابلة
  للتجديد لمدد أخرى بما لا يجاوز سنتين.**»

  **Side by side — the change, and it is the thing to get right:**

  | | **Law 150/1950 Art. 143 (IN FORCE TODAY)** | **Law 174/2025 Art. 124 (from 1 Oct 2027)** |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Misdemeanours** | **6 months** | **4 months** |
  | **Felonies** | **18 months** | **12 months** |
  | **Life imprisonment / death** | **2 years** | **18 months** |
  | **General 3-month / 5-month gateways** | 3 months general, 5 months for a felony | **identical** — 3 months general, 5 months for a felony |
  | **Cassation / appellate-felony-court override** | 45 days, renewable, **without** any ceiling | 45 days, renewable, **up to a maximum of 2 YEARS** |

  ⚠️ **Two traps in one table.** (1) **The three-month and five-month gateways do NOT change** — an agent who "knows
  the new code" and skips the 5-month gateway will be wrong. (2) **The override changes in the opposite direction
  from everything else:** today it has **no ceiling**; from 2027 it **is capped at two years.** So for a
  murder or death-penalty case the new code is **stricter**, not more generous.

## 17.6 Interrogation, counsel and surveillance under the new code — new Arts. 104, 105, 117 ✅ read verbatim

- ✅ **New Art. 104 — first appearance (⭐ the article the new code adds and the old one lacks):** «**يجب على عضو
  النيابة العامة عند حضور المتهم لأول مرة في التحقيق أن يدون جميع البيانات الخاصة بإثبات شخصيته، ويحيطه بحقوقه
  كتابة وبالتهمة المنسوبة إليه، ويثبت في المحضر ما قد يبديه في شأنها من أقوال، وأن يمكنه من الاتصال بذويه ومحاميه
  وذلك بعد تنبيهه إلى أن من حقه الصمت، وذلك كله مع مراعاة توفير المساعدة اللازمة للأشخاص ذوي الإعاقة والمسنين وفقًا
  للإجراءات المقررة قانونًا.**»
  → open a record of his identity; **inform him of his rights IN WRITING AND of the charge**; record his statements;
  **enable him to contact his FAMILY and his LAWYER**; and 🆕 **all of that AFTER WARNING HIM THAT HE HAS THE RIGHT
  TO REMAIN SILENT**; with **assistance for persons with disabilities and the elderly.**
  ⚠️ ⚠️ **This is the express statutory silence warning in Egyptian law, and it is NEW. The equivalent article in
  force (Art. 123) does not contain it. See §14.1.**
- ✅ **New Art. 105** — **no interrogation and no confrontation before CALLING the accused's lawyer**; **if he has
  none, or his lawyer does not attend after being invited, the investigator MUST APPOINT ONE of his own motion**; an
  urgency route where the investigator asks the **Bar's branch syndicate (نقابة المحامين الفرعية)** to appoint a
  lawyer for an immediate interrogation; **if the lawyer does not attend, the interrogation proceeds** — but **the
  instructed or appointed lawyer may attend if he arrives before it ends and inspect what was done in his absence**;
  the accused **must state his lawyer's name** in the investigation record, at the Prosecution's criminal registry, or
  to the officer in charge of the place of detention, and **the lawyer may do it himself**; **the lawyer may record
  defences, requests or observations**; and **the investigator issues, on the appointed lawyer's request, an order
  ASSESSING HIS FEES** — **guided by the fee schedule issued by decision of the Minister of Justice after consulting
  the Bar** — and **the fees have the force of judicial fees.**
- ✅ **New Art. 117 — surveillance and communications interception (the new code's Art. 206 / 206 bis, ⭐ the privacy
  gate).** It is **one long article** and it does three separate things. This is the article a client with a
  communications-privacy problem will be subject to **after 1 October 2027**; **§5.5 is what governs today.**
  - **1. The surveillance power.** **Members of the Public Prosecution of at least chief-prosecutor rank
    (رئيس نيابة على الأقل)**, investigating the **felonies in Book Two, Parts ONE, TWO, TWO bis, THREE and FOUR of the
    Penal Code**, have — in addition to the Prosecution's established competences — the power to issue **a REASONED
    ORDER (أمر مسبب) for a period not exceeding THIRTY DAYS** to:
    - **seize letters, messages, telegrams, newspapers, publications and parcels** (ضبط الخطابات والرسائل والبرقيات
      والجرائد والمطبوعات والطروف);
    - **monitor wired and wireless communications** (مراقبة الاتصالات السلكية واللاسلكية);
    - 🆕 **monitor SOCIAL-MEDIA ACCOUNTS and their various contents that are NOT AVAILABLE TO THE PUBLIC, e-mail, and
      text, audio or image messages on phones and devices and any other technical means** (حسابات مواقع التواصل
      الاجتماعي ومحتوياتها المختلفة غير المتاحة للجميع، والبريد الإلكتروني، والرسائل النصية أو المسموعة أو المصورة
      على الهواتف والأجهزة وأي وسيلة تقنية أخرى) — **and to seize the media containing them**;
    - **or make recordings of conversations that took place in a PRIVATE PLACE** where that serves the appearance of
      the truth.
    **And the order may be RENEWED for one or more similar periods.** ⚠️ **Note the transfer of the gate-keeper:**
    under **Art. 206 in force** the prior reasoned order comes from **the district judge (القاضي الجزئي)** — §5.5.
    Under the new article it is issued **by the Prosecution itself**, at chief-prosecutor rank. **That is a real
    change in who checks the wiretap, and it is the single most consequential thing in new Art. 117.**
  - **2. The detention-duration power.** The same members have, in these investigations, **the power of the district
    judge (القاضي الجزئي) over the DURATION of pretrial detention** — **EXCEPT** for the felonies in **Book Two,
    Part Three** of the Penal Code.
  - **3. The consultation-chamber power.** They additionally have **the power of the `محكمة الجنح المستأنفة` sitting in
    the consultation chamber (غرفة المشورة) referred to in Art. 123 of this law**, when investigating the offences in
    **Book Two, PART ONE and SECTION ONE OF PART TWO** of the Penal Code — **on condition that the detention period
    does not exceed FIFTEEN DAYS each time** (بشرط ألا تزيد مدة الحبس في كل مرة على خمسة عشر يومًا).
  - ⚠️ **PARTIAL, and this is the caveat to carry:** the **proxy extraction renders this article without its paragraph
    breaks**, so I **cannot state which clause is paragraph 2, 3 or 4.** New Art. 123 (the detention-extension article)
    cross-refers to «**الفقرة الثالثة من المادة ١١٧**», which pins **one** of them as para. 3 — but I did **not**
    verify the numbering. ⚠️ **Also: the scope list is Parts One, Two, Two bis, THREE and Four — which is ONE PART
    WIDER than Art. 206 bis in force (Parts One, Two, Two bis and Four, §5.5).** 🟡 **I have not verified the subject
    matter of each Part from the Penal Code's own table of contents**, so **do not label the Parts by subject**; cite
    the Part numbers as the article does.

## 17.7 Release and bail under the new code — new Arts. 125–131 ✅ read verbatim

- ✅ **New Art. 125** — the **Prosecution may order PROVISIONAL RELEASE (إفراج مؤقت)** or termination of the measure
  **at any time**, on its own motion or on the accused's request, **with or without bail**, **on condition that he
  undertakes to appear whenever the Prosecution requires**.
- ✅ **New Art. 126** — where release is **not mandatory**, **he is not released with or without security until he has
  designated a domicile, a MOBILE PHONE NUMBER, or an E-MAIL ADDRESS** as set out in **Art. 72 para. 2.**
  🆕 **The phone number and e-mail address are new: the "domicile" condition has been modernised for electronic
  notification.**
- ✅ **New Art. 127** — release or termination **may be made conditional on a SURETY (كفالة)**, the amount fixed by the
  **prosecution member, the District Judge, or the Misdemeanour Appeal Court in the consultation chamber**, as the
  case may be. **HALF (نصف) is allocated as the sanction for non-appearance** at any step of the investigation, the
  case, the presentation for execution, and the other duties imposed on him; **the other half to government
  expenses first, then to any financial penalties imposed.** **If the surety is fixed WITHOUT ALLOCATION, it secures
  appearance, non-evasion of execution and the other duties.** ⚠️ **The old Art. 146 did not fix the split at half;
  the new article does.** *Useful for a client: the unallocated half is what he gets back.*
- ✅ **New Art. 128** — payment by the **accused or by someone else**, **in cash, government or government-guaranteed
  bonds, an accepted bank cheque, a bank guarantee letter, or any NON-CASH PAYMENT MEANS under the Law Regulating
  the Use of Non-Cash Payment Means, Law No. 18 of 2019**; and **a solvent third party's undertaking to pay, or a
  bank guarantee letter, is accepted, taken down in the investigation record or by a registry report — and that
  record or report HAS THE FORCE OF AN ENFORCEABLE INSTRUMENT.**
  🆕 **The reference to Law 18/2019 is how a fresh Egyptian code modernises a bail provision — note it, because an
  agent asked "can I pay bail by transfer" needs it.**
- ✅ **New Art. 129** — **if the accused fails to perform one of the obligations without acceptable excuse, the FIRST
  part of the surety becomes the property of the Government BY A REASONED DECISION (بقرار مسبب) of the competent
  investigation or trial authority**; **the SECOND part is returned if a no-case decision or an acquittal is made.**
  ⚠️ **This is a real change: the old Art. 148 made the first part state property «بغير حكم» — without any judgment.
  The new article requires a REASONED DECISION.** **Do not describe the old rule as if it survives.**
- ✅ **New Art. 130** — where his **condition does not allow a surety**, he may be required to **report to the
  competent police station at set times, having regard to his special circumstances**; and he may be **asked to
  choose a place of residence other than the scene of the crime**, or **banned from frequenting a specified place.**
  *(Substantively Art. 149 in force.)*
- ✅ **New Art. 131** — **a release order does NOT prevent a NEW prosecution order of ARREST or DETENTION** where the
  **evidence against him has strengthened**, or **he has breached the duties imposed on him**, or **circumstances
  require that step** — **without prejudice to Arts. 123 and 124.** *(Art. 150 in force.)*

## 17.8 Trial, the felony appeal and cassation under the new code

- ✅ **New Art. 407 — the felony appeal deadline is the SAME as today:** «**يحصل الاستئناف بتقرير في قلم كتاب المحكمة
  التي أصدرت الحكم، وذلك خلال أربعين يومًا من تاريخ صدور الحكم.** فإذا كان مرفوعًا من النيابة العامة يجب أن يكون
  التقرير موقعًا من **محام عام على الأقل**. وإذا كان الاستئناف مرفوعًا من هيئة قضايا الدولة يجب أن يكون التقرير
  موقعًا من **مستشار بها على الأقل**. **وللنائب العام أن يستأنف الحكم خلال ستين يومًا** من تاريخ صدوره، وله أن يقرر
  بالاستئناف في قلم كتاب المحكمة المختصة بنظر الاستئناف.»
  → **40 days** for the report at the registry of the court that issued the judgment; **60 days** for the
  **Attorney-General**, who may also report at the **appellate** court's registry. ✅ **Identical in effect to
  Art. 419 bis 4 in force.**
- ✅ **New Art. 412** — where an **in-presence DEATH sentence** is **not appealed** within the period, the
  **Prosecution must follow Art. 46 of the Cassation Law, Law 57/1959.** 🚨 **This is the cross-reference that keeps
  Law 57/1959 alive — see §12.**
- ✅ **New Art. 413** — **an appeal does NOT suspend execution of a first-degree felony judgment UNLESS the appellate
  felony court decides to suspend it, or the sentence is DEATH.** *(Art. 419 bis 9 in force — carried forward.)*
- ✅ **New Arts. 414–420 — RETRIAL (إعادة النظر), not cassation.** Grounds: the supposed murder victim is found
  alive; two contradictory judgments on the same facts where one convict's innocence follows; a witness or expert
  convicted of **false testimony** or a **forged document** proved to have influenced the judgment; the judgment was
  based on a **civil or family-court judgment later annulled**; **and new facts or documents discovered after the
  judgment that would establish the convict's innocence.** Brought by the **Attorney-General and the convict or his
  special agent or legal representative**, or by his **relatives or spouse after his death**; **a non-prosecution
  applicant files a petition with the Attorney-General**, who **must bring it to the Court within THREE MONTHS of
  its submission**; **a security of EGP 5,000** must be deposited by a convict-applicant in the first four grounds
  (unless the **legal-aid committee** of the Court of Cassation exempts him), allocated to the **Art. 422** fine;
  the parties are notified at least **eight days** before the session; and the Court of Cassation **decides after
  hearing the parties**, **quashing and acquitting if innocence is apparent**, otherwise **remitting to a differently
  constituted bench**, or **deciding the merits itself where retrial is impossible** (death, mental disorder,
  prescription).
- 🚨 **The new code contains NO cassation chapter.** Verified directly: the phrase **«الطعن بالنقض» does not appear
  as a heading anywhere in the 139 pages**, and **«قانون حالات وإجراءات الطعن أمام محكمة النقض» appears exactly once
  — in new Art. 412.** **Therefore, when the new code applies, cassation continues under Law 57/1959 (§12)**, and the
  **60-day report period, the EGP 300 security, the EGP 20,000 misdemeanour exclusion** and the **Art. 30 grounds**
  remain the law.

## 17.9 The no-case order, referral, and appeals against orders under the new code ✅ read verbatim

**These are the new code's replacements for the حفظ الأوراق machinery in §§4.1 and 9 of this file.** ⚠️ **None of it
is in force yet; §4.1 and §9 are what govern today.**

- ✅ **New Art. 152 — the no-case order (أمر بأن لا وجه لإقامة الدعوى), the successor to حفظ الأوراق:**
  «إذا رأت النيابة العامة بعد التحقيق أنه **لا وجه لإقامة الدعوى** تصدر أمرًا بذلك، **وتأمر بالإفراج عن المتهم
  المحبوس** ما لم يكن محبوسًا لسبب آخر، **ولا يكون صدور الأمر بأن لا وجه لإقامة الدعوى في الجنايات إلا من المحامي
  العام أو من يقوم مقامه.** ويجب أن يكون الأمر **مكتوبًا**، وأن يشتمل على **الأسباب** التي بني عليها. **ويبين
  بالأمر اسم المتهم، ولقبه، وسنه، ومحل ميلاده، وسكنه، ومهنته، ورقمه القومي أو رقم وثيقة سفره، وموطنه إن كان أجنبيًا،
  والواقعة المنسوبة إليه ووصفها القانوني.** ويُعلن الأمر **للمتهم وللمجني عليه وللمدعي بالحقوق المدنية**، وإذا كان
  أيهم قد توفى يكون الإعلان **لورثته جملة دون ذكر أسمائهم** في آخر موطن كان لمورثهم.»
  → Note the two teeth: **in felonies only the Attorney-General (المحامي العام) or his deputy may issue it**, and it
  **must be reasoned and announced to the victim and the civil claimant**, not only to the accused.
  *(This is the new code's version of Arts. 61–62 in force — see §4.1.)*
- ✅ **New Art. 163 — a no-case order is a bar, with one door:** «الأمر الصادر من النيابة العامة بأن لا وجه لإقامة
  الدعوى **يمنع من العودة إلى التحقيق، إلا إذا ظهرت أدلة جديدة قبل انتهاء المدة المقررة لانقضاء الدعوى الجنائية**.»
  And **what counts as new evidence** is spelled out: **witness statements, records (محاضر) and documents carrying
  other evidence that was not presented to the Prosecution**, where they would **strengthen evidence found
  insufficient** or **add the clarification that leads to the truth.**
- ✅ **New Art. 164 — appealing the no-case order:** «**يجوز للمتهم وللمدعي بالحقوق المدنية استئناف الأمر الصادر من
  النيابة العامة بأن لا وجه لإقامة الدعوى**، ما لم يكن صادرًا في تهمة موجهة **ضد موظف أو مستخدم عام أو أحد رجال
  الضبط لجريمة وقعت منه أثناء تأدية وظيفته أو بسببها**، ما لم تكن من الجرائم المنصوص عليها في **المادة ١٢٣ من
  قانون العقوبات**.»
  - **Who:** the **accused AND the civil claimant**.
  - **How long:** **by report at the registry (تقرير في قلم الكتاب) within TEN DAYS from the date of ANNOUNCEMENT
    of the order** (الإعلان بالأمر).
  - **Where:** to the **`محكمة جنايات أول درجة` sitting in the consultation chamber` for felonies**, and to the
    **`محكمة الجنح المستأنفة` sitting in the consultation chamber for misdemeanours.**
  - ⚠️ **The official bar:** not available where the charge is against a **public official, public employee or a
    judicial officer** for a crime committed **in the course of or because of** his duty — **unless it is a Penal
    Code Art. 123 crime.**
  - **On cancelling the order**, the consultation chamber **must return the case to the Prosecution** specifying the
    offence committed, the applicable legal provision, the prosecution witness statements and the content of the other
    evidence — **for referral to the competent court.**
  - 🚨 **«وتكون القرارات الصادرة من غرفة المشورة وفق الأحكام هذا الفصل نهائية»** — **the chamber's decisions under
    this chapter are FINAL.** ⚠️ **That is a bigger change than it looks: it makes the felony consultation chamber
    the last word on un-blocking a prosecution, with no cassation from that decision.** *(Compare Art. 205 in force,
    which routes detention appeals through the ordinary order-appeal machinery in Arts. 165–168 — §6.3.)*
- ✅ **New Art. 165** — **all parties** may appeal **orders on jurisdiction questions**; **the appeal does not suspend
  the investigation**, and **a finding of no jurisdiction does not nullify the investigation steps taken**; the
  period is **ten days from the announcement of the order to the parties.**
- ✅ **New Art. 166 — the detention and extension appeal (⭐ this is the article, not 205):**
  «**يجوز للمتهم أن يستأنف الأمر الصادر بحبسه احتياطيًا أو بمد مدة الحبس**، وللنيابة العامة إذا استلزمت ضرورة
  التحقيق **أن تستأنف الأمر الصادر بالإفراج المؤقت** عن المتهم المحبوس احتياطيًا.»
  → **The accused may appeal a detention order AND an extension order.** The **Prosecution may appeal a provisional
  release order** where the investigation requires it. ⚠️ **Confirmed by reading the article: new Art. 205 is
  unrelated** — it merely applies Arts. 108, 116, 152 and 157 to the investigating judge's orders. **An agent who
  has "Art. 205" in a note from the new code has the wrong article.**
- ✅ **New Art. 157 — referral in felonies, and a NEW duty to appoint counsel (⭐):** a felony case is brought by a
  **charge report (تقرير اتهام)** from the **المحامي العام** or his deputy **to the `محكمة جنايات أول درجة`**, stating
  the accused's details, his **national number**, the offence attributed to him **with its constituent elements**,
  **all aggravating and mitigating circumstances**, and **the articles of law to be applied**, **with a list of the
  content of the witness statements and the other evidence of proof attached**;
  «**ويُندب المحامي العام من تلقاء نفسه محاميًا لكل متهم بجناية صدر أمر بإحالة إلى محكمة جنايات أول درجة إذا لم يكن
  قد وكّل محاميًا للدفاع عنه**» — **the المحامي العام MUST APPOINT A LAWYER OF HIS OWN MOTION for every accused
  charged with a felony referred to the first-degree felony court who has not instructed one.**
  And the Prosecution must **announce the referral order to the parties within the TEN DAYS following its issue.**
  ⚠️ **There is no equivalent duty in the 1950 code at this stage — the 1950 duty sits at Art. 124 (interrogation) and
  Art. 237 (trial). Read together with Const. Art. 54's final paragraph, the new article makes the appointment
  automatic and mandatory at referral in felonies.**
- ✅ **New Art. 108** — every order must include the **accused's name, surname, occupation, residence and NATIONAL
  NUMBER**, the **charge**, the **date**, the **travel-document number and domicile if he is foreign**, the
  **signature of the prosecution member and the official seal**; a **summons order** must fix a **set date**; and a
  **seizure-and-bring order (أمر الضبط والإحضار)** must instruct the **public force to arrest the accused and bring
  him before a prosecution member if he refuses to attend voluntarily.**
- ✅ **New Art. 116** — a **detention order** must additionally state, beyond the Art. 108 data, **the offence
  attributed to the accused and its prescribed penalty, the REASONS on which the order is based, and an instruction
  to the manager of the reform centre or detention place to accept the accused and place him in it** — and
  **the same rule applies to extension orders.**
  *(These are the new code's equivalents of Arts. 136 and 138 in force — §6.1.)*

## 17.10 The practical bottom line as at 19 October 2026, and what to tell a client

**For a non-lawyer in Egypt TODAY:**

1. **Law 150/1950 governs.** Everything in **§§1–16** of this file applies. Law 174/2025 applies **nothing**.
2. **A statute that is not yet in force creates no rights and imposes no duties.** You cannot rely on the new code's
   **4-month** misdemeanour cap, its **seven** alternative measures, its **Art. 119** ban on police contact with a
   detainee without written permission, its **Art. 104** silence warning, or its **Art. 157** automatic appointment of
   counsel in felonies **until 1 October 2027.** **Today those protections do not exist**, and a lawyer who argues
   them today will lose.
3. **What DOES give something today is the Constitution** — Arts. **52, 54, 55, 56, 57, 95, 96, 97, 98** (§1).
   These are in force now, they bind the police and the Prosecution now, and **Art. 54's 24-hour rule, Art. 54's
   lawyer requirement, Art. 55's right to silence and Art. 55's exclusion of coerced statements** are the four
   provisions to reach for.
4. **And the 1950 code's own protections are real but procedural** — **Art. 36** (two consecutive 24-hour periods),
   **Art. 40** (no detention without an order, humane treatment), **Art. 43** (a prosecutor who learns of unlawful
   detention must go immediately and release), **Art. 124** (no interrogation or confrontation before the lawyer is
   called; if none, one is appointed), **Art. 125** (no separation of accused and lawyer), **Art. 143** (the caps and
   the consultation chamber), **Art. 205** (the appeal against detention and extension), **Art. 152** (the victim
   cannot ask for detention or be heard on release).
5. **On the transitional question generally:** the new code's own promulgating **Arts. 2 and 3** are *exceptions*
   carved out of an assumed general rule that a procedural law applies immediately to pending cases once it
   commences. ⚠️ **I verified those two articles are transitional provisions; I did NOT verify a general
   transitional rule in the new code.** **Since the code has never commenced, the point is academic until
   1 October 2027** — but it is the question to expect on 2 October 2027.

**Four sentences to give a client, which a non-lawyer can act on:**

> The code in force in Egypt today is still **Law 150/1950**. If someone is arrested, the police must tell him why
> **immediately**, tell him his rights **in writing**, let him call **his family and a lawyer immediately**, and get
> him before the prosecution **within 24 hours** — and the prosecution must question him **within another 24 hours**
> and then either release him or send him to a judge (**Constitution Art. 54; CrPC Arts. 36, 39–40**). He **cannot be
> questioned without his lawyer**, and if he has no lawyer **the investigator must appoint one for him** —
> **"I have nothing to say until my lawyer is present" is a sentence he is entitled to say, and any statement the
> prosecution says he made while being coerced is void** (**CrPC Arts. 124–125; Constitution Art. 55**). Detention
> is then capped at **4 days by the prosecution, 45 days by the district judge, then 6 months in a misdemeanour and
> 18 months in a felony, or two years if the charge carries life or death** — with one **45-day renewable** exception
> for life/death cases, and the accused has a **right to appeal every detention and extension order**
> (**CrPC Arts. 201–205 and 143**). **A new code (Law 174/2025) exists but has been postponed to 1 October 2027 and
> has never been applied** — do not let anyone tell you it is your protection today.

# 20. Trap list for the agent — the twelve things that go wrong most

1. **Saying the new code is in force.** ❌ It is **not**. Law 150/1950 governs until **1 October 2027**, extended by **Law 160/2026**. **Every** new-code number (4 months, 12 months, 18 months, seven measures, Art. 119) is 🟡 **future law** and worthless as a present argument.
2. **Mixing the two sets of detention caps.** **6 / 18 / 24 months in force** (Art. 143) vs **4 / 12 / 18 months** (new Art. 124). The **3-month and 5-month gateways are identical in both** — so a "new code" answer that skips the 5-month gateway is wrong under either.
3. **Missing that a felony judgment is now APPEALABLE.** Since **17 January 2024** (Law 1/2024, Arts. **419 bis – 419 bis 9**), the report must be filed **within 40 days** at the **trial court's registry**. Advising cassation as the only route is a **lost appeal**.
4. **Confusing the three clocks.** **10 days** (misdemeanour appeal, Art. 406) · **40 days** (felony appeal, Art. 419 bis 4) · **60 days** (cassation, Law 57/1959 Art. 34). And the **Attorney-General's** periods are **30 days** (appeal, Art. 406) and **60 days** (felony appeal, Art. 419 bis 4) and **60 days** (cassation).
5. **Running a detention clock from the wrong start.** **Art. 36:** the officer must **hear** him **immediately** and send him within **24 h**; the Prosecution then has **another 24 h** to interrogate and must order detention or release. **Two consecutive periods, not one.**
6. **Thinking the victim can push for detention.** ✅ **Art. 152**: the victim and the civil claimant **may not apply for detention and may not be heard on release.** Their role is the **money**.
7. **Filing the victim's civil claim for the first time in the appellate court.** ✅ **Art. 251**: it may be made **until the close of the pleadings in the FIRST-instance court** — «**وليس أمام محكمة الاستئناف**». It is lost on appeal.
8. **Assuming prescription runs cleanly.** ✅ **Art. 16**: **never suspended.** ✅ **Art. 17**: interrupted by investigation, accusation, trial, criminal order, or evidence-gathering acts **taken against or officially notified to** the accused — and it restarts from the **last** act. ✅ **Art. 18**: interruption as to one accused interrupts as to **all**. **Compute from the last interrupting act, not from the offence.**
9. **Believing torture times out.** ✅ **Const. Art. 52** — **no prescription, ever.** And ✅ **CrPC Art. 15 para. 2** adds **Penal Code Arts. 117, 126, 127, 282, 309 bis and 309 bis (a)** and all of **Book Two Part Two Section One** to the same list.
10. **Treating a محضر, a بلاغ and a شكوى as one thing.** ✅ **Art. 3**: where the law requires a **شكوى**, there is **no prosecution and no investigation step** without it, **no arrest** without the complainant's declaration (Art. 39), and it **expires in three months** from knowledge of the crime **and** the offender. **Naming the wrong instrument is how a case dies.**
11. **Challenging a confession only by alleging torture.** Lead with the **procedure** instead: an interrogation held without the lawyer having been **called** (Art. 124) — or with the accused **separated** from his lawyer (Art. 125) — is **void independently**, and a nullity affecting the judgment is **an express cassation ground** (Law 57/1959 **Art. 30(3)**). **No bruise required.**
12. **Quoting new Art. 205 for the detention appeal.** ❌ **Wrong article.** It is **new Art. 166**. New Art. 205 is about applying **new Arts. 108, 116, 152 and 157** to the investigating judge's orders.
